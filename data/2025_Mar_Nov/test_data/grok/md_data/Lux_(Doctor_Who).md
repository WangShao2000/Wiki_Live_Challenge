### Appearance
In the episode "Lux", which aired on 19 April 2025, the Fifteenth Doctor (Ncuti Gatwa) and his companion Belinda Chandra (Varada Sethu) arrive in Miami, Florida, in 1952 while attempting to return Belinda to her home time in 2025. As the plot progresses, Mr. Ring-a-Ding transforms from a playful, two-dimensional figure into a more grotesque, three-dimensional entity, revealing its true identity as Lux Imperator. Lux breaks the fourth wall, entering the viewers' world and engaging with fictional fans of *Doctor Who*, in a sequence that comments on the show's fandom and leaked production details.

The Doctor defeats Lux by exploiting its vulnerability to sunlight and fire, ultimately destroying the film canisters that sustain its presence. The entity is portrayed as part of the Pantheon of Discord, a group of god-like beings introduced in prior episodes, and its defeat involves the Doctor using energy from his bi-regeneration process. Supporting characters, such as Reginald Pye (Linus Roache), interact with Lux by using it to recreate lost loved ones, adding emotional depth to the narrative.

Lux was created by showrunner and writer Russell T Davies for the fifteenth series of *Doctor Who*. The episode was directed by Amanda Brotchie and produced under executive producers including Davies, Julie Gardner, and Jane Tranter. Production involved blending live-action with animation, a process highlighted in behind-the-scenes content.

The name "Lux" derives from the Latin word for "light", reflecting the character's domain over illumination, projectors, and atomic glow. Davies integrated themes of media's societal impact, nostalgia's dangers, and historical racism, using Lux to explore how fiction can both escape and confront real-world issues.
