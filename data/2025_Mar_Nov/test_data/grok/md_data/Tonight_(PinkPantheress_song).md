### Background
PinkPantheress, whose real name is Victoria Beverley Walker, gained prominence in the early 2020s through viral TikTok videos featuring her bedroom-produced tracks that blended Y2K-era samples with drum and bass elements.[1][2] Her debut mixtape, To Hell with It (2021), and studio album, Heaven Knows (2023), established her in the pop and electronic music scenes.[3] By 2024, she began working on her second mixtape, Fancy That, which was recorded between 2024 and 2025.[4] The project features production contributions from Aksel Arvid, The Dare, and Count Baldor, among others, and runs approximately 20 minutes across nine tracks.[5][6]

"Tonight" was co-written by PinkPantheress with Brendon Urie (of Panic! at the Disco) and members of the band's early lineup, including George Ryan Ross, Jonathan Jacob Walker, and Spencer Smith.[7] Production was shared by PinkPantheress, Count Baldor, Aksel Arvid, and additional producer Phil, with mixing by Nickie Jon Pabón and mastering by Colin Leonard.[7] The song samples elements from Panic! at the Disco's work, integrating them into PinkPantheress's style.[8] Fancy That presents a narrative arc exploring relationships, guilt, emotional distance, paranoia, obsession, and unrequited love, with "Tonight" positioned as the third track in this sequence.[9]

### Composition and lyrics
"Tonight" is a dance-pop and UK garage track lasting 2 minutes and 55 seconds.[7][10] It features a fast-paced rhythm, hypnotic production with deep sub-bass, and sample-heavy instrumentals.[11][12] Critics have described its sound as blending PinkPantheress's earlier jungle and garage influences with a more contemporary pop structure, including breathy vocals and tight hooks.[13][14] The instrumentation includes elastic, groovy basslines and perky elements that contribute to a danceable feel.[15][16]

Lyrically, the song explores anticipation and physical desire in a romantic encounter.[17] The pre-chorus repeats the line "You want sex with me? Come talk to me," while the chorus emphasizes urgency with phrases such as "Talk later, that's why I'm going tonight / Your sex on fire, that's where we're going tonight."[7] The outro echoes the pre-chorus structure.[7] Reviewers have noted the lyrics' flirtatious and confident tone, marking it as one of PinkPantheress's more direct expressions of intimacy.[18][19] Within the context of Fancy That, "Tonight" contributes to the mixtape's overarching story of relationships, focusing on longing and fleeting connections amid synth-driven beats.[9]

### Release and music video
"Tonight" was released as a digital download and streaming single on 4 April 2025, serving as the lead single for Fancy That, which followed on 9 May 2025.[20][21] The mixtape's tracklist positions "Tonight" after "Girl Like Me" and before "Stars."[22] A deluxe edition of the mixtape, titled Fancy Some More?, was issued in October 2025, expanding to 31 tracks with remixes and collaborations.[23][24] This version includes a remix of "Tonight" by Basement Jaxx, which transforms the original into a "throbbing, shimmery big-room banger."[25]

The music video for "Tonight," directed with elaborate costumes and choreography, premiered on YouTube on the same day as the single's release.[26] It depicts PinkPantheress in a regency-era ballroom setting, where she disrupts a formal performance with a theatrical, high-energy display.[27][28] As of December 2025, the video has accumulated over 8 million views.[26] Social media discussions highlighted its coquette aesthetic and princess-like themes.[29]

### Critical reception
Critics offered generally positive assessments of "Tonight," focusing on its production and thematic elements. In a review of Fancy That, Flood magazine described the track as a "punky Panic! at the Disco dance," noting its contribution to the mixtape's cohesive, animated dynamics.[8] The LMC Experience characterized it as "strong in synth," delving into "longing and fleeting intimacy with increasing flirtation and desire" as part of the album's love story narrative.[9] Pitchfork, reviewing the remix edition Fancy Some More?, praised the Basement Jaxx remix for elevating the original's lightweight nature into an energetic dance track.[25]

Individual single reviews were mixed but leaned favorable. Music Talkers called it "dreamy, deep, and decadent," highlighting the hypnotic production and sub-bass.[11] A Reddit discussion on r/popheads appreciated its balance of old and new sounds, returning to UK garage and jungle roots.[13] Album of the Year user reviews described it as "danceable" with "top notch production" and "flirtatious and confident," though one noted it as "watered down" compared to her earlier work.[14][18][30] Ratings Game Music viewed it as exploring "anticipation and excitement of a long-awaited romantic encounter,"[17] while HotNewHipHop praised its completeness and groovy bassline.[15] Rolling Stone referred to it as "sultry," positioning PinkPantheress as an artist who "heats things up" in the video context.[27] Stan Island found the instrumental "perky and intriguing" but felt the vocals detracted from it.[31] Rate Your Music users expressed overall impression, classifying it as dance-pop.[10]

### Commercial performance
"Tonight" debuted on the UK Singles Chart at number 35, its peak position, and spent six weeks on the chart from 17 April to 22 May 2025.[32] On Spotify, it reached a peak of number 184 in the United Kingdom with over 569,000 streams.[33] The song also appeared on Spotify charts in other countries, though specific positions were lower.[34] It entered dance charts in the United States, as noted by Billboard in April 2025, alongside projects by other artists.[35]
