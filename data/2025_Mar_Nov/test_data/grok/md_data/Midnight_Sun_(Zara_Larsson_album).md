### Background
Larsson's previous album, Venus, was released in February 2024.[1] Following its release, she expressed interest in exploring themes tied to her Swedish heritage for her next project.[2] In interviews, Larsson described Midnight Sun as capturing the essence of a Swedish summer, where the sun remains visible late into the night, evoking both joyful and melancholic memories.[3] She stated that the album reflects a personal connection to Scandinavian culture and represents a period of self-discovery in her late twenties.[4]

The album was initially considered for the title Puss Puss, which later became the name of its closing track.[5] Larsson established her own label, Sommer House, in 2023, and Midnight Sun marks her first release under this imprint alongside Epic Records.[6]

### Recording and production
Recording for Midnight Sun took place between 2024 and 2025.[7] Larsson worked with a core team of collaborators, including British producer MNEK, who served as executive producer, and producers Margo XS and Zhone.[8] Songwriter Helena Gao contributed to multiple tracks.[9] Additional personnel included engineers such as Kaelen Russel and mixer Tom Norris.[10]

Larsson aimed for a cohesive sound that blended dance-pop with electropop influences, emphasizing high-energy production.[11] She noted that the process encouraged her to be more expressive in her songwriting and collaboration.[12] Tracks were recorded in various locations, though specific studios were not publicly detailed.

### Music and lyrics
Midnight Sun spans 10 tracks with a total runtime of 31:54.[13] The album explores themes of love, self-empowerment, ambition, and personal growth, often framed through the metaphor of endless summer nights.[14]

The opening title track "Midnight Sun" is an electropop song about embracing eternal light and freedom.[15] "Blue Moon" follows with upbeat rhythms addressing fleeting emotions.[16] "Pretty Ugly" introduces chaotic energy with punk-inspired elements, discussing contradictions in relationships.[17] "Girl's Girl" and "Crush" focus on attraction and confidence, featuring pulsating beats.[18][19]

Mid-album tracks like "Eurosummer" and "Hot & Sexy" incorporate rave and dance influences, with lyrics on seasonal romance and sensuality.[20][21] "The Ambition" reflects on career pressures and the need for validation.[22] "Saturn's Return" addresses the challenges of maturation in one's late twenties, with vulnerable lyrics such as "It feels so good to know that I don’t know what I’m doing. I love that I’m free in my naivety."[23] The album closes with "Puss Puss", a dismissive take on romance.[24]

### Release and promotion
Midnight Sun was announced in June 2025, with the tracklist revealed in September.[25] It was released on 26 September 2025 in digital, CD, and vinyl formats, including limited editions such as a clear vinyl and a Lisa Frank-themed CD.[26] A Dolby Atmos mix was also made available.[27]

Promotion included the release of three singles. "Pretty Ugly" was issued on 25 April 2025 as the lead single.[28] The title track followed on 13 June 2025.[29] "Crush" served as the third single on 15 August 2025.[30] Music videos accompanied each single.[31]

Larsson announced the Midnight Sun Tour on 2 September 2025.[32] The tour commenced on 28 October 2025 in Munich, Germany, with European dates concluding in November 2025.[33] A North American leg began on 28 February 2026 in Portland, Oregon, and ended on 12 April 2026 in Houston, Texas, with support from Amelia Moore.[34] The tour included over 30 dates across Europe and North America.[35]

### Critical reception
Midnight Sun received generally positive reviews. At Metacritic, which assigns a normalized rating out of 100 to reviews from professional publications, the album received an average score of 89 based on four reviews, indicating "universal acclaim".[36] Aggregator AnyDecentMusic? gave it 8.0 out of 10, based on their assessment of the critical consensus.[37] Album of the Year compiled a critic score of 80 based on seven reviews.[38]

DIY praised the album's "forward-thinking, lush alive-ness" and variations in electronica, dance, Scandi-pop, and R&B.[39] AllMusic described it as a "catalog highlight" for its brevity and blend of honesty with pop elements.[40] Rolling Stone noted its "high-energy dance-pop joy" that refuses to slow down.[41] Clash highlighted Larsson's focus on "starry-eyed, joyous Scandi-pop".[42] Dork commended the album for allowing Larsson to embrace her personality beyond polished radio pop.[43] God Is in the TV suggested Larsson deserves broader recognition comparable to major artists.[44] However, Spectrum Culture criticized it as a "sultry summer pop album" released too late, claiming it "misses every shot".[45]

User reception was mixed but leaned positive, with an average score of 73 on Album of the Year based on over 3,000 ratings.[46]

### Commercial performance
Midnight Sun debuted at number one on the Swedish Albums Chart, marking Larsson's third number-one album in her home country.[47] It remained in the top five for three weeks.[48]

In the United Kingdom, the album entered the UK Albums Chart at number 36.[49] In the United States, it debuted at number 119 on the Billboard 200 with 11,000 album-equivalent units in its first week.[50] It also reached number three on the Billboard Top Dance/Electronic Albums chart, a career high for Larsson.[51]

The album charted in 13 territories overall, including top-10 positions in several European markets.[52] First-week global streams exceeded 3.8 million on Spotify.[53]

Midnight Sun is the fifth studio album by Swedish singer Zara Larsson, released on 26 September 2025 by Sommer House and Epic Records. It is her fourth international studio album. The album incorporates dance-pop and electropop elements, drawing inspiration from the phenomenon of the midnight sun in Sweden. Larsson collaborated with producers such as MNEK, Margo XS, and Zhone, and songwriter Helena Gao on the project. Midnight Sun received generally positive reviews from critics and debuted at number one on the Swedish Albums Chart. It also charted within the top 40 in the United Kingdom and entered the US Billboard 200.

The album was supported by three singles: "Pretty Ugly", released in April 2025; the title track "Midnight Sun", released in June; and "Crush", released in August. To promote the album, Larsson embarked on the Midnight Sun Tour, which began in October 2025.

It’s waiting for you, it will come back for you, and you will come back for it. Throughout Midnight Sun, I get to just capture that total Scandinavian vibe, which is something that I have grown up with—it’s a huge part of me, my happiest memories and my saddest ones, too. A part of my soul is a Swedish summer night. This record encompasses that, how life is so beautiful it makes you cry. It just feels like me—knowing myself, this album is just really, really me. And also, no one can do me the way I can."[60]
