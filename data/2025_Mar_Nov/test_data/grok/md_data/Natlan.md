## Geography

Natlan is situated in the western part of Teyvat, bordering the Desert of Hadramaveth in Sumeru to the east. The landscape features volcanic formations, natural hot springs resulting from geothermal activity, lengthy gorges, valleys, blazing fields, and mesas reminiscent of real-world sites like Monte Roraima or the Blyde River Canyon. Key areas include the Ancient Sacred Mountain, Basin of Unnumbered Flames, Coatepec Mountain, Tequemecan Valley, Toyac Springs, and the Easybreeze Holiday Resort. Environmental elements incorporate savannah flora such as baobab and acacia trees, alongside hazards like liquid Phlogiston—a Pyro-derived substance that can harm characters unless countered by specific abilities—and abyssal blights that require elemental reactions to dispel.

Saurians, dragon-like creatures descended from ancient dragons, inhabit the region and play a central role in exploration and combat. These include types such as Tepetlisaurs (burrowing), Yumkasaurs (grappling), Koholasaurs (swimming), Qucusaurs (flying), Iktomisaurs (sensing), and Tatankasaurs (charging).

## Society and culture

Natlan's inhabitants are organized into six tribes, united under the Pyro Archon to combat abyssal threats. The tribes are: Children of Echoes (miners and dancers, linked to Tepetlisaurs), Scions of the Canopy (extreme sports practitioners, with Yumkasaurs), People of the Springs (relaxed hot spring dwellers, with Koholasaurs), Flower-Feather Clan (gliders, with Qucusaurs), Masters of the Night-Wind (night realm experts, with Iktomisaurs), and Collective of Plenty (gatherers, with Tatankasaurs). Tribal names draw from Nahua cosmology's Thirteen Heavens.

Cultural elements include graffiti art (both street and spiritual forms), breakdancing, hip-hop, rap, and the display of trinkets. Music features instruments like the balafon, fula flute, didgeridoo, castanets, and guitar. Character names and terms derive from diverse sources, such as Hopi, Yucatec Maya, Hawaiian, Yoruba, Nahua, and Māori languages. Warriors embody "Ancient Names" passed down generations, honoring past deeds. Historically, residents faced restrictions on leaving due to ties with the Wayob—spiritual entities aiding against the Abyss—potentially causing memory loss or misfortune outside Natlan, though these rules relaxed in the storyline.

## History and lore

Natlan's lore depicts it as a land of eternal flames and ongoing wars against abyssal darkness, with humans and Saurians coexisting since ancient times. The nation's history includes purges of abyssal influences from realms like the Night Kingdom, leading to eased travel restrictions. Central sites like the Stadium of the Sacred Flame host championships where champions are selected. The etymology of "Natlan" likely stems from Nahuatl terms for place or abundance, with possible links to Aztlán or Atlantis.

## In Genshin Impact

In gameplay, Natlan introduces mechanics like Nightsoul Abilities, where characters enter enhanced states using Phlogiston for improved mobility and combat. Players can indwell Saurians for traversal, such as burrowing or flying. Quests include Tribal Chronicles, replacing traditional story quests and unlocking reputation systems with bounties. Playable characters from Natlan include Mualani (Hydro catalyst), Kinich (Dendro claymore), Kachina (Geo polearm), and Mavuika (Pyro), each with tribe-specific abilities. Access requires completing earlier quests, with shortcuts for higher adventure ranks.

## Development and release

HoYoverse first teased Natlan in earlier versions, with full release in version 5.0 on August 28, 2024, for platforms including PlayStation, PC, iOS, and Android. Subsequent patches in 5.2, 5.5, and 5.8 expanded areas like Ochkanatlan and the Easybreeze Holiday Resort. The update emphasized Pyro-themed exploration and Saurian integration.

## Reception

Natlan's release generated mixed feedback. Some players appreciated its expansive map, innovative mechanics, and lore depth, describing it as engaging for exploration. However, others expressed disappointment with aspects like map design and narrative pacing, viewing it as less compelling than prior regions.

### Controversy over cultural representation

Prior to and following release, Natlan faced criticism for cultural appropriation and whitewashing in character designs. Critics pointed out that characters inspired by deities and figures from Māori, Yoruba, and other cultures—such as Mavuika (from Mahuika) and Ororon (from Ọlọrun)—were depicted with pale skin tones, not reflecting the ethnic diversity of those sources. Voice actors including Khoi Dao (Albedo), Zeno Robinson (Sethos), Valeria Rodríguez (Sucrose), Jenny Yokobori (Yoimiya), Alejandro Saab (Cyno), Anne Yatco (Raiden Shogun), and Allegra Clark (Yelan) publicly called for improvements, emphasizing the need for accurate representation in a globally popular game. A fan petition gathered over 51,000 signatures demanding changes. HoYoverse did not issue a public response to these concerns as of December 2025. Comparisons were made to other games like *Smite*, which handled similar inspirations with greater fidelity.
