# Brave Bunnies

- **Overview**: Brave Bunnies is an animated television series for preschool children, originating from Ukraine, that emphasizes themes of friendship, exploration, and learning through play.
- **Creation and Premiere**: Created by Olga Cherepanova, the series premiered in February 2021 in the United Kingdom, with a second season following in March 2024.
- **Premise**: It follows bunny siblings Bop and Boo as they travel on a bus, meeting various animal friends and engaging in new activities, promoting social skills in a light-hearted manner.
- **Production**: Produced by Glowberry in collaboration with international partners like Ánima Estudios and WildBrain, the show features 2D animation and short episodes suitable for young viewers.
- **Broadcast and Reach**: Aired in over a dozen countries, including Ukraine, the UK, and Australia, with distribution expanding through partnerships.
- **Reception**: The series has received generally positive feedback from audiences, reflected in its IMDb rating, though critical reviews are limited.

Brave Bunnies is a Ukrainian animated children's television series created by Olga Cherepanova.[27] The 2D-animated show, aimed at preschool audiences aged 2 to 5, follows the adventures of bunny siblings Bop and Boo as they travel the world on the Bunny Bus with their family, meeting new animal friends, playing games, and learning social skills through comedic and musical elements.[10][11][25] Produced by Glowberry in co-production with Spanish studio Ánima Estudios (initially) and later Canadian company WildBrain, the series consists of short episodes typically lasting 7 minutes.[10][11][45] It premiered on February 6, 2021, on the Milkshake! block of Channel 5 in the United Kingdom,[18][19] with subsequent broadcasts in countries including Ukraine, Australia, Israel, Latvia, South Korea, Finland, Italy, Spain, Norway, Germany, and Sweden.[10][21] A second season premiered on March 18, 2024, in the UK.[43][46][49] As of 2025, the series has expanded through international distribution deals and merchandising, including toys and books.[10][20][29]

## Premise
The series centers on Bop and Boo, a brother and sister bunny duo described as curious and brave, who embark on road trips around a fictional "Bunny World" aboard the Bunny Bus.[10][11] Accompanied by their parents, Ma and Pa, and four younger Bunny Babies, they stop at various locations to interact with different animals, such as beavers, rhinos, or llamas.[11][53] Each episode involves creating new games or activities to foster friendships, incorporating elements of comedy, songs, and basic social lessons like sharing and cooperation.[6][10] The narrative emphasizes exploration and positive relationships without major conflicts, making it suitable for young children.[11]

## Characters
The main characters include:

- **Bop**: The older brother bunny, voiced by Wilbur Conabeare in the English version.[11][53] He is portrayed as enthusiastic and adventurous.
- **Boo**: The younger sister bunny, voiced by Margot Powell.[11][53] She is curious and often initiates interactions with new friends.
- **Bunny Ma**: The mother, voiced by Harriet Kershaw.[11][53]
- **Bunny Pa**: The father, voiced by Jensen Adeyanju (or Adyanju in some listings).[11][53]
- **Narrator**: Voiced by Chetna Pandya in season 1 and Samantha Spiro in later episodes.[11][53]
- Recurring animal friends: These include Bev Beaver (voiced by Darcey Ewart), Kit the Kangaroo (voiced by Tabitha Storton), and others like Dwayne the Moose (Aeson Klemens) and Granny Llama (Elizabeth Bower).[11]

The characters are designed with bright, kid-friendly visuals by art director Anna Sarvira.[10]

## Production
Brave Bunnies was created by Olga Cherepanova, co-founder of Glowberry, who served as creative producer.[25][27][29][30][32] The series was developed as a cross-platform children's brand, blending education and entertainment.[6][10] Key production roles included series director Tim Fehrenbach (UK), executive story editor John van Bruggen (Canada), art director Anna Sarvira (Ukraine), and executive producer Sergii Molchanov (Ukraine).[10]

The first season, comprising 52 episodes, was a co-production between Glowberry and Ánima Estudios.[10][32] In December 2022, WildBrain acquired the rights and joined as a co-producer for season 2, with production handled at WildBrain's Vancouver studio.[20][30][45][51] Season 2 consists of 47 episodes.[44][53] The series previewed at MIP JUNIOR 2020, where it attracted significant viewership in the screening library.[10][28]

Production continued amid challenges in Ukraine, with Cherepanova noting the show's role in promoting communication among children globally.[29] The English-language version features Dolby Digital sound and a 16:9 HD aspect ratio.[11]

## Broadcast
The series debuted on Ukraine's PlusPlus channel on December 5, 2020, before its international premiere.[49] In the UK, it aired on Milkshake! starting February 6, 2021, with season 2 following on March 18, 2024.[18][43][46][50] ViacomCBS (now Paramount) acquired rights for Nick Jr. UK and Milkshake! in October 2020.[18]

By October 2021, broadcasts had expanded to Australia (November 2023 internet release),[14] Israel, Latvia, South Korea, Finland, Italy, Spain, Norway, Germany, and Sweden.[10][21] In Germany, it launched on Super RTL on October 15, 2021.[21] Distribution was initially handled by Aardman Animations,[10][28] with WildBrain securing further deals in November 2023.[54]

The show airs weekdays in short slots, such as 06:00 on Channel 5 in the UK, with a runtime of 5-7 minutes per episode.[17][53]

## Reception
Brave Bunnies has an IMDb rating of 7.7 out of 10 based on over 1,000 user votes as of 2025.[11] It received one nomination, though details are unspecified.[11] Industry coverage highlights its appeal to preschoolers and its role as Ukraine's first global children's animated property.[24][25][29] Reviews are sparse in mainstream media, but audience feedback praises its positive themes and engaging animation.[11] The series' continuation into a second season indicates sustained interest.[30][45]

## Merchandise
Merchandising includes toys from Spin Master (global master toy partner since 2022)[10][20] and books published by Penguin Random House (UK) and De Agostini (Italy, first collection in September 2021).[10] Licensing partners include Big Picture Licensing (UK) and Panaderia Licensing & Marketing (Mexico).[10] Related products aim to extend the brand's educational elements.[10]
