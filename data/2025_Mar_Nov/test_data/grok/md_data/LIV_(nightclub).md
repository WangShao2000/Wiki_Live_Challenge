## History
The Fontainebleau Miami Beach hotel, where LIV is housed, has a musical background dating back to the mid-20th century, with its Tropigala Lounge previously hosting performers such as Elvis Presley and Frank Sinatra. The site of LIV was formerly known as the La Ronde Room in the 1950s.

LIV opened in 2008 following a $1 billion renovation of the Fontainebleau hotel. It was developed by the Miami Marketing Group (MMG), with David Grutman as a partner. In 2017, the club underwent a $10 million renovation. In September 2024, LIV became the first nightclub equipped with an L-Acoustics L Series loudspeaker system.

A second LIV venue opened at the Fontainebleau Las Vegas in 2023, featuring similar design elements including state-of-the-art sound and lighting systems. The Las Vegas location has hosted events such as DJ residencies and post-event parties for major sporting occasions.

## Venue
LIV Miami occupies more than 18,000 square feet within the Fontainebleau Miami Beach hotel. It includes a main dance floor, three full-service bars, VIP tables, and private skyboxes. The club operates from Wednesday to Sunday, typically from 11 p.m. to 5 a.m. Music genres played include hip hop, house, rock, and electronic dance music, with live artist presentations.

The Las Vegas counterpart features a multilevel layout with VIP suites, a video wall, and advanced audio-visual technology.

## Ownership and operations
LIV is owned and operated by David Grutman through Groot Hospitality, which evolved from the Miami Marketing Group. Grutman has expanded the brand beyond nightlife into other hospitality ventures.

## Notable events and performers
LIV Miami has hosted numerous events, including Lil Wayne's post-prison release party in 2010. The club regularly features DJs such as David Guetta and Calvin Harris. In December 2025, it served as the venue for open workouts and professional boxing matches during the fight week for the Jake Paul vs. Anthony Joshua bout, organized by Most Valuable Promotions and streamed on Netflix Sports YouTube. Other events have included celebrity appearances during Art Basel, such as Leonardo DiCaprio in 2025.

The club has been associated with various incidents, including reports of unusual occurrences since its opening. LIV has appeared in DJ Mag's Top 100 Clubs rankings, including in 2025.
