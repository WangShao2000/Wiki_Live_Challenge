# 2007 NFC Championship Game

The 2007 NFC Championship Game featured the New York Giants defeating the Green Bay Packers 23–20 in overtime on January 20, 2008, at Lambeau Field in Green Bay, Wisconsin. This game determined the National Football Conference (NFC) representative for Super Bowl XLII. Played in extremely cold conditions with a temperature of −1 °F (−18 °C) and wind chill of −23 °F (−31 °C), it ranks as the third-coldest game in NFL history. The Giants' victory marked their advancement to the Super Bowl, where they upset the undefeated New England Patriots. For the Packers, the loss ended their season and was quarterback Brett Favre's final game with the team.

**Key Points:**
- Research indicates the game was notable for its frigid weather, which influenced play and remains one of the coldest NFL postseason contests.
- The Giants, as underdogs, overcame the favored Packers in overtime on a 47-yard field goal by kicker Lawrence Tynes after he missed two earlier attempts.
- No prominent controversies surrounded the officiating or outcome, though the cold conditions drew attention from players and media.
- The event highlighted the end of an era for the Packers with Favre's departure, while boosting the Giants' momentum toward their Super Bowl win.

### Background
The 2007 NFL season saw the Green Bay Packers finish with a 13–3 regular-season record, securing the No. 2 seed in the NFC and a first-round bye. They advanced by defeating the Seattle Seahawks 42–20 in the divisional round. The New York Giants, with a 10–6 record, earned a wild-card berth as the No. 5 seed. They progressed through the playoffs by beating the Tampa Bay Buccaneers 24–14 in the wild-card round and the top-seeded Dallas Cowboys 21–17 in the divisional round. The teams had met earlier in the regular season on September 16, 2007, with the Packers winning 35–13 at Giants Stadium.

The Packers were favored by 7.5 points, partly due to home-field advantage and Favre's experience. The game was the seventh postseason meeting between the franchises, with the Packers leading the series 4–2 entering the contest; prior encounters included five NFL Championship Games.

### Game Conditions
The game occurred under severe weather, with temperatures at kickoff recorded at −1 °F and a wind chill of −23 °F, accompanied by 12 mph winds. This made it the third-coldest game in NFL history, behind the 1967 NFL Championship (−13 °F) and the 1981 AFC Championship (−9 °F). Players used various methods to combat the cold, including heated benches, Vaseline on skin, and multiple layers of clothing. Attendance was 72,740, and the game lasted 3 hours and 33 minutes.


The 2007 NFC Championship Game was an American football game played on January 20, 2008, at Lambeau Field in Green Bay, Wisconsin, between the New York Giants and the Green Bay Packers. It served as the National Football Conference (NFC) championship for the 2007 NFL season, with the winner advancing to Super Bowl XLII against the AFC champion New England Patriots. The Giants won 23–20 in overtime on a 47-yard field goal by Lawrence Tynes, marking the first overtime NFC Championship Game since 1998. The contest took place in extremely cold weather, with a kickoff temperature of −1 °F (−18 °C) and a wind chill of −23 °F (−31 °C), ranking it as the third-coldest game in NFL history. The victory completed the Giants' road playoff success, leading to their upset win over the undefeated Patriots in the Super Bowl. For the Packers, the loss concluded a strong season and was quarterback Brett Favre's final game with the franchise before his initial retirement.


### Regular Season and Playoff Paths
The 2007 NFL regular season concluded with the Green Bay Packers achieving a 13–3 record, winning the NFC North division and earning the No. 2 seed in the NFC playoffs. This performance granted them a first-round bye. In the divisional round, they hosted and defeated the Seattle Seahawks 42–20.

The New York Giants finished with a 10–6 record, securing a wild-card spot as the No. 5 seed in the NFC. They began their postseason by defeating the Tampa Bay Buccaneers 24–14 in the wild-card round on the road. In the divisional round, they upset the No. 1-seeded Dallas Cowboys 21–17, also away.

The teams had faced each other during the regular season on September 16, 2007, at Giants Stadium, where the Packers won 35–13. Entering the championship game, the Packers were favored by 7.5 points, with an over/under of 41.5.

### Historical Context
This was the seventh postseason meeting between the Giants and Packers, with the Packers holding a 4–2 advantage prior to the game. The first five encounters were NFL Championship Games in 1938, 1939, 1944, 1961, and 1962. It was the fifth time the teams met in both the regular season and playoffs in the same year.

## Game Summary

### First Quarter
The Giants received the opening kickoff and drove 71 yards in 14 plays, culminating in a 29-yard field goal by Lawrence Tynes for a 3–0 lead. The Packers' subsequent drives resulted in punts, as their offense struggled early.

### Second Quarter
The Giants extended their lead to 6–0 with a 37-yard field goal by Tynes. The Packers responded immediately with a 90-yard touchdown pass from Favre to Donald Driver, taking a 7–6 lead. Later, Mason Crosby added a 36-yard field goal for Green Bay, making it 10–6 at halftime.

### Third Quarter
The Giants regained the lead with a 1-yard touchdown run by Brandon Jacobs, followed by an extra point for 13–10. The Packers answered with a 12-yard touchdown pass from Favre to Donald Lee, leading 17–13. New York then scored on a 4-yard run by Ahmad Bradshaw, taking a 20–17 advantage.

### Fourth Quarter
Crosby tied the game at 20–20 with a 37-yard field goal. Tynes missed field goal attempts from 43 yards and 36 yards late in the quarter, forcing overtime.

### Overtime
The Packers won the coin toss but Favre threw an interception to Corey Webster on the second play. The Giants capitalized, with Tynes kicking a 47-yard field goal to win 23–20.

## Weather Impact
The extreme cold affected gameplay, with players reporting numb fingers and difficulty gripping the ball. Tynes suffered a foot injury from the conditions but recovered for the Super Bowl. The weather ranked the game among the coldest in league history, influencing strategies and player performance.

## Aftermath
The Giants advanced to Super Bowl XLII on February 3, 2008, defeating the 18–0 New England Patriots 17–14. The Packers' season ended at 14–4 overall, and the game was Favre's last with the team before retiring in March 2008 and later unretiring to play for other franchises. The loss was described by Packers personnel as a missed opportunity.
