## Background and release

Swift wrote most of Speak Now independently, but collaborated with Johnson on "If This Was a Movie".[3] Johnson had previously worked with Swift on "You'll Always Find Your Way Back Home" for the Hannah Montana: The Movie soundtrack in 2009 and featured her on Boys Like Girls' "Two Is Better Than One".[3] The song was produced by Swift and Nathan Chapman.[4]

It appeared as the 16th track on the deluxe edition of Speak Now, available at Target stores in the United States.[1][2] On November 8, 2011, it was released as a promotional single for digital download on iTunes and Amazon.[3]

In 2019, Swift began re-recording her first six studio albums after her masters were sold to Scooter Braun.[5] The re-recorded "If This Was a Movie (Taylor's Version)" was produced by Swift and Christopher Rowe.[1] Although originally associated with Speak Now, it was not included on Speak Now (Taylor's Version) (2023) to preserve that album's status as entirely self-written.[1] Instead, it was released on March 17, 2023, as part of the extended play The More Fearless (Taylor's Version) Chapter, alongside re-recordings of "Safe & Sound" and "Eyes Open", and the previously unreleased "All of the Girls You Loved Before".[1][11] The release coincided with the start of Swift's Eras Tour.[1][5]

## Composition

"If This Was a Movie" is a country pop ballad with a duration of 3 minutes and 57 seconds.[2][4] It features a recurring guitar riff, harmonies in the chorus, and Swift's vocal twang.[9] The lyrics depict a narrator reminiscing about a past relationship and imagining a reunion akin to scenes in romantic films, such as standing in the rain or flashbacks to happier times.[2][3] The chorus repeats: "Come back, come back, come back to me like / You would, you would if this was a movie".[2]

The title's use of "was" instead of the subjunctive "were" has drawn commentary, with some viewing it as informal modern usage rather than incorrect.[3] Lyrically, it shares themes with Swift's earlier songs like "Breathe" from Fearless (2008) and "Bye Bye Baby" from Fearless (Taylor's Version) (2021).[3]

## Critical reception

Reviews of "If This Was a Movie" have been positive. Billboard awarded it four out of five stars, describing it as a fast-paced ballad that demonstrates Swift's maturity, moving beyond high school themes to more age-appropriate content.[9] Rolling Stone ranked it 75th out of 286 Swift songs, highlighting its plea for reconciliation and the line "But I take it all back now!"[10] Other commentators have called it nostalgic, romantic, and cinematic.[12] In a ranking of Swift's albums, Consequence associated it with the music video for "Delicate" from Reputation (2017), noting cinematic elements.[13] Some fans and critics have debated its placement on Speak Now due to the co-writing credit.[14]

## Commercial performance

Upon its 2011 promotional release, "If This Was a Movie" debuted at number 10 on the US Billboard Hot 100, marking one of several Speak Now tracks to chart that week.[7][15] It also reached number 17 on the Canadian Hot 100.[16]

The 2023 re-recording debuted at number 1 on the US Country Digital Song Sales chart, with 11,300 units sold, becoming the first song to top that chart twice in different versions.[8][17] It entered at number 4 on the all-genre Digital Song Sales chart and appeared on the Billboard Hot 100.[18] Internationally, it charted on the UK Singles Chart Update at number 50 and the UK Singles Sales Chart at number 13.[19]

As of 2023, the re-recording had been streamed over 91 million times on Spotify.[20]

## Live performances

Swift performed "If This Was a Movie (Taylor's Version)" live for the first time during her Eras Tour in Minneapolis, Minnesota, on June 23, 2023, as one of the surprise songs.[21][22] It was played on piano.[23]
