### Early life and education
Lill was born Calum Andrew Lill in September 1995 in Manchester, England.[1][2] He grew up in a working-class family and developed an interest in acting during childhood.[3] His mother initially funded his acting lessons, but financial difficulties led his grandparents to provide additional support to help him pursue training.[3]

In 2013, at age 18, Lill moved to London to study acting.[4] He earned a Bachelor of Arts degree in acting from the Arts Educational Schools (ArtsEd) in 2018.[2][5]

### Career
After graduating, Lill appeared in several short films in 2018, including *I Can See Clearly Now* as Walter, *Sleep Warm* as Tom, and *Heads or Tails* as a CID officer.[2][6] He also performed in stage productions at the Royal Exchange Theatre in Manchester, with roles in *Our Town*, *This Is*, and *Scuttlers*.[2] In an earlier stage role, he appeared in *The Sociable Plover* alongside Jack Robertson, earning a nomination for Duo Performance at the Fringe Theatre Awards.[7]

Lill made his television debut in 2019 with guest appearances as Conor Henson in an episode of the BBC medical drama *Holby City* and as Chez Wilson-Needham in an episode of the BBC soap opera *Doctors*.[6][8] In 2021, he joined the Channel 4 soap opera *Hollyoaks* in the recurring role of PC Carlton Smith, a police officer and colleague of George Kiss, appearing in 13 episodes from March to July.[9][10][11]

In September 2023, Lill began portraying solicitor Joel Deering in the ITV soap opera *Coronation Street*.[1][12] The character was introduced as a love interest for Dee-Dee Bailey and later revealed as a villain involved in grooming, abuse, and murder storylines.[13][14] Lill appeared in 77 episodes until the character's death in June 2025.[1][6][15] For his performance, he was nominated for Best Villain at the 2024 Inside Soap Awards and Villain of the Year at the 2025 British Soap Awards.[16][17][18][19] He did not win either award.[20][21]

Following his departure from *Coronation Street*, Lill took on stage roles. From December 2024 to January 2025, he played the villain Fleshcreep in a pantomime production of *Jack and the Beanstalk* at Bournemouth's Pavilion Theatre.[22][23] He reprised the role of Fleshcreep in another production of *Jack and the Beanstalk* at the Oak Theatre at Scarisbrick Hall near Ormskirk from December 13 to 24, 2025, alongside cast members including David Dale as Dame Trott, Danny Childs as Jack Trott, Sue Fulton as Princess Jill, Mary Shanker as Mary the Fairy, and Michael Lawless as King Bling.[24] In 2025, he also starred as Dave in a revival of the two-hander thriller *The Sociable Plover* at the Old Red Lion Theatre, opposite Jack Robertson as Roy Tunt.[25][26]

Before securing consistent acting work, Lill held various jobs, including selling cars, working at Junkyard Golf Club, and running a paella stand at festivals.[3][27] He has also worked as a screen acting tutor and offers one-on-one acting coaching.[28][29]

### Personal life
Lill has been in a relationship with Roberta Mcclarron, who works in public relations and marketing, since early 2021.[30][31] The couple moved to Manchester together and became engaged in June 2024.[30][32] In December 2025, they announced they were expecting their first child, due in April 2026.[33][34]
