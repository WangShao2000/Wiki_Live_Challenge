# Calum Lill

**Calum Andrew Lill** (born September 1995) is a British actor. He is best known for his portrayal of the villainous solicitor Joel Deering in the ITV soap opera *Coronation Street* from 2023 to 2024. Lill also appeared as PC Carlton Smith in the Channel 4 soap opera *Hollyoaks* in 2021 and has performed in various stage productions, including *The Sociable Plover* and pantomime roles.

## Early life and education
Calum Andrew Lill was born in September 1995 [1]. He is from Manchester, England [2]. Lill trained as an actor at the Liverpool Institute for Performing Arts (LIPA) [2].

## Career

### 2019–2023: Early roles and career struggles
After graduating, Lill began his television career in 2019 with guest appearances in British medical dramas. He played the character Chez Wilson-Needham in an episode of the BBC soap opera *Doctors* and portrayed Conor Henson in an episode of *Holby City* later that same year [3][4].

In 2021, Lill was cast in the Channel 4 soap opera *Hollyoaks* in the recurring role of PC Carlton Smith [5]. He appeared in approximately 12 episodes, where his character was involved in a storyline regarding the abuse of John Paul McQueen by PC George Kiss [3][6].

Following his stint on *Hollyoaks*, Lill experienced a period of unemployment in the acting industry, exacerbated by the COVID-19 pandemic and the 2023 SAG-AFTRA strikes which affected the wider industry [7][8]. During this hiatus, he worked various non-acting jobs to support himself, including selling cars and working at a paella stand at music festivals [7][9]. Lill has stated in interviews that he found car sales difficult and unfulfilling, noting that he nearly quit the acting profession entirely due to financial pressures and a lack of roles [10][11]. While working at the festival food stand, he met actors Rupert Hill and Jenny Platt, former *Coronation Street* cast members, who encouraged him to persist with his acting career [8].

### 2023–2025: *Coronation Street*
In 2023, Lill successfully auditioned for the ITV soap opera *Coronation Street*. He was working as a car salesman when he received the call from his agent confirming he had secured the part [11]. He made his first on-screen appearance as Joel Deering, a duty solicitor, in September 2023 [12].

Initially introduced as a charming professional and a romantic interest for established character Dee-Dee Bailey (Channique Sterling-Brown), the character of Joel Deering was later revealed to be a "villain" involved in grooming and abusing vulnerable young women [13][14]. The storyline escalated throughout 2024, revealing Joel as the secret assailant of the character Lauren Bolton (Cait Fitton), whom he had attacked and left for dead [13][15].

Lill's tenure on the show concluded with a high-profile "whodunit" storyline surrounding Joel Deering's death. The character was killed off in September 2024, with the identity of his killer kept secret from the audience until a flashback episode aired in November 2024 revealed that Lauren Bolton had struck the fatal blow in self-defense [7][16]. Lill described the conclusion of the arc as "soap justice" [16].

Following his departure, it was confirmed in May 2025 that Lill would return to *Coronation Street* for a brief appearance, reportedly involving a single episode or flashback sequence [13].

### Theatre and other work
In addition to his television work, Lill has performed in theatre. In May 2025, he starred as "Dave" in a 20th-anniversary revival of Tim Whitnall's play *The Sociable Plover* at the Old Red Lion Theatre in Islington, London [17][18]. Reviews praised his performance, with critics noting his ability to convey a "quiet air of menace" and comparing his stage presence to his television work [18][19].

Lill has also appeared in pantomime productions. He played the antagonist Fleshcreep in *Jack and the Beanstalk* at the Bournemouth Pavilion during the 2024/2025 Christmas season [2][20]. He reprised the role of Fleshcreep for a production at the Oak Theatre in Scarisbrick, Lancashire, in December 2025 [2].

## Personal life
Lill resides in Manchester [21]. He has been in a relationship with Roberta McClarron since 2021 [4]. The couple announced their engagement in June 2024 [22]. Lill proposed during a trip to the countryside; he later shared a humorous anecdote on social media revealing that he had to wear a swimsuit belonging to his *Coronation Street* co-star Dolly-Rose Campbell in the hot tub after his partner removed his swimwear from their suitcase as a prank [22][23].
