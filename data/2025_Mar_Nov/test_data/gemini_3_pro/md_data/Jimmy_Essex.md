# Jimmy Essex

**Jimmy Essex** (born November 18, 1984) is a British actor, dancer, and musician. He is best known for his role as Adam Donovan in the Channel 4 soap opera *Hollyoaks*, which he played from 2016 to 2018. Prior to his acting career, Essex was a member of the music group MITSOTU, which reached the Judges' Houses stage of *The X Factor* in 2012. His other credits include the Netflix fantasy series *The Sandman* and the stage play *GHBoy*.

## Early life and education
Essex was born in London, England, and grew up in the Harrow area [1]. He trained in performing arts at the Urdang Academy in London [2].

## Career

### Music and dance
Essex began his career in the music industry as a dancer and singer. He worked as a backing dancer for high-profile artists, including Janet Jackson and The Saturdays [3].

In 2011, Essex formed the "urban pop" group MITSOTU (an acronym for *Music Is The Soul Of The Universe*) alongside James Collins and Holly Cooper [3][4]. The trio, who met at college, performed at various schools and events to build a following [4]. In 2012, the group auditioned for the ninth series of the ITV reality music competition *The X Factor* [4]. They were mentored by Louis Walsh in the "Groups" category and advanced to the Judges' Houses stage of the competition but were eliminated before the live shows [5][6].

### Television and film
In 2016, Essex was cast in the Channel 4 soap opera *Hollyoaks* as Adam Donovan [7]. He was introduced alongside actors Maxim Baldry and Luke Jerdy, who played his brothers Liam and Jesse, respectively [8]. The character was established as a hairdresser and a half-brother to the established character Grace Black [8][9].

Essex's storylines on *Hollyoaks* included romantic entanglements with Maxine Minniver and Darcy Wilde, as well as complex family dynamics involving his father, Glenn Donovan [10]. Essex departed the series in 2018 after two years in the role [11]. His character was written out in a dramatic exit storyline in which Adam was shot and killed by his father, Glenn, after it was revealed Adam had been working as a police informant against him [11][12]. Essex expressed satisfaction with the conclusion of his character's arc, describing the exit as "shocking" and effective for the show's narrative [11].

Following his departure from *Hollyoaks*, Essex appeared in the 2022 Netflix series *The Sandman*, based on the comic book by Neil Gaiman. He portrayed the character Carrion in the episodes "Collectors" and "Lost Hearts" [13][14][15]. His film credits include the role of Peter in the horror film *A Dark Path* (2023) and Felix in the short film *Daddy Issues* (2024) [13].

### Theatre
In late 2020, Essex made his professional stage debut in the play *GHBoy* at the Charing Cross Theatre in London [16]. Written by Paul Harvard, the production explored themes of chemsex, trauma, and mental health within the gay community in East London [16][17]. Essex played the lead role of Robert, a man dealing with grief following the death of his father while navigating addiction and relationships [16][17].

## Personal life
Essex is openly gay [18]. He has been in a relationship with musician Charles Simmons since 2013 [9][19]. The couple announced their engagement in January 2019, following a proposal by Simmons during a holiday in Sri Lanka [9][20].
