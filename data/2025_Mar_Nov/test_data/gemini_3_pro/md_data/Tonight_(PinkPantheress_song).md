# "Tonight" (PinkPantheress song)

**"Tonight"** is a song by English singer and record producer PinkPantheress. It was released on 4 April 2025 through Warner Records as the lead single from her second mixtape, *Fancy That* (2025) [1][2]. Written and produced by PinkPantheress, Aksel Arvid, and Count Baldor, the track is a dance and bassline song that incorporates a sample of "Do You Know What I'm Seeing?" (2008) by the American pop rock band Panic! at the Disco [3][4][5].

Lyrically, the song marks a thematic shift for the artist, moving from introspective storytelling to more direct expressions of sexual desire and romantic initiation [5][6]. The accompanying music video, directed by Charlotte Rutherford, features a Regency era aesthetic inspired by period dramas, depicting the singer and her peers at a chaotic manor house party [7][8].

Upon release, "Tonight" received generally positive reviews from music critics, who noted its production quality and the artist's vocal performance [9][10]. Commercially, the single reached number 35 on the UK Singles Chart and number 88 on the Irish Singles Chart [1][11]. In the United States, it peaked at number 5 on the *Billboard* Hot Dance/Pop Songs chart [1]. A remix featuring English singer Jade Thirlwall was released on 10 October 2025 as part of the remix album *Fancy Some More?* [1][12].

## Background and release

Following the release of her debut studio album *Heaven Knows* (2023) and the subsequent "Capable of Love Tour" in 2024, PinkPantheress took a brief hiatus from live performances to focus on her health [1]. In January 2025, she posted an image of herself in a recording studio to social media with the caption, "Oh, we are soooo back," signaling her return to music [1]. In a March 2025 interview with *Mixmag*, she stated that her new material focused on "letting expectations go" and attempting to balance her status as a pop star with experimental sounds [1][4].

PinkPantheress announced "Tonight" as the lead single for her then-upcoming project, *Fancy That* [7][13]. The song was released to digital retailers and streaming platforms on 4 April 2025 [2][14]. Warner Records subsequently promoted the song to Italian radio on 11 April 2025 [1]. Prior to the release, the singer addressed her reputation for releasing short tracks, joking on social media that she did not want to see "no more song length jokes," as "Tonight" runs for nearly three minutes [7][15].

## Composition and lyrics

"Tonight" is a dance, dance-pop, and bassline track with a duration of 2 minutes and 54 seconds [1][3][5]. The production features "wobbling synths," a "driving beat," and elements of house and drum and bass [5][9][16]. The song is built around a sample of the orchestral arrangements and strings from the song "Do You Know What I'm Seeing?" by Panic! at the Disco, which appears on their 2008 album *Pretty. Odd.* [4][17]. Consequently, the songwriters of the sample—Brendon Urie, Ryan Ross, Spencer Smith, and Jon Walker—are credited as co-writers on "Tonight" alongside PinkPantheress, Aksel Arvid, and Count Baldor [2][3].

Lyrically, the song explores themes of anticipation, desire, and "fleeting intimacy" [6]. Music journalists noted that the lyrics represent a departure from the artist's typically reserved and "wistful" songwriting style [5]. The chorus features direct sexual advances, with the narrator asking, "You want sex with me? / Come talk to me" [18][19]. In previous interviews, PinkPantheress had stated she avoided explicit lyrics because her parents listened to her music; however, reviewers observed that "Tonight" abandons that filter in favor of "impulsivity" and confidence [5][19]. The song utilizes a spoken-word delivery in the introduction and shifts to a more playful, flirtatious tone in the chorus [6].

## Critical reception

"Tonight" received generally favorable reviews from music critics. Peter Källman of *MusicTalkers* described the production as "hypnotic" and "magical," praising the sub-bass and the inclusion of cowbells in the verses [9]. He further stated that PinkPantheress's vocals were unique within the pop landscape and that she "absolutely excels" on the track [9]. *HotNewHipHop* characterized the song as a "pretty complete track" with an "addicting bassline," noting that the chorus is memorable and the delivery is "wildly entrancing" [10].

*Ratings Game Music* gave the song a rating of 3.5 out of 5, highlighting the "upbeat tempo" and "mesmerizing melodies" while noting the shift toward a "bolder, more seductive vibe" compared to her previous work [20]. A review from *Neon Music* stated that the song "flips the lights on at the party," praising the directness of the lyrics and the "Bridgerton-meets-bassline" aesthetic [19]. Conversely, a reviewer for *Stan Island* offered a more mixed perspective, describing the track as a "light and fluffy effort" that might require editing for radio due to the explicit lyrics, suggesting that while the production was professional, the vocals were a "distraction" [21].

## Music video

The music video for "Tonight" was directed by Charlotte Rutherford and released on 4 April 2025, coinciding with the single's release [14][22]. The video employs a period drama aesthetic, specifically evoking the Regency era and the style of the television series *Bridgerton* [1][8][19].

The visual depicts PinkPantheress and a group of friends traveling by bicycle and carriage to a grand manor house [1][7]. Inside, they disrupt a formal gathering, engaging in a "chaotic party" that includes passionate kissing and pillow fights [1][5]. The video contrasts the rigid formality of the setting with the "rowdy" behavior of the characters [1]. *Promonews* described the video as a "dazzling promo" that serves as a "fully immersive period piece with echoes of contemporary club culture" [8]. PinkPantheress stated that her intention was to create a "more classic pop video" utilizing sets and styling distinct from her usual aesthetic [1].

## Commercial performance

In the United Kingdom, "Tonight" debuted and peaked at number 35 on the Official Singles Chart, spending six weeks on the chart [11][23]. It also reached number 70 on the Official Streaming Chart [11]. In Ireland, the song reached number 88 on the Irish Singles Chart [1].

In the United States, "Tonight" did not enter the *Billboard* Hot 100 but peaked at number 25 on the Bubbling Under Hot 100 chart [1][24]. It found success on genre-specific charts, reaching number 5 on the *Billboard* Hot Dance/Pop Songs chart [1]. Elsewhere, the track charted at number 7 on the New Zealand Hot Singles chart and number 12 on the Japan Hot Overseas chart [1].

## Live performances

PinkPantheress performed "Tonight" live at the Glastonbury Festival on 27 June 2025 [1]. On 30 July 2025, she appeared on *The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon*, performing the song as part of a medley that also included the *Fancy That* tracks "Illegal" and "Girl Like Me" [1]. In September 2025, she performed a stripped-down version of the song accompanied by a live band for NPR's *Tiny Desk Concerts* series [1][25]. The song was also included in the set list for her "An Evening With... PinkPantheress" tour (2025–2026) [1].

## Remixes

On 10 October 2025, a remix of "Tonight" featuring English singer Jade Thirlwall (credited as JADE) was released [1][12]. This version was included on *Fancy Some More?*, the remix album companion to the *Fancy That* mixtape [26]. The remix features an additional verse by Thirlwall, which critics described as "flirty" and "teasing," with lyrics such as "I'll be your high, I'll be your pill, your ecstasy" [27].

Reviewing the remix album for *The Daily Cardinal*, a critic noted that while the Jade remix "adds little to the remixed track other than her own flirty verse," it successfully enhances the romantic tone of the original [27]. *The Daily Mississippian* expressed a mixed opinion, suggesting that the addition of a new verse felt "redundant" but acknowledged the project satisfied fans' desire for new content [28].
