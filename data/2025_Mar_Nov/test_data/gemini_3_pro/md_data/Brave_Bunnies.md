# Brave Bunnies

**Brave Bunnies** is a 2D animated television series aimed at preschool children, typically aged two to five. Created by Olga Cherepanova and developed by the Ukrainian production company Glowberry, the series follows the adventures of two rabbit siblings, Bop and Boo, as they travel with their family on a bus, exploring the world and interacting with various animal characters. The show emphasizes themes of diversity, socialization, and emotional intelligence, utilizing a curriculum designed to foster curiosity and empathy.

The series was originally co-produced by Glowberry and the Spanish animation studio Ánima (formerly Ánima Kitchent). Following the Russian invasion of Ukraine in 2022, production was disrupted; in late 2022, the Canadian media company WildBrain acquired a majority stake in the intellectual property to ensure the completion and distribution of the second season. *Brave Bunnies* premiered in Ukraine in December 2020 and has since been broadcast internationally on networks such as Channel 5's Milkshake! in the United Kingdom, ABC Kids in Australia, and Rai Yoyo in Italy. The series has received industry recognition, including a Pulcinella Award for Best Upper Preschool TV Series.

## Premise and Characters

The narrative centers on a family of rabbits known as the Brave Bunnies who travel across their world in a vehicle called the Bunny Bus. The primary protagonists are the siblings Bop and Boo. In each episode, the family stops at a new location where the siblings encounter a young animal of a different species. The plot typically involves the bunnies finding a way to befriend the new character despite their differences, often by inventing a game that accommodates the specific traits or needs of their new friend [1][2].

The series is designed to support social and emotional development. According to production notes, the storylines follow a curriculum based on "Humancentric Learning," which focuses on four pillars: creativity, caring, curiosity, and courage [1]. The show aims to demonstrate how to accept differences and navigate early socialization [3].

### Main Characters
*   **Bop:** The brother bunny, described as curious and courageous [1].
*   **Boo:** The sister bunny, who accompanies Bop on their adventures [1].
*   **Ma and Pa:** The parents of Bop and Boo, who facilitate the family's travels and offer support [4].
*   **Bunny Babies:** The four younger siblings of Bop and Boo [4].

## Production

### Development and Season 1
*Brave Bunnies* was created by Olga Cherepanova, the co-founder and creative producer of the Ukrainian production company Glowberry (part of FILM.UA Group) [1][2]. The visual style of the series was developed by the Ukrainian illustrator Anna Sarvira, who served as the art director [1][5]. The series was developed as a co-production between Glowberry and the Spanish animation studio Ánima [2].

For the first season, the British studio Aardman Animations served as the global content distributor [1][2]. The production team included director Tim Fehrenbach and head writer John van Bruggen [2]. The first season consists of 52 episodes, each running for approximately seven minutes [6].

### Impact of the War in Ukraine and Restructuring
The production of the series was significantly impacted by the Russian invasion of Ukraine in February 2022. Glowberry, based in Kyiv, faced operational challenges as staff members were forced to flee or were displaced by the conflict [4][7].

To ensure the continuation of the franchise, Glowberry and Ánima entered into a partnership with the Canadian entertainment company WildBrain in December 2022. Under this agreement, WildBrain acquired a majority stake in the *Brave Bunnies* intellectual property. WildBrain's Vancouver studio took over the co-production of Season 2, working alongside Glowberry and Ánima [7][8]. WildBrain also assumed responsibility for global distribution and licensing, replacing Aardman Animations in this capacity [9].

### Season 2
Production on the second season resumed in late 2022 following the restructuring. The second season, also consisting of 52 seven-minute episodes, was scheduled for delivery beginning in June 2023 [7]. The narrative of the second season continues the road trip format, with the family revisiting old friends and meeting new ones [9].

## Broadcast and Release

*Brave Bunnies* made its world premiere on December 5, 2020, on the PLUSPLUS channel in Ukraine [3].

### International Broadcast
The series has been sold to broadcasters in over 80 countries [7].
*   **United Kingdom:** The series premiered on Channel 5's preschool block, Milkshake!, on February 6, 2021 [10][11]. It subsequently launched on Nick Jr. UK in September 2021 [12].
*   **Australia:** The show airs on ABC Kids [2].
*   **Italy:** It premiered on Rai Yoyo and the streaming service Rai Play in April 2021 [13].
*   **Germany:** The series was acquired by Super RTL [2].
*   **Asia-Pacific:** In 2022, a deal was announced for the series to air on the Cartoonito preschool block on Cartoon Network in Southeast Asia, Australia, and New Zealand, as well as streaming on HBO GO in Southeast Asia, Taiwan, and Hong Kong [1].
*   **North America:** WildBrain's Family Jr. channel acquired the exclusive linear telecast rights for Canada [7].

### Streaming
In July 2022, a dedicated YouTube channel named "Sunflower TV" was launched to provide Ukrainian content to refugee children across Europe and the UK; *Brave Bunnies* was among the featured programming [4].

## Reception and Awards

The series has received positive attention from industry professionals and audiences. Upon its release in the United Kingdom, it reportedly ranked as the number one commercial channel program in its time slot [13]. In Italy, the show reached the top viewing position on Rai Yoyo shortly after its premiere [13].

### Awards and Nominations
*   **Pulcinella Awards (2021):** *Brave Bunnies* won the award for Best Upper Preschool TV Series (4–6 years) at the Cartoons on the Bay festival [14].
*   **Quirino Awards:** The series was nominated for Best Ibero-American Animation Series [15][16].
*   **Kidscreen Awards:** The production company Beakus noted that the series received a nomination, and the show was highlighted in industry screenings [6][17].

## Merchandise and Licensing

The licensing program for *Brave Bunnies* covers toys, publishing, and apparel.
*   **Toys:** Spin Master serves as the global master toy licensee. The company launched a line of toys, including figures and playsets, in Germany and Italy in 2022 [7][18].
*   **Publishing:** Penguin Random House, under its Ladybird imprint, acquired publishing rights for the United Kingdom. The first titles, including *Brave Bunnies Spring to the Rescue* and *Brave Bunnies Make a Friend*, were released in March 2022 [13][19]. In Italy, books are published by Planeta Libre (De Agostini), and in Australia by Five Mile Publishing [7].
*   **Licensing Management:** Following the 2022 restructuring, WildBrain CPLG manages the global licensing and merchandising rights for the brand [7]. Big Picture Licensing previously acted as the master agent for EMEA and APAC [18].
