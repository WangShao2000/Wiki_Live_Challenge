# Love Me Forever (Pinkshift album)

**Love Me Forever** is the debut studio album by the American rock band Pinkshift. It was released on October 21, 2022, through Hopeless Records. Produced by Will Yip, the album marks the band's first full-length release following their 2021 EP, *Saccharine*. The album features the trio of vocalist Ashrita Kumar, guitarist Paul Vallejo, and drummer Myron Houngbedji.

Musically, *Love Me Forever* blends elements of pop-punk, grunge, post-hardcore, and alternative rock. The lyrical content addresses themes of mental health, social and political frustration, the experience of people of color (POC) in the rock scene, and personal trauma. The album was supported by several singles, including "nothing (in my head)," "i'm not crying you're crying," "GET OUT," and "in a breath."

Upon release, *Love Me Forever* received positive reviews from music critics, who praised the band's energetic performance, Kumar's vocal delivery, and the album's genre-blending sound. Publications such as *Kerrang!* and *Loudwire* included the record on their year-end lists of the best albums of 2022.

## Background and recording

Pinkshift formed in 2018 at Johns Hopkins University in Baltimore, Maryland, where members Ashrita Kumar (vocals), Paul Vallejo (guitar), and Myron Houngbedji (drums) were students [1]. The band gained initial attention with the viral success of their 2020 single "i'm gonna tell my therapist on you" and their 2021 EP *Saccharine* [2]. Following this success, the members decided to forego careers in medicine and engineering to pursue music full-time [1][3].

In 2022, Pinkshift signed with Hopeless Records [2]. They recorded *Love Me Forever* in March 2022 at Studio 4 in Conshohocken, Pennsylvania, with producer Will Yip, known for his work with bands such as Turnstile, Mannequin Pussy, and The Wonder Years [4][5]. The recording process took place over a period of three weeks [5]. Kumar described the recording experience with Yip as being like "summer camp," noting that the producer acted as a "coach" who encouraged the band to push their performances [5]. The band had completed the majority of the writing before entering the studio, allowing them to focus on execution and production during the sessions [5].

## Composition and themes

### Musical style
*Love Me Forever* is characterized by a high-energy sound that draws from various rock subgenres. Critics have categorized the album as pop-punk [2], grunge [6], post-hardcore [6], and alternative rock [2]. *Flood Magazine* noted that the album contains "undeniable nods to No Doubt at their most visceral," while also incorporating elements of punk and pop [6]. *Kerrang!* observed that the band moves beyond standard pop-punk, citing the track "cherry (we're all gonna die)" as reminiscent of the hardcore punk band Turnstile, and noting the "fighty power" of "BURN THE WITCH" [7]. The album also features slower, more melodic moments, such as the piano ballad "in a breath" [6][8].

### Lyrics and themes
The lyrical content of *Love Me Forever* is deeply personal and socially conscious. Vocalist Ashrita Kumar has stated that the album explores the concept that "love is rage," explaining that "when you love something, you fight for it" [7]. Themes include anxiety, depression, insecurity, and heartbreak [6].

Specific tracks address distinct subject matter:
*   **"nothing (in my head)"** deals with the overwhelming nature of social media and the digital age [9].
*   **"Trust Fall"** explores grievances from an abusive relationship [10].
*   **"BURN THE WITCH"** was inspired by Kumar's personal experience of being ousted from a leadership position on a college dance team, using the metaphor of a witch hunt to analyze groupthink and maliciousness towards women [3][10].
*   **"in a breath"** is a piano-driven ballad that offers a vulnerable look at the desire for peace and stability [10].
*   **"Dreamer"** serves as a powerful closer that channels passion and resilience [3].

The band also uses their music to center their perspective as people of color in a rock scene that has historically been predominantly white [6][11]. Kumar noted that the album is about "embracing yourself and everything that comes with it including the anger and sadness" [3].

## Release and promotion

Pinkshift released the single "nothing (in my head)" as the first preview of the album [2]. This was followed by the release of "i'm not crying you're crying," which serves as the album's opening track [12]. Other singles released prior to the album included "GET OUT" and the ballad "in a breath" [3][13].

*Love Me Forever* was released on October 21, 2022, on vinyl, CD, and digital platforms via Hopeless Records [14][15]. To support the album, Pinkshift embarked on a headlining North American tour titled "The Forever Tour," which ran from October to December 2022 [2][3].

In July 2024, the band released a remix EP titled *Love Me for the Summer*, which featured reimagined versions of tracks from *Love Me Forever* [16].

## Critical reception

*Love Me Forever* received positive reviews from contemporary music critics. *Kerrang!* awarded the album a high rating, describing it as "sharp, skilful, and bristling with infectious energy," and stating that it "announces Pinkshift as an already killer band" [7]. The magazine later ranked the album at number 19 on its list of "The 50 Best Albums of 2022," praising the band for proving they are "the future of the scene" [17].

*Flood Magazine* called the record a "stunning burst of influences and experiences," highlighting the vulnerability beneath the aggression and calling it "utterly indispensable" [6]. *Consequence* praised the album's urgency and the band's ability to blend "confidence, anxiety, rage, and empathy," noting that the trio plays off each other in a way that "makes it impossible to listen while sitting still" [12].

*New Noise Magazine* commended the album for going "above and beyond their contemporaries in the alternative rock scene," specifically praising the rhythm section of Vallejo and Houngbedji and Kumar's vocal versatility [8]. *Loudwire* included *Love Me Forever* in its list of the "Best Rock + Metal Debut Albums of 2022," comparing its impact to No Doubt's *Tragic Kingdom* on an "adrenaline rush" [9].
