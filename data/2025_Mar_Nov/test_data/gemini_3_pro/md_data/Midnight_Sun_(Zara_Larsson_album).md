# Midnight Sun (Zara Larsson album)

***Midnight Sun*** is the fifth studio album by Swedish singer and songwriter Zara Larsson. It was released on September 26, 2025, through Sommer House and Epic Records [1][2]. The album serves as the follow-up to her 2024 album *Venus* and features executive production by British musician MNEK, a frequent collaborator of Larsson's [3][4]. The record also features production and songwriting contributions from Margo XS, Zhone, and Helena Gao [1][5].

Conceptually, *Midnight Sun* was inspired by Larsson's Swedish heritage, specifically the phenomenon of the midnight sun and the concept of a summer night that never ends [1][6]. Musically, it is a dance-pop and electropop record that incorporates elements of Euro-dance, trance, and Brazilian funk [2][4][7]. The album explores themes of hedonism, ambition, self-doubt, and the complexities of adulthood [2][8].

The album was preceded by the release of three singles: "Pretty Ugly", the title track "Midnight Sun", and "Crush" [2][7]. Upon release, *Midnight Sun* received generally positive reviews from music critics, who praised its energetic production and Larsson's vocal performance, with some publications describing it as a "return to form" [9][10]. Commercially, the album debuted at number one in Sweden and reached the top 10 in Norway and the top 40 in the United Kingdom [2]. In the United States, it debuted at number three on the *Billboard* Top Dance/Electronic Albums chart [11]. To promote the album, Larsson embarked on the Midnight Sun Tour, which began in Europe in October 2025 [12][13].

## Background and recording

Following the release of her fourth studio album, *Venus* (2024), Larsson began working on new material that would reflect her origins. In interviews, Larsson stated that she wanted to create a project that honored her "Swedish pop heritage" [1]. She described the central motif of the album as a "Swedish summer where the sun never goes down," aiming to evoke the feeling of an endless summer night that remains accessible even during winter [1][6].

For *Midnight Sun*, Larsson reunited with British singer-songwriter and producer MNEK, with whom she had previously collaborated on the 2015 single "Never Forget You" [5][14]. MNEK served as the album's executive producer [3]. The creative team for the album was relatively small and consistent, consisting primarily of Larsson, MNEK, producer Margo XS, and songwriter Helena Gao [1][15]. Larsson described the recording process as a period where she took significant creative control, calling the resulting body of work her "most personal" to date [2]. The album was recorded between 2024 and 2025 [16].

## Composition

### Musical style and themes
*Midnight Sun* is primarily an electropop and dance-pop album [2][3]. Critics noted the album's "maximalist" production style, which blends various electronic subgenres including trance, house, and R&B [2][4]. *Clash* magazine described the sound as "irresistible Scandipop" designed for dancefloors [9]. The record is characterized by high-energy tempos and polished production, drawing comparisons to the works of fellow Swedish artist Tove Lo [3].

Lyrically, the album addresses themes of personal growth, ambition, and the "grey areas" of adult life [2]. While many tracks focus on hedonism and celebration, others touch upon vulnerability and self-doubt [8]. Larsson stated that the record encompasses "how life is so beautiful it makes you cry" [5].

### Songs
The album opens with the title track, "Midnight Sun", an electro-pop song characterized by energetic breakbeats and atmospheric synths [17]. It was described by *Rolling Stone* as "teetering between ebullient and delirious" [17]. The second track, "Blue Moon", is a pop ballad with a pulsing beat that explores summer love [8].

The lead single, "Pretty Ugly", incorporates elements of "crunk" and early 1990s rave music [3][7]. Lyrically, it rejects traditional societal expectations of "ladylike" behavior, with Larsson embracing chaos and "ugly" behavior [7][18]. "Eurosummer" evokes a beach environment and has been described as a "breezy slice of pop escapism" [3][19].

"Hot & Sexy" features a sample of reality television personality Tiffany Pollard and blends 1980s electro with Brazilian funk influences [4][20]. The track "The Ambition" is a "perfectionist's lament" that deals with the pressure of fame and the desire for validation [3][21]. The album concludes with "Puss Puss" (Swedish for "kiss kiss"), a track that mixes vulnerability with a "sweet Y2K pop aesthetic" [8][22].

## Release and promotion

*Midnight Sun* was released on September 26, 2025, by Sommer House and Epic Records [2][20]. In addition to the standard digital and physical releases, a limited "Lisa Frank Edition" CD was made available, featuring exclusive artwork by the design brand Lisa Frank, Inc. [20][23].

### Singles
"Pretty Ugly" was released as the album's lead single on April 25, 2025 [7]. The song reached number 14 on the Swedish charts [7]. The music video was directed by Charlotte Rutherford [1].
The title track, "Midnight Sun", was released as the second single on June 13, 2025 [17]. It peaked at number four in Sweden and received a nomination for Best Dance Pop Recording at the 68th Annual Grammy Awards [17].
"Crush" was released as the third single on August 15, 2025 [2].

### Tour
To support the album, Larsson announced the Midnight Sun Tour. The tour's European leg began on October 28, 2025, in Munich, Germany, and is scheduled to conclude the year with a show in Stockholm [12][13]. A North American leg of the tour is scheduled to commence in March 2026, visiting cities such as Los Angeles, Toronto, and New York [24][25]. Prior to her own headlining tour, Larsson supported Tate McRae on the North American leg of the "Miss Possessive Tour" in August and September 2025 [2][26].

## Critical reception

*Midnight Sun* received widespread acclaim from music critics upon its release [2][11]. At Metacritic, which assigns a normalized rating out of 100 to reviews from mainstream publications, the album received a weighted average score of 80, indicating "generally favorable reviews" [10][27].

*Clash* writer Luke Winstanley awarded the album an 8 out of 10, calling it a "return to form" following her previous album *Venus*. Winstanley praised the album's focus, describing it as "starry-eyed, joyous Scandi-pop built to ignite dancefloors" [9]. *DIY* gave the album a 9 out of 10, commending the reunion with MNEK and describing the record as "entirely sun-drenched, exuberantly formulated pop" that cements Larsson as a "genre VIP" [4][27].

*AllMusic* also reviewed the album positively (9/10), highlighting the "Rihanna-like vocals" and the balance between euphoric pop and introspective moments [2][27]. *Dork* magazine noted that while the album opens with polished, radio-friendly pop, it ultimately reflects Larsson's "confident personality and independence" [2]. *Rolling Stone* praised the album for sustaining "high-energy dance-pop joy" throughout its runtime [2].

## Commercial performance

In Larsson's home country of Sweden, *Midnight Sun* debuted at number one on the Sverigetopplistan chart, becoming her fourth project to top the chart [2]. Across Europe, the album debuted at number 10 in Norway, number 18 in Germany, number 33 in Denmark, and number 36 in the United Kingdom [2].

In the United States, the album debuted at number three on the *Billboard* Top Dance/Electronic Albums chart and number 22 on the Top Album Sales chart [11]. It also reached number 31 on the ARIA Albums Chart in Australia and number 38 in New Zealand [2].
