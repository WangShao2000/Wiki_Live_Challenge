# Natlan

**Natlan** is a fictional region in the open-world action role-playing game *Genshin Impact*, developed by HoYoverse. Introduced in Version 5.0 on August 28, 2024, it serves as the sixth major nation explorable by players, following Mondstadt, Liyue, Inazuma, Sumeru, and Fontaine [1] [2]. Associated with the Pyro element and ruled by the God of War, Mavuika, Natlan is situated to the west of the Great Red Sand of Sumeru [3] [4].

The region is characterized by its tribal societal structure, volcanic geography, and the presence of Saurians—dragon-like creatures that coexist with humanity [5] [3]. The narrative arc of Natlan focuses on the nation's perpetual conflict against the Abyss and the "Night Warden Wars," a recurring struggle to protect the land [6]. Upon its reveal, Natlan became the subject of significant controversy regarding the representation of skin tones in its character designs relative to the real-world cultures that inspired the region [7] [8].

## Gameplay

Natlan introduces several exploration and combat mechanics distinct from previous regions in *Genshin Impact*, primarily centered around the region's unique resource, Phlogiston, and the abilities of its indigenous creatures.

### Nightsoul Mechanics
The "Nightsoul" system is a set of mechanics exclusive to characters originating from Natlan.
*   **Nightsoul's Blessing:** Characters can enter this state, typically via their Elemental Skill. It enhances mobility and combat capabilities. While in this state, characters consume "Nightsoul points" to maintain special attacks or movement modes [5] [9].
*   **Nightsoul Transmission:** This mechanic allows players to switch to a Natlan character mid-air or during specific movement states (such as sprinting or swimming) to immediately activate that character's unique traversal ability. This action consumes Phlogiston [10] [11].
*   **Nightsoul Burst:** When a Natlan character is in the party and elemental damage is dealt to enemies, a "Nightsoul Burst" is triggered, which can provide buffs to the team based on equipped artifacts or talents [9] [12].

### Phlogiston
Phlogiston is a high-temperature thermal energy resource found throughout Natlan. It is represented by a gauge separate from the player's stamina. Phlogiston is used to extend the duration of Nightsoul's Blessing abilities and to power the abilities of indwelled Saurians [13] [14]. Players can replenish this resource by interacting with specific environmental objects, such as Phlogiston vents or statues [11].

### Saurian Indwelling
Players can transform into, or "indwell," various species of Saurians found in the open world. Each Saurian type offers specific exploration advantages:
*   **Tepetlisaurus:** Capable of burrowing underground and climbing cliffs [15] [16].
*   **Koholasaurus:** Able to move rapidly across water and "liquid phlogiston" [17] [15].
*   **Yumkasaurus:** Uses a grappling mechanic to traverse vertical spaces and gaps [15] [17].
*   **Qucusaurus:** Capable of long-distance gliding and flight [17] [18].
*   **Iktomisaurus:** Possesses special sensory abilities to interact with the environment [19] [20].
*   **Tatankasaurus:** A sturdy Saurian capable of dashing and breaking obstacles [21] [22].

## Setting

### Geography
Natlan is located west of the Sumeru desert [23]. The landscape is defined by volcanic activity, featuring mesas, canyons, rainforests, and natural hot springs [3] [23]. The region includes areas such as the Basin of Unnumbered Flames, Tequemecan Valley, and the Stadium of the Sacred Flame, which serves as a central hub for the region's martial tournaments [3] [24].

### Tribes
Unlike the centralized governments of Liyue or Fontaine, Natlan is organized into a confederation of six major tribes, each with its own customs, elemental association, and companion Saurian species [25] [4].

| Tribe | Element | Saurian | Primary Traits |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Children of Echoes** | Geo | Tepetlisaurus | Known for mining, dance battles, and gem crafting. Located in canyons [15] [25]. |
| **People of the Springs** | Hydro | Koholasaurus | Specialized in fishing, guiding, and relaxation. Located near hot springs [15] [15]. |
| **Scions of the Canopy** | Dendro | Yumkasaurus | Skilled in hunting and courier services. Located on cliffs and suspended structures [15] [25]. |

## Plot

The storyline of Natlan, detailed in the Archon Quest Chapter V: "Incandescent Ode of Resurrection," revolves around the nation's struggle against the Abyss [1].

### Premise
Natlan is described as a nation of war, where citizens engage in the "Pilgrimage of the Return of the Sacred Flame," a tournament to select warriors for the "Night Warden Wars" against Abyssal forces [6] [24]. The Pyro Archon, Mavuika, oversees these conflicts. A central element of Natlan's defense is the "Ode of Resurrection," a ritual that allows fallen warriors to be revived under specific conditions [24].

### Synopsis
The Traveler arrives in Natlan and becomes involved in the local Pilgrimage, allying with warriors such as Kachina, Mualani, and Kinich [24]. It is revealed that the "Night Kingdom," a spiritual realm acting as Natlan's Ley Lines, is being corrupted by the Abyss [6]. Mavuika reveals a plan devised 500 years prior to permanently defeat the Abyss, which required her to sacrifice her divinity and memories to maintain the Sacred Flame [24].

In the quest's conclusion, the Fatui Harbinger Il Capitano plays a pivotal role. To prevent the collapse of the Night Kingdom and save Mavuika from a fatal sacrifice required by the "Shade of Death" (Ronova), Capitano sacrifices his own immortality, merging his life force with the Lord of the Night. This act stabilizes the Ley Lines and allows the souls of the fallen to find peace, securing Natlan's future [32] [33].

## Development and Release

Natlan was first teased in the *Teyvat Chapter Storyline Preview: Travail* video released in 2020, which established its themes of war and resurrection [1]. The region was officially released in Version 5.0 on August 28, 2024 [2]. The design of Natlan draws inspiration from a synthesis of real-world cultures, including those of Pre-Columbian America (such as Aztec and Maya civilizations), West Africa, and Oceania [7] [1].

## Reception and Controversy

Following the release of the "Ignition Teaser" in July 2024, HoYoverse faced criticism regarding the character designs in Natlan. Critics and players pointed out that while the region draws explicit inspiration from Latin American, African, and Aboriginal Australian cultures, the majority of the playable characters were depicted with light skin tones [7] [8].

This led to accusations of colorism and "whitewashing" within the community [8] [34]. The controversy escalated when several English voice actors for *Genshin Impact* publicly expressed their disappointment. Actors such as Khoi Dao (voice of Albedo) and Zeno Robinson (voice of Sethos) criticized the lack of melanin in the character designs, arguing that it failed to respect the real-world deities and cultures being referenced [35] [35]. For instance, the character Ororon was noted to be based on Ọlọrun, the supreme deity of the Yoruba religion, yet was depicted with pale skin [8] [34].

Despite the backlash and calls for changes to the character models, HoYoverse did not issue a direct public statement addressing the specific design choices regarding skin tone during the launch window [8] [36].
