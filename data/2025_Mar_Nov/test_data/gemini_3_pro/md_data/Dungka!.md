# Dungka!

"**Dungka!**" (stylized in all uppercase) is a song recorded by the Filipino boy band SB19 for their third extended play (EP), *Simula at Wakas* (2025). It was released on April 25, 2025, by Sony Music Philippines as the sixth track on the EP. Written by the band's leader Pablo and produced by the duo Radkidz (composed of Pablo and his brother Joshua Daniel Nase), the track is an upbeat pop and electronic dance song containing elements of rave and techno music.

Lyrically, the song serves as a "diss track" directed at detractors, utilizing the title—a play on the Tagalog phrase "doon ka" (lit. 'go over there')—to dismiss negativity and criticism. The song also incorporates themes of Filipino street culture and resilience against "crab mentality."

The accompanying music video, directed by Kerbs Balagtas, was released alongside the single. It depicts a chaotic yet vibrant portrayal of everyday life in the Philippines and features cameo appearances by numerous Filipino celebrities and internet personalities, including Vice Ganda, Mimiyuuuh, and members of the band Ben&Ben. The video also served as a promotional collaboration with SNK Corporation for the fighting game *Fatal Fury: City of the Wolves*.

Commercially, "Dungka!" debuted at number seven on the US *Billboard* World Digital Song Sales chart and peaked at number 21 on the *Billboard* Philippines Hot 100. In October 2025, the song won Pop Song of the Year and the People's Choice Award for Song at the inaugural Filipino Music Awards.

## Background and release

Following the release of their 2023 EP *Pagtatag!*, SB19 continued their "trilogy" of extended plays, which began with *Pagsibol* (2021). The group announced the title of the third installment, *Simula at Wakas* (lit. 'Beginning and End'), in early 2025 [1] [2]. The EP was marketed as the conclusion to this conceptual trilogy [3].

On April 7, 2025, the group unveiled the tracklist for the EP, revealing "Dungka!" as the sixth track [4]. The song was released on digital streaming platforms on April 25, 2025, coinciding with the release of the full EP [5] [6].

## Composition and lyrics

"Dungka!" is three minutes and 35 seconds long [5]. It was written by Pablo (credited as John Paulo Nase) and produced by Radkidz, a production team consisting of Pablo and Joshua Daniel Nase [7] [8]. Musically, the track is described as an upbeat "rave track" [6] that incorporates elements of dance, electronic, techno, and hyperpop genres [9] [4].

The title "Dungka!" is a wordplay with dual meanings. In Tagalog slang, it functions as a contraction of "doon ka" (go over there), used as a dismissal of unwanted presence or negativity [10] [4]. In the Hiligaynon language, the word *dungka* means "to dock" or "to arrive at a port" [11] [12].

Lyrically, the song addresses the group's critics and "bashers." The lyrics demand that those who doubt the group or bring negativity should leave, utilizing the refrain "kung 'di mo 'to gusto, tumabi ka d'yan" (if you don't like this, move aside) [13] [4]. Media outlets noted that the song critiques "crab mentality"—a metaphor for people pulling others down who are trying to succeed—and touches upon social themes, including references to traditional politicians (*trapos*) during election campaigns [13] [11].

## Music video

The official music video for "Dungka!" premiered on YouTube on April 25, 2025 [6]. Directed by Kerbs Balagtas, the video runs for approximately six minutes and showcases various aspects of Filipino street culture [6] [4].

### Synopsis and reception
The video portrays a chaotic, energetic street environment in Metro Manila. Scenes depict common local activities such as street basketball, Zumba sessions in parking lots, karaoke singing, riding jeepneys, and children playing street games like *10-20* [6] [10]. The visual narrative positions SB19 within the community, performing amidst the crowds rather than apart from them [10].

The video features a specific antagonist character played by social media personality Ghost Wrecker, who is shown eating a crab, a visual metaphor for the "crab mentality" criticized in the lyrics [11] [14].

### Cameos
The music video is improved by cameo appearances from a wide range of Filipino celebrities, content creators, and musicians. Notable figures appearing in the video include:
*   Vice Ganda [6]
*   Mimiyuuuh [6]
*   Maymay Entrata [6]
*   Alodia Gosiengfiao [6]
*   Ben&Ben members Pat Lasaten and Agnes Reoma [6]
*   Sassa Gurl [6]
*   Smugglaz and Shehyee [6]
*   Kween Yasmin [6]

### *Fatal Fury* collaboration
The music video also served as a cross-cultural collaboration between SB19 and the Japanese video game developer SNK Corporation to promote the fighting game *Fatal Fury: City of the Wolves* [3] [15]. The collaboration was inspired by the game's tagline "REV IT UP" [15]. The video integrates gameplay footage and animated sequences from the video game, blending the group's choreography with the game's battle mechanics [16] [17].

## Commercial performance

"Dungka!" achieved commercial success both in the Philippines and internationally upon its release. In the United States, the song debuted at number seven on the *Billboard* World Digital Song Sales chart for the week of May 10, 2025 [18] [19]. This marked SB19's third entry into the top 10 of that specific chart, following "Gento" and "Dam" [19].

In the Philippines, the song debuted and peaked at number 21 on the *Billboard* Philippines Hot 100 [4] [20]. It also reached number 14 on the *Billboard* Top Philippine Songs chart [21].

## Live performances

SB19 included "Dungka!" in the setlist for their *Simula at Wakas World Tour*, which kicked off at the Philippine Arena in Bulacan on May 31 and June 1, 2025 [6] [22]. The group also performed the song on Philippine television variety shows, including *It's Showtime* [4]. Additionally, the song was performed as part of their appearance on the *Billboard Philippines* Studios series in May 2025 [23].

## Accolades

"Dungka!" received recognition at the inaugural Filipino Music Awards, held on October 21, 2025, at the SM Mall of Asia Arena. The song won in two categories: Pop Song of the Year and the People's Choice Award for Song [24] [25].
