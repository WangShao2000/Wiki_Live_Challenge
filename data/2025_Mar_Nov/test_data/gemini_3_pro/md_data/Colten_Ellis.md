# Colten Ellis

**Colten Ellis** (born October 5, 2000) is a Canadian professional ice hockey goaltender for the Buffalo Sabres of the National Hockey League (NHL). He was selected in the third round, 93rd overall, by the St. Louis Blues in the 2019 NHL Entry Draft.

Prior to his professional career, Ellis played major junior hockey in the Quebec Maritimes Junior Hockey League (QMJHL). During his tenure in the QMJHL, he established league records for the most career shutouts and the lowest goals-against average in a single season. In 2021, he was awarded the Jacques Plante Memorial Trophy as the league's best goaltender.

Ellis made his NHL debut with the Buffalo Sabres in October 2025, becoming the first goaltender from Cape Breton Island to play in the NHL.

## Early life

Ellis was born on October 5, 2000, in River Denys, Nova Scotia, a small community on Cape Breton Island [1][2]. He is the son of Brian and Jo-Ann Ellis and has one brother, Matt [2][3]. He began playing organized hockey with the Whycocomagh Minor Hockey Association [2].

During his youth career, Ellis played for the Cape Breton West Islanders in the Nova Scotia U18 Major Hockey League. In 2017, he goaltended the team to the Telus Cup, Canada's national under-18 club championship. The Islanders became the first team from Atlantic Canada to win the tournament [2][4]. In the championship game, Ellis made 43 saves and stopped a breakaway in overtime to secure the victory [5].

## Playing career

### Junior
Ellis was originally drafted by the Cape Breton Screaming Eagles in the fourth round (59th overall) of the 2016 QMJHL Entry Draft [6]. He appeared in three games for Cape Breton during the 2016–17 season as a call-up while playing U18 hockey [6]. In June 2017, the Screaming Eagles traded Ellis to the Rimouski Océanic in exchange for a first-round draft pick [6].

In his rookie season with Rimouski (2017–18), Ellis recorded 33 wins and 6 shutouts [2]. He was named to the QMJHL All-Rookie Team and the Second All-Star Team [7]. He was also awarded the Raymond Lagacé Trophy as the league's defensive rookie of the year [2][8]. Following his second season with Rimouski, Ellis was selected by the St. Louis Blues in the third round of the 2019 NHL Entry Draft [9].

In June 2020, Rimouski traded Ellis to the Charlottetown Islanders in exchange for a third-round pick in 2020 and first- and second-round picks in 2021 [10]. During the 2020–21 season, Ellis recorded a 23–1–0 record [11]. He set a new QMJHL single-season record with a 1.78 goals-against average (GAA) and an .926 save percentage [12]. On March 21, 2021, Ellis recorded his 18th career shutout in a 4–0 win against the Cape Breton Eagles, breaking the all-time QMJHL record for career shutouts previously held by Philippe Cadorette [5][13].

Following the 2020–21 season, Ellis was named to the QMJHL First All-Star Team and received the Jacques Plante Memorial Trophy, awarded annually to the goaltender with the best goals-against average in the league [12][14].

### Professional
#### St. Louis Blues organization (2021–2025)
On March 1, 2021, Ellis signed a three-year, entry-level contract with the St. Louis Blues [15]. He began his professional career during the 2021–22 season, splitting time between the Blues' affiliates, the Springfield Thunderbirds of the American Hockey League (AHL) and the Worcester Railers of the ECHL [16]. He made his professional debut with Worcester on October 23, 2021, making 42 saves in a win against the Maine Mariners [2].

Ellis spent the majority of the 2022–23 season with the Tulsa Oilers in the ECHL, where he struggled with consistency and injuries, recording a 3.46 GAA and an .888 save percentage in 27 games [2][17]. He later described this period as a time when he had to "relearn his love for hockey" [2].

In the 2023–24 season, Ellis played for the Orlando Solar Bears (ECHL) and the Springfield Thunderbirds (AHL). He was named the ECHL Goaltender of the Month for December 2023 [2]. He eventually earned a larger role in Springfield, posting a .924 save percentage in 16 AHL games that season [17].

During the 2024–25 season, Ellis served as a primary goaltender for the Springfield Thunderbirds. He recorded a 22–14–3 record with a 2.63 GAA and a .922 save percentage, which ranked fourth in the AHL [18].

#### Buffalo Sabres (2025–present)
On October 5, 2025, the St. Louis Blues placed Ellis on waivers for the purpose of assigning him to the AHL [19]. The following day, October 6, 2025, he was claimed by the Buffalo Sabres [18]. The acquisition came as Buffalo dealt with injuries to goaltender Ukko-Pekka Luukkonen and performance issues with other goaltenders on the roster [19][20].

Ellis made his NHL debut on October 22, 2025, starting in a home game against the Detroit Red Wings [21]. He made 27 saves on 29 shots to secure a 4–2 victory [22]. With this start, Ellis became the first goaltender from Cape Breton Island to play in the NHL [2][23]. He was named the game's first star [23].

On December 9, 2025, during a game against the Edmonton Oilers, Ellis sustained a concussion following a collision with Oilers forward David Tomasek in the first period [24][25]. He was removed from the game by concussion spotters and placed on injured reserve on December 11, 2025 [24][26]. At the time of his injury, Ellis held a 4–3–0 record with the Sabres [27].

## International play

Ellis represented Canada White at the 2016 World Under-17 Hockey Challenge. In 2018, he was a member of the Canadian team at the IIHF World U18 Championships in Russia [28].

In December 2024, Ellis was named to Team Canada's roster for the Spengler Cup in Davos, Switzerland [29]. He served as the starting goaltender, helping Canada advance to the semifinals. He finished the tournament with a 2.68 GAA and a .911 save percentage, the second-best statistical performance among goaltenders in the tournament [30].
