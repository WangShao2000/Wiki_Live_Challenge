# Papal inauguration of Pope Leo XIV

The **papal inauguration of Pope Leo XIV** was the liturgical service celebrated on May 18, 2025, to formally mark the beginning of the Petrine ministry of the 267th pope of the Catholic Church. Pope Leo XIV, formerly Cardinal Robert Francis Prevost, was elected on May 8, 2025, following the death of Pope Francis in April 2025. The inauguration took place in St. Peter's Square in Vatican City.

The event, officially known as the Mass for the Beginning of the Petrine Ministry of the Bishop of Rome, was attended by an estimated 150,000 to 200,000 people, including official delegations from approximately 150 countries and various religious leaders [1], [2], [3]. During the ceremony, the new pope received the papal pallium and the Ring of the Fisherman, the primary symbols of papal authority [4], [5]. Leo XIV is the first pope from the United States and the first from the Order of Saint Augustine [6], [7].

## Background

Pope Francis died on April 21, 2025, at the age of 88, following a period of hospitalization for respiratory issues [8], [9]. The subsequent papal conclave began on May 7, 2025, with 133 cardinal electors present in the Sistine Chapel [10], [8].

On May 8, 2025, on the fourth ballot, the College of Cardinals elected Robert Francis Prevost, an Augustinian friar and the Prefect of the Dicastery for Bishops, as the new Supreme Pontiff [11], [8]. He took the papal name Leo XIV, a choice that observers noted paid homage to Pope Leo XIII and his focus on social teaching, while also sharing a name with a fictional pope from the 1986 film *Saving Grace* [12], [13].

Following his election, the new pope celebrated his first Mass with the cardinals in the Sistine Chapel on May 9 and delivered his first *Urbi et Orbi* blessing [6], [10]. The inauguration Mass was scheduled for May 18, the Fifth Sunday of Easter [4], [14].

## Inauguration Mass

The Mass began at 10:00 a.m. Central European Summer Time (CEST) [1], [14]. The liturgy followed the "Order of Rites of the Beginning of the Petrine Ministry as Bishop of Rome," a liturgical book approved by Pope Benedict XVI in 2005 and revised in 2013 [5].

### Procession and Rites at the Tomb
Before the Mass, Pope Leo XIV descended to the tomb of Saint Peter inside St. Peter's Basilica to pray. He was accompanied by the patriarchs and major archbishops of the Eastern Catholic Churches [1], [4]. During this time, the pallium and the Ring of the Fisherman were placed by the tomb [4].

The pope then processed from the basilica into St. Peter's Square while the *Laudes Regiae* (Royal Praises) were chanted, invoking the intercession of saints for the new pontiff [1], [5].

### Imposition of the Pallium and Ring
The rites specific to the inauguration occurred before the Liturgy of the Word.
*   **The Pallium:** Cardinal Mario Zenari, the Apostolic Nuncio to Syria, representing the order of Cardinal Deacons, placed the papal pallium on Pope Leo XIV's shoulders [4], [3]. The pallium used was the same style as that worn by metropolitan archbishops, distinct from the modified versions used early in the pontificates of Benedict XVI and Francis [4].
*   **The Ring of the Fisherman:** Cardinal Luis Antonio Tagle, representing the order of Cardinal Bishops, presented the Ring of the Fisherman to the pope [3]. The ring is made of gold-plated silver and depicts Saint Peter with the keys, along with the pope's regnal name [4], [5].
*   **Prayer and Obedience:** Cardinal Fridolin Ambongo Besungu of Kinshasa, representing the Cardinal Priests, led the prayer for the new pope [4], [3]. Subsequently, twelve representatives of the Catholic Church—including clergy, religious, and laity—approached the pope to profess their obedience [15], [3].

### Liturgy
The Gospel was proclaimed in both Greek and Latin to signify the unity of the Eastern and Western Churches [16], [5]. Pope Leo XIV used the pastoral staff (ferula) previously used by Pope Paul VI and Pope John Paul II [4], [15].

## Homily

Pope Leo XIV delivered a homily focused on the themes of unity, reconciliation, and service. He began by quoting Saint Augustine, the founder of his religious order: "Lord, you have made us for yourself, and our heart is restless until it rests in you" [13], [17].

The pope described the mission of the papacy through the dimensions of "love and unity" [18], [13]. He stated:
> "I would like that our first great desire be for a united Church, a sign of unity and communion, which becomes a leaven for a reconciled world." [2], [13]

Addressing global challenges, Leo XIV noted "too much discord, too many wounds caused by hatred, violence, prejudice, the fear of difference, and an economic paradigm that exploits the Earth's resources and marginalizes the poorest" [17], [3]. He referenced his episcopal motto, *In illo Uno unum* ("In the One, [we are] one"), urging the faithful to become "one family" in Christ [6], [2].

He also paid tribute to his predecessor, Pope Francis, stating that during the vacancy of the Holy See, the faithful felt "like sheep without a shepherd," but were sustained by the late pope's final blessing [13], [19].

## Attendees

The Vatican reported that approximately 150,000 to 200,000 people attended the Mass in St. Peter's Square and the surrounding area [1], [2].

### Official Delegations
Over 100 government delegations and roughly 150 total delegations attended the event [1], [2]. Notable political figures included:
*   **United States:** Vice President JD Vance and Secretary of State Marco Rubio [1], [18].
*   **Ukraine:** President Volodymyr Zelenskyy [1], [20].
*   **Peru:** President Dina Boluarte [1], [18].
*   **Colombia:** President Gustavo Petro [1].
*   **Argentina:** Minister of Foreign Affairs Gerardo Werthein [1].

### Religious Representatives
Ecumenical and interfaith relations were a significant aspect of the attendance. Bartholomew I, the Ecumenical Patriarch of Constantinople, was present, continuing the tradition of close relations between the Holy See and the Orthodox Church [1], [2]. Representatives from the Anglican Communion, the Lutheran World Federation, the World Methodist Council, and various Evangelical and Pentecostal communities also attended [2].

Delegations from non-Christian faiths were also present, including representatives from the Jewish, Muslim, Hindu, Buddhist, Sikh, Zoroastrian, and Jain communities [2], [19].

## Post-Inauguration Events

Following the Mass, Pope Leo XIV greeted the heads of state and government delegations inside St. Peter's Basilica [1]. On May 19, the day after the inauguration, he held private audiences with specific leaders, including U.S. Vice President JD Vance, who presented the pope with a Chicago Bears jersey bearing the name "Pope Leo" and the number "XIV" [1].

### Possession of the Basilicas
As is custom, the new pope took formal possession of the major basilicas of Rome in the days following the inauguration:
*   **May 20, 2025:** Basilica of Saint Paul Outside the Walls [1], [5].
*   **May 25, 2025:** Archbasilica of Saint John Lateran (the cathedral of the Bishop of Rome) and the Basilica of Santa Maria Maggiore [1], [5].

## Misinformation and Deepfakes
In the weeks surrounding the election and inauguration, the Vatican contended with a proliferation of AI-generated deepfake videos depicting Pope Leo XIV [21], [22]. These videos included fabricated footage of the pope making controversial political statements, praising military leaders in Burkina Faso, and resigning from the papacy [23], [21], [24]. The Vatican's Dicastery for Communication issued warnings regarding these fabrications, and the pope himself addressed the issue of artificial intelligence, calling for ethical governance of the technology [21], [25], [26].
