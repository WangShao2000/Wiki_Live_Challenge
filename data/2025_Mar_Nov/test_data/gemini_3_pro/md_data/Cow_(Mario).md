# Cow (Mario)

**Cow** (Japanese: ウシ, Hepburn: *Ushi*), also referred to as **Moo Moo** (Japanese: モーモー, Hepburn: *Mō Mō*), is a fictional species of bovine from Nintendo's *Mario* franchise. Originally appearing as background elements and obstacles in the *Mario Kart* series, they made their debut in *Mario Kart 64* (1996). Distinguished by their rounded appearance and association with farm-themed race tracks, the Cows have become a recurring fixture in the franchise's spin-off titles, including the *Mario Party* and *Yoshi* series.

In the 2025 video game *Mario Kart World* for the Nintendo Switch 2, a specific female Cow was introduced as a playable driver for the first time in the series' history. This inclusion garnered significant attention from video game journalists and fans, leading to the character becoming a breakout popularity trend on social media, while also sparking minor controversies regarding in-game mechanics and animal rights depictions.

## Concept and creation

### Design and naming
The design of the Cow in the *Mario* franchise is a stylized, cartoonish depiction of a real-world Holstein Friesian cattle. They are characterized by white bodies with black spots, beady eyes, small horns, and pink udders [1][2]. In their original appearance in *Mario Kart 64* (1996), the Cows were simple, low-polygon models without accessories. Starting with *Mario Kart DS* (2005), their design was updated to include a blue collar with a hanging yellow bell [1].

For the release of *Mario Kart World* (2025), the playable Cow character was given a more distinct model to differentiate her from the standard obstacles. This design includes a nose ring and animations that allow for driving vehicles; while she typically walks on all four legs, she is depicted holding handlebars with her front hooves while driving [3][4].

The nomenclature for the species has varied throughout the franchise's history. In Japanese, they are referred to as *Ushi* (Cow) or *Mō Mō* (Moo Moo) [2]. Western localizations have used both terms interchangeably. "Moo Moo" is frequently used in the names of courses (e.g., Moo Moo Farm, Moo Moo Meadows) and in guidebooks describing them as obstacles [5][6]. However, the playable character introduced in *Mario Kart World* is explicitly named "Cow" on the character select screen [7][8].

### Development of playable character
The transition of the Cow from a background element to a playable character occurred during the development of *Mario Kart World*. According to art director Masaaki Ishikawa, the concept originated from a "silly sketch" drawn by a designer during the creation of a ranch-themed area [3]. The sketch depicted a cow driving a truck, which amused the development team.

Producer Kosuke Yabuki noted that the team was looking for new character additions following the extensive roster of *Mario Kart 8 Deluxe* and felt that utilizing "NPC drivers" (non-playable characters turned drivers) would fit the game's theme of an interconnected world [3][9]. The Cow was the first of these "NPC drivers" to be prototyped; her successful implementation led to the inclusion of other traditional enemies and obstacles, such as Cheep Cheep and Pokey, as playable racers [3].

## Characteristics

### Species behavior
In their role as course elements, Cows are generally portrayed as passive creatures. In *Mario Kart 64*, they remain stationary behind fences in the background [10]. In *Mario Kart Wii* and subsequent titles featuring the "Moo Moo Meadows" track, they serve as dynamic obstacles. They wander across the track, acting as heavy barriers that cause players to flip over or spin out upon collision [1][10]. They are often associated with the in-universe brand "Moo Moo Meadows Milk," which appears on sponsorship signage and delivery trucks throughout the series [1][10].

### Playable character
The playable Cow in *Mario Kart World* is classified as a heavyweight character, sharing statistics with characters such as Wario and Donkey Kong [8]. Unlike the human and anthropomorphic characters in the game, the Cow does not have unlockable alternate costumes [2][11]. Her character emblem is a stylized icon of a cow's face [8].

In terms of animation, the character maintains a quadrupedal stance when moving outside of a vehicle but adopts a driving posture where her front hooves operate the steering wheel or handlebars. Developers noted the humorous contrast between her realistic animal behavior and the act of driving [3].

## Appearances

### *Mario Kart* series
The Cows are most prominent in the *Mario Kart* series, appearing in almost every main installment since 1996.

*   **Mario Kart 64** (1996): Cows debuted in the course "Moo Moo Farm" (Mushroom Cup). They appear as 2D sprites and low-poly models in the background and do not interact with the racers [1][12].
*   **Mario Kart: Double Dash!!** (2003): While physical Cows do not appear on tracks, they are featured on the side of passing trucks in the "Mushroom City" course, advertising Moo Moo Farm [5][8].
*   **Mario Kart DS** (2005): The "Moo Moo Farm" course returns as a retro track. The Cows in the background were updated with 3D models featuring bells on their collars [1][8].
*   **Mario Kart Wii** (2008): Cows appear as active obstacles in the "Moo Moo Meadows" course. They wander onto the track during the second and third laps, creating hazards for players [1][13].
*   **Mario Kart 7** (2011): Cows appear on signage for "Moo Moo Dairy" in the "Coconut Mall" course [10].
*   **Mario Kart 8** / **Mario Kart 8 Deluxe** (2014/2017): "Moo Moo Meadows" returns as a retro course with high-definition graphics. The Cows appear in multiple color variations, including brown and black spots [1][13]. A Mii Racing Suit based on the Cow design was added via the *Booster Course Pass* [1].
*   **Mario Kart Tour** (2019): The game features a "Moo Moo Offroader" kart and the Cow-themed Mii Racing Suit [1][8].
*   **Mario Kart World** (2025): Released on June 5, 2025, for the Nintendo Switch 2, this title marks the first playable appearance of the Cow [7][11]. The character was unlocked in version 1.0.0 but was made available from the start in subsequent updates [8]. The course "Moo Moo Meadows" returns again, featuring a golden statue of a cow riding a bike [2][8].

### Other video games
Outside of racing, Cows appear in various sub-series of the *Mario* franchise.

*   **Mario Party series**: Cows appear as background elements in the "Western Land" board in *Mario Party 2* (1999) and its remake in *Super Mario Party Jamboree* (2024) [7][8]. They also appear in the "Fowl Play" minigame in *Mario Party 3* (2000) and on the "Perilous Palace Path" board in *Mario Party: Island Tour* (2013) [8].
*   **Yoshi series**: In *Yoshi's Woolly World* (2015), a "Moo Moo Yoshi" pattern is unlockable [1]. In *Yoshi's Crafted World* (2019), Cows appear as cardboard crafts and a wearable costume for Yoshi [8].
*   **Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games**: Cows appear in the "Dream Equestrian" event, which is set in Moo Moo Meadows [7][13].
*   **Minecraft**: The *Super Mario Mash-Up Pack* replaces the standard *Minecraft* cows with the *Mario* franchise design [2][8].
*   **Mario Pinball Land**: A Cow makes a rare appearance as an interactive element [7].

## Reception

### Critical response
The Cows have generally been received as a charming, albeit minor, element of the *Mario* franchise's aesthetic. Their role as obstacles in *Mario Kart Wii* was noted for adding a dynamic, chaotic element to the racing experience [10].

The announcement and release of the Cow as a playable character in *Mario Kart World* in 2025 resulted in a surge of media coverage. *GameSpot* described the Cow as the "early breakout character" of the game, noting that fans immediately gravitated toward the absurdity of a farm animal driving a kart [14]. *The New York Times* writer Rylee Kirk attributed the character's popularity to the "status elevation" from a background prop to a protagonist [2].

### Internet popularity
Following the April 2025 Nintendo Direct that revealed the character, the Cow became a subject of internet memes and fan art. On platforms such as TikTok and Instagram, gameplay clips of the Cow were frequently paired with the song "Mooo!" by Doja Cat [2]. Fans also created edits of previous *Mario Kart* track thumbnails, inserting the Cow into various scenarios, such as a *Mad Max*-themed edit of Moo Moo Meadows [2][7].

### Controversies
The inclusion of the Cow in *Mario Kart World* sparked minor controversies. The animal rights organization PETA (People for the Ethical Treatment of Animals), which has previously criticized the *Mario* franchise for its depiction of the Tanooki Suit, issued a statement regarding the Cow. The group expressed concern over the character's ability to consume "Dash Food" items that resemble beef burgers, labeling it as a depiction of cannibalism [2][14].

This "cannibalism" theory became a topic of discussion among players and critics. Some fans argued that the food items were likely vegetarian substitutes, while others embraced the dark humor of the situation [14]. When asked about the theory in an interview, producer Kosuke Yabuki declined to clarify the specific ingredients of the in-game food, stating only that the team was "happy with [the Cow's] reception" [14].
