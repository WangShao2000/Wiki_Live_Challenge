# Natlan
Natlan is a fictional nation in the video game Genshin Impact. It is one of the seven regions of the world of Teyvat, associated with the Pyro element and ruled by the Pyro Archon known as the “God of War.” Natlan lies west of Sumeru’s deserts and is characterized by volcanic landscapes and natural hot springs, as well as a society divided into multiple tribes[1]. According to in-game lore, six major tribes inhabit Natlan – the Meztli, Nanatzcayan, Huitztlan, Tlalocan, Mictlan, and Teteocan – each with distinct customs and territories[2]. The region is also home to dragon-like creatures called Saurians that have evolved alongside its people and serve as trusted companions in daily life[3].
## Culture and Setting
Natlan’s society is defined by a strong warrior culture. The people are organized into tribes that embrace combat and martial prowess as a way of life[4]. Reverence for warfare is deeply ingrained in local traditions – the Pyro Archon herself is revered as the Lady of War, reflecting the nation’s ideal of strength through conflict. Ritual battles and competitions are common, and victory in combat is seen as a source of honor and divine favor. Uniquely, Natlan holds that ordinary humans can rise to godhood through heroic feats: its Archon is not an eternal deity but originally a mortal champion elevated by the nation’s customs[5]. This belief that “humans can become gods” is inscribed in Natlan’s ethos and has fostered a culture where inhabitants aspire to prove their valor[5].
The tribal structure also contributes to Natlan’s rich cultural tapestry. Each tribe maintains its own traditions, deities, and Wayob (guardian spirits), yet all swear fealty to the Pyro Archon in times of war. Historically, travel outside Natlan was rare; for generations, Natlanese were hesitant to leave their homeland due to oathbound customs and the perils beyond their borders. These insular tendencies began to wane after the nation’s recent trials, allowing Natlan’s people to reconnect with the wider world. Despite internal differences, the tribes are united in defending Natlan’s sacred flame and its independence against external threats.
## Inspiration and Design
The lore and aesthetic of Natlan draw heavily from real-world indigenous cultures. Many elements of Natlan’s design are inspired by Pre-Columbian Mesoamerican civilizations (especially Aztec and Mayan), with additional influences from West African and even Polynesian motifs[6][7]. For example, several tribe and character names in Natlan – such as Tlalocan, Mictlan, and Xbalanque – reference Aztec and Mayan mythology. Likewise, the concept of warrior tribes and ritual combat evokes the ethos of these ancient cultures. West African influence is also evident; the name of a Natlan character, Iansan, echoes a Yorùbán deity (Iyánsa), and tribal customs in the story parallel themes from African folklore[8][9]. This fusion of cultural inspirations creates a unique setting within Genshin Impact, distinguishing Natlan through its vibrant blend of mythologies and traditions[4][10]. Developers have woven these influences into architecture, music, and character design – from pyramid-like steppe ruins and totemic sculptures, to tribal costumes rich with feathers and flames – to authentically capture the fiery, warlike spirit of Natlan.
## History and Archon
Natlan’s Pyro Archon is Mavuika, also called the Lady of War, who presides over the nation’s affairs. Unlike the other Archons of Teyvat (who are immortal gods), Mavuika was originally a human warrior who ascended to Archon status – making her the only mortal to join the ranks of The Seven in the current era[5]. She rose to power centuries ago through Natlan’s customary trial of champions, earning the divine seat and the Gnosis (magical conduit of the Archon’s power). Mavuika’s reign has been shaped by an era of turmoil: 500 years before the game’s events, during the cataclysm that destroyed the nation of Khaen’riah, Natlan bore the brunt of a major Abyss invasion[11]. Mavuika, together with six legendary tribal heroes, fought valiantly to repel the Abyss forces and protect her land[11]. In the aftermath of that Archon War, anticipating that the Abyss would one day return, Mavuika made a drastic sacrifice – she gave up her mortal life and enshrined her essence within Natlan’s Sacred Flame, vowing to resurrect when her people would need her again[12]. This self-sacrifice left Natlan without its true Archon for centuries, though the nation’s warriors continued to uphold her “rules of war” in her absence.
In the present storyline of Genshin Impact, Mavuika is indeed reborn from the Sacred Flame as foretold, reuniting the tribes and resuming her role as Natlan’s protector. The Archon’s return is a central plot of Chapter V of the game: the Traveler arrives in Natlan amidst an endless war simulation orchestrated by the Fatui (an antagonistic faction), and witnesses Mavuika’s awakening. To reclaim her title, the revived Pyro Archon must prove herself through a series of trials across the six tribes, recovering sacred relics of each. Ultimately, Natlan’s narrative builds to a climactic confrontation with the Abyss Order and the Fatui Harbingers. In a standout moment, Mavuika engages in a one-on-one duel with Il Capitano, the strongest of the Fatui Harbingers, on a volcanic battlefield[13]. Their battle – depicted in the cutscene “Clash of Frost and Flame” – shakes the very foundations of Natlan, as Mavuika unleashes her full power to shatter the cursed “Night Realm” enveloping her land. With the Abyssal corruption purged and Capitano forced to withdraw, the tribes of Natlan are finally freed from the cycle of strife that bound them. The conclusion of Natlan’s arc cements Mavuika’s status as a revered Archon reborn and sets the stage for the game’s next chapter.
## Development and Release
Natlan was hinted at in Genshin Impact’s lore long before it became accessible to players. Early references appeared in character stories and the official manga prologue, which in its English translation even used the name “Murata” for the Pyro Archon (a placeholder derived from the term Muratan, meaning a Natlan native)[14]. The region was officially unveiled in the Teyvat Chapter Storyline Preview video, where it was portrayed as the “land of war” governed by the flame. Natlan became a playable region with the release of Version 5.0 on August 28, 2024[15], making it the sixth major nation introduced in the game (following Mondstadt, Liyue, Inazuma, Sumeru, and Fontaine). This update unlocked Natlan’s explorable areas and Archon Quest Chapter V for players, who could venture beyond Sumeru’s western border into Natlan’s fiery territories. The new region brought significant gameplay innovations: it introduced the Saurian Indwelling mechanic, allowing players to partner with Saurians from each tribe for traversal and puzzles, as well as the Nightsoul’s Blessing state powered by a unique resource called Phlogiston[16][17]. These features leverage Natlan’s Pyro-rich environment and tribal theme to add fresh combat and exploration dynamics.
Subsequent patches expanded Natlan’s content, revealing more sub-regions and characters. In Version 5.3 (early 2025), Mavuika herself was released as a playable character, coinciding with the climax of Natlan’s story arc[18]. Her character is depicted wielding a claymore and the Pyro element, with abilities reflecting the “sacred flame” she embodies. Natlan’s addition to Genshin Impact was met with enthusiasm from the player community, who praised its distinctive cultural flavor and the resolution of long-running lore threads. Critics and fans noted that Natlan’s design successfully amalgamates diverse cultural inspirations into a cohesive fantasy setting, and its storyline – centered on themes of honor, sacrifice, and rebirth – provided a dramatic and satisfying chapter in the game’s overarching narrative.
