# Calum Lill
Calum Andrew Lill (born September 1995) is a British actor. He is best known for his roles in the television soap operas Hollyoaks and Coronation Street, portraying Police Constable Carlton Smith in the former and solicitor Joel Deering in the latter[1]. Lill began his screen career with guest appearances on the BBC series Doctors and Holby City in 2019[1]. In 2021, he joined the cast of Hollyoaks as PC Smith, and in 2023 he was cast as Joel Deering in Coronation Street[2]. His Coronation Street character was central to a whodunit storyline that culminated in the character’s on-screen death in 2024[3]; Lill later returned to the show in 2025 to reprise the role in flashback scenes[3].
## Career
Lill made his first television appearances in 2019 with guest roles on the daytime drama Doctors and the medical series Holby City[1]. In 2021, he appeared in the Channel 4 soap opera Hollyoaks in a recurring role as Police Constable Carlton Smith[1].
In August 2023, it was announced that Lill had been cast in the long-running ITV soap Coronation Street, portraying duty solicitor Joel Deering[4]. The character was introduced as a love interest for Dee-Dee Bailey and became part of a major storyline on the show[5]. In late 2024, Joel Deering was revealed to be a villainous character and was killed off on-screen as part of a murder plot[3]. Despite the character’s death, Lill returned to Coronation Street in 2025 to film flashback scenes featuring Joel during a brief storyline revisiting the murder[3].
Outside of television, Lill has also performed on stage. In 2025, he starred in a revival of Tim Whitnall’s play The Sociable Plover at the Old Red Lion Theatre in London[1]. He has appeared in Christmas pantomime productions as well, playing the villain Fleshcreep in Jack and the Beanstalk during the 2024–25 panto season in Bournemouth and reprising the role at the Oak Theatre in Scarisbrick in December 2025[1][6].
