# Dungka!
Dungka! (stylized in all caps as DUNGKA!) is a song by the Filipino boy band SB19. It was released on April 25, 2025, as part of their third extended play (EP) Simula at Wakas, under Sony Music Philippines[1]. Musically, "Dungka!" is an energetic pop track incorporating rave and hyper-pop elements[2]. Its lyrics deliver a defiant message to critics and naysayers, with the title derived from the Filipino phrase “doon ka” meaning “go over there” or colloquially “go away”[3]. Upon release, the song gained significant attention for its vibrant music video showcasing Filipino street culture and for achieving strong chart performance.
## Background and release
SB19 announced Simula at Wakas as the final installment of a trilogy of EPs that began with Pagsibol (2021) and Pagtatag! (2023)[4]. Ahead of the EP’s release, the group released the single “DAM” in February 2025 as a comeback track[1]. Simula at Wakas (which translates to "Beginning and End") was then released on April 25, 2025, including "Dungka!" as one of its focal tracks[1]. All five members of SB19 took a hands-on role in the EP’s creation, with member John Paulo Nase (known as Pablo) leading the songwriting and co-producing tracks alongside his brother Josue under their production moniker RadKidz[5].
"Dungka!" was not pre-released as a standalone single but was highlighted upon the EP’s launch with a music video on the same day. In interviews, SB19 described the EP as a celebration of their journey and evolution[6]. Within that context, “Dungka!” serves as a bold statement track, thanking supporters and pushing back against detractors. The song’s title is a play on a common Filipino expression “doon ka”, meaning “go there,” which the band uses as a tongue-in-cheek way to tell detractors to step aside[3].
## Composition and lyrics
"Dungka!" is an upbeat P-pop song that blends multiple genres. Critics have noted its “maximalist” production, filled with electronic rave-style beats and even hyperpop influences[2]. The track features rap verses delivered by members Pablo and Josh, as well as powerful group vocals, over a pounding dance rhythm[5]. The production is characterized by heavy bass and synth layers, giving it an intense, high-energy sound. The song was produced by SB19’s Pablo and Josue (RadKidz), who infused it with experimental sounds while keeping a pop structure[5].
Lyrically, "Dungka!" is often described as SB19’s clapback at negativity and online criticism. The lyrics address social media bashers, “crab mentality” (a local term for pulling others down), and judgmental stereotypes in a confrontational yet witty manner[7][8]. The hook centers on the phrase “dungka!”, a slang contraction of “doon ka,” meaning “go over there.” This phrase is repeated as a refrain telling off naysayers to basically “go away”[3]. Despite its aggressive premise, SB19 delivers the message in a playful tone. Reviewer accounts note that the song manages to be empowering and fun at the same time, turning a dismissive phrase into a celebratory anthem of self-confidence[8][3]. By using everyday colloquial language and cultural references, the track remains authentic to Filipino listeners while being accessible to new audiences[9]. SB19 has mentioned that “Dungka!” reflects their refusal to be dragged down by criticism, aligning with the EP’s broader themes of perseverance and renewal.
## Music video
The music video for "Dungka!" premiered on April 25, 2025, coinciding with the EP release. Running nearly six minutes long, the video is a vibrant montage that pays homage to Filipino street life and culture[10]. It depicts the members of SB19 performing in lively community settings: neighborhood basketball courts, streets lined with food vendors, and local block parties. Scenes in the video showcase everyday activities and iconic Filipino pastimes – for example, men playing street basketball, groups doing Zumba dance exercises in a parking lot, kids hopping through 10-20 (a traditional Filipino jump-rope game), people singing karaoke, and vendors selling street foods like fishballs and kwek-kwek[11][10]. In the background, there are also visuals of political rally posters and a mock election campaign, adding a touch of social commentary to the festive atmosphere[10].
A notable aspect of the "Dungka!" video is its many cameo appearances by well-known Filipino personalities[12]. SB19 enlisted a star-studded lineup of guest cameos to embody various facets of Filipino pop culture. Appearances include YouTube gamer Ghost Wrecker, comedian and TV host Vice Ganda, cosplay celebrity Alodia Gosiengfiao, TikTok comedian Sassa Gurl, and social media star Mimiyuuuh[12]. The video also features rappers Smugglaz and Shehyee, online entertainers Kween Yasmin and Malupiton, as well as Pat Lasaten and Agnes Reoma (members of the OPM band Ben&Ben), among others[12]. Each cameo guest is integrated into the scenes—such as Vice Ganda dramatically arriving in a neighborhood scene—to amplify the song’s message of pride and solidarity in Filipino identity.
Throughout the video, SB19 and the cast joyously perform and interact with the community around them. The visual narrative subtly responds to the song’s themes: for instance, one comedic scene shows a character (portrayed by Ghost Wrecker) literally devouring a crab, a tongue-in-cheek reference to “crab mentality”[7]. Other scenes address gender stereotypes and social biases in a humorous way, reinforcing the song’s stance against judgment and prejudice[7][8]. The overall tone of the "Dungka!" music video is celebratory and defiant. Critics praised its authentic portrayal of Filipino everyday life, noting that it feels “stubbornly, brilliantly local” while delivering a universally relatable message of resilience[9][13].
## Reception
Upon release, "Dungka!" received a positive reception from both fans and music critics. Commentators highlighted it as one of the standout tracks on Simula at Wakas, applauding SB19’s bold creative choices. Bandwagon Asia described the song as “one of the biggest surprises of the EP” for its daring sound and maximalist production style[2]. The Southeast Asian culture magazine VMAN similarly complimented "Dungka!" as a "joyful portrait of Filipino life" and noted that it refuses to dilute its local flavor, effectively turning a very Filipino experience into something with broad appeal[9]. The music video in particular was widely acclaimed for its cultural richness and the playful inclusion of celebrity cameos. Philippine media outlets like GMA News and the Philippine Daily Inquirer remarked on the video’s celebration of Filipino culture and community spirit[10]. Fans on social media also embraced the song’s message, often quoting the titular catchphrase “Dungka!” as a rallying cry against negativity[14][7].
In terms of commercial performance, "Dungka!" achieved notable chart success for a Filipino release. In May 2025, it debuted on the Billboard Philippines Hot 100 at number 21, marking SB19’s latest entry on their home country’s main chart[15]. Internationally, the song also made an impact: "Dungka!" entered the U.S. Billboard World Digital Song Sales chart at number 7[16]. This debut gave SB19 another top-ten hit on that global digital sales chart, underlining the group’s growing international fanbase.
The track’s popularity was further cemented by industry accolades. In October 2025, "Dungka!" won Pop Song of the Year at the inaugural Filipino Music Awards[17]. This award, presented by an organization affiliated with Billboard Philippines and other media, recognized "Dungka!" as the year’s top pop song in the Philippines, with SB19 beating fellow nominees including Sarah Geronimo and BGYO[18]. The song was also honored as Song of the Year at the 2025 PPOP Music Awards, a ceremony celebrating Pinoy pop acts, highlighting how strongly "Dungka!" resonated within the P-pop community. These achievements reflect the song’s wide appeal and the impact it had in 2025 as an empowering anthem.
By the end of 2025, "Dungka!" had become one of SB19’s signature tracks. Its blend of modern pop bravado, Filipino cultural elements, and an empowering message helped solidify SB19’s reputation as trailblazers in contemporary Pinoy pop music. The phrase “Dungka,” once a regional slang, entered the broader vernacular of fans as a memorable slogan symbolizing SB19’s triumphant stance against detractors[3][14].
