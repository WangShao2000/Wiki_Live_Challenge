# Tonight (PinkPantheress song)
“Tonight” is a song by British singer-songwriter PinkPantheress. It was released on 4 April 2025 by Warner Records UK as the lead single from her second mixtape Fancy That[1]. The track features lush string arrangements and a sleek, dance-oriented beat; OutNow magazine described it as a “playful, bass-driven anthem”[1]. Lyrically, the song depicts the excitement of a late-night romantic encounter. “Tonight” peaked at number 35 on the UK Singles Chart. Its music video, directed by Charlotte Rutherford, shows PinkPantheress and her friends disrupting a formal Regency-era ballroom gathering, dancing freely and breaking with tradition[2][3].
## Background and release
PinkPantheress announced “Tonight” in early April 2025 as the first single from her upcoming mixtape Fancy That[4]. The song was written and produced by PinkPantheress together with collaborators aksel arvid and Count Baldor[2]. In announcing the song, she said she was “so excited to finally bring out the first single of [her] new project” and expressed hope that listeners would enjoy it[5]. This single marked her first new solo release since “Turn It Up” in 2024[6]. The track was released digitally on 4 April 2025 via Warner Records UK[1].
## Composition
Musically, “Tonight” opens with lush cinematic strings before transitioning into a sleek, bass-driven dance groove[7]. OutNow noted the song’s “dance-ready groove” and PinkPantheress’s signature sweet-toned vocals with a flirtatious edge[7]. The production balances elegance with energy, combining orchestral elements with a contemporary pop beat. Critics have observed that the lyrics focus on anticipation and excitement for an evening together, fitting the song’s hypnotic, late-night vibe[8].
## Music video
The official music video for “Tonight” was directed by Charlotte Rutherford[2]. It is set in an opulent, grand mansion and portrays a formal Regency-era ballroom party. In the video, PinkPantheress and her crew crash the elegant gathering in period costumes (including corsets and powdered wigs) and begin dancing exuberantly[2][3]. The visuals deliberately contrast the rigid formality of the setting with the vibrant, free-spirited dance and celebration that ensue.
## Chart performance
“Tonight” entered the UK Singles Chart in mid-April 2025 and reached a peak position of number 35. It remained on the chart for six weeks. (It also charted in other countries, reflecting its commercial reach beyond the UK.)
