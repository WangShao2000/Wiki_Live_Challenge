# Jimmy Essex
Jimmy Essex (born 1984 or 1985) is a British actor, dancer and musician. He first came to public attention as a singer on the ninth series of The X Factor in 2012, where he performed as part of a three-member band. Essex later gained prominence for his role as Adam Donovan in the Channel 4 soap opera Hollyoaks, appearing from 2016 to 2018 as a regular cast member. He has since taken on other acting projects in film and theatre, including a lead role in the 2020 stage play GHBoy.
## Early career and The X Factor
Essex was born in the mid-1980s and is from Harrow in Greater London[1]. He trained in performing arts and initially worked in music and dance. In 2012, he participated in the UK singing competition The X Factor as part of a mixed-gender band called MITSOTU (an acronym for "Music Is The Soul Of The Universe"). The trio—comprising Essex and his bandmates James Collins and Holly Cooper—auditioned in front of an audience of 6,000 and received four “yes” votes from the judging panel, advancing past the first round[2]. All three members had prior experience as backing dancers for pop artists, with Essex having performed alongside American singer Janet Jackson during her tour[3]. The group ultimately did not progress to the live finals of The X Factor, being eliminated during the competition’s later stages. After their stint on the show, MITSOTU continued to perform at various venues, including a set at the Kensington Roof Gardens in London, before eventually disbanding.
Outside of music, Essex pursued opportunities in acting and modeling. In 2014, he appeared as a dancer in the short film Mr Bowler Presents – Hideaway. He also worked as a commercial performer, such as featuring in a Coca-Cola advertisement filmed in Madrid in 2016. These early forays in entertainment helped Essex transition his career focus from music to acting on television.
## Hollyoaks (2016–2018)
In March 2016, it was announced that Essex had been cast in the Channel 4 soap opera Hollyoaks, joining the established series as part of a new family introduced on-screen[4]. He portrayed Adam Donovan, one of three half-brothers added to the show’s cast as the siblings of existing character Grace Black. Essex made his first appearance as Adam in April 2016[4]. His character Adam was a former hairdresser with a moral streak, often acting as the more level-headed brother compared to his troublemaking siblings. Over his tenure in Hollyoaks, Adam Donovan was featured in several prominent storylines, including a romantic love triangle plot and involvement in the show’s criminal underworld.
Essex remained with Hollyoaks for two and a half years, during which time his character’s narrative took a darker turn. In 2018, the character Adam became entangled in a revenge and gang conflict storyline that culminated in a dramatic exit. In the episode broadcast on 23 May 2018, Adam Donovan is shot and killed by his own father, Glenn Donovan, in a surprise twist that marked the end of Essex’s run on the show[5]. The shocking death of Adam—who had gradually shifted from “good guy” to morally compromised before his demise—was noted for its impact on viewers and the soap’s plot[5]. After the episode aired, Essex recorded a goodbye message for fans and colleagues, in which he expressed gratitude to the Hollyoaks team and said he was happy with his character’s dramatic send-off[6][5]. His performance and the exit storyline were well received, and Adam Donovan’s death was longlisted for the Inside Soap Awards 2018 in the category of “Best Exit.”
## Stage and other work
Following his departure from Hollyoaks, Essex expanded his acting repertoire with roles in independent films and stage productions. He had a supporting role as Peter in the British horror film A Dark Path, released in 2020. The same year, Essex made his West End theatre debut in the play GHBoy, a drama written by Paul Harvard and staged at London’s Charing Cross Theatre in December 2020[7][8]. In GHBoy, Essex starred as Robert Finch, a gay man navigating grief, substance abuse, and relationship struggles against the backdrop of London’s chemsex scene[8]. The production and its subject matter offered Essex a new challenge as a stage actor. He noted that he was excited to tackle more diverse LGBTQ+ stories beyond the typical coming-out narrative, and he valued the “innocence and vulnerability” of his character Robert[9][10]. Despite disruptions in the theatre industry due to the COVID-19 pandemic, GHBoy ran during its planned schedule in late 2020, and Essex’s performance received praise for showcasing his range outside of soap opera roles.
Continuing to work in both screen and stage, Essex also appeared in a 2019 LGBTQ+ travel mini-series called Trailblazers, created by Gay Times and Celebrity Cruises, where he featured as himself. In 2022, he had a small part in the Netflix fantasy series The Sandman. In 2025, Essex took on a role in the short independent film Daddy Issues, playing a character named Felix. This mix of projects across different media reflects Essex’s versatility as a performer. He has additionally remained connected to television by taking guest roles; notably, in November 2025 he made a one-episode appearance in the BBC soap EastEnders, portraying a character involved in a brief bar altercation storyline.
## Personal life
Jimmy Essex is openly gay[11]. He has been in a long-term relationship with musician Charles Simmons since 2013[12]. In January 2019, Essex announced his engagement to Charles during a vacation in Sri Lanka, after Charles proposed with a diamond ring on the trip[13][14]. The engagement news was met with public congratulations from Essex’s friends and former Hollyoaks co-stars on social media. The couple later married in 2020; Essex referred to Charles as his husband in interviews by the end of that year[9]. In a 2020 discussion, Essex described Charles as his “best friend” and spoke about spending the COVID-19 lockdown period together[15]. The pair also appeared together in the aforementioned Trailblazers travel series, where they shared their enthusiasm for food and travel as a couple. Essex and his husband reside in London.
Outside of his acting career, Essex stays active on social media and has engaged in charity events. For example, during his Hollyoaks tenure he participated in a sponsored “sleep-out” event for the youth homelessness charity Centrepoint, alongside some of his castmates, to raise awareness and funds for the cause. He has also spoken in interviews about his interests in fantasy fiction and his admiration for actors like James McAvoy[16]. In his personal time, Essex enjoys fitness and often shares updates about his projects and daily life with fans on his Instagram account, where he has over 100,000 followers.
