## Stage Performances

A defining aspect of Jimmy Essex's career is his deep-seated passion for theatre, which he considers his original artistic calling [1], [5]. This commitment culminated in his professional West End stage debut in December 2020. He took on the lead role of Robert Finch in the play *GHBoy*, a production centered on the sensitive and complex themes of chemsex addiction, infidelity, self-discovery, and trauma recovery [1], [5]. The play, written by Paul Harvard, resonated deeply with Essex, who found the material personally meaningful due to its exploration of identity, stigma, and misconceptions surrounding gay culture [1]. His performance at the Charing Cross Theatre marked a significant milestone, transitioning him from screen-based work to the demanding environment of live theatre [1], [5]. The production ran from December 3, 2020, to December 20, 2020, indicating a short but intense run in the heart of London's theatre district [5]. This debut was not merely a career step but a fulfillment of a lifelong vocation for Essex, as he articulated in interviews, framing the role as a profound artistic statement [5]. Beyond this major role, his involvement in the arts extends to choreography and teaching. As of 2018, he co-founded Beyond Performing Arts, an innovative school dedicated to the performing arts, where he serves as an Acting & Dance Teacher and Choreographer [2]. This role demonstrates his contribution to nurturing the next generation of performers and his expertise in both acting and movement, solidifying his reputation as a comprehensive artist with a strong foundation in classical and contemporary performance disciplines.
