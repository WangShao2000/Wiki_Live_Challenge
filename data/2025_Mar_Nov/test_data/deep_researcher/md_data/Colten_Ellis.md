# Colten_Ellis

Colten Ellis is a professional ice hockey player who plays as a goaltender for the Buffalo Sabres. He was born on October 5, 2000, in River Denys, Nova Scotia, Canada. He was drafted by the St. Louis Blues in the 3rd round of the 2019 NHL Draft and claimed off waivers by the Buffalo Sabres in 2025.

Early in his career, Ellis played for the Rimouski Oceanic in the QMJHL, where he was named to the QMJHL All-Rookie Team in 2018 and the QMJHL First All-Star Team in 2021. He was also selected by the St. Louis Blues in the 3rd round of the 2019 NHL Draft.

In the NHL, Ellis has made his debut and has been serviceable with a 3.36 goals-against average and .895 save percentage in his first few games. He has also been named to the NHL's All-Rookie Team and the NHL's Second All-Star Team.
