## Introduction
The Morris Park Aerodrome, established in 1908, is a significant landmark in the history of aviation. It was the first officially established airfield in the United States, located in what is now the Morris Park section of the Bronx, New York. The aerodrome, occupying the grounds of the former Morris Park Racecourse, was in operation for only two years before the land was developed for residential use. Despite its short lifespan, the aerodrome played a crucial role in early aviation history, hosting the first exhibition of aircraft in the United States.

## Operations
The Morris Park Aerodrome, in operation from 1908 to 1909, was the first flying field in the United States. The aerodrome served as the site of the first exhibition of aircraft in the United States, hosting a number of notable events and demonstrations. The aerodrome was a focal point for early aviation enthusiasts and played a significant role in the development of aviation in the United States.

## Current Status
Today, the site of the Morris Park Aerodrome is no longer in use as an airfield. However, efforts are being made to honor the aerodrome's aviation history. An archivist at Fordham Prep has tracked down evidence of the aerodrome's significance, and there are plans to commemorate its legacy in the Morris Park neighborhood.
