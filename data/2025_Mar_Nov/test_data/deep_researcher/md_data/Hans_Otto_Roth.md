# Hans_Otto_Roth


Hans Otto Roth (29 April 1890 – 1 April 1953) was a Transylvanian Saxon politician and lawyer who made significant contributions to Romanian politics, particularly in advocating for the rights of the German minority in Romania. Roth was known for his unsuccessful attempts to counter the rise of radical Nazism and subsequently the German Party within Romania. He was a prominent figure in Romanian German history, representing the Transylvanian Saxon community and taking a public stance against Nazi movements.

## Early Life and Education

## Political Career

Roth's political career spanned several decades, and he held significant positions within the Romanian political landscape. He was a member of the Chamber of Deputies and the Senate of Romania, representing the Transylvanian Saxon minority. His political activities were characterized by his strong opposition to the rise of radical Nazism and its influence on the German Party in Romania.

## Career and Achievements

Roth's most notable achievement was his relentless efforts to counter the rise of radical Nazism in Romania. He worked tirelessly to protect the rights and interests of the German minority, and his stance against Nazi movements was well-documented. Despite his efforts, he faced significant challenges and opposition, including persecution by the communist authorities after the Second World War.
