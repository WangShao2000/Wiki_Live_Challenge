# A_New_Old_Play

A New Old Play is a 2021 Chinese film directed by Qiu Jiongjiong that explores the life and career of Qiu Fu, a renowned clown in Sichuan opera. The film chronicles Qiu Fu's life from the 1920s to the 1980s through his remembrances during his journey into the netherworld. The play delves into the birth of the New-New Theatre and Opera School in Sichuan Province, depicting the impact of civil war and political changes on the development of Sichuan opera.

## Plot
In the film, Qiu Fu, the topmost clown-actor of the 20th century Sichuan opera, passes away in an accident in 1980. Half-unwillingly, he sets off for the Ghost City, where he recounts his vivid memories of his life, including his childhood, performances, family tragedies, and the political events he experienced. The film intertwines personal and national history, presenting a vaudevillian theater of China's many pivots.

## Cast
The film stars Yi Sicheng as Qiu Fu, a renowned Sichuan opera actor of the New-New Theatre School. Guan Nan portrays Tong Huafeng, Qiu's wife, and Qiu Zhimin plays Pocky, a military doctor. Other notable actors include Haoyu Chen, Guan Nan, Tang Hao, Xue Xuchun, Tao Gu, and Xu Gang.

## Production History
The film was produced by Icarus Films and directed by Qiu Jiongjiong. It premiered at the First Look Festival in North America in March 2022, making its debut in the film industry. The production of the film was a significant event in the Chinese film industry, showcasing the rich cultural heritage of Sichuan opera.

## Critical Reception
Critical reception of the film has been generally positive. Some reviewers praised its unique storytelling and the way it intertwined personal and national history. The film has received awards and nominations, highlighting its cultural significance. However, others have noted its lengthy runtime and complex narrative structure, which may be challenging for some viewers.
