# Belmont_Glass_Works

Belmont Glass Works, a glassmaking company founded in 1866 in Bellaire, Ohio, was one of the early glass manufacturers in the region. The firm operated until 1890 and produced a variety of glassware, including chimneys, lamps, and bar goods. The factory was located along the east side of the county, which is known as Bellaire. During its operation, the company was known for its innovation and production of high-quality glass products.

# History

Belmont Glass Works, also known as the Belmont Glass Company, was established in 1866 in a riverfront village along the east side of the county, which is known as Bellaire. At that time, the community had a population of around 2,000. The firm was named after Belmont County, Ohio, where the plant was located.

- **Founding and Early Operations**: The company began operations in 1866, marking the start of glassmaking in Bellaire. Initially, the products were blown glass goods, such as chimneys (the glass surrounding the wick in a lantern), lamps, and bar goods. The factory was known for its high-quality glass and innovative production methods.

- **Production**: Belmont Glass Works produced a wide range of glassware, including:
  - **Chimneys**: Glass surrounding the wick in a lantern.
  - **Lamps**: Various types of lamps for households and businesses.
  - **Bar Goods**: Tools and equipment used in bars and restaurants, such as condiment sets, shakers, and mixers.
  - **Other Glassware**: The company also produced miscellaneous glass items, such as perfume bottles, decorative pieces, and lighting fixtures.

- **Legacy**: After the Belmont Glass Company/Works closed in 1890, the plant's legacy lived on—as former employees helped establish other glass factories in the region. The company's production methods and product line continued to influence the glass industry in Bellaire and beyond.
