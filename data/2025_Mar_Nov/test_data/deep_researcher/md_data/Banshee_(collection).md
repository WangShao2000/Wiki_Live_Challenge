# Banshee_(collection)


## Lead
Banshee (Irish: Bheansidhe) is the fourth collection by British designer Alexander McQueen, released for the Autumn/Winter 1994 season of his eponymous fashion. The collection is deeply rooted in Irish folklore and is a significant part of McQueen's fashion history, inspiring future collections.

## Introduction
Banshee, a female spirit in Irish folklore, is a harbinger of death, often wailing or shrieking to warn of a family member's demise. Alexander McQueen, a renowned British fashion designer, drew inspiration from the mythological figure to create his fourth fashion collection, Banshee.

## History
In Alexander McQueen's second runway show, he drew inspiration from Irish folklore about banshees who were heard wailing when a boat sank. This collection, released for the Autumn/Winter 1994 season, marked a significant moment in his career, showcasing his ability to blend storytelling, history, and raw emotion into his designs. The collection featured intricate patterns, dark tones, and dramatic silhouettes, reflecting the haunting and powerful essence of the banshee.

## Inspiration
The banshee is an integral part of the brand's history, with the collection paying homage to the legacy of Lee Alexander McQueen. The inspiration is particularly evident in the Spring Summer 2025 collection, where the banshee theme was woven into the fabric of the collection, inspiring the design of garments, hair, and accessories.
