# Dungka!

Dungka! is a pop diss track by the Filipino boy band SB19, released in 2025 for their third extended play, "Simula at Wakas". The title "Dungka!" is derived from the Sanskrit term "right-handed white conch", which is revered in the Himalayas. In Hiligaynon, the word means "to arrive at the port" or "to gain or obtain something". The song is a playful message of defiance, demanding haters to leave, and is a vibrant anthem honoring LGBTQ+ icons who inspire with pride, power, and authenticity.

In the music video, Dungka! takes a dig at bashers and trapos alike while celebrating and showcasing Filipino life. The video stitches together a tapestry of the archipelago's pulse, vivid, messy, and magnetic. It is packed with hidden meanings, cameos, and bold messages, making it one of the most iconic releases in P-pop culture. Dungka! is significant in the context of Filipino identity and pride, resonating with the community and celebrating diversity.

The song was created through a process that involved the band's leader, Pablo, and his brother, Joshua Daniel Nase, co-producing it. It was written and produced with the goal of addressing the negative aspects of society and promoting a positive message. The song's significance lies in its cultural and social impact, reflecting the aspirations and struggles of the Filipino youth.
