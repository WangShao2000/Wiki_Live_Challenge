# Tony_Hawk's_Pro_Skater_2_(GBA_video_game)

Tony Hawk's Pro Skater 2 (GBA video game) is a 2001 extreme sports skateboarding video game developed by Vicarious Visions and published by Activision for the Game Boy Advance. The game was released in North America on June 11, 2001, in Europe on June 22, 2001, and in Japan on December 14, 2001.

The game features a wide range of levels and challenges, allowing players to skateboard and perform tricks in various environments. It includes a variety of characters, including Tony Hawk, Steve Caballero, and Jason Lee, among others. The gameplay emphasizes precision and fluidity, with a focus on collecting points through tricks and performing well in competitions.

Tony Hawk's Pro Skater 2 received positive reviews from players, who praised its controls, graphics, and soundtrack. Many players also highlighted the game's depth and replayability, making it a favorite among fans of the series. The game is known for its faithful adaptation of the original console version, which allowed players to experience the game on a portable platform.

The notable features of Tony Hawk's Pro Skater 2 include its innovative gameplay, which emphasized precise and fluid movements, and its diverse range of environments and characters. The game's soundtrack, featuring various skate music tracks, was also well-received.
