# 2025_Pennsylvania_Supreme_Court_election


The 2025 Pennsylvania Supreme Court retention election was held on November 4, 2025, to determine whether three Pennsylvania Supreme Court justices would serve additional terms. The three justices up for retention were Christine Donohue, Kevin Dougherty, and David Wecht, all of whom are Democrats.

## Candidates

Christine Donohue, Kevin Dougherty, and David Wecht are all current justices on the Pennsylvania Supreme Court. They were up for a yes-or-no retention vote, where voters were asked to vote whether to give each justice another ten-year term.

## Process

Pennsylvania elects its statewide judges to 10-year terms, then holds nonpartisan "retention elections" — a simple yes or no vote — to determine whether the judges should continue serving. In the 2025 Pennsylvania Supreme Court retention election, the three justices faced a simple yes-or-no question at the end of the ballot.

## Outcome

Justices Christine Donohue, Kevin Dougherty, and David Wecht have won additional terms on the Pennsylvania Supreme Court. The election results were announced on November 4, 2025, and they were retained by a majority of voters. This outcome maintained the 5-2 Democratic majority on the state court.

## Controversies

Republicans, including President Donald Trump, had pushed voters to unseat the three justices as what has been a historically quiet race gained attention. Despite the push from some political figures, the justices were retained by a majority of voters.
