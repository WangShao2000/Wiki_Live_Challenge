# 2025_World_Seniors_Championship


The 2025 World Seniors Championship was a snooker tournament held from May 7 to May 11, 2025, at the Crucible Theatre in Sheffield, England. The event was open to amateur players over 40 and professional players over 45, provided they were not ranked in the top 100 in the world. Well-known snooker players such as Tony Drago, Jimmy White, and Ken Doherty participated in the tournament. The event was part of the World Seniors Snooker Tour and held significant importance in the snooker community.
