# Second-Ponce_de_Leon_Baptist_Church

Lead Section:
Second-Ponce de Leon Baptist Church is a prominent church in the Garden Hills section of Buckhead, Atlanta, founded in 1854 as Second Baptist Church. It merged with two other churches in 1932 to form the modern-day church. The church's mission is to "take the whole gospel to the whole person," as demonstrated by Jesus during His ministry on earth. It is a community engagement and spiritual growth hub in Atlanta.

Main Body:
1. History: The church evolved from Second Baptist Church, established in 1854, to the current church.
2. Location: Located at 2715 Peachtree Road NE, it is a significant landmark in the Buckhead community.
3. Significance: The church plays a crucial role in the community, focusing on community engagement and spiritual growth.
4. Notable Events: Notable events include the purchase of property in 1947 for a new church on Wieuca Road.
5. Notable Figures: Pastor and staff members contribute to the church's mission and community impact.
