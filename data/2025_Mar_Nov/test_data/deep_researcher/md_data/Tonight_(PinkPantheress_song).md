# Tonight_(PinkPantheress_song)

## Lead
Tonight is a song by British singer and songwriter PinkPantheress. It was released through Warner Records on April 4, 2025, as the lead single from her second mixtape, 'Fancy That' (2025). Musically, Tonight is a dance, dance-pop, house, and bassline track, produced by PinkPantheress, Aksel Arvid, and Count Baldor.

## Release
Tonight was released on April 4, 2025, as the lead single from PinkPantheress's second mixtape, 'Fancy That' (2025).

## Producer
Tonight was produced by PinkPantheress, Aksel Arvid, and Count Baldor. PinkPantheress served as the main producer and vocalist, with Aksel Arvid and Count Baldor contributing as co-producers.

## Lyrics
Tonight is a synth-powered dance pop track with lyrics about getting ready to get intimate with a loved one. The song features the line: "I'm waking up, ready to get it on, tonight."

## Notable Performances
Tonight has been released as a music video, which can be found on YouTube. The music video was directed by Charlotte Rutherford.
