# Kururin_Squash!


## Lead Section

Kururin Squash! is a puzzle video game developed by Eighting and published by Nintendo for the Nintendo GameCube, released exclusively in Japan on October 14, 2004. The game features a character named Kururin, who navigates through mazes, collecting coins and avoiding enemies. It introduces a variety of new items and obstacles, including water levels with a submarine, guns, boxing gloves, twisters, flame-throwing, and boss battles.

## Gameplay

In Kururin Squash!, the player controls Kururin, a constantly rotating stick, as they navigate through mazes to reach the goal without hitting any obstacles. The game introduces a new mechanic where players can collect coins, enemies, and bosses, each with their own unique challenges. Water levels feature a submarine, and there are also levels where players can use guns, boxing gloves, twisters, flame-throwing, and engage in boss battles.

## Development

Kururin Squash! is the third and final game in the Kururin series. It follows the original game, Kuru Kuru Kururin, and its sequel, Kururin Paradise, both of which were released in Japan only. The game was developed by Eighting and published by Nintendo for the GameCube platform.

## Reception

Kururin Squash! received a mixed reception from critics. The game was praised for its innovative mechanics and engaging gameplay, but it was also criticized for its limited scope and lack of variety. While it was not a commercial success, it still holds a place in the puzzle game genre.

## Conclusion

Kururin Squash! is a puzzle video game that adds new elements to the series, including water levels, guns, boxing gloves, and boss battles. The game was well-received for its innovative gameplay but faced criticism for its limited scope. It remains a notable entry in the Kururin series and the Nintendo GameCube library.
