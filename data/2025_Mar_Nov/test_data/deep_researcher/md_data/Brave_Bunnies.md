# Brave_Bunnies

Brave Bunnies is a Ukrainian children's animated television series for preschool children aged 2 to 5. Created by Olga Cherepanova, the series follows the adventures of two rabbit children, Bop and Boo, who show their courage and explore the world. The show is known for its themes of friendship and adventure, and has gained popularity worldwide.

## History
The series was originally created by Ukrainian production Glowberry and premiered on major TV channels. It has since become one of the most popular children's television shows in dozens of countries. The show follows a family of bunnies, Bop and Boo, who go on a journey around the world to find new friends. At each stop, they discover different animals and create new games to help build new friendships.

## Notable Characters
The main characters of the series are Bop and Boo, two rabbit children who show their courage and explore the world. They are accompanied by their family and the Bunny Bus, which takes them on their adventures.
