# The_Rector_of_Justin

The Rector of Justin is a 1964 psychological fiction novel by Louis Auchincloss. Set in a socially exclusive American boarding school, the story centers on Frank Prescott, the founder of the prestigious Justin Martyr School. The novel delves into the complexities of Prescott's life and career, exploring his virtues, failings, and personal crises across eighty years. It was recognized for its literary merit when Louis Auchincloss, a renowned American lawyer, novelist, historian, and essayist, was honored as a "Living Landmark" by the New York Landmarks Conservancy in the year 2000. The novel was a finalist for the National Book Awards in 1965.


The Rector of Justin explores the life and career of Frank Prescott, the influential headmaster of the famous Justin Martyr School. A former teacher, Prescott found his calling in founding the school and guiding its students through their formative years. The novel follows his journey, examining his personal and professional challenges, and the impact he has on the lives of his students and faculty.


The novel's plot is rich and nuanced, delving into the moral and ethical dilemmas faced by its protagonist. It is a mystery story, with an amateur detective who remains puzzled by the extraordinary success of an Episcopal boys' school. Through a series of reflections and observations, the novel provides a deep insight into the complexities of human nature and the challenges of leadership.


Louis Auchincloss, the author of "The Rector of Justin," was an American lawyer, novelist, historian, and essayist. Known for his ability to weave personal experiences into his novels, Auchincloss was celebrated for his literary contributions. His work often explored the themes of social and moral issues, and he was recognized for his craftsmanship and depth of character development.


The novel received critical acclaim and is regarded as one of Auchincloss's most accomplished works. It is a remarkable exploration of the life of a headmaster, offering a unique perspective on the challenges and rewards of leadership in the educational sphere.


In summary, "The Rector of Justin" is a thought-provoking novel that delves into the complexities of human nature and the challenges of leadership. Through the life of Frank Prescott, the novel provides a deep insight into the life of a headmaster at a prestigious boarding school.
