# Papal_inauguration_of_Pope_Leo_XIV


On May 18, 2025, at 10 a.m. in St. Peter's Square, Vatican City, Pope Leo XIV celebrated the inauguration Mass of his pontificate. This Mass, a solemn liturgy, marked the official beginning of Pope Leo XIV's papacy and underscored the bond with the apostolic succession.

## The Ceremony

The inauguration Mass was a significant event in the history of the Catholic Church. It was a solemn liturgy that highlighted the continuity of the papal office and the bond with the apostolic succession. The celebration took place in St. Peter's Square, the heart of the Vatican and a place of immense historical and spiritual significance.

## Participants

Pope Leo XIV presided over the Mass in St. Peter's Square, greeted by world leaders and a crowd of 200,000 people.
