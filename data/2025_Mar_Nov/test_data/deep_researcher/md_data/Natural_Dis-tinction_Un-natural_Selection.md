# Natural_Dis-tinction_Un-natural_Selection

Natural Distinction Un-natural Selection is a concept that highlights the interplay between natural and artificial elements, and how human activities impact the natural world. It is often represented in artistic and fashion contexts, such as the Spring/Summer 2009 collection by British fashion designer Alexander McQueen.

The collection contrasted the beauty of the environment with the impact of human industry. The first phase of the collection used soft colours, organic motifs, and flowing lines to present an image of nature unspoiled by man. The second phase featured prints manipulated to represent the intervention of human industry on nature.

In a broader philosophical sense, natural selection is a mechanism of evolution where organisms better adapted to their environment are more likely to survive and pass on their genes. This concept is often used to explain the evolution of species over time.

The term 'Natural Distinction Un-natural Selection' can be seen as a metaphor for the influence of human activities on the natural world, and the contrast between natural and artificial elements. This idea is not only relevant in the fashion industry but also in various fields such as environmental science, ecology, and philosophy.
