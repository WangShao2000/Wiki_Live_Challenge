# Sarabande_(collection)

Sarabande is the 29th collection by British fashion designer Alexander McQueen, released for the Spring/Summer 2007 season. The collection is known for its theatrical and historical references, featuring black-and-white ensembles with ruffled shirts and embroidery reminiscent of Spanish blackwork lace. The Sarabande Foundation, established by Lee Alexander McQueen, supports creatives with exceptional artistic ability through scholarships and heavily subsidized studio space.
