# 2025_Enderlin_tornado


The 2025 Enderlin tornado, which struck on June 20, 2025, in Enderlin, North Dakota, was the first EF5 tornado in the United States in 12 years. With peak winds exceeding 210 mph, the tornado caused significant damage, including the derailment of a train and the destruction of multiple homes and buildings. The event ended the 12-year EF5 drought in the United States and is considered the deadliest tornado to occur in North Dakota since the F4 tornado that struck Elgin in 1978.

## Intensity

The tornado was classified as an EF5, the highest rating on the Enhanced Fujita Scale, with estimated peak winds exceeding 210 mph. It is considered the strongest tornado to impact the United States in 12 years.

## Damage

The tornado caused significant damage, including the derailment of a train and the destruction of multiple homes and buildings. On October 6, 2025, the National Weather Service (NWS) Grand Forks, with additional analysis by expert engineering teams, upgraded the tornado to an EF5 rating.

## Impact

The 2025 Enderlin tornado is considered the deadliest tornado to occur in North Dakota since the F4 tornado that struck Elgin in 1978. The event ended the 12-year EF5 drought in the United States and highlighted the severe impacts of such powerful tornadoes.
