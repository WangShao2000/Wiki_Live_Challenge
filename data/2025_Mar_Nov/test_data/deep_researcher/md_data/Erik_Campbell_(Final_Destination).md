# Erik_Campbell_(Final_Destination)

Erik Campbell is a character in the supernatural horror film "Final Destination Bloodlines" (2025), the sixth installment in the Final Destination franchise. He is a main character in the film, introduced as the son of Brenda Campbell and a step-grandson of Paul and Iris Campbell. Erik is a child of Brenda Campbell, an assumed eldest son of Howard and Brenda Campbell, and the older brother of Bobby and Julia Campbell. He was created by Guy Busick, Lori Evans Taylor, and Jon Watts and portrayed by Richard Harmon.

Erik Campbell is a rebellious and bold character. He has a sarcastic sense of humor, wears black clothes, has many piercings and tattoos, and doesn't believe in the supernatural. He works as a tattoo artist and is known for his protective and caring nature towards his family. Erik's character is central to the filmmakers' goal of subverting the audience's expectations, and he plays a crucial role in the plot of the film.

Erik Campbell's character is notable for his unique background and the way he is added to the family tree. He is the only character in the franchise who was never meant to die, which adds a layer of complexity to the story. In the film, Erik faces a dangerous situation and ultimately sacrifices his life to save his family, leading to a dramatic and memorable death.
