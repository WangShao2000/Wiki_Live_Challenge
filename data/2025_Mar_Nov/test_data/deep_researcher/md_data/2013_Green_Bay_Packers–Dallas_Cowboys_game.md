# 2013_Green_Bay_Packers–Dallas_Cowboys_game

In the highly anticipated Week 15 matchup, the Green Bay Packers came back from a 23-point deficit to defeat the Dallas Cowboys 37-36 at AT&T Stadium in Arlington, Texas, on December 15, 2013. This dramatic victory marked the Packers' largest comeback in franchise history and secured their playoff hopes.

### Context
Both teams entered Week 15 with middling records, with the Packers sitting at 6–6–1 and the Cowboys at 7–6 (Packers 37-36 Cowboys (Dec 15, 2013) Final Score, ESPN, <https://www.espn.com/nfl/game/_/gameId/331215006/packers-cowboys>).

### Key Players
- **Matt Flynn**: The Packers' starting quarterback who orchestrated the comeback (Packers 37-36 Cowboys (Dec 15, 2013) Final Score, ESPN, <https://www.espn.com/nfl/game/_/gameId/331215006/packers-cowboys>).
- **Tony Romo**: The Cowboys' starting quarterback who led the team to a strong start but faltered in the second half (Packers 37-36 Cowboys (Dec 15, 2013) Final Score, ESPN, <https://www.espn.com/nfl/game/_/gameId/331215006/packers-cowboys>).

### Notable Events
- **First Half**: The Cowboys started the game strong, taking a 26-3 lead going into halftime, with kicker Dan Bailey converting four field goals (Packers 37-36 Cowboys (Dec 15, 2013) Final Score, ESPN, <https://www.espn.com/nfl/game/_/gameId/331215006/packers-cowboys>).
- **Second Half**: The Packers rallied, with Flynn throwing four touchdown passes in the second half. Eddie Lacy had the winning score on a 1-yard plunge after an interception by Tony Romo (Packers 37-36 Cowboys (Dec 15, 2013) Final Score, ESPN, <https://www.espn.com/nfl/game/_/gameId/331215006/packers-cowboys>).
- **Final Score**: Green Bay Packers 37, Dallas Cowboys 36 (Packers 37-36 Cowboys (Dec 15, 2013) Final Score, ESPN, <https://www.espn.com/nfl/game/_/gameId/331215006/packers-cowboys>).

### Impact
The victory was significant for both teams. For the Packers, it secured their playoff hopes and highlighted the resilience of their team (Packers 37-36 Cowboys (Dec 15, 2013) Final Score, ESPN, <https://www.espn.com/nfl/game/_/gameId/331215006/packers-cowboys>). For the Cowboys, it was a heartbreaking loss, as they needed a field goal to win but fell short (Cowboys 36 Packers 37 Final Score: Dallas Passes On Opportunity To, Blogging the Boys, <https://www.bloggingtheboys.com/2013/12/15/5213964/cowboys-36-packers-37-final-score-dallas-passes-on-opportunity-to>).
