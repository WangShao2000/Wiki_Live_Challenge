# If_This_Was_a_Movie


## Lead Section
"*[If This Was a Movie]*" is a pop-rock ballad by the American singer-songwriter Taylor Swift. The song is a part of her third studio album, *Speak Now* (2010), and was re-recorded for the deluxe edition of the album. It features a fast-paced melody and references classic movie tropes, such as a lover waiting in the rain and a happy ending.

## Background
Taylor Swift co-wrote "If This Was a Movie" with Martin Johnson, making it the only co-written song on *Speak Now*. The song was produced by Nathan Chapman. Swift's desire for the deluxe edition of *Speak Now* to be an entirely self-written album is what led to the co-writing of this song with Martin Johnson.

## Lyrics and Meaning
The lyrics of "If This Was a Movie" are about a character who is waiting for an ex-lover to return, much like a scene in a perfect movie. The character is longing for the past and wishing for a happy ending, but the reality is different. The song's lyrics include phrases like "Last night, I heard my own heart beating. Sounded like footsteps on my stairs. Six months gone and I'm still reaching. Even though I know you."

The deeper meaning of the song is a commentary on the difference between real life and the perfect romances we see in movies. The central movie conceit transforms from wishful fantasy to a bitter meta-commentary as domestic haunting imagery ("footsteps on my stairs") is introduced, reflecting the reality of a broken relationship.

## Songwriting
Taylor Swift and Martin Johnson co-wrote "If This Was a Movie." The song was produced by Nathan Chapman, who is known for his work with Taylor Swift. The song features a fast-paced melody and references classic movie tropes, such as a lover waiting in the rain and a happy ending. The song's lyrics are a reflection of the emotional journey of a character who is longing for a lost love and a happy ending, but is faced with the reality of a broken relationship.
