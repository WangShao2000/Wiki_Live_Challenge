# Voyage_to_the_Blue_Planet


Voyage to the Blue Planet was a 2024 concert tour by the American rock band Weezer, celebrating the 30th anniversary of their debut album, the Blue Album. The tour, which took place from September 4 to October 12, 2024, featured performances of Weezer's songs from their debut album and other albums, with special guests. The tour was also captured in a concert film, titled "Weezer's Voyage to the Blue Planet: The Concert Film", directed by Garrett Nicholson. The band aimed to bring their fans along to the "blue planet" and heal it with the power of the Blue Album.

Weezer, an American rock band formed in Los Angeles in 1992, is known for their hit songs and energetic performances.
