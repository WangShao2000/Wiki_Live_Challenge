# LIV_(nightclub)


LIV is a nightclub with two locations in the United States: LIV Miami, located inside the Fontainebleau Miami Beach, and its sister club, LIV Las Vegas. The club was founded in 2008 by David Grutman in Miami Beach and has since become a significant player in the nightlife industry. LIV Miami, located on the bottom level of the Fontainebleau Miami Beach Hotel, offers over 18,000 square feet of space and a vibrant nightlife experience.

David Grutman, the founder of Groot Hospitality, opened LIV Miami and has since pioneered the nightlife experience, making LIV a symbol of Miami's world-famous party culture. LIV has redefined nightlife on Miami Beach and has been leading the pack in Miami's club scene since its debut in 2008. The club's significance is not just limited to Miami; it has also expanded to Las Vegas, where it continues to offer a high-energy, luxurious night out.

LIV Nightclub is known for its unique blend of music, dancing, and VIP services, making it one of the most exclusive and popular nightclubs in the United States. It has been recognized as one of the best nightclubs in the world by various publications, including DJ Magazine.
