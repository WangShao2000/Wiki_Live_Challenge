# Love_Me_Forever_(Pinkshift_album)

Lead section:
The album 'Love Me Forever' is the debut studio album by the American rock band Pinkshift, released on October 21, 2022. The album features 12 tracks and is described as a blend of grunge and pop punk. It includes singles such as "i'm not crying you're crying" and "nothing (in my head)". The band, formed in Baltimore, Maryland in 2019, has gained attention for their unique sound and energetic performances.

Band History:
Pinkshift, an American rock band, was formed in Baltimore, Maryland in 2019. The band members met at Johns Hopkins University and began writing music. They have gained attention for their unique sound and energetic performances, blending elements of grunge and pop punk. Their debut album, 'Love Me Forever', was released on October 21, 2022.
