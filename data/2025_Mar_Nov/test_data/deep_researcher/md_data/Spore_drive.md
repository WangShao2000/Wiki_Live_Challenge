# Spore_drive

The Spore drive, formally known as the displacement-activated spore hub drive, is a fictional spacecraft propulsion system introduced in the 2017 television series Star Trek: Discovery. It is a revolutionary technology that allows ships to travel faster than the speed of light by utilizing mycelium spores. The Spore drive was developed in the 23rd century and was installed on two ships, but it has faced challenges, including a malfunction that resulted in the loss of all crew on one of the ships.
