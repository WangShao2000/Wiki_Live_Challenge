# 2025_Somerset–London_tornado


In the late evening hours of May 16, 2025, a large and deadly nocturnal EF4 tornado moved through the Kentucky cities of Somerset and London. The tornado, which packed maximum winds of 170 mph, caused significant damage, leading to multiple deaths and extensive property destruction.

## Details about the tornado
The EF4 tornado, which was one of the most destructive tornadoes of the year, moved through southeastern Kentucky. It was part of a larger severe weather event that affected multiple states. The tornado's path was long and destructive, stretching for over 60 miles. The tornado touched down in Russell County, approximately 4 miles southeast of Russell Springs, and continued to move through Somerset and London.

## Impact and aftermath
The tornado caused extensive damage, leading to multiple fatalities and injuries. The death toll from the tornado has risen to 19, including one person in Russell County, one person in Pulaski County, and 17 in Laurel County. The tornado caused significant structural damage to homes, businesses, and infrastructure in the affected areas.
