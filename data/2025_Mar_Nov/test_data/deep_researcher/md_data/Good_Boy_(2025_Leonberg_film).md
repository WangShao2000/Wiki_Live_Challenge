# Good_Boy_(2025_Leonberg_film)

Good Boy is a 2025 American supernatural horror film directed by Ben Leonberg. The movie stars Leonberg's own Nova Scotia Duck Tolling Retriever dog, Indy, in the lead canine role. Set in a rural woodland house, the film follows Indy and his owner Todd, a young man with a chronic lung disease, as they discover supernatural forces lurking in the shadows. Indy must protect Todd from these malevolent entities, leading to a thrilling and haunting experience.
