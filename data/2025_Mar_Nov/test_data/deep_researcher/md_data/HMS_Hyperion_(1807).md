# HMS_Hyperion_(1807)

HMS Hyperion (1807) was a 32-gun frigate of the Royal Navy, copied from the design of the French frigate Magicienne. Commissioned in 1808, she served in various capacities during the Napoleonic Wars and the War of 1812. Notably, in 1818, HMS Hyperion was involved in the capture of the Rattlesnake after a 48-hour chase. The ship was broken up in 1833 after a relatively short career.
