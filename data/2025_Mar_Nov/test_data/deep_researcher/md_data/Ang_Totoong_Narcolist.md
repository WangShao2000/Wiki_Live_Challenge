# Ang_Totoong_Narcolist

Lead Section:
The "Ang Totoong Narcolist" (The True Narcolist) is a series of five online videos that accused the Duterte family and allies of involvement in the illegal drug trade. Created by a man known as "Bikoy," the videos sparked controversy and legal actions. The series of videos, which were uploaded on April 2, 2019, named individuals and groups as part of a drug syndicate, including the mayor of Davao City, Bong Go, and the son of the president, Paolo Duterte. In a series of five online videos entitled the “Ang Totoong Narcolist” (The real narco list), Bikoy – wearing a hooded sweatshirt to obscure his identity – claimed that drug money was funneled into the bank accounts of the mayor's brother and accused the Duterte family of being involved in the illegal drug trade.

Main Body:
On April 2, 2019, Peter Joemel Advincula, a former criminal, created a series of videos titled "Ang Totoong Narcolist" (transl. The True Narcolist) and uploaded them on YouTube. The videos alleged involvement of the Duterte family in the illegal drug trade and named individuals and groups as part of a drug syndicate, including the mayor of Davao City, Bong Go, and the son of the president, Paolo Duterte. The videos sparked a controversy and led to legal actions against the accused and the creator of the videos. The Philippine National Police (PNP) is now looking for the man behind the viral “Totoong Narco List” video accusing the Duterte family of being involved in the illegal drug trade.
