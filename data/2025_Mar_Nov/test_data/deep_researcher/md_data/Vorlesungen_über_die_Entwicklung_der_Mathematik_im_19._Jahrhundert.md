Felix Klein (1849-1925) was a German mathematician who made significant contributions to the fields of geometry and mathematical physics. He was a professor at the University of Leipzig and the Technical University of Munich. Klein was known for his work on the Erlangen program, which sought to classify geometries based on their symmetry groups. (Citation needed)
