# Here_for_It_All

Here for It All is the 16th studio album by American singer-songwriter Mariah Carey. It was released on September 26, 2025, and serves as a return to music for Carey after a seven-year hiatus. The album is a collection of songs that pay homage to the Black pop music of the 1970s and 1980s, showcasing Carey's versatility in experimenting with pop and R&B sounds influenced by funk, disco, soul, and gospel. 

Carey has expressed her excitement and personal connection to the album, stating, "Here for It All is about being here for it all. With 'Jesus I Do,' it felt very reminiscent of some of your earlier gospel songs. It also brings a new level of self-love and self-care that I've never really addressed in my music before" (source: [1]).

Critics have praised the album, with Rich Juzwiak from Pitchfork stating, "The diva’s 16th studio album is thoughtfully constructed and surprisingly candid about the present condition of her voice. It’s a no-makeup sound for a persona devoted to glamour" (source: [2]).

Mariah Carey's fans and critics alike have praised the album for its emotional depth and Carey's vocal adaptability. The album is a body of work that feels personal, prayerful, and is a testament to Carey's enduring talent and creativity.
