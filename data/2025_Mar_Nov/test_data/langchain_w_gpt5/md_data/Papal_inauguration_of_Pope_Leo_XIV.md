# Papal inauguration of Pope Leo XIV

The papal inauguration of Pope Leo XIV was celebrated with a public Mass for the beginning of the Petrine ministry in St Peter’s Square on 18 May 2025 at 10:30 Rome time. The rite followed the post-1963 form for the start of a pope’s ministry, featuring the imposition of the pallium, the presentation of the Ring of the Fisherman, a ritual expression of obedience, and the proclamation of the Laudes Regiae. The congregation included cardinals, bishops, clergy, the diplomatic corps, heads of state and government, representatives of ecumenical and interfaith communities, and a crowd estimated at more than 200,000 people. The principal liturgical texts and rubrics were published in the official papal booklet for the celebration, and the Holy See and major international news organizations reported the event live and in subsequent coverage. The inauguration occurred ten days after the election of Robert Francis Prevost as Pope Leo XIV, the 267th Bishop of Rome and the first American-born pope, on 8 May 2025. [4](https://www.reuters.com/world/europe/cardinals-set-second-day-conclave-elect-new-pope-2025-05-08/), [9](https://apnews.com/live/conclave-pope-catholic-church-updates-5-8-2025), [12](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Leo-XIV), [16](https://www.vatican.va/news_services/liturgy/libretti/2025/20250518-libretto-inizio-ministero-petrino.pdf), [21](https://www.reuters.com/world/europe/pope-leo-xiv-hold-inauguration-mass-may-18-2025-05-09/)

## Identity and existence of Pope Leo XIV

- The Holy See’s official list of Roman Pontiffs records Pope Leo XIV as the successor to Pope Francis, identifying him as Robert Francis Prevost, with the beginning of his pontificate in May 2025. [1](https://www.vatican.va/content/vatican/it/holy-father.html)
- The official Vatican page for Pope Leo XIV provides his name, biographical details, and papal documents (messages, letters, homilies, speeches) issued since the start of his ministry. [2](http://www.vatican.va/content/leo-xiv/en.html)
- The Holy See Press Office bulletins refer to “His Holiness Pope Leo XIV” in regular coverage of papal audiences and acts (examples include audiences on 9 and 10 December 2025 and an apostolic journey in late November–early December 2025), confirming the seated pontificate. [3](https://press.vatican.va/content/salastampa/en/bollettino/pubblico/2025/12/09/251209a.html)
- Encyclopaedia Britannica records Leo XIV’s election on 8 May 2025 and lists him among the popes of the modern era, corroborating identity and succession. [12](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Leo-XIV), [13](https://www.britannica.com/topic/papacy/List-of-popes-and-antipopes)
- International coverage by Reuters, AP, BBC, The New York Times, and The Guardian on 8 May 2025 reported the election of Cardinal Robert Francis Prevost as Pope Leo XIV and identified him as the first American-born pope. [4](https://www.reuters.com/world/europe/cardinals-set-second-day-conclave-elect-new-pope-2025-05-08/), [9](https://apnews.com/live/conclave-pope-catholic-church-updates-5-8-2025), [10](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cpd4lpnpy7no), [11](https://www.nytimes.com/2025/05/08/world/europe/pope-leo-xiv-robert-francis-prevost.html), [29](https://www.theguardian.com/global/video/2025/may/08/robert-francis-prevost-becomes-first-pope-from-us-and-will-be-known-as-leo-xiv-video-report)

## Date, time, and location of the inauguration

- The Holy See scheduled the “Santa Messa per l’Inizio del Ministero Petrino del Vescovo di Roma” for Sunday, 18 May 2025, at 10:30 in St Peter’s Square. The Press Office document states that entry was open and non-ticketed. [4](https://www.reuters.com/world/europe/cardinals-set-second-day-conclave-elect-new-pope-2025-05-08/), [5](https://apnews.com/live/conclave-pope-catholic-church-updates-5-8-2025), [21](https://www.reuters.com/world/europe/pope-leo-xiv-hold-inauguration-mass-may-18-2025-05-09/)
- The official Vatican libretto, prepared for the papal chapel celebration in St Peter’s Square, contains the full order of service for 18 May 2025. [16](https://www.vatican.va/news_services/liturgy/libretti/2025/20250518-libretto-inizio-ministero-petrino.pdf)
- L’Osservatore Romano previewed the liturgy on the eve of the celebration, noting the pre-Mass prayer at the Tomb of St Peter and the location in St Peter’s Square. [18](https://www.osservatoreromano.va/it/news/2025-05/quo-114/messa-per-l-inizio-del-ministero-petrino.html)

## Liturgical rites and structure

The inauguration followed the Ordo for the Beginning of the Petrine Ministry developed after the end of papal coronations, with elements introduced in 1978 and codified in 2005–2013:

- Pre-Mass station and prayer: The Pope, accompanied by Eastern Catholic Patriarchs, descended to the Sepulchre of St Peter for prayer before processing to the outdoor altar. [16](https://www.vatican.va/news_services/liturgy/libretti/2025/20250518-libretto-inizio-ministero-petrino.pdf)
- Laudes Regiae: The traditional acclamation (“Christus vincit, Christus regnat, Christus imperat”) was sung at the beginning of the rite. [16](https://www.vatican.va/news_services/liturgy/libretti/2025/20250518-libretto-inizio-ministero-petrino.pdf)
- Blessing and aspersion: The Ordo provides for the preparation and sprinkling of holy water prior to the Mass. [16](https://www.vatican.va/news_services/liturgy/libretti/2025/20250518-libretto-inizio-ministero-petrino.pdf)
- Liturgy of the Word: Scriptural readings included Acts 4:8–12, Psalm 117, 1 Peter 5:1–5, 10–11, and John 21:15–19. The Gospel was proclaimed in Latin and Greek, following established post-1978 practice. [16](https://www.vatican.va/news_services/liturgy/libretti/2025/20250518-libretto-inizio-ministero-petrino.pdf), [15](https://www.vatican.va/news_services/liturgy/2006/documents/ns_lit_doc_20061221_ministero-petrino_en.html)
- Imposition of the pallium and presentation of the Ring of the Fisherman: A cardinal deacon imposed the pallium upon the Pope, and a cardinal presented the Ring of the Fisherman. The rite text and prayers are printed in the libretto. [16](https://www.vatican.va/news_services/liturgy/libretti/2025/20250518-libretto-inizio-ministero-petrino.pdf)
- Ritual of obedience: Representatives of the People of God approached and expressed obedience to the new Pope, according to the formula adopted in recent inaugurations. [16](https://www.vatican.va/news_services/liturgy/libretti/2025/20250518-libretto-inizio-ministero-petrino.pdf), [15](https://www.vatican.va/news_services/liturgy/2006/documents/ns_lit_doc_20061221_ministero-petrino_en.html), [17](https://www.vatican.va/news_services/liturgy/2013/documents/ns_lit_doc_20130222_riti-inizio-pontificato_it.html)
- Eucharistic celebration: The Mass continued with the homily, Creed, universal prayer (with intercessions in multiple languages), the Liturgy of the Eucharist, and the concluding rites. [16](https://www.vatican.va/news_services/liturgy/libretti/2025/20250518-libretto-inizio-ministero-petrino.pdf)
- Homily: The official text of Pope Leo XIV’s homily, delivered during the Mass, greeted the authorities and diplomatic corps and emphasized themes of service and charity. [20](http://www.vatican.va/content/leo-xiv/en/homilies/2025/documents/20250518-inizio-pontificato.html)

Context of the Ordo and recent precedent:

- The Ordo for the Beginning of the Petrine Ministry was approved by Benedict XVI on 20 April 2005, framing inaugurations as solemn Masses with pallium and ring rather than coronations. [14](https://www.britannica.com/topic/papacy/List-of-popes-and-antipopes), [15](https://www.vatican.va/news_services/liturgy/2006/documents/ns_lit_doc_20061221_ministero-petrino_en.html)
- In 2013, rites specific to the start of the pontificate were ordered to occur before and outside the Mass rather than within it, streamlining the celebration. [17](https://www.vatican.va/news_services/liturgy/2013/documents/ns_lit_doc_20130222_riti-inizio-pontificato_it.html)
- Benedict XVI’s 24 April 2005 inauguration followed the Ordo with an inaugural homily that reflected on the pallium and ring as symbols of pastoral ministry. [28](http://www.vatican.va/content/benedict-xvi/it/homilies/2005/documents/hf_ben-xvi_hom_20050424_inizio-pontificato.html)

## Principal celebrants, participants, and attendees

- Eastern Catholic Patriarchs accompanied the Pope to the Confessio at the Tomb of St Peter and participated in the opening rites. [18](https://www.osservatoreromano.va/it/news/2025-05/quo-114/messa-per-l-inizio-del-ministero-petrino.html), [19](https://www.osservatoreromano.va/it/news/2025-05/quo-115/gioia-e-commozione-tra-il-popolo-di-dio.html)
- L’Osservatore Romano reported the ministers of key rites:
  - Cardinal Mario Zenari imposed the pallium. 
  - Cardinal Fridolin Ambongo led prayers for the Pope.
  - Cardinal Luis Antonio Tagle presented the Ring of the Fisherman. [19](https://www.osservatoreromano.va/it/news/2025-05/quo-115/gioia-e-commozione-tra-il-popolo-di-dio.html)
- Representatives for the ritual of obedience included cardinals, a bishop, clergy, religious, lay faithful, and young people drawn from several continents, reflecting the universal character of the Church. [19](https://www.osservatoreromano.va/it/news/2025-05/quo-115/gioia-e-commozione-tra-il-popolo-di-dio.html)
- Notable attendees included:
  - Italy’s President Sergio Mattarella and Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni.
  - Peru’s President Dina Boluarte.
  - The United States’ Vice President JD Vance and Secretary of State Marco Rubio.
  - Ecumenical Patriarch Bartholomew and representatives of other faith traditions.
  - The diplomatic corps and more than 150 official delegations. [19](https://www.osservatoreromano.va/it/news/2025-05/quo-115/gioia-e-commozione-tra-il-popolo-di-dio.html), [23](https://apnews.com/article/vatican-pope-leo-xiv-cd19c352d0f2b4efec44df8ecc73af38)

## Symbols and regalia

- Pallium: The papal pallium described in the libretto is a straight sash of white wool, curved to rest on the shoulders over the chasuble, with two black flaps (front and back), six black silk crosses, and three pins representing the nails of the Cross. The pallium symbolizes the Pope’s role as shepherd and his communion with the Church of Rome and the metropolitan churches. [16](https://www.vatican.va/news_services/liturgy/libretti/2025/20250518-libretto-inizio-ministero-petrino.pdf)
- Ring of the Fisherman: The ring bears an image of St Peter with the keys and the net and carries the reigning pope’s name; it signifies the duty entrusted to Peter to confirm the brethren. Standard designs have been gold, though recent practice includes gold-plated silver. [16](https://www.vatican.va/news_services/liturgy/libretti/2025/20250518-libretto-inizio-ministero-petrino.pdf), [24](https://www.britannica.com/topic/Fishermans-Ring)
- Comparisons since the end of coronations:
  - Benedict XVI initially adopted a wider, wrap-around pallium distinct from the archiepiscopal form, before returning to the circular style in 2008 due to practical considerations. [27](https://www.newliturgicalmovement.org/2006/06/cns-story-popes-pallium-noticeably.html), [26](https://clericalwhispers.blogspot.com/2008/06/benedict-xvi-changing-pallium-again.html)
  - Francis received an archiepiscopal-style pallium and chose a gold-plated silver ring originally designed for Paul VI, continuing the more modest regalia norms. [25](https://www.archivioradiovaticana.va/storico/2013/03/19/pope_francis_receives_pallium,_ring_at_start_of_inaugural_mass/en1-674747), [26](https://www.dw.com/en/hundreds-of-thousands-attend-inauguration-of-pope-francis/a-16682578)

## Planning, protocols, music, and media coverage

- Ceremonial order and music: The libretto outlines the ceremonial order (Confessio prayer, Laudes Regiae, aspersion, readings, imposition of pallium, delivery of ring, obedience, homily, Eucharist) and lists chant and hymn selections, including Tu es Petrus, Vidi aquam, Gloria (De angelis), Credo III, and Tu es pastor ovium. [16](https://www.vatican.va/news_services/liturgy/libretti/2025/20250518-libretto-inizio-ministero-petrino.pdf)
- Attendance and crowd management: L’Osservatore Romano estimated more than 200,000 people in St Peter’s Square and the Via della Conciliazione. The Pope made a pre-Mass circuit of the square in the popemobile. [19](https://www.osservatoreromano.va/it/news/2025-05/quo-115/gioia-e-commozione-tra-il-popolo-di-dio.html), [32](https://www.reutersconnect.com/item/pope-leo-xivs-inaugural-mass-at-the-vatican/dGFnOnJldXRlcnMuY29tLDIwMjU6bmV3c21sX1JDMjdLRUFCTEZPSA)
- Delegation seating and protocol: Italian diocesan guidance noted early-morning movements, popemobile tour, Mass start, and the presence and seating of delegations (including Italy, the United States, and Peru). [35](https://ilpopolopordenone.it/2025/05/leone-xiv-domenica-18-maggio-ore-10-messa-di-insediamento/)
- Broadcast and media: The Vatican News service livestreamed the Mass; international broadcasters and agencies covered the event, including Reuters, AP, BBC, and France24. [36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CO2aYIDuYzE), [21](https://www.reuters.com/world/europe/pope-leo-xiv-hold-inauguration-mass-may-18-2025-05-09/), [23](https://apnews.com/article/vatican-pope-leo-xiv-cd19c352d0f2b4efec44df8ecc73af38), [22](https://www.france24.com/en/video/20250518-leo-xiv-s-inauguration-mass-begins-in-st-peter-s-square)
- Logistics: The Holy See announced in advance that entry to the inauguration Mass was open and did not require tickets. [5](https://apnews.com/live/conclave-pope-catholic-church-updates-5-8-2025)

## Public and expert reactions; commentary and disputes

- International agencies described the inauguration as emphasizing unity and service. AP reported that the homily offered “a message of love and unity,” with greetings to civil authorities and the diplomatic corps. [23](https://apnews.com/article/vatican-pope-leo-xiv-cd19c352d0f2b4efec44df8ecc73af38)
- BBC and Reuters reported the receipt of the pallium and ring and the formal opening of the pontificate. [10](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cpd4lpnpy7no), [21](https://www.reuters.com/world/europe/pope-leo-xiv-hold-inauguration-mass-may-18-2025-05-09/)
- L’Osservatore Romano’s detailed account noted appeals for peace in Gaza, Ukraine, and Myanmar during the Regina Caeli after Mass. [19](https://www.osservatoreromano.va/it/news/2025-05/quo-115/gioia-e-commozione-tra-il-popolo-di-dio.html)
- No reliable sources reported significant disputes about the conduct or validity of the inauguration rites.

## Immediate aftermath and early acts

- On 9 May 2025, Vatican News announced the inauguration schedule and that heads and members of Roman Curia institutions would continue provisionally until otherwise provided (donec aliter provideatur), indicating continuity in governance during the transition. [15](https://www.vaticannews.va/en/pope/news/2025-05/inaugural-mass-of-pope-leo-xiv-to-be-held-on-may-18.html)
- Following the Mass on 18 May 2025, AP footage documented the Pope greeting attending political leaders and delegations at the Vatican. [33](https://apnews.com/video/new-pope-receives-political-figures-who-attended-investiture-mass-in-vatican-02a0553fc7bc4e5aa026be994923b0d3)
- Subsequent Holy See Press Office bulletins recorded audiences and acts of governance in the weeks and months following the inauguration, evidencing the start of ordinary papal ministry. [3](https://press.vatican.va/content/salastampa/en/bollettino/pubblico/2025/12/09/251209a.html)

Major media documented the announcement on the loggia of St Peter’s Basilica and the presentation to the faithful.
