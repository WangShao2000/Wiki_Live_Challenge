# Natlan

Natlan is a major fictional region in the action role‑playing game Genshin Impact, developed and published by HoYoverse (miHoYo). Introduced as the game’s sixth explorable nation, Natlan is associated with the Pyro element and is thematically framed as the “Nation of War.” It opened to players with Version 5.0 “Flowers Resplendent on the Sun‑Scorched Sojourn” on August 28, 2024, adding new traversal systems, characters, quests, and a distinctive cultural and visual identity within the world of Teyvat [1][2][3]. Subsequent updates through late 2024 and 2025 expanded Natlan’s map, mechanics, and story, culminating in the Pyro Archon (Mavuika) becoming playable and the Traveler gaining Pyro resonance during the Chapter V Archon Quest arc [9]. The region’s design emphasizes co‑existence with evolved “Saurians,” large traversal‑focused creatures that players can temporarily inhabit, and introduces region‑specific energy systems (Phlogiston, Nightsoul’s Blessing) that shape exploration and combat [2][12].

Natlan’s reveal and launch drew extensive media coverage, with reception praising its traversal innovations and high‑energy aesthetic, and criticism focusing on cultural representation in character design. Several outlets documented a pre‑release backlash led by fans and voice actors who argued HoYoverse’s Natlan character roster insufficiently reflected darker skin tones and specific cultural inspirations, continuing an ongoing debate since Genshin’s Sumeru era [13][14][15][16]. HoYoverse has not issued a formal public response in the cited coverage.

## Terminology and primary referent

In reliable sources surveyed as of December 12, 2025, “Natlan” most prominently refers to the Pyro nation/region in Genshin Impact. Official materials and industry coverage consistently use “Natlan” to denote this in‑game territory, its storyline, and associated systems and characters [1][2][3][11]. Searches did not reveal other notable, unrelated referents (such as companies, places, or brands) covered by major outlets; merchandising and trailers branded “Natlan” are directly tied to Genshin Impact [5][18].

## Creation, development, and release timeline

- Pre‑launch reveal and trailers:
  - Natlan preview teasers and the animated impressions short “Blaze to Natlan” were released ahead of Version 5.0. Official channels hosted multiple videos, including the “Blaze to Natlan” animated trailer and a longer preview (“To the Light of Rainbow Flames”), as well as a gameplay trailer showcased at Gamescom 2024 on August 21, 2024 [5][6][4].
- Launch:
  - Version 5.0 “Flowers Resplendent on the Sun‑Scorched Sojourn” launched on August 28, 2024, opening Natlan, introducing new mechanics (Saurian indwelling, Phlogiston/Nightsoul systems), and debuting new playable characters. HoYoverse announced the update via official patch notes, a PlayStation Blog developer post, and a press release [1][2][3].
- Continued expansion:
  - Version 5.2 “Tapestry of Spirit and Flame,” released November 20, 2024, expanded aerial exploration with new Saurians (Qucusaurs, Iktomisaurs) and added tribes (Flower‑Feather Clan, Masters of the Night‑Wind), associated events, and characters (Chasca, Ororon) [8].
  - Version 5.3 “Incandescent Ode of Resurrection,” posted January 1, 2025, continued Natlan’s climax. It made the Pyro Traveler available and introduced Mavuika and Citlali as playable characters, new bosses, and system and quality‑of‑life updates [9].
  - Version 5.5 “Day of the Flame’s Return,” announced for March 26, 2025, expanded Natlan’s southwestern region around the Great Volcano of Tollan, added the Tatankasaurus Saurian companion, and new events and characters (Varesa, Iansan) [10].
- Platform availability and cross‑play:
  - Natlan content is available across PlayStation, PC, iOS, and Android; Genshin Impact added Xbox Series X|S support in November 2024 while the Natlan patch cycle was ongoing [3].

## Setting, geography, and design overview

- Location and terrain:
  - Natlan lies west of Sumeru’s Desert of Hadramaveth and features rugged canyons, cliffs, rivers, hot springs, and volcanic landscapes [2]. The region introduces unique traversal contexts (underground, aerial, aquatic) through Saurian abilities and terrain features tied to Phlogiston energy [2][12].
- Society and structure:
  - Natlan is organized around six tribes with distinct cultures and relationships to local Saurians. Version 5.0 opened content for three tribes—Children of Echoes, Scions of the Canopy, People of the Springs—with additional tribes introduced in subsequent patches [2][8][11].
- Stadium of the Sacred Flame:
  - A central narrative venue where tribes select Champions and rivalry plays out via tournaments, providing a focal point for the Chapter V Archon storyline and tribal quest arcs termed “Tribal Chronicles” [2][1][11].
- Energetic and symbolic systems:
  - The Pyro element manifests as “Phlogiston” in Natlan, a region‑specific resource powering traversal devices and combat functions, and enabling Phlogiston Engravings that confer “Nightsoul’s Blessing,” a temporary state granting enhanced abilities [2]. These systems are integral to exploration challenges and some enemy mechanics [1][9].

## Gameplay mechanics associated with Natlan

- Saurian indwelling:
  - Instead of mounts, Natlan lets players “indwell” Saurians—temporarily transforming into specific species to gain movement and interaction skills. Phlogiston management governs the use of many Saurian abilities; some functions are available only to certain Saurians [12].
  - Examples and uses (non‑exhaustive):
    - Tepetlisaurus: burrowing underground, cliff climbing, traversal via Molten Fissures [12].
    - Yumkasaurus: grappling propulsion via Coilgrass Sigils, harvesting and firing explosive flora [12].
    - Koholasaurus: high‑speed swimming, aquatic leaping, access to Spiritdoors and Spiritways [12].
    - Qucusaur and Iktomisaur (added in 5.2): extended gliding, mid‑air mobility, hidden object detection, and crafting via graffiti/runic interactions [8].
- Character traversal kits:
  - Natlan characters integrate movement options aligned with tribal motifs—e.g., surfboarding (Mualani), grappling‑hook aerial movement (Kinich), terrain drilling/climbing (Kachina)—and party utility tied to regional specialties (e.g., restoring Stamina or Phlogiston) [2][1][11].
- Systems and progression:
  - Reputation returns via Tribal Chronicles (three‑act narratives per tribe) and Obsidian Totem Pole boards. A new leveling track (“Tona’s Flame”) with Pyro Sigils offers rewards. Phlogiston/Nightsoul mechanics influence device activation, combat, and exploration pacing [1].

## Major updates, content, and characters

- Version 5.0 (August 28, 2024):
  - New areas: Tequemecan Valley, Coatepec Mountain, Toyac Springs, Basin of Unnumbered Flames [1].
  - New characters and Event Wishes:
    - Mualani (5★ Hydro, catalyst), Kinich (5★ Dendro, claymore), Kachina (4★ Geo, polearm; obtainable for free after Archon V Act I), with banners scheduled across two phases [1][2][11].
  - New exploration and systems: Saurian Indwelling, Phlogiston Engravings/Nightsoul, tribal Reputation and Chronicles, artifact systems updates, new domains, and wildlife [1].
- Version 5.2 (November 20, 2024):
  - Tribes added: Flower‑Feather Clan and Masters of the Night‑Wind; new aerial Saurians (Qucusaur, Iktomisaur); new bosses/events; playable debuts of Chasca (5★ Anemo bow) and Ororon (4★ Electro bow) [8].
- Version 5.3 (January 1, 2025):
  - Story climax and gameplay:
    - Pyro resonance granted to Traveler; Mavuika (5★ Pyro, claymore) and Citlali (5★ Cryo, catalyst) playable; new bosses (“Lord of Eroded Primal Fire,” “Wayward Hermetic Spiritspeaker”). Permanent systems (e.g., Repertoire of Myriad Melodies) and extensive quality‑of‑life updates [9].
- Version 5.5 (March 26, 2025):
  - Regional expansion around the Great Volcano of Tollan; new Saurian companion Tatankasaurus; events and characters (Varesa, Iansan) [10].

## Media and promotion

HoYoverse promoted Natlan through animated shorts, teasers, and gameplay trailers on official channels and at events:

- Animated impressions trailer “Blaze to Natlan” and the preview teaser “To the Light of Rainbow Flames” were released on YouTube and distributed via official news posts [5][6].
- A Natlan gameplay trailer premiered at Gamescom 2024 (August 21), reinforcing traversal and combat themes and cross‑platform availability messaging [4].

## Reception and impact

- Gameplay and traversal:
  - Review coverage praised Natlan’s traversal innovations and mobile execution. GamesRadar+ called the latest patch “the best and most approachable one yet” in the context of continued improvements to storytelling and world design, while acknowledging ongoing gacha monetization concerns [17].
  - Hands‑on previews documented faster, more fluid combat pacing and confirmed with developers that Natlan’s traversal abilities function outside the region with reduced uptime (due to Phlogiston’s regional tie), and that F2P accessibility is maintained via indwelling Saurians—though character kits can be more efficient in some contexts [19].
- Community and platform reach:
  - Natlan’s cycle coincided with Genshin’s continued platform expansion and anniversary rewards across platforms with cross‑progression [3]. The breadth of official trailers, event showcases, and partner amplification reflects sustained marketing emphasis on region launches [18][4].

## Cultural representations, controversies, and criticism

- Pre‑release backlash:
  - Multiple outlets reported widespread criticism alleging “whitewashing” and insufficient representation in Natlan character designs. Commentary noted perceived inspirations linked to African, Latin American, and Pacific cultures/deities, alongside predominantly light‑skinned depictions in trailers. Prominent English voice actors (including Anne Yatco, Valeria Rodríguez, Khoi Dao, Alejandro Saab, and Zeno Robinson) publicly urged HoYoverse to improve representation and research fidelity [13][14][15][16].
  - Coverage referenced a Change.org petition calling for better representation and an end to cultural appropriation and whitewashing; the petition surpassed 100,000 signatures in 2024–2025 cycles [21][13][14][15].
- Context and developer response:
  - The cited reporting did not include an official public statement by HoYoverse responding to the specific Natlan character design criticisms at the time of publication [14][15][16]. Outlets placed the Natlan discourse within a longer pattern of debates around Sumeru’s representation in 2022 and continuing into 2025.
- Attribution and perspectives:
  - Articles from Polygon, Eurogamer, IGN, and Kotaku presented the criticisms as significant community and industry discourse, grounded in comparisons to real‑world cultural figures and broader representation practices in games. The reporting described a range of viewpoints—from calls for redesigns and consultancy to defenses of artistic reinterpretation—without asserting factual conclusions beyond attributed opinions and petition metrics [13][14][15][16][21].

## Name origin and etymology

HoYoverse’s official communications do not provide an etymology for “Natlan.” Developer posts and patch notes describe the region’s design goals, mechanics, and tribal structure without formal discussion of the name’s linguistic origin [2][1][8][9]. Media coverage frequently situates Natlan’s visual and cultural influences within broad frames (e.g., Africa, the Pacific Ring of Fire, Latin America) rather than formal etymological claims [14].

## Availability and access requirements

- Access to Natlan is streamlined for newer players:
  - Players who complete the Mondstadt Archon Quest Prologue: Act III unlock an entrance Teleport Waypoint to Natlan; starting Natlan’s Archon Quests requires Adventure Rank 28+ and completion of Liyue’s Chapter I: Act III [2][3].
- Cross‑play and progression:
  - Natlan content supports cross‑progression and co‑op across PlayStation, PC, iOS, and Android; the game added Xbox Series X|S in November 2024 [3].

## Notability

Natlan is notable for:

- Introducing a large, mechanically distinct region that expands traversal and environmental interaction via Saurian indwelling, Phlogiston/Nightsoul systems, and tribal Reputation narratives [1][2][12].
- Serving as the narrative focus of Archon Quest Chapter V, resolving major Pyro‑aligned storylines, elevating the Pyro Archon Mavuika, and granting Pyro resonance to the Traveler [9].
- Prompting widely covered industry and community discussions on cultural representation, with multiple major outlets documenting voice actor engagement and petitions [13][14][15][16][21].
