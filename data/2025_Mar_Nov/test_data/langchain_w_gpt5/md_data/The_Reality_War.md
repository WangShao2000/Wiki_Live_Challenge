# The Reality War

The Reality War is the eighth and final episode of Doctor Who’s 2025 series (marketed as Season 2 on BBC iPlayer and Disney+), written by showrunner Russell T Davies and directed by Alex Sanjiv Pillai. It was broadcast in the United Kingdom on BBC One and BBC iPlayer on 31 May 2025 and launched simultaneously on Disney+ in territories outside the UK. The episode runs approximately 66 minutes and was also screened as a special event in cinemas across the UK and Ireland. It concludes Ncuti Gatwa’s tenure as the Fifteenth Doctor with a regeneration sequence that features the appearance of Billie Piper, whose role the BBC stated would be clarified in due course. The finale drew substantial media attention and mixed critical responses, with reviewers praising performances and spectacle while questioning plotting and continuity choices. [1][2][3][4]

## Background and production

The Reality War was written by Russell T Davies and directed by Alex Sanjiv Pillai, with Chris May as producer and executive producers including Davies, Julie Gardner, Jane Tranter, Joel Collins and Phil Collinson. The BBC billed the finale as an extra‑length episode concluding the season’s narrative threads. In pre‑broadcast publicity, Davies described a story of large stakes spanning returning foes and existential threats, promising “shocks, scares and revelations.” [1][2]

## Broadcast and release

- United Kingdom: The episode premiered on BBC One and BBC iPlayer on 31 May 2025. The BBC promoted the finale as part of a global, same‑day launch and hosted special cinema screenings across the UK and Ireland. [1][2][12]

- International: Outside the UK, the episode launched simultaneously on Disney+ where available; Disney’s press site issued official episode imagery and synopsis copy. [11]

- Runtime and accessibility: The BBC Programmes page lists a running time of 1 hour and 6 minutes, with signed and audio described versions made available. [2]

## Synopsis

The BBC’s official synopsis describes a large‑scale conflict and a moral dilemma centered on protecting a single life: “Battle rages across the skies as the Unholy Trinity unleash their deadly ambition. The Doctor, Belinda and Ruby have to risk everything in their quest to save one innocent life.” [2][11]

## Marketing and promotion

Ahead of broadcast, the BBC announced a global simulcast on 31 May 2025 across BBC One, BBC iPlayer, and Disney+ (where available), with the finale additionally screening in UK and Irish cinemas. Official social media posts amplified the simulcast and event screening messaging. [1][12]

## Reception

Critical reception was mixed, with significant attention paid to the finale’s regeneration and to its narrative scope.

- The Telegraph described the episode as an “overwrought mess” that culminated in a head‑turning twist, framing the regeneration into Billie Piper as a bold but contentious choice. [5]

- IGN called the finale “overstuffed” and “underwritten,” arguing that it “fumbles nearly every major beat,” while acknowledging standout moments and appearances. [6]

- Den of Geek found the season “extremely promising” but concluded that the finale “ends in a strange, unsettling place,” noting ambitious ideas alongside uneven execution. [7]

- The Guardian’s recap encouraged viewers to “enjoy the ride,” observing rapid movement from scene to scene and highlighting the episode’s busy, maximal approach. [8]

BBC News covered the broadcast as a major television event, reporting Gatwa’s farewell statements and underscoring the uncertainty about Piper’s ongoing role, which the BBC said “remains to be seen.” [4]

## Viewership

Radio Times reported overnight UK ratings of approximately 2.2 million viewers, noting that The Reality War was the second‑best performing episode of the season based on overnight figures, with consolidated ratings to follow. Cinema screenings were not included in overnight television ratings. [10]

## Legacy and impact

The Reality War marked the conclusion of Ncuti Gatwa’s era as the Fifteenth Doctor. In post‑broadcast press material, Gatwa reflected on handing over “the keys to that beloved blue box,” while Russell T Davies praised Gatwa’s tenure and teased future developments. Billie Piper’s appearance drew substantial coverage; the BBC indicated that details about “who, how, why and when” would be clarified subsequently. [3][4][9]

## Availability

The episode is available to stream in the UK on BBC iPlayer and broadcast via BBC One, with international availability through Disney+. [1][2][11]
