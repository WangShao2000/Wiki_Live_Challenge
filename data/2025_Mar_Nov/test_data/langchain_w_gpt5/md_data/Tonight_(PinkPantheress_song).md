# Tonight (PinkPantheress song)

Tonight is a single by English singer-songwriter PinkPantheress, released on April 4, 2025, through Warner Records UK/Parlophone as the lead single from her 2025 mixtape Fancy That. The dance-pop track runs approximately 2:54 and incorporates an orchestral sample from Panic! at the Disco’s 2008 song Do You Know What I’m Seeing?, resulting in additional songwriting credits for members of the band’s classic lineup. A Bridgerton-inspired music video directed by Charlotte Rutherford was issued the same day. The song entered the UK’s Official Singles Chart Top 100 and spent six weeks on the chart; in the United States it debuted at number 5 on Billboard’s Hot Dance/Pop Songs chart, and it also reached number 12 on Billboard Japan’s Hot Overseas chart. Critics highlighted its hook-filled structure and playful directness, with the track included in Billboard’s “Best Songs of 2025 So Far” list and spotlighted by outlets including Consequence and NME. A set of dance-oriented remixes—featuring JADE, Basement Jaxx, and Joe Goddard—arrived in October 2025 as part of PinkPantheress’s remix project Fancy Some More? [1][2][3][5][6][7][8][9][10][11][12][19][20][21][22]

## Background and release

Following the release of her debut album Heaven Knows (2023), PinkPantheress began previewing new club-focused material in early 2025. On April 4, 2025, she released Tonight as the first single from her second mixtape Fancy That, accompanied by a period-drama-inspired video directed by fashion photographer and director Charlotte Rutherford. Coverage from NME and Rolling Stone emphasized the video’s Regency aesthetic and noted that Tonight marked the beginning of her 2025 release run leading up to Fancy That’s May rollout. In posts highlighted by NME, she teased the video as “Bridgerton mode.” [6][7]

The release was supported across major streaming platforms globally on April 4, 2025. Platform listings identify it as a standalone digital single, later included on Fancy That. Qobuz credits frame the project as “A Warner Records UK Release” with PinkPantheress’s recordings licensed to Parlophone. [1][2][3][6][7][24]

## Composition and lyrics

Tonight is a synth-led dance-pop track that blends UK club lineage with catchy, compact hooks. It features a thumping four-on-the-floor pulse and rapid, zippy synths that critics associated with contemporary house, bassline, and broader dance-pop idioms. The song opens with baroque-flavored string figures sampled from Panic! at the Disco’s Do You Know What I’m Seeing? (2008), which lends a wistful, cinematic sheen to the intro before the beat drops. The sample’s use is reflected in the song’s credits, which include Brendon Urie, Ryan Ross, Spencer Smith, and Jon Walker. [3][7][8][9]

Lyrically, the track is unusually direct and flirty for PinkPantheress, with the chorus pivoting on a blunt proposition (“You want sex with me? … Come talk to me, come on,” as quoted in press coverage), framing the encounter as breathless dancefloor flirtation. Consequence described how the Panic! strings “fold into a lush, club-ready beat” under her featherlight delivery, while Pitchfork characterized the track’s candor as turning “sexual candor into sweetness” amid its brisk rhythmic drive. [7][8][9]

## Music video

The official music video for Tonight premiered on April 4, 2025. Directed by Charlotte Rutherford, it presents a playful Regency/Bridgerton-style house party reimagined through a contemporary pop lens—corsets, pearls, powdered wigs, and lavish manor interiors cut against a modern, DJ-led bash. Rolling Stone and NME noted the Bridgerton inspiration explicitly. The video’s YouTube description lists Rutherford as director and Chris Murdoch as producer. [5][6][7]

## Promotion and live performances

- Digital/visuals: The single arrived with the official video and a lyric video on YouTube on April 4, 2025. [5][14]
- Social media and dance trend: A compact dance challenge to the chorus circulated on TikTok and Instagram in the days following release. Billboard reported that PinkPantheress and rapper Doechii posted a branded clip of the dance on April 10, 2025, tagging H&M after an appearance at the retailer’s Los Angeles event. [19]
- Radio/TV: PinkPantheress performed Tonight in BBC Radio 1’s Live Lounge as part of her October 2025 session. She also made her U.S. late-night TV debut on July 30, 2025, performing a medley of Illegal, Girl Like Me, and Tonight on The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon; both Clash and Rolling Stone covered the performance and staging. [15][16][18]

## Critical reception

Coverage of Tonight was broadly favorable, with praise focused on its melody-forward construction, playful lyricism, and sleek club textures.

- Consequence selected it in Staff Picks for Best Songs of the Week upon release, noting the way it folds Panic! at the Disco’s baroque strings into a “lush, club-ready beat,” with her “featherlight vocals” maintaining ease over the bounce. [8]
- Rolling Stone spotlighted the single and video on release day, commenting on the period-drama concept and quoting the song’s candid chorus lines. [7]
- In its Fancy That review, Pitchfork highlighted Tonight’s direct hooks and described how the track turns “sexual candor into sweetness” under a fast, pulsing beat. [9]
- Billboard included Tonight on its “Best Songs of 2025 So Far” list, calling it a “high-stakes dancefloor confessional” driven by thumping bass and frenetic synths, and also featured it in a staff list of the best U.K. & Ireland songs of the year’s first half. [11][12]
- NME’s news coverage on release underlined the video’s Bridgerton conceit and the song’s role inaugurating the Fancy That era. A niche review in Stanisland described the track as light, 1990s-inflected dance-pop with comedic simplicity—useful for representing a minority critical view. [6][8]

## Commercial performance

- United Kingdom: Tonight entered the UK’s Official Singles Chart Top 100 and spent six consecutive weeks on the chart, covering the period from the week ending April 17, 2025, to May 22, 2025. The Official Charts Company also described it as a Top 40 single in a New Music Friday roundup in May 2025. In October 2025, the track appeared at number 19 on the Official Trending Chart alongside renewed interest in PinkPantheress’s catalog. [11][22][23]
- United States: The track debuted at number 5 on Billboard’s Hot Dance/Pop Songs chart dated April 19, 2025, aided by approximately 3.6 million U.S. streams in its first tracking week, according to Billboard’s Chart Beat column. [10]
- Japan: On Billboard Japan’s Hot Overseas chart (dated April 28, 2025), Tonight entered at number 12. Billboard Japan also issued a news item announcing the single’s release to the market. [12][13]
