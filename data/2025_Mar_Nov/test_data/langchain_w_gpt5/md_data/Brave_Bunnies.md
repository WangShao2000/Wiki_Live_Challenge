# Brave Bunnies

Brave Bunnies is a 2D-animated preschool television series created by Olga Cherepanova and originated at Ukrainian studio Glowberry. The show centers on bunny siblings Boo and Bop who travel with their family across a diverse “Bunny World,” meeting new animal friends and inventing games to communicate despite differences. Season one comprises 52 seven-minute episodes and premiered on the Ukrainian channel PLUSPLUS on December 5, 2020; the series later aired internationally in territories including the United Kingdom (Channel 5’s Milkshake! and Nick Jr), Germany (Super RTL), Australia (ABC Kids and Stan), Finland (Yle), Spain (Clan), Israel (HOP!), and South Korea (Daekyo/CNC Media International). Season two, also 52 seven-minute episodes, was co-produced by WildBrain (Vancouver) with Ánima and released in 2024 following a 2022 partnership that positioned WildBrain as majority stakeholder and global distributor for the franchise (with Glowberry retaining rights in Ukraine and Ánima in Spain). Brave Bunnies has been highlighted in industry markets, including ranking among the most-viewed programs in the MIPJunior Screenings Library, and has supported consumer products such as Spin Master’s preschool toy line and books published by Ladybird in the UK and DeA Planeta Libri in Italy. The series emphasizes social skills, empathy, curiosity, and respect for diversity, with scripts developed alongside child psychologists. [1][2][4][6][7][8][9][10][12][14][15][16][17][18][21][22]

## Premise and format

Brave Bunnies is designed for preschool audiences aged approximately two to five. Each seven-minute episode follows siblings Boo and Bop on an ongoing family road trip in their Bunny Bus with Ma, Pa, and four younger bunny babies. In each story, they meet animals with different habitats, customs, and ways of playing, and they improvise new games to connect, communicate, and build friendships. The brand’s stated values include curiosity, creativity, empathy, openness, responsibility, and respect for diversity. [4][2]

## Creation and development

The series was conceived and created by Ukrainian writer-producer Olga Cherepanova at Glowberry. Cherepanova has described the aim as encouraging children to be curious about the world around them and to celebrate its diversity. [1][4]

Key creative personnel on the first season included:
- Series director: Tim Fehrenbach (UK). [12]
- Head writer/executive story editor: John van Bruggen (Canada). [2]
- Art director: Anna Sarvira (Ukraine), who developed the visual style and served as lead art director from 2019 to 2023. [22]

Voice recording for the English-language version was handled by Fitzrovia Post in London, working with Glowberry on casting; many roles were performed by child actors. [12]

## Production and animation

Brave Bunnies is produced in 2D animation. Season one originated at Glowberry with co-production by Ánima, the Ibero-American animation studio with operations in Mexico and Spain. International distribution for the first season was led by Aardman Animations. [2][21][7]

In 2022, WildBrain entered a comprehensive partnership with Glowberry and Ánima to continue the franchise after production was disrupted by the Russian invasion of Ukraine. Under this deal, WildBrain became the majority stakeholder in Brave Bunnies, with Glowberry and Ánima as minority stakeholders. WildBrain’s Vancouver studio co-produced season two with Ánima; WildBrain assumed global distribution for the franchise, with exceptions for Glowberry in Ukraine and Ánima in Spain, and its licensing arm WildBrain CPLG took on global consumer products management. [1]

## Seasons and episodes

- Season 1: 52 episodes x 7 minutes (104 total minutes), produced 2019–2020, premiered in Ukraine in December 2020, and rolled out internationally through 2021–2022. [2][6][7][8][10]
- Season 2: 52 episodes x 7 minutes, co-produced by WildBrain and Ánima, released in 2024. [1][22]

Across the two seasons, the series totals 104 episodes. [14]

## Broadcast and streaming history

Initial premiere:
- Ukraine: World premiere on PLUSPLUS on December 5, 2020. [6]

United Kingdom:
- Channel 5 (Milkshake!): UK premiere on February 6, 2021 (with weekend launch times announced by the production and post team). [10][12]
- Nick Jr: Launched on September 6, 2021. [13]

Europe:
- Germany: Super RTL (Toggolino) TV debut on October 15, 2020, following earlier availability on the Toggolino App and TV NOW. [8]
- Spain: Clan TV premiered the series on August 30, 2020. [8]
- Finland: Yle (Pikku Kakkonen and Yle Areena). [11]
- Norway: NRK Super/NRK TV digital availability beginning August 2020, with TV rollout in October 2020. [8]
- Sweden: SVT Play VOD start on September 20, 2020; TV launch on SVT Barnkanalen on October 24, 2020. [8]
- Italy: Rai Yoyo acquired the series, with Season 1 and 2 distribution confirmed by WildBrain. [14]
- Poland: TeleTOON+ (Canal+ Poland) acquired the series. [14]

Middle East and Asia-Pacific:
- Israel: HOP! TV carried the series by early 2021. [10]
- South Korea: Carriage via Daekyo (season one) and CNC Media International (subsequent distribution). [2][14]
- Southeast Asia, Taiwan, Hong Kong: Cartoonito on Cartoon Network and streaming on HBO GO under a WarnerMedia agreement covering 20 territories; ANZ included in the rollout. [9]
- Australia: ABC Kids carried season one; Stan acquired season one in 2023. [10][14]

North America:
- Canada: Family Jr (WildBrain Television) acquired season one for exclusive linear broadcast. [1]
- United States: Season one became available on WildBrain+ SVOD through Comcast. [14]

Latin America:
- The series was acquired for HBO Max in Latin America with rollout announced for mid-2022. [10]

## International distribution and licensing

Distribution and broadcast sales:
- Season one international sales were arranged by Aardman Animations. [2][7]
- From 2022, WildBrain handles global distribution for the franchise, with Glowberry retaining rights in Ukraine and Ánima retaining rights in Spain. [1]
- A 2023 wave of deals by WildBrain included acquisitions by TeleTOON+ (Poland), Channel 5’s Milkshake! (UK), Rai Yoyo (Italy), CNC Media International (South Korea), and Stan (Australia), as well as availability on the WildBrain+ SVOD channel via Comcast in the U.S. [14]

Licensing and brand management:
- Prior to 2022, Big Picture Licensing served as the UK/Eire agent and master agent across EMEA and APAC; partners included La Panadería Licensing & Marketing (Latin America), CPLG (select European territories prior to WildBrain CPLG’s management), CLM (Africa), Centa IP (Australia/New Zealand), and Mediogen (Israel). [9]
- In Italy, KidsMe manages local brand rights, including publishing agreements. [19]
- From 2022, WildBrain CPLG manages global consumer products for the brand (outside Ukraine), aligning licensing with new content distribution. [1]

## Language versions and dubs

Brave Bunnies has been localized for broadcasts across multiple territories:
- English-language versions aired in the UK (Milkshake!, Nick Jr), with voice recording and casting by Fitzrovia Post. [12]
- Finnish-language dub aired on Yle under the title “Urheat pupuset.” [11]
- Localized versions have been broadcast in territories whose primary networks include Super RTL (Germany), Rai Yoyo (Italy), Clan (Spain), HOP! (Israel), and Daekyo/CNC Media International (South Korea). [2][8][14]

## Reception

Industry recognition:
- Brave Bunnies drew high interest at MIPJunior, ranking among the most-viewed projects in the Screenings Library, with Aardman and Total Licensing reporting strong screening figures in late 2020. [7][24]

Audience and market performance:
- In the UK, the series reached a combined audience of approximately 4.4 million viewers according to Channel 5/Nick Jr broadcasters, reported contemporaneously alongside the launch of Ladybird’s tie-in books. [15]

Trade press commentary on programming values:
- Distribution and licensing announcements have highlighted the show’s focus on curiosity, creativity, acceptance, and openness as core values for preschool audiences. [14]

## Awards and festival selections

Brave Bunnies received visibility and presentations at major industry markets and forums. It was singled out for high viewership in the MIPJunior Screenings Library in 2020 and was promoted at MIPCOM that year. Publicly available sources emphasize market recognition rather than competitive festival awards, and detailed award listings beyond MIPJunior figures have not been independently documented in the cited sources. [7][24]

## Educational themes and approach

Producers have described Brave Bunnies as blending social skill-building with comedy, music, and age-appropriate visuals, aiming to model empathy, respect for diversity, and inclusive play. Glowberry notes the brand’s emphasis on curiosity, creativity, openness, responsibility, and respect for diversity. Scripts were developed with participation from child psychologists. The APAC broadcast through Cartoonito framed the series within Cartoonito’s “humancentric learning” pillars (creativity, caring, curiosity, and courage). [4][10][9]

## Consumer products and tie-in media

Toys:
- Spin Master is the global master toy partner, with an initial preschool range announced in November 2021 and retail rollout beginning April 2022. The first wave included figures, playsets, plush, games, and puzzles, with initial markets including the UK, Australia, Italy, Germany, and Spain, followed by additional territories later in 2022. [16][17]

Publishing:
- UK: Ladybird (Penguin Random House Children’s) launched a Brave Bunnies preschool book line, initially releasing titles including Spring to the Rescue and Make a Friend, timed with UK broadcast milestones. [15]
- Italy: DeA Planeta Libri launched a multi-title line beginning in October 2021, supported by partnerships through KidsMe and De Agostini. [18][21]
- Australia/New Zealand: Five Mile Press (Regency Media’s publishing division) was appointed to publish activity, sticker, coloring, novelty, flap, and educational titles for the brand. [20]

Licensing management:
- WildBrain CPLG manages global licensing and merchandising (outside Ukraine) under the 2022 WildBrain partnership. Prior to that, Big Picture Licensing coordinated multi-territory agents across EMEA, APAC, and Latin America. [1][9]

## Notable developments

In 2022, WildBrain, Glowberry, and Ánima announced a partnership to continue Brave Bunnies after the war in Ukraine disrupted production. WildBrain became the majority stakeholder in the IP, took on global distribution and licensing management, and began co-production of season two in Vancouver with Ánima’s studio network. Public statements by WildBrain and Glowberry framed the partnership as ensuring the brand’s future and supporting Ukrainian creatives in continuing the series. Season two’s production concluded with a 2024 release. [1][3][22]
