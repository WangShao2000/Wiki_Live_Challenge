# Calum Lill

Calum Lill is a British actor known for television and stage roles, notably portraying solicitor Joel Deering in the ITV soap opera Coronation Street between 2023 and 2024, with a brief return reported in 2025. His earlier screen work includes a guest role on BBC One’s Doctors and a recurring appearance in Channel 4’s Hollyoaks as PC Carlton Smith in 2021. Lill trained at Arts Educational Schools London (ArtsEd), earning a BA (Hons) in Acting in 2018, and has appeared on stage in Tim Whitnall’s The Sociable Plover at London’s Old Red Lion Theatre (May 2025) and as the pantomime villain Fleshcreep in Jack and the Beanstalk at Bournemouth Pavilion (December 2024–January 2025) and the Oak Theatre at Scarisbrick Hall (December 2025). His Coronation Street storyline—centred on Joel’s criminal behaviour and a subsequent whodunnit—attracted substantial coverage; he was among Coronation Street’s nominations highlighted for the Inside Soap Awards 2024, and his longlisting for British Soap Awards Villain of the Year was reported by regional media. Beyond acting, Lill’s public appearances have included participating with castmates in the AJ Bell Great Manchester Run. [2] [3] [6] [7] [8] [9] [10] [11] [12] [16]

## Early life and education

Publicly available biographical details are limited, but professional profiles and interviews indicate that Lill trained at Arts Educational Schools London, graduating with a BA (Hons) in Acting in 2018. ## Career

### Television

Lill’s television credits include:

- Doctors (BBC One): He appeared in Series 20 episode “Division,” playing Chez Wilson-Needham. [1]

- Holby City (BBC One): Radio Times listed Holby City among Lill’s prior credits; details of the specific episode are not widely published in mainstream sources accessible here. [19]

- Hollyoaks (Channel 4): Lill played PC Carlton Smith in 2021 in a recurring capacity. Contemporary reporting in reputable entertainment outlets noted the character’s involvement in a storyline connected to the death of George Kiss. [2] [18]

- Coronation Street (ITV): Lill joined the cast in 2023 as duty solicitor Joel Deering, initially introduced in the Lauren Bolton storyline before becoming romantically involved with Dee-Dee Bailey. [2] [19] The character’s arc developed into one of the soap’s major plots, revealing Joel as a manipulative predator who groomed young women and targeted Lauren. [3] His exit formed part of a whodunnit narrative in which Joel’s death was followed by a live ITVX reaction show; the killer was revealed in a flashback episode as Lauren Bolton, with Lill discussing the twist and its “soap justice.” [4] [5] Subsequent reporting indicated Lill would reprise Joel for a short stint in 2025, though ITV did not issue an official comment at the time. [21]

### Stage

- The Sociable Plover (Old Red Lion Theatre, London): Lill starred opposite Jack Robertson in Medium Rare Productions’ twentieth-anniversary revival of Tim Whitnall’s dark comedic thriller, directed by Christa Harris. The run played from 13 to 24 May 2025. [7] Independent coverage described the production’s tone and structure, with the cast credited and the work’s tension highlighted. [22]

- Jack and the Beanstalk (Bournemouth Pavilion Theatre): Lill performed as the villain Fleshcreep in the 2024–2025 season, running from 7 December 2024 to 5 January 2025. In venue publicity, he discussed returning to live theatre after a year of screen work and the significance of pantomime for young audiences. [8]

- Jack and the Beanstalk (Oak Theatre, Scarisbrick Hall): Lill reprised Fleshcreep at the newly opened Oak Theatre in December 2025, in a production by Anton Benson Productions. Regional and trade coverage confirmed the casting and dates. [9] [10]

### Public appearances and media

Lill has been active in charity and public events alongside Coronation Street colleagues. BBC News reported that he was among participants at the start of the Greater Manchester half-marathon in 2024, a fundraising event that raised about £1.5 million for national and local charities. [12]

## Reception

Lill’s portrayal of Joel Deering drew significant attention as the storyline escalated. In interview coverage, he reflected on the narrative’s intensity and his effort to keep the performance grounded, while official Coronation Street content and media recaps framed the plot’s progression toward a whodunnit climax with a live reaction show. [3] [4] [5] On stage, The Sociable Plover’s revival was noted by theatre blogs for its pacing and atmosphere, with Lill credited among the leads. [7] [22]

## Awards and honours

- Inside Soap Awards 2024: Coronation Street listed “Calum Lill – Joel Deering” among its nominations highlighted for the year’s awards. [11]

- British Soap Awards (Villain of the Year): Regional press reported Coronation Street’s social media spotlight on Lill’s longlisting for Villain of the Year, with Lill acknowledging the response from viewers. [6]

## Personal life

Lill announced his engagement to his partner, Roberta, in June 2024, with entertainment press reporting the news and sharing his social media post about the proposal. [14] In late 2025 he posted a holiday-themed announcement that the couple are expecting their first child, adding “New character dropping April ’26”; the pregnancy news was covered by multiple outlets. [13] [15] Lill’s family has occasionally featured in media coverage—for example, a widely viewed video of his grandmother’s reaction to a Coronation Street twist—but detailed private information has not been broadly published by reliable sources.
