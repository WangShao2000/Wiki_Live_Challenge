# Multo (song)

Multo is a 2024 single by the Filipino band Cup of Joe, released by Viva Records and sung in Filipino. Announced as a surprise on September 13, 2024 and commercially issued digitally on September 14, 2024, the track later appeared on the band’s debut album Silakbo in January 2025. Media coverage in the Philippines characterized the song as a melancholic synth‑pop/pop‑rock ballad about being “haunted” by lingering emotions after loss or a breakup. In April 2025, Billboard Philippines reported that Multo made chart history by debuting on the Billboard Global 200, with GMA News likewise reporting the global entry; the song also reached No. 1 in the Philippines on Billboard Philippines’ Hot 100 and finished 2025 as the top song on both Spotify Philippines and Apple Music Philippines. At the inaugural Filipino Music Awards (FMA) in October 2025, Multo won Song of the Year. The title “Multo” (“ghost” in Filipino) has been used by other Philippine artists for separate songs; among these releases, Cup of Joe’s Multo has received the widest independent coverage and national recognition. ## Composition and lyrics

Press coverage described Multo as a melancholic single blending synth‑pop and pop‑rock elements, marked by a “lingering” hook and more “ambitious and experimental” sonics for the band compared with their previous releases. Billboard Philippines characterized the track as one that “hits you straight in the feels,” examining “the ghosts of our past” and the clarity in the ruins of failed relationships. Rolling Stone Philippines framed Multo as the song that reshaped Cup of Joe’s image “from upbeat pop performers to serious storytellers,” noting its slow-burn rise as audiences connected with its themes on social media. The lyrics, entirely in Filipino, use ghostly imagery (“Minumulto na ’ko ng damdamin ko,” “Dinadalaw mo ’ko bawat gabi”) to convey lingering memories and emotional haunting. [19] [17] [7]

## Background and release

Billboard Philippines reported that Cup of Joe announced Multo as a surprise single on September 13, 2024, just three hours before release, positioning it as the next entry after the band’s 2024 run of Misteryoso and Lahat Ng Bukas. The digital single went live on major platforms on September 14, 2024, and the track was later included on the January 2025 debut album Silakbo. Rolling Stone Philippines’ feature situated Multo within the band’s broader shift toward introspective storytelling and chronicled the song’s growth over months—citing audience engagement, a widely viewed stripped-down live performance, and a broader pattern of long-tail hits on Philippine charts. [19] [31] [13] [12] [17]

## Music video

- Release and director: The official music video for Multo was released in March 2025 and directed by Kris Cazin. [13]
- Cast: Elijah Canlas and Miles Ocampo star in the video. [13] [12] [9]
- Creative concept: GMA News reported that the band wrote the screenplay themselves, with the narrative juxtaposing domestic scenes of togetherness against moments of isolation and hallucination, suggesting a loved one’s presence as a ghost. PULP Magazine similarly described the video’s cinematic portrayal of love, loss, and lingering emotions. [12] [13]
- Official upload: The music video is available on Cup of Joe’s official YouTube channel. [9]

## Reception

- Critical commentary:
  - Billboard Philippines: Gabriel Saulog wrote that Multo “hits you straight in the feels” and is “a bit more ambitious and experimental” sonically, with “a hook that lingers deep upon its first full listen.” [19]
  - Rolling Stone Philippines: The outlet argued Multo marked Cup of Joe’s “arrival as serious pop storytellers,” highlighting its slow-burn breakthrough and the band’s transformation. [17]
- Year-end recognition:
  - Spotify Philippines named Multo the country’s top song of 2025. [3]
  - Apple Music Philippines listed Multo at No. 1 on its Top OPM Songs of 2025. [82]
- Awards:
  - Filipino Music Awards (FMA) 2025: Multo won Song of the Year, as reported by Bandwagon Asia’s coverage of the event held at SM Mall of Asia Arena. [84]

## Commercial performance

- Global charts:
  - Billboard Global 200: Billboard Philippines reported Cup of Joe as the first Filipino act to debut on the Billboard Global 200, with Multo entering at No. 181 for the chart tracked by Luminate; GMA News likewise reported the global entry. [62] [59]
  - Billboard Global Excl. U.S.: Billboard Philippines noted a debut at No. 74 for Multo on the Global Excl. U.S. chart, calling it the highest debut and the first Filipino song to reach the Top 100 on that chart. [62]
- Philippines:
  - Billboard Philippines Hot 100: According to Billboard Philippines’ official channels, Multo reached No. 1 on the Hot 100 and sustained a lengthy run at the top; a post by Billboard Philippines stated the song held the No. 1 position on the Hot 100 and Top Philippine Songs charts for 27 weeks. [57]
- Streaming and video:
  - Wish 107.5 Bus live performance: A widely viewed official performance video underscored the song’s popularity. [5]

Note: Chart rankings and milestones are attributed to Billboard Philippines (for both national and global chart reporting) and GMA News (for coverage of the Global 200 entry). [62] [57] [59]

## Other songs titled “Multo”

“Multo” is a common Tagalog term meaning “ghost,” and several unrelated songs share the title. These releases are distinct from Cup of Joe’s single and do not appear to have comparable independent national media coverage:

- Magnus Haven — Multo (2020): Available on Spotify; lyrics pages are cataloged on Genius. [27] [43]
- JERGE — Multo (2024): Released as a single with an official music video; documented on streaming platforms and lyric sites. [24] [49] [42]
- Healy After Dark — Multo (2024): Released via O/C Records with official audio and lyric video uploads; label-provided metadata is available on YouTube. [50] [33] [53]

Additionally, The Youth’s catalog includes the track “Multo Sa Paningin – Multong Bakla,” which uses “multo” in its title but is not simply titled “Multo.” This appears on releases documented by MusicBrainz and compilation listings. [21] [22] [23]

If multiple encyclopedia entries were to be created, a disambiguation page would be appropriate to distinguish among these unrelated songs. Based on the breadth of independent coverage, Cup of Joe’s Multo demonstrably meets general notability standards via significant reporting in national music media, chart publications, and year-end lists, whereas other tracks titled “Multo” are primarily documented through platform listings and label uploads.
