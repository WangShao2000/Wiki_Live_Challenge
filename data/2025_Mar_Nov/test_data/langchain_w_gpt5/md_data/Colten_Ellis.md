# Colten Ellis

Colten Ellis (born October 5, 2000) is a Canadian professional ice hockey goaltender for the Buffalo Sabres of the National Hockey League (NHL). He catches left, is listed at 6 ft 1 in and 191 lb, and was born in Whycocomagh, Nova Scotia.[1] Ellis was selected by the St. Louis Blues in the third round, 93rd overall, of the 2019 NHL Entry Draft.[4][5] He established the Quebec Major Junior Hockey League (QMJHL) record for career shutouts with 18 on March 21, 2021 and tied the league’s single‑season shutout mark (7) while setting the single‑season goals‑against average record (1.78) during the 2020–21 season.[7][8][9][10] After signing a three‑year entry‑level contract with the Blues in March 2021 and spending parts of four seasons in the AHL and ECHL, Ellis was claimed off waivers by Buffalo on October 6, 2025 and made his NHL debut with a 27‑save win on October 22, 2025.[1][2][3][6]

## Early life and background

Ellis was born in Whycocomagh, Nova Scotia, and developed through minor hockey in Cape Breton.[1] As a midget AAA goaltender with the Cape Breton West Islanders, he backstopped the team to Nova Scotia’s first Telus Cup national title in 2017. In the championship game, he made 43 saves in a 5–4 overtime win against the Blizzard du Séminaire Saint‑François in Prince George, British Columbia, with Hockey Canada naming him one of the game’s three stars.[16][17]

## Junior career

Ellis’ QMJHL rights were originally held by the Cape Breton Screaming Eagles, with whom he appeared in three games as a 16‑year‑old before being traded to the Rimouski Océanic in June 2017.[9] He became Rimouski’s starter in 2017–18 and was among league leaders in his rookie season, later earning QMJHL recognition including the Raymond Lagacé Trophy (Defensive Rookie of the Year) and placement on the league’s All‑Rookie Team and Second All‑Star Team.[23]

After two more seasons with Rimouski, Ellis was acquired by the Charlottetown Islanders ahead of the 2020–21 campaign.[8] During 2020–21, he set multiple QMJHL records: his 18th career shutout on March 21, 2021 moved him past Philippe Cadorette for the all‑time mark; his seventh shutout of the season tied the single‑season record; and his 1.78 goals‑against average established a new single‑season league record. He finished the year 23–1–0 with a .926 save percentage and won the Jacques Plante Memorial Trophy as the league’s top goaltender.[7][8][9][10]

## NHL draft and contract history

- Ellis was drafted by the St. Louis Blues in the third round, 93rd overall, at the 2019 NHL Entry Draft.[4][5]
- He signed a three‑year entry‑level contract with St. Louis in March 2021.[8][26]
- On June 11, 2024, the Blues signed Ellis to a one‑year, two‑way contract extension ($775,000 NHL / $85,000 AHL).[4]
- On March 6, 2025, the Blues agreed to a two‑year, two‑way extension with Ellis, worth $775,000 (NHL) and $175,000 (AHL) in 2025–26, and $775,000 (NHL) and $225,000 (AHL) in 2026–27.[5]

## Professional career

Ellis began his professional career in the ECHL with the Worcester Railers in 2021–22 and made his first AHL appearances with the Springfield Thunderbirds the same season.[11][10] He spent 2022–23 with the Tulsa Oilers (ECHL) and split 2023–24 between the Orlando Solar Bears (ECHL) and Springfield (AHL). In Orlando, he was recognized as ECHL Goaltender of the Month for December 2023 (5–0–0, 1.58 GAA, .954 SV%) and ECHL Goaltender of the Week for December 18–23, 2023.[19][20][21]

In 2024–25, Ellis held the crease for Springfield, posting a 22–14–5 record with a 2.63 goals‑against average, a .922 save percentage, and three shutouts in 42 regular season games; his save percentage ranked fourth in the AHL.[13] He was named the AHL Player of the Week for the period ending March 9, 2025 after allowing only two goals on 97 shots across three appearances (0.72 GAA, .979 SV%), including a 32‑save shutout.[12]

Ellis was recalled by the Blues in April 2025.[13] During 2025 training camp he was placed on waivers on October 5 and claimed by the Buffalo Sabres on October 6, 2025.[6][1] He made his NHL debut on October 22, 2025, making 27 saves in Buffalo’s 4–2 win against the Detroit Red Wings; the Sabres had indicated earlier that day he was expected to start, with family and his fiancée attending.[2][3]

As of December 12, 2025, Ellis is on the Buffalo Sabres roster for the 2025–26 season.[1][25]

## International play

Ellis represented Canada at the 2018 IIHF Under‑18 World Championship; Hockey Canada and team announcements subsequently noted his 2018 U‑18 participation when naming him to Canada’s roster for the Spengler Cup.[15] In December 2024, Ellis was selected to represent Team Canada at the Spengler Cup in Davos; he became the first active Springfield Thunderbirds player to take part in the tournament.[14][15]

At the midget AAA level, Ellis’ 2017 Telus Cup performance was a significant early milestone, highlighted by 43 saves in the final and multiple three‑star selections during the event.[16][17]

## Playing style

In October 2025, Buffalo head coach Lindy Ruff described Ellis as a goaltender with strong puck‑handling, quickness, and a calm demeanor.[18] Prior to his draft year, Ellis was profiled as an athletic “new‑era” netminder with notable puck‑tracking skills by Habs Eyes on the Prize, which also identified areas for continued development typical of young goalies.[23]

## Personal life

Ellis was born in Whycocomagh, Nova Scotia.[1] Ahead of his NHL debut on October 22, 2025, he stated that his family and fiancée would be in attendance at KeyBank Center.[3]

## Career statistics and records

- Junior (QMJHL): With Rimouski (2017–18 to 2019–20) and Charlottetown (2020–21), Ellis set the QMJHL all‑time record for career shutouts (18; achieved March 21, 2021), tied the single‑season shutout record (7 in 2020–21), and set the single‑season GAA record (1.78 in 2020–21).[7][8][9][10]
- ECHL: Across stints with Worcester (2021–22), Tulsa (2022–23), and Orlando (2023–24), Ellis has appeared in 82 regular‑season ECHL games, recording a 39–27–12 mark with a 3.11 GAA and .904 save percentage.[11]
- AHL: In 2024–25 with Springfield, he recorded 22 wins, a 2.63 GAA, and a .922 save percentage, with three shutouts; his save percentage ranked fourth in the league.[13] He earned AHL Player of the Week honors on March 9, 2025.[12]
- NHL: Ellis made his NHL debut on October 22, 2025, stopping 27 of 29 shots in a 4–2 win over Detroit.[2]
