# Arne Furumark

## Lead Section

Arne Furumark was a Swedish archaeologist and scholar recognized for his significant contributions to the study of Aegean and eastern Mediterranean prehistory, particularly in the areas of Bronze Age pottery and archaeological chronology. He is widely regarded for establishing rigorous typologies of Mycenaean and Minoan ceramics, which have had a lasting influence on the field of prehistoric archaeology. ## Major Contributions

- **Pottery Typology**: Furumark is best known for developing detailed typologies and chronological frameworks for Mycenaean pottery. These systems provided a basis for identifying and dating archaeological strata across the eastern Mediterranean[[1]](https://www.jstor.org/stable/4173351).
- **Bronze Age Chronology**: His work contributed significantly to the absolute and relative dating of the Aegean Bronze Age, aiding the synchronization of Mediterranean chronologies. This scholarly framework forms the core of subsequent studies and reference handbooks on the subject.
- **Influence on Archaeological Method**: Furumark's methodological rigor in the categorization and analysis of artifacts influenced broader archaeological practice, reinforcing the importance of typological classification and its role in interpreting cultural and trade relationships in the Bronze Age[[1]](https://www.jstor.org/stable/4173351).
