# Cow (Mario)

The term "Cow (Mario)" refers to the recurring appearance of cow characters and imagery within the Mario video game franchise developed and published by Nintendo. While cows are not central figures in the series, they hold a notable place due to their whimsical inclusion as obstacles or background elements, primarily in the Mario Kart racing games. The representation of cows in the Mario series, especially through tracks like "Moo Moo Farm," "Moo Moo Meadows," and "Moo Moo Ranch," has contributed to their recognition among players and media, making them a memorable part of the franchise's broader cast of animals. Their cameo appearances and quirky behavior reflect the playful design philosophy behind Mario games, and they have received attention in gaming journalism and fan communities for their unique charm and impact.

## Concept and Creation

Cows in the Mario franchise are designed as non-interactive background elements or track obstacles that enhance environmental diversity and inject humor into gameplay. Unlike protagonists such as Mario, Luigi, or antagonists like Bowser, cattle are not characterized with distinctive personalities or central plots. Their primary purpose is to act as obstacles, requiring players to avoid collisions during races or to serve as visual quirks in platform and exploration games.

The most prominent display of cow imagery is found within the Mario Kart series, specifically in tracks named "Moo Moo Farm" (Mario Kart 64), "Moo Moo Meadows" (Mario Kart Wii and Mario Kart 8/8 Deluxe), and "Moo Moo Ranch" (Mario Kart Tour). The choice to include cows appears to align with Mario Kart's creative direction, aiming for lively and memorable courses filled with unexpected elements. According to Nintendo designers, course creation in Mario Kart intentionally focuses on variety and entertainment, using animals like cows to emphasize the unpredictability and humor of the franchise[1][2][3].

## History and Game Appearances

### Early Appearances

- The first well-known cow appearance in the Mario franchise came in "Mario Kart 64," released in 1996, where cows roam a farm-themed racing course called "Moo Moo Farm." These cows act as moving obstacles, and colliding with them causes players to lose speed[1].
- The successive tracks, "Moo Moo Meadows" (debuting in "Mario Kart Wii," 2008), maintained and elaborated on this concept, with enhanced visuals and more interactive obstacles[2].

### Subsequent Games

- "Moo Moo Meadows" appeared as a retro course in "Mario Kart 8" and "Mario Kart 8 Deluxe," retaining the importance of cows as interactive features on the track. The courses are frequently celebrated for their pastoral setting and the playful obstacle mechanics provided by cows[4][5].
- In "Mario Kart Tour," "Moo Moo Ranch" provides another variation, again featuring cows as characteristic obstacles[4].
- Outside the Mario Kart franchise, cows have made occasional appearances. For example, "Super Mario Odyssey" features realistic cow models in the Sand Kingdom, serving as scenery and environmental flavor but without direct gameplay impact[4].

## Official Commentary and Design Philosophy

There is little direct commentary from Nintendo explicitly on the cow characters, but insights into the broader course design philosophy have been disclosed. In an interview featured in "Iwata Asks: Mario Kart Wii," Nintendo designer Katsuya Eguchi explained that the team aims to populate courses with unexpected, lively elements to keep players engaged. Animals such as cows are used to generate a sense of familiarity, variety, and humor in rural-themed tracks[3].

While cows are not central to the narrative of the Mario series, their inclusion reflects a design choice to blend simplicity and entertainment, resonating with the overarching ethos behind the Mario Kart franchise.

## Reception and Cultural Impact

Even though cow characters are peripheral in the Mario franchise, their inclusion in Mario Kart tracks has been consistently noted in professional reviews, media coverage, and fan discussions:

- Gaming outlets such as IGN have cited "Moo Moo Meadows" as one of the franchise's most memorable tracks, emphasizing the enduring appeal of the cows' random placement and charming animation[5].
- Polygon describes the cows in "Moo Moo Farm" as more than mere obstacles, instead highlighting their role in amplifying the game's whimsy and rural atmosphere[6].
- Player communities often reference the cows in forums, social media posts, and fan art, indicating a sense of affection toward their quirky presence.

Books covering the history of Mario, such as "Super Mario: How Nintendo Conquered America" and "The History of Nintendo," mention cows mainly in the context of Mario Kart tracks, underscoring their reputation as quirky environmental features[7][8].

While there is limited evidence of prominent merchandise or major fan tributes directly tied to the cows, their persistent mention in media and frequent inclusion in "best of" lists indicate a lasting cultural footprint within the Mario universe.

## Summary of Notable Appearances

- **Mario Kart 64 (1996):** "Moo Moo Farm" introduces cows as moving obstacles[1].
- **Mario Kart Wii (2008):** "Moo Moo Meadows" expands the concept with improved graphics and additional obstacles[2].
- **Mario Kart 8 / 8 Deluxe:** "Moo Moo Meadows" reappears, maintaining the legacy of cow obstacles[4].
- **Mario Kart Tour:** "Moo Moo Ranch" features similar cow mechanics[4].
- **Super Mario Odyssey:** Realistic cow scenery in the Sand Kingdom[4].

## Legacy

Although cows in the Mario series have not achieved the status of principal characters, their role as obstacles and environmental features in the Mario Kart series has earned them a distinct spot in the franchise's identity. Their recurring appearance demonstrates Nintendo's commitment to playful, accessible game design and has contributed to the charm and memorability of several Mario titles.
