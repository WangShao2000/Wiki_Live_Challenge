# Jimmy Essex

## Overview

Jimmy Essex (born 1989) is an English actor and musician recognized primarily for his work in television, most notably his portrayal of Adam Donovan in the Channel 4 soap opera *Hollyoaks*. Active in the entertainment industry since the early 2010s, Essex has also built a career as a singer-songwriter, known for his soulful pop releases and live performances. He has spoken publicly about LGBT representation in entertainment and has been involved in advocacy for mental health awareness.
