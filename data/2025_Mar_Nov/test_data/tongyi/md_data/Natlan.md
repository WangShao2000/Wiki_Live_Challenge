# Natlan
[Natlan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natlan) is one of the seven major nations of Teyvat, the fantastical continent in miHoYo’s open-world action RPG *Genshin Impact*. Introduced as the sixth playable region with the game’s version 5.0 update on August 28, 2024, Natlan stands out for its rich cultural tapestry, unique exploration mechanics, and influential storyline focusing on the ideals of war and renewal.


# Geographic Location

Natlan is situated in the western portion of Teyvat, positioned beyond the Great Red Sand desert that borders the nation of Sumeru. It is visually characterized by vast volcanic landscapes, scorching heat, natural hot springs, and deeply rooted traditions. Its geography includes towering cliffs, lush valleys, canyon systems, and enormous stretches of lava flows. The terrain is so diverse that it fosters a variety of distinct environments, each supporting different tribes and Saurian species.


# Cultural Influences and Historical Background

Natlan draws its aesthetic and societal inspiration from multiple indigenous cultures, primarily pre-Columbian America and sub-Saharan Africa, with additional influences from Oceania. This blend is reflected not only in its architecture and attire but also in ceremonial practices, music, and spiritual beliefs. The nation is renowned for its vibrant use of color, intricate tribal patterns, and musical instruments such as castanets and Spanish guitars—a nod to Latin American and Aztec roots—alongside African drums and chants.

The core of Natlan’s society is built around martial prowess, governed by the Pyro Archon Mavuika, who is revered as the God of War and embodies the spirit of conflict and triumph. Her ideal, “War,” is embedded into every aspect of the nation’s daily life, from governance to entertainment. This influence permeates legends, myths, and everyday customs.

## Game Features and Mechanics

### Exploration and Movement

One of Natlan’s defining features is its innovative approach to movement. Players encounter Saurians throughout the landscape and can transform into them temporarily via "Indwelling" to navigate terrain inaccessible otherwise. Each Saurian offers specific utilities:

- **Tepetlisaurus:** Excels at tunneling underground and scaling vertical surfaces.
- **Yumkasaurus:** Uses directional pulls and high-speed grapples to traverse obstacles.
- **Koholasaurus:** Moves rapidly across water and liquid Phlogiston, enabling fluid exploration in aquatic settings.

In addition to Saurian forms, players can explore Natlan with specially-designed characters like Kinich, who uses a grappling hook; Mualani, who surfs on a sharkboard; and Kachina, who can climb walls using her “Turbo Twirly” device. These mechanics enhance player freedom and reinforce the region’s thematic emphasis on agility and endurance.

### Phlogiston and Nightsoul System

Natlan introduces two core resource systems: Phlogiston and Nightsoul.

- **Phlogiston:** Acts as an additional energy pool for traversal and certain character abilities. It is generated by interacting with various world elements such as Everflame Seeds, Flamegranates, and other environmental objects. Once spent, Phlogiston replenishes automatically, allowing prolonged mobility.
- **Nightsoul:** A multi-layer system affecting both combat and exploration:
    - **Nightsoul Blessing:** Grants characters a burst of mobility and elemental effects upon entering the Natlan region.
    - **Nightsoul Burst:** Triggers when party members deliver elemental damage, enhancing combat effectiveness with less downtime.
    - **Nightsoul Transmission:** Allows instant skill activation during tactical switches, adding further flexibility to party management.

These systems work together to give Natlan’s mechanics depth, making it unique among all regions in the game.


## Storytelling and Lore

The primary narrative arc of Natlan centers on its themes of war, sacrifice, and redemption. Characters such as Kachina, Mualani, and Kinich become involved in pivotal tournaments known as the Pilgrimage of the Sacred Flame, where warriors compete for honor and the affection of their tribe’s leaders. The climax of the Archon Quest involves a dramatic showdown between Kachina and Mualani, highlighting not only personal growth but also the bonds of friendship forged through adversity.

Underlying this struggle is the concept of the Night Kingdom—a mystical afterlife realm tied closely to Natlan’s ley lines. Souls of departed warriors wander within this domain until their stories are acknowledged or forgotten, creating a complex tapestry of memory, death, and rebirth. Legendary battles and spiritual artifacts play key roles in shaping both individual destinies and the fate of the nation itself.


## Release and Character Introduction

Natlan was released in phases, initially opening to players who had completed the Mondstadt Archon Quest and reached Adventure Rank 28. Three of the six tribes and corresponding Saurians became available at launch (Version 5.0), with subsequent updates introducing new areas and characters over the course of 2024–2025.

Key playable characters from Natlan include:
- **Mualani:** Hydro catalyst wielder, specializes in surf-based mobility and versatile combat tactics.
- **Kinich:** Dendro claymore warrior, utilizes climbing and grappling hooks to dominate vertical arenas.
- **Kachina:** Geo polearm user, excels in wall-climbing and close-range attacks.

These characters debuted during event wish sequences, offering players new opportunities for exploration and team composition.


## Critical Reception and Controversy

While Natlan received praise for its creativity, fresh mechanics, and stunning visuals, it also faced significant controversy surrounding character design and representation. Many critics and fans noted that despite clear inspiration from African and Latin American cultures, the majority of Natlan’s protagonist characters appeared light-skinned or white, sparking widespread backlash on social media platforms. Voice actors from various regions publicly criticized miHoYo’s decisions, citing accusations of colorism and cultural appropriation. Multiple Change.org petitions gathered tens of thousands of signatures calling for improved diversity and authenticity in future content.

Despite these criticisms, the region’s gameplay innovations—including enhanced mobility options and dynamic combat systems—were well-received by reviewers. Critics highlighted how the traversal mechanics, especially the ability to switch between Saurian indwelling and character-specific skills, made exploration more rewarding than previous regions.


## Festivals and Rituals

Within Natlan’s lore, several festivals are held annually. The most notable is the Pilgrimage of the Sacred Flame, a grand tournament where warriors from all tribes compete for recognition and glory. Other events reflect seasonal changes and religious observances, integrating storytelling elements into gameplay through side quests and reputation rewards.

## Conclusion

Natlan is not just a geographical addition to Teyvat—it represents a substantial expansion in narrative scope, gameplay innovation, and visual design. Despite controversies related to representation, its integration of diverse cultural references, immersive mechanics, and deep lore solidifies its role as a centerpiece of the *Genshin Impact* experience. As players continue to unlock new areas and stories, Natlan remains emblematic of the ongoing dialogue between creative inspiration and respectful inclusivity in global gaming.
