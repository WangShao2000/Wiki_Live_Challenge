# Here for It All

_**Here for It All**_ is the sixteenth studio album by American singer-songwriter Mariah Carey, released on September 26, 2025 through her imprint Mariah and the music company Gamma. This album marks her return as an independent artist, representing a new chapter in her ongoing musical journey and her first project since 2018’s _Caution_. The album was preceded by three highly anticipated singles—"Type Dangerous," "Sugar Sweet," and "Play This Song"—and features collaborations with notable artists such as Anderson .Paak, Kehlani, Shenseea, and the Clark Sisters.


### Background and Release

After a seven-year hiatus between studio albums, Mariah Carey revealed in mid-2023 that she was working on new material, describing the upcoming record as reflecting her personal evolution over the past decade. The album follows her signing of a multi-album deal with Gamma and executive production by L.A. Reid. Her lead single, "Type Dangerous," debuted in June 2025 and marked a significant milestone—Carey earned her 50th entry on the US Billboard Hot 100 chart with its premiere at number 95, highlighting her enduring influence and reach.

The album was officially unveiled through a series of teasers and previews on social media, including a celebratory montage in July 2025 that highlighted her catalog and accomplishments. Carey selected the title track early in the creative process, intending it to serve as a resonant conclusion to the album’s narrative arc. Her approach aimed for emotional authenticity, providing listeners with insight into both her personal experiences and professional resilience.


### Musical Style and Influences

Musically, _Here for It All_ is an eclectic blend of contemporary R&B and pop, drawing inspiration from funk, disco, soul, and gospel. The sound reflects a broader exploration beyond the stylistic boundaries established in earlier works, particularly _Caution_, showcasing her embrace of retro textures and modern sensibilities. Standout moments on the album include the energetic disco-funk groove of "I Won’t Allow It," the sentimental reworking of Paul McCartney and Wings’ "My Love," and the gospel-infused closer "Jesus I Do" featuring the Clark Sisters.

Notable for its craftsmanship, each song is constructed with well-defined melodic structures and emotionally rich lyrics. Some tracks demonstrate a gentler approach in terms of vocal delivery compared to previous albums, with Carey opting for candid portrayals of her artistic maturity. While certain critics noted a restrained nature in her vocal performance, they acknowledged the emotional depth and sincerity conveyed throughout the project.

#### Promotional Events

- **BET Awards**: Carey performed "Type Dangerous" at the BET Awards in June 2025 and was honored with the Ultimate Icon Award, marking her return to the televised awards scene after two decades.
- **Capital FM Summertime Ball**: Headlining Wembley Stadium, she delivered one of the first live performances of "Type Dangerous" along with classics like "We Belong Together" and "Touch My Body."
- **Brighton Pride**: As headline act, Carey expressed gratitude to fans and celebrated her longstanding bond with the LGBTQ+ community, performing fan favorites and songs from the upcoming album.
- **Sandringham Estate Heritage Live Festivals**: Presented a high-energy set featuring hits from her back catalog and new material, maintaining engagement despite reduced vocal agility compared to her prime.
- **Amazônia Live Concert in Belém**: Performed on a floating stage above the Guamá River as part of a special TV event focused on environmental preservation, blending her music with advocacy.
- **Jimmy Fallon Show**: Appeared on The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon in late September 2025, performing "Play This Song" with Anderson .Paak and promoting the album directly to a national audience.


### Commercial Performance

On its debut week, _Here for It All_ entered the US Billboard 200 at number seven, selling 47,000 album-equivalent units, including 39,000 pure sales. The album topped four Billboard charts: Billboard 200, Top Album Sales, Top Current Album Sales, and Independent Albums. In the United Kingdom, it reached number 31 on the UK Albums Chart, while in Australia it peaked at number 19 on the ARIA Top 50 Albums Chart.


### Critical Reception

Critics generally responded positively to _Here for It All_, praising its ambitious genre-blending and Carey’s vulnerability. Aggregate scores include a Metascore of 73 out of 100 based on reviews from eight outlets, signifying "generally favorable reviews." Major publications offered varied perspectives:

- **Rolling Stone**: Described the album as “retro and relaxed,” with a focus on Motown, jazz, and soul influences.
- **Pitchfork**: Offered a score of 7.0, noting Carey’s candid portrayal of her current vocal state.
- **NME**: Awarded four out of five stars, calling it “carefree and occasionally brilliant.”
- **The Independent**: Also gave four stars, emphasizing the album’s polished and confident presentation.

Some critiques pointed out moments of restraint and questioned whether the album diverged enough from Carey’s established style. Nevertheless, many reviewers considered it an emotionally honest document of her current life stage.


### Accolades

At the 2025 MTV Video Music Awards, Mariah Carey won the Moon Person trophy for Best R&B Video for "Type Dangerous," marking her first ever VMA win. She also received the prestigious Michael Jackson Video Vanguard Award, recognizing her profound impact on music and culture.


### Personnel and Credits

Throughout the project, Carey collaborated with a team of renowned musicians and producers including Anderson .Paak, Daniel Moore II, Harv, the Stereotypes, Bongo ByTheWay, and Rogét Chahayed, among others. The Clark Sisters made guest appearances, reinforcing the album’s gospel elements.

Technical credits feature mixing and mastering work by Brian Garten, Colin Leonard, and Kevin Davis, ensuring polished and cohesive audio quality.

### Conclusion

_**Here for It All**_ encapsulates Mariah Carey’s sustained relevance and adaptability as a musical icon. Through rich genre fusion, lyrical honesty, and continued innovation, the album reaffirms her status as a force in contemporary R&B and pop. Its commercial success, critical recognition, and notable awards underscore the significance of this return to form—one that balances legacy with fresh vision for the future.
