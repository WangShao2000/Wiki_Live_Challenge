# Good Boy (2025 Leonberg film)

_Good Boy_ is a 2025 American supernatural horror film directed by Ben Leonberg in his feature debut. Co-written with Alex Cannon, the film stars Leonberg's own Nova Scotia Duck Tolling Retriever dog, Indy, as the central protagonist, with Shane Jensen playing Todd, the owner, alongside Arielle Friedman and Larry Fessenden in supporting roles. The story is told entirely through the perspective of Indy, focusing on his sense of loyalty and heightened awareness of malevolent forces that only he can perceive within a rural family home where his human companion is gravely ill.


### Plot

The narrative follows Todd (Shane Jensen) and his trusted canine companion Indy after they relocate from the city to Todd’s late grandfather’s secluded country house, hoping for a fresh start following a serious medical crisis. Upon settling in, Todd continues to suffer from chronic respiratory issues while both begin to experience unsettling, inexplicable phenomena within the house. Uniquely, only Indy senses the approaching threats—ghostly apparitions and shadowy figures lurking at the edges of perception, which ultimately threaten Todd’s life. As Todd grows increasingly ill and erratic, Indy must draw upon his keen instincts and unwavering devotion to protect his master from unseen dangers, leading to emotionally charged confrontations and sacrifices. The film blurs the boundaries between reality and spectral menace, using tense atmosphere, close-up visuals from a dog’s viewpoint, and haunting sound design to heighten suspense and convey Indy’s confusion and fear.

These actors contribute to the ensemble supporting performance, with most characters’ faces often obscured to align with the film’s unique point-of-view.


### Production

Produced by Ben Leonberg and Kari Fischer, _Good Boy_ was filmed over an extended 400 days to capture authentic performances from Indy and maintain creative control over the perspective-driven narrative. Cinematography was handled by Ben Leonberg under the pseudonym Wade Grebnoel. The score was composed by Sam Boase-Miller, whose work emphasizes tension and emotion, underscoring the haunted house atmosphere and the bond between dog and owner.

The film’s production focused heavily on practical techniques and naturalistic animal behavior rather than CGI, placing trust and safety at the forefront of filming practices. Leonberg’s background as both filmmaker and animal owner influenced the production approach, setting a new standard for responsible animal-centered storytelling.


### Release and Distribution

_Good Boy_ premiered at the 2025 South by Southwest (SXSW) Film & TV Festival on March 8, drawing attention for its innovative concept and heartfelt execution. The film had its theatrical release in the United States on October 3, 2025, distributed by IFC Films. It was subsequently released for digital streaming via Shudder on November 21, 2025, expanding its availability internationally.


### Reception and Critical Response

Critics lauded _Good Boy_ for its ingenuity and emotional resonance. Many reviews highlighted the film’s singular viewpoint, impressive technical craft, and the compelling nature of Indy’s performance. The critical consensus on Rotten Tomatoes describes _Good Boy_ as “a visually striking, emotionally devastating horror film that eschews genre conventions to deliver a uniquely haunting and conceptually ambitious experience.” At the time of release, the film held a score of 92% based on 51 critic reviews.

The Guardian praised the film’s inventive pacing and sensitive portrayal of loyalty, calling it “a 73-minute haunted house riff that has ingenious moments but gets repetitive,” noting the near-limitless potential of the canine-centric format.

Roger Ebert’s review underscored the technical prowess in capturing Indy’s sensory world, though raised concerns about the feature-length execution, suggesting the concept might have been more effective as a short film. Despite this, the reviewer emphasized that “Indy’s steadfast efforts and protectiveness provide the emotional core of the film.”

### Box Office Performance

Despite its low budget of just $70,000, _Good Boy_ achieved remarkable financial success. It grossed over $8.5 million worldwide, making it one of the highest-grossing films relative to its production costs in 2025. The film’s domestic box office earnings reached $6.6 million, with international earnings totaling approximately $2.1 million.

### Legacy and Impact

_Good Boy_ set a precedent for animal-centered storytelling, demonstrating how the emotional connection between dogs and humans can be leveraged for complex, mainstream cinematic experiences. Its unique narrative framework and commitment to humane treatment of animals garnered widespread praise from both critics and advocacy organizations.

The film’s reception and commercial achievement reinforced the viability of experimental horror within independent filmmaking circles, encouraging filmmakers to consider unconventional approaches to classic genres. The legacy of _Good Boy_ is evident in subsequent discussions around perspective-based horror and the increasing representation of non-human protagonists in cinema.


**In summary**, _Good Boy_ represents a significant milestone in genre innovation, combining heart, horror, and compassion to create a film that resonated deeply with audiences and critics alike. Its influence extends beyond the box office into broader conversations about ethical filmmaking and the potential for transformative stories led by animal actors.
