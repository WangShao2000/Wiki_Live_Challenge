# Colten Ellis

Colten Ellis (born October 5, 2000) is a Canadian professional ice hockey goaltender currently playing for the Buffalo Sabres of the National Hockey League (NHL). A native of River Denys, Nova Scotia, Ellis was drafted 93rd overall by the St. Louis Blues in the 2019 NHL Entry Draft and made history in 2025 by becoming the first NHL goaltender from Cape Breton Island.


# Early Life and Minor Hockey

Born into a family of four, including one brother and a cousin also involved in elite junior hockey, Ellis grew up in the small community of River Denys on Cape Breton Island. He began his hockey journey playing with the Whycocomagh Minor Hockey Association before progressing through regional teams such as the Nova Junior X-Men at the U15 level and the Cape Breton West Islanders at the U18 level. During his time with the Cape Breton West Islanders, he led his team to the first-ever Telus Cup, delivering strong tournament performance with a 2.20 goals against average and a .929 save percentage in finals action.


# Junior Career

Ellis’s skill and perseverance propelled him through the ranks. In the Quebec Major Junior Hockey League (QMJHL), he was selected in the fourth round (59th overall) by the Cape Breton Screaming Eagles during the 2016 QMJHL Entry Draft. After splitting his time between minor and major junior leagues, Ellis traded to the Rimouski Océanic ahead of the 2017–18 season. In his rookie campaign with Rimouski, Ellis quickly became the starting goaltender, leading the league in wins (33) and shutouts (6), recording a 2.35 goals against average and a .913 save percentage. His performance earned him the Raymond Lagacé Trophy for defensive rookie of the year and a spot on both the QMJHL All-Rookie and Second All-Star Teams.

In 2018–19, Ellis was named to the CHL/NHL Top Prospects Game, highlighting his rising stature among North American prospects. The following season, despite injury interruptions, Ellis continued to develop, maintaining an outstanding 2.47 GAA and .910 save percentage in the regular season and posting remarkable playoff numbers. By March 2021, after setting the all-time QMJHL career shutout record (18) and reaching a single-season tie for most shutouts, he signed a three-year entry-level contract with the St. Louis Blues. Ellis concluded his QMJHL career with a league-best 1.78 GAA and .926 save percentage, establishing himself as one of the top goaltenders in junior hockey history.


# Professional Career

### 2021–2022 Season: ECHL and AHL Debut

Ellis turned pro in the 2021–22 season, joining the ECHL’s Worcester Railers. His professional debut came in October 2021, where he stopped 42 shots in a 4–3 win over Maine Mariners. Later, he advanced to the American Hockey League (AHL), making his Springfield Thunderbirds debut in November 2021 and registering a career-best 3.93 goals against average and .880 save percentage across six games. In the ECHL, Ellis made 34 appearances for Worcester, finishing with a 3.21 GAA and .905 save percentage.

### 2022–2023 Season: Rising Through the Ranks

After inconsistent performances and injuries in his early pro seasons, Ellis rebounded with renewed focus. He participated in training camp evaluations and gained experience in both the AHL and ECHL with teams like Tulsa Oilers and Orlando Solar Bears. In December 2024, Ellis was recognized as the Warrior Hockey ECHL Goaltender of the Month after going 5–0–0 with a .954 save percentage and a 1.58 goals against average, marking one of his standout months in professional competition.

### Late 2023–2025: Breakthrough and NHL Opportunity

By October 2024, Ellis secured a contract extension with the St. Louis Blues. Over subsequent seasons, he demonstrated consistent improvement in the AHL, sharing third in the league in wins with a .926 save percentage and a 2.41 goals against average. In October 2025, the Blues placed Ellis on waivers, making him available to other organizations. The Buffalo Sabres claimed Ellis off waivers and soon assigned him to their NHL roster, positioning him for his NHL debut.


## NHL Debut and Achievements

On October 22, 2025, Ellis made his NHL debut for the Buffalo Sabres in a game against the Detroit Red Wings. Named the first star of the game, he delivered a crucial performance, stopping key shots and helping his team secure a victory. This debut marked a milestone as he became the first goaltender from Cape Breton Island to play in the NHL, inspiring pride in his home community.

In his early NHL tenure, Ellis continued to showcase resilience and skill. In his first eight games with the Sabres, he posted a 4–3 record, a 3.25 goals against average, and a .895 save percentage. While still adjusting to the challenges of NHL competition, Ellis's calm demeanor and steady play were praised by coaches and fans alike.


## International Play

Ellis represented Canada at several international events. In April 2018, he started as the goaltender for Team Canada at the IIHF World U18 Championships held in Russia, demonstrating leadership and poise under pressure. He later joined the Canadian team for the prestigious Spengler Cup in Davos, Switzerland, during December 2024. Ellis played all three tournament games, recording a .911 save percentage and a 2.68 goals against average. His contributions were pivotal in high-pressure matches, including semifinal action against Straubing Tigers, even as Team Canada fell just short of a medal.

## Conclusion

Colten Ellis’s journey from River Denys to NHL success story is marked by grit, determination, and technical mastery. From dominating junior leagues in Canada to stardom in the ECHL and AHL, Ellis consistently improved his craft, earning respect for his composure and athleticism between the pipes. As he continues to challenge himself on the biggest stage, his story stands as an inspiration for aspiring athletes in Nova Scotia and beyond.
