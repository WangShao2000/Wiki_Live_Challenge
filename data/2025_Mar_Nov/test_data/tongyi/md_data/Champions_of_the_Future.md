# Champions of the Future

Champions of the Future (COTF) is an international kart racing competition organized by the RGMMC Group and sanctioned by FIA Karting (CIK-FIA). Launched in 2020, COTF has established itself as one of the leading competitions in the global karting calendar, promoting talent through a diverse set of programs, inclusive formats, and strategic expansion into new markets. The series is structured across three core programs, offering opportunities for drivers in all major categories and supporting pathways to higher motorsport disciplines.


### History and Development

COTF was founded in 2020 with the intention of fostering international competition among top-tier karting drivers. The inaugural season featured two classes—OK and OK-Junior—with single-round finals held in other categories such as KZ2 and 60 Mini. The first season crowned Nikita Bedrin (OK) and Arvid Lindblad (OK-J) as champions after a two-round series in Portugal.

The competition quickly evolved beyond its initial scope. By 2021, Rafael Camara (OK) and Freddie Slater (OK-J) secured season titles following a competitive weekend at Genk, Belgium. In 2022, Joe Turney (OK) and Nathan Tye (OK-J) became champions after dynamic events including a Winter Series in Italy and multiple Grand Prix weekends across Europe. The 2023 season saw Alex Powell (OK) and Lewis Wherrell (OK-J) earn their crowns, while 2024 brought victories for Joe Turney (OK) and Dries Van Langendonck (OK-J). The most recent 2025 Euro Series concluded with Kenzo Craigie (OK) and Noah Baglin (OK-J) claiming championship honors after a series marked by intense rivalries and climactic final rounds.

COTF's rapid growth during these years led to notable collaborations, including its partnership with F1 Academy to establish the Champions of the Future Academy Program in 2023. This initiative aimed to increase female participation and inclusion, providing support for female drivers across multiple karting categories and offering access to premier testing opportunities with top manufacturers and Formula 1-affiliated organizations.


### Program Structure

Champions of the Future is organized around three principal programs, each designed to cater to different aspects of driver development and competition:

#### Champions of the Future Euro Series

The Euro Series is the flagship program, focusing on the OK and OK-Junior categories and attracting leading teams and drivers from around the world. It features five to six meetings per season held primarily in European countries, with additional venues outside the EU.

#### Champions of the Future Shifters

Introduced in 2024, the Shifters program expanded the competition to all eight CIK-FIA homologated classes, including gearbox categories (KZ, KZ2, KZ2-Masters) and junior/advanced racing formulae (OK-N, OKN-J, 60 Mini). The program provides specialized environments for technical advancement and highlights high-performance karting under professional conditions.

#### Champions of the Future Academy Program

Established in collaboration with F1 Academy, the Academy Program targets young drivers (ages 8–15) and prioritizes inclusivity, cost control, and talent identification. The series employs arrive-and-drive formats, includes a chassis lottery, and limits entry fees to reduce financial barriers. The top female competitors are supported financially and granted access to exclusive programs, including official tests with Formula 1 organizations.

These programs operate simultaneously and provide clear pathways from grassroots karting to advanced international competition.


### Season Format and Calendar

Each Champions of the Future event typically runs over a four-day weekend spanning from Thursday to Sunday or Wednesday to Saturday, depending on circuit arrangements. Events include:

- Free practice sessions: Drivers familiarize themselves with the track and equipment.
- Qualifying heats: Determine grid positions for the main races.
- Super heats and finals: Intensive qualifying rounds followed by knockout-format finals that decide overall podium finishes.
- Points system: Based on CIK-FIA regulations, points are awarded across qualifying heats and finals, with detailed tie-breaking rules in place.

In 2025, the Euro Series consisted of five to six rounds across circuits in Spain, France, Denmark, Sweden, Italy, and the United Kingdom. Shifters added further race meetings featuring KZ, KZ2, and KZ2-Masters categories, with finals held in high-profile locations such as Valencia and Franciacorta.

### Expansion and International Reach

COTF’s vision extends beyond its existing footprint. Plans are underway for national-level series launching in 2026:

- **British COTFA**: Hosted by Motorsport UK, this national championship will serve as a feeder for international competition.
- **UAE COTFA**: A two-round title across November 2025 and February 2026, integrating local and international drivers.
- **Macanese COTFA**: Set to debut in January 2026 at Coloane Circuit, introducing Macau’s motorsport community to global standards.
- **Champions of the Future America**: Beginning in late January 2026, the American series will consist of ten rounds across California, Nevada, and potentially other southwestern U.S. venues, featuring Micro, Mini, KA100, and IAME X30 classes.

These initiatives underscore COTF’s commitment to broadening accessibility, promoting diversity, and offering increasingly robust pathways to professional motorsport.


### Broadcast and Media Exposure

Champions of the Future events are broadcast live on global platforms including YouTube and Motorsport.tv, providing fans and participants worldwide with real-time access to results, interviews, and highlights. Apex Timing supplies detailed race data, and high viewership figures demonstrate growing popularity and interest in the series.


### Controversies and Governance

Despite its successes, COTF has faced challenges. In May 2025, twenty KZ drivers boycotted the Valencia final due to concerns over Dunlop tire performance. The issue highlighted complex regulatory dynamics, as immediate supplier changes were precluded by ongoing contracts and formal tender processes. The incident reflected broader conversations within international karting about product consistency and athlete safety.


### Driver Development Programs

Since 2025, COTF has broadened its offerings through the Champions of the Future Racing Academy—a year-long Formula 4 development program partnered with Motorsport Performance Academy and led by renowned driver Augusto Farfus. Eight seats annually offer intensive training, dedicated cars and staff, mental conditioning, and synchronized coaching, replicating real race weekends for drivers ready to advance to car racing.


### Costs and Entry Fees

Entry fees reflect efforts to maintain affordability:

- **Senior/Junior (OK & OK-J)**: €695 per round, excluding additional fees for free practice.
- **Academy Program**: €29,950 covers a new kart, tires, fuel, logistics, and full-season participation. Wildcard entries may incur separate fees.

Additional charges relate to outfitting, fines for procedural violations, and damages to provided material, with strict non-refundability policies in case of driver withdrawal.


### Conclusion

Champions of the Future has rapidly transformed from a regional karting series into a cornerstone of the international motorsport ecosystem. Through innovative structures, strong partnerships, inclusive policies, and ambitious expansion, COTF continues to shape the future of karting talent development and competitive excellence. Its journey exemplifies the power of strategic vision, industry collaboration, and responsiveness to emerging challenges and opportunities in motorsport.
