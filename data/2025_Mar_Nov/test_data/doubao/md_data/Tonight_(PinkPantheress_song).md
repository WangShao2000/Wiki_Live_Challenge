# Tonight (PinkPantheress song)

"Tonight" is a song by English singer-songwriter and record producer PinkPantheress. It was released on April 4, 2025, through Warner Records UK as the lead single from her second mixtape, "Fancy That" [1]. The song marks a departure from PinkPantheress's typical wistful introspective style, featuring direct and flirty lyrics with the central question "You want sex with me?" . At 2 minutes and 57 seconds long, "Tonight" is notably longer than many of her previous releases [2].

## Background and release

"Tonight" was written and produced by PinkPantheress alongside Aksel Arvid and Count Baldor [3]. Count Baldor previously collaborated with PinkPantheress on "Angel," which was featured on the Barbie soundtrack [1]. Aksel Arvid, a Norwegian producer, also contributed to other tracks on the "Fancy That" mixtape [1].

The song samples the orchestral arrangement and strings from Panic! at the Disco's "Do You Know What I'm Seeing?" from their 2008 album "Pretty. Odd" [1]. This sampling choice represents part of PinkPantheress's genre-hopping approach on "Fancy That," which includes references to various facets of British culture .

Prior to its release, PinkPantheress teased the song on social media, joking about its length. "Ion wanna see NO MORE song length jokes after this song comes out on Friday IKTR," she wrote, referencing her reputation for releasing short songs .

## Composition and style

Musically, "Tonight" is described as a "synth-driven, bassline-laced bop" . The song opens with sweeping cinematic strings before diving into a dancefloor-ready beat [3]. It leans into PinkPantheress's glossier, synth-pop side—the same aesthetic she explored on tracks like "Turn It Up" and her 2023 debut album "Heaven Knows" [1].

Lyrically, the song features direct and flirty content that represents a departure for PinkPantheress. In an interview on Twitter Spaces, she explained why her music typically skews PG: "Because I know my parents listen to my music," she said. "I never say anything too explicit because I always feel like they're watching... It's 'cause I got the strict parents - Kenyan mom, African mom, you're not getting away with a lot of stuff."

The song's lyrics describe a moment-to-moment mutual infatuation, with lines like "I waited all this time, now I guess it's time I can hold ya" and "You're hot, on fire, that's why I'm going tonight" [4].

## Music video

The official music video for "Tonight" was directed by Charlotte Rutherford, who also helmed PinkPantheress's "Nice to Meet You" video [1]. The video is described as a "riotous regency-meets-rave fantasy" [1] or "Bridgerton-esque" .

In the video, PinkPantheress appears as the belle of the ball, surrounded by well-dressed friends dancing around a grand mansion in a regency-era setting . The video arrives after the artist departed her set at The Lot in a horse and carriage, emphasizing the regency theme .

The music video was released on April 4, 2025, alongside the single [5]. It has been described as a standout visual that complements the song's playful and unapologetic new era [6].

## Commercial performance

"Tonight" achieved commercial success upon release. In the United Kingdom, the song reached the Top 40 charts [7]. According to chart data, it peaked at #35 on the charts and spent 5 weeks in the charting positions [8].

The song also performed well on streaming platforms. On Spotify, "Tonight" accumulated over 1 million streams, with 1,075,724 streams recorded [9]. It debuted high on the Hot Dance/Pop Songs chart [10].

## Critical reception

"Tonight" received generally positive reviews from music critics. On The Singles Jukebox, the song received an average rating of 8.06 out of 10 from a panel of critics . Individual ratings ranged from 6 to 10, with many critics praising the song's catchy melody and playful nature.

Critics noted that "Tonight" represents a growth in PinkPantheress's artistry. Writing for DIY Magazine, the song was described as setting "a dancefloor-ready, pulsing tone for the rest of the project" and marking "a huge milestone for me as an artist" according to PinkPantheress herself .

Some critics appreciated the song's departure from PinkPantheress's typical style. Al Varela gave the song a 10/10 rating, while Ian Mathers noted: "Given the number of samples on the new PinkPantheress that make me feel very sickos.jpg" . Kayla Beardslee described it as "cute and bubbly (and long) enough that bits of it have been slipping into my head for several weeks, uninvited but definitely not unwelcome" .

However, not all reviews were entirely positive. Some critics noted that while enjoyable, the song didn't represent a significant innovation for the artist. One reviewer on Douban Music wrote: "开头还挺吸引人… 编排很紧凑，副歌部分鼓机堆的很结实，写了个漂亮简单的小旋律，adlib 这种过时的玩意玩玩也就算了，剩下没啥好夸的，没什么新的东西，而且确实有点虎头蛇尾" (Translation: "The beginning is quite attractive... The arrangement is tight, the chorus has a solid drum machine build, and it writes a beautiful simple melody. Playing with outdated things like adlib is fine, but there's nothing else to praise. There's nothing new, and it 确实 has a bit of an anticlimactic ending") [11].

## Legacy and impact

"Tonight" is considered a pivotal release in PinkPantheress's career. It represents her stepping into "a fun, sexy and unapologetic new era" [3]. The song's success helped establish the tone for the entire "Fancy That" mixtape, which PinkPantheress described as "the most tied together project I've applied myself to as an artist" .

The song's popularity led to several remixes being released as part of the "Fancy Some More?" project. These include remixes by Basement Jaxx and Joe Goddard (Hot Chip) [12]. The Basement Jaxx remix was released on October 10, 2025 [13].

"Tonight" also spawned a collaboration version with JADE (formerly of Little Mix), titled "Tonight + JADE" [14]. This version was included on the "Fancy Some More?" remix project [12].
