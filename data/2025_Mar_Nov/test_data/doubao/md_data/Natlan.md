# Natlan

**Natlan** is a fictional region in the action role-playing game *Genshin Impact*, developed and published by HoYoverse. It is one of the seven nations of Teyvat, the game’s primary setting, governed by Mavuika, the Pyro Archon and god of war. The region draws cultural inspiration from diverse Indigenous traditions, including Mesoamerican, African, and Polynesian civilizations. Natlan gained prominence as the setting for *Genshin Impact*’s Version 5.0 update and has been the subject of both acclaim for its worldbuilding and controversy regarding cultural representation.

## Setting and Geography

Natlan is situated west of Sumeru’s Great Red Sand desert, accessible via gorges and valleys. Its landscape is defined by volcanic activity, featuring natural hot springs and blazing fields where dragon-like creatures called "saurians" dwell. These saurians, descendants of ancient dragons, coexist with Natlan’s human inhabitants and include distinct variants such as the water-element Koholasaurs (their name combining the Hawaiian word for humpback whale, *koholā*, with "saur") and the wind-element Qucusaur (linked to the Maya-K’iche’ deity Qucumatz).

Key locations within Natlan include the Sacred Flame Arena, where the region’s central ritual—the "归夜圣火巡礼" (Guīyè Shènghuǒ Xúnlǐ, or "Returning Night Sacred Fire Pilgrimage")—takes place, and settlements associated with its six tribal groups.

## Cultural and Societal Structure

### Etymology

The name "Natlan" derives from Nahuatl, the language of the Aztec civilization. The suffix *-tlan* in Nahuatl denotes "place of," while the prefix *Na-* is linked to figures like the Aztec deity Nanahuatzin (literally "Pimply One"), a god of fire and sacrifice.

### Tribes

Natlan’s society is organized into six tribes, each aligned with a different elemental power. Three tribes have been formally revealed:



* **流泉之众 (Liúquán zhī Zhòng, "People of the Springs")**: A water-aligned tribe known for its emphasis on leisure, including music, fishing, and hot spring gatherings. Its name, *Meztli*, references the Aztec moon god Metztli.

* **沃陆之邦 (Wòlù zhī Bāng, "Collective of Plenty")**: A lightning-aligned tribe associated with physical strength; its members have been depicted in roles like fitness instructors.

* **花羽会 (Huāyǔ Huì, "Flower-Feather Clan")**: A wind-aligned tribe whose members serve as mediators. Its name reflects Mesoamerican traditions of using feathers as symbols of authority.

### Religion and Rituals

The Pyro Archon Mavuika serves as Natlan’s spiritual and political leader. Her name and role draw from Mahuika, the Māori goddess of fire, and her banner features a sun motif inspired by the Aztec Sun Stone, which also influences Natlan’s "Vision" (in-game magical artifact) design.

The Sacred Fire Pilgrimage is Natlan’s most important ritual: tribal representatives compete in the Sacred Flame Arena to collect "角逐之焰" (Juézhú zhī Yàn, "Competition Flames"), which sustain the region’s sacred fire and protect it from the "Abyss" (a primordial force of darkness). Pre-ritual ceremonies include a ball game modeled on Mesoamerican sacred 球赛，where players use hips, elbows, and knees to hit a 1–4 kilogram rubber ball through goalposts—echoing real-world traditions linked to death and rebirth mythology.

## Gameplay and Media

### Soundtrack

Natlan’s soundtrack, composed by HoYo-Mix, blends musical elements from its cultural inspirations. Teaser tracks feature Spanish guitar, castanet rhythms, Swahili lyrics, and Mesoamerican-Polynesian chants performed by the Vienna University Philharmonic Chorus. The score has received praise for its authenticity and scope.

### Narrative Role

Archon Quest Chapter V, set in Natlan, centers on the Sacred Fire Pilgrimage and Mavuika’s leadership against the Abyss. Playable characters introduced alongside the region include 玛拉妮 (Malani) of the People of the Springs, 伊安珊 (Ianshan) of the Collective of Plenty, and 恰斯卡 (Chasca) of the Flower-Feather Clan.

## Reception and Controversies

### Cultural Representation Debate

Natlan has sparked widespread discussion over its depiction of characters inspired by non-European cultures. Following the reveal of early characters like Kachina, Kinich, and Mualani in the Version 4.8 teaser trailer, fans criticized their predominantly light skin tones—a departure from the African, Mesoamerican, and Polynesian groups that inspired Natlan.

A [Change.org](https://Change.org) petition calling for an end to "cultural whitewashing" surpassed 120,000 signatures by November 2024. Voice actors associated with *Genshin Impact* publicly joined the criticism: Valeria Rodriguez (voice of Sucrose) condemned the misrepresentation of the Yoruba deity Ọlọrun in the character Ororon, stating, "If you’re going to use real-world deities, respect them". Alejandro Saab (voice of Cyno) and Anne Yatco (voice of Raiden Shogun) similarly expressed disappointment, with Yatco urging HoYoverse to "listen to its fanbase when valid criticism is made". Media outlets including *Kotaku* and *Polygon* framed the controversy as one of the game’s most significant cultural debates, comparing it to prior criticism of Sumeru’s character designs.

### Critical Acclaim

Despite the controversy, Natlan’s exploration mechanics and worldbuilding have received positive reviews. Critics praised the integration of cultural elements into gameplay, such as the sacred ball game and saurian companion systems, as well as the soundtrack’s attention to detail.
