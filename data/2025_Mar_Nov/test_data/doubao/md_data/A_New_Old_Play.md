# A New Old Play

A New Old Play (Chinese: 椒麻堂會) is a 2021 epic film directed and written by Qiu Jiongjiong [1]. As Qiu's seventh film, it marks his first fictional feature, following six experimental documentaries . The film is a unique hybrid of fiction, historical reminiscence, and experimental theatre [2].

## Plot

The film follows Qiu Fu, a leading clown-role actor in 20th-century Sichuan opera, who dies and must travel to the ghost city under the escort of two underworld officials [3]. As he negotiates his entry into the afterlife, his departing soul reviews his life, including his childhood, performances, family tragedies, and political perils .

The narrative spans five decades of Chinese history from the 1930s to the 1980s, filtered through the experiences of Qiu Yu, a Sichuan opera clown character based on the director's own grandfather, Qiu Fuxin, a renowned comedic performer with the Xinyouxin Opera Troupe .

## Production

A New Old Play was produced by Uluka Productions, Midnight Blur, and Hippocampe Productions . The film was shot in Leshan, Sichuan, China .

Qiu Jiongjiong, who is also a renowned artist, handcrafted elaborate miniature sets for the film, including a model village, a fantastical river landscape, and a golden sun shaped like a Buddha's head . These visual elements create a unique aesthetic that blends traditional Chinese art with experimental filmmaking techniques.

## Reception

The film has received critical acclaim for its innovative approach and historical scope. Richard Brody of The New Yorker praised it as "daring and imaginative, with affecting intimacy and candid observations" . Austin Considine of The New York Times called it a "magnificently layered historical epic... at once tragedy and farce, \[that] breathes new life into a story as old as civilization" .

Maria Giovanna Vagenas of Senses of Cinema wrote that "director Qiu creates a huge epochal fresco and revives the ancient Sichuan Opera tradition with the magic touch of a modern-day Méliès" . Cinema Scope magazine described it as "the brightest light in the Chinese independent cinema world at this moment" .

## Awards and nominations

A New Old Play has received numerous accolades, including 11 wins and 13 nominations overall . Major awards include:



* Special Jury Prize, Locarno Film Festival 2021

* Special Jury Mention, Golden Apricot Yerevan International Film Festival 2021

* Young Jury Prize, Festival des 3 Continents 2021

* Asian Pacific Screen Award 2021

The film has been screened at numerous international film festivals, including Busan International Film Festival, São Paulo International Film Festival, Black Nights Film Festival Tallinn, Singapore International Film Festival, Göteborg Film Festival, and International Film Festival Rotterdam .

## Distribution

The film was released in 2022 by Icarus Films in the United States [4]. It has a runtime of 179 minutes (2 hours and 59 minutes) [5] and is presented in Mandarin with English subtitles [4].

## Legacy

A New Old Play represents a significant achievement in contemporary Chinese independent cinema. It demonstrates Qiu Jiongjiong's distinctive visual style and his ability to blend personal history with broader historical themes [6]. The film has been recognized as a landmark work that pushes the boundaries of Chinese cinema [6].

The National Museum of Asian Art presented an online retrospective of Qiu's work titled "The Daring, Innovative Films of Qiu Jiongjiong" in conjunction with the film's North American theatrical release .
