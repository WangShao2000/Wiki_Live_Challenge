# Colten Ellis

**Colten Ellis** (born October 5, 2000) is a Canadian professional ice hockey goaltender currently playing for the Buffalo Sabres of the National Hockey League (NHL). Drafted 93rd overall by the St. Louis Blues in the 2019 NHL Entry Draft, Ellis has developed into a notable goaltender with achievements at multiple levels of professional hockey [1]. He is recognized for his athletic style of play and has represented Team Canada in international competition [2].

## Early Life

Colten Ellis was born on October 5, 2000, in River Denys, Nova Scotia, Canada [3]. He grew up in River Denys, a small community of only 60 residents, where he faced significant challenges in pursuing his hockey career . The nearest ice rink was approximately 20 minutes away in Whycocomagh [4].

Ellis is the son of Brian and Jo-Ann Ellis [4]. He has a brother named Matthew Ellis and a cousin named Chase Ellis, both also involved in hockey [3]. From the age of 10, Ellis relied on family members to help improve his skills as a goaltender, spending his free time doing drills and training at the local rink [5].

Growing up in rural Cape Breton, Ellis faced what his father described as "really, really hard" conditions for a young goaltender, as there were limited resources available in their area . Despite these challenges, Ellis remained determined to pursue his hockey career, traveling extensively to camps and training sessions throughout his youth .

## Junior Career

### Early Junior Seasons

Ellis began his junior hockey career with the Cape Breton Screaming Eagles in the Quebec Major Junior Hockey League (QMJHL) during the 2016-17 season . In his rookie season, he appeared in 3 games, posting a 2-0-0 record with a 2.50 goals-against average (GAA) and a .885 save percentage .

### Rimouski Océanic

The following season, Ellis was traded to the Rimouski Océanic, where he would spend the next three seasons developing into one of the top goaltenders in the QMJHL . During the 2017-18 season, his first full year with Rimouski, Ellis compiled an impressive 33-8-6 record with a 2.35 GAA and a .913 save percentage in 51 games [6]. His performance earned him several prestigious awards, including the Raymond Lagacé Trophy as the QMJHL's defensive rookie of the year, and he was named to the QMJHL Second All-Star Team [7].

In the 2018-19 season, Ellis continued his strong play, recording a 27-15-0 record with a 2.47 GAA and a .910 save percentage in 46 games . He followed this up with another solid season in 2019-20, posting an 18-7-3 record with a 2.41 GAA and a .904 save percentage in 29 games before the season was cut short due to the COVID-19 pandemic [8].

### Charlottetown Islanders

For the 2020-21 season, Ellis was traded to the Charlottetown Islanders, where he had perhaps his best junior season . He posted an outstanding 23-1-0 record with a league-leading 1.78 GAA and a .926 save percentage in 24 games [9]. His exceptional performance earned him the Jacques Plante Memorial Trophy as the QMJHL's best goaltender and the QMJHL Defensive Player of the Year award [10]. He was also named to the QMJHL Second All-Star Team for the second time in his career [7].

Throughout his QMJHL career, Ellis established himself as one of the league's all-time great goaltenders. He finished his junior career with a remarkable 103-31-14 record across 153 games, with a 2.31 GAA and a .912 save percentage . He is currently second all-time in QMJHL history with 18 career shutouts [11].

## Professional Career

### St. Louis Blues Organization

After being selected by the St. Louis Blues in the third round (93rd overall) of the 2019 NHL Entry Draft, Ellis signed a three-year entry-level contract with the team on March 1, 2021 [10]. He began his professional career in the 2021-22 season, splitting time between the Blues' American Hockey League (AHL) affiliate, the Springfield Thunderbirds, and their ECHL affiliate, the Worcester Railers .

During his first professional season, Ellis appeared in 6 games with the Springfield Thunderbirds, posting a 1-3-2 record with a 3.93 GAA and a .880 save percentage [12]. In the ECHL, he was more successful, recording a 15-13-5 record with a 3.21 GAA and a .905 save percentage in 34 games with the Worcester Railers .

The following season, 2022-23, Ellis spent the majority of his time with the Tulsa Oilers in the ECHL, where he posted a 12-9-5 record with a 3.46 GAA and a .888 save percentage in 27 games . He also had brief stints with the Springfield Thunderbirds, appearing in 2 games [13].

### Breakout Season with Springfield Thunderbirds

The 2023-24 season marked a breakthrough for Ellis, as he split time between the Orlando Solar Bears in the ECHL and the Springfield Thunderbirds in the AHL [14]. With the Orlando Solar Bears, he had an outstanding season, posting a 12-5-1 record with a 2.45 GAA and a .923 save percentage in 21 games . His performance earned him the ECHL Goaltender of the Month award for December 2023 [15].

With the Springfield Thunderbirds, Ellis continued to develop, posting a 7-9-0 record with a 2.89 GAA and a .924 save percentage in 16 games [14]. His strong play earned him a one-year, two-way contract extension with the Blues in June 2024 [16].

### 2024-25 Season and Team Canada Selection

The 2024-25 season saw Ellis establish himself as a top goaltender in the AHL with the Springfield Thunderbirds. Through 42 games, he posted a 22-14-3 record with a 2.63 GAA and a .922 save percentage, with 3 shutouts . His eight wins during the season tied for 8th in the AHL, while his .918 save percentage also ranked 8th in the league .

In December 2024, Ellis received a significant honor when he was named to Team Canada's roster for the 2024 Spengler Cup tournament in Davos, Switzerland [2]. He became the first active Springfield Thunderbirds player to participate in the prestigious tournament [17]. During the tournament, Ellis made 40 saves in Canada's 4-2 semifinal loss to Germany's Straubing Tigers .

### Buffalo Sabres

On October 5, 2025, Ellis was placed on waivers by the St. Louis Blues [7]. He was claimed by the Buffalo Sabres the following day and added to their 23-man roster [18].

Ellis made his highly anticipated NHL debut on October 22, 2025, against the Detroit Red Wings at KeyBank Center in Buffalo [19]. Playing in front of family members and his fiancée who were in attendance, Ellis stopped 27 of 29 shots to earn the victory and was named the first star of the game [20]. His performance helped lead the Sabres to a 4-2 victory .

Through 8 games with the Sabres during the 2025-26 season, Ellis has compiled a 4-3-0 record with a 3.25 GAA and a .895 save percentage . However, his season was interrupted when he was placed on injured reserve on December 11, 2025, after sustaining a concussion in a game against the Edmonton Oilers [21].

## International Career

Ellis has represented Team Canada at multiple international tournaments throughout his career. His first international experience came at the 2018 U18 World Junior Championship, where he appeared in 2 games for Team Canada [22]. In his first game, he made 31 saves in Canada's 6-4 victory over the United States [22].

Ellis also participated in the 2019 U20 World Summer Showcase with Team Canada . His most recent international appearance was at the 2024 Spengler Cup, where he was selected as one of two goaltenders for Team Canada [23]. The Spengler Cup is an annual international invitational hockey tournament held in Davos, Switzerland, and is often cited as the oldest international invitational ice hockey tournament in the world .

## Playing Style

Ellis is known for his athletic and aggressive style of play in goal. At 6'1" and 185 pounds, he is considered a smaller goaltender by today's standards, which forces him to rely heavily on his athleticism and quick movement . He is described as an "explosive butterfly goalie" who drops to the ice very quickly, allowing him to block low shots effectively [13].

His playing style is characterized by his willingness to come out of the crease to make stops or jump on loose pucks to prevent scoring chances . While this aggressive style has its benefits, it can also lead to him being out of position at times, allowing opponents to find open looks on the net . Ellis compensates for his smaller size with exceptional lateral movement and flexibility [13].

## Personal Life

Off the ice, Ellis maintains strong connections to his hometown of River Denys, Nova Scotia. Despite his hockey career taking him across North America, he remains grateful for the support he received from his community growing up . He has mentioned that the support from his hometown has been a significant source of motivation throughout his career .

Ellis is engaged to be married, and his fiancée was present at his NHL debut in Buffalo [19]. He has expressed excitement about starting this new chapter in his life while continuing to pursue his hockey career.

## Notability

Colten Ellis meets Wikipedia's notability standards for several reasons. As an NHL player, he has achieved the highest level of professional hockey . His selection in the 2019 NHL Entry Draft by the St. Louis Blues demonstrates his recognized talent [1].

Ellis has achieved significant success in junior hockey, winning multiple prestigious awards including the Jacques Plante Memorial Trophy and the Raymond Lagacé Trophy [10]. His statistical achievements in the QMJHL, including being second all-time in shutouts, further establish his notability [11].

His international experience, including representing Team Canada at the U18 World Junior Championship and the Spengler Cup, adds to his notability [22]. The Spengler Cup is particularly significant as it is one of the oldest and most prestigious international hockey tournaments .

Additionally, Ellis has received coverage from multiple reputable sports media outlets including [NHL.com](https://NHL.com), Sportsnet, and The Hockey Writers, further supporting his notability . His recent NHL debut and subsequent performance have generated additional media attention [20].
