# Fontaine (Genshin Impact)

## Lead Section

**Fontaine** is one of the seven nations of Teyvat in the action role-playing game **Genshin Impact** [1]. It is the nation that worshipped Focalors, the Hydro Archon and God of Justice [1]. Fontaine is known for its advanced technology, cultural achievements, and complex judicial system . The region serves as the setting for Archon Quest Chapter IV in the game [1].

Geographically, Fontaine is located in the northeastern part of Sumeru Desert [2]. It is characterized by its unique underwater exploration mechanics, with both surface and underwater regions to discover . The nation consists of multiple regions including the Court of Fontaine Region, Beryl Region, Belleau Region, and several others added through game updates .

The culture of Fontaine is deeply rooted in justice, theater, and technological innovation . The nation is renowned for its performing arts, with theater being described as essential to ordinary life . Fontaine is also home to The Steambird, the largest news publication in Teyvat [3].

Notable characters from Fontaine include Furina (the human form of Focalors), Neuvillette (the Chief Justice and Hydro Dragon Sovereign), and various playable characters introduced through game updates [4]. The region features a complex storyline involving a prophecy that foretells the dissolution of Fontainians in water [5].

## 1. Geography and Location

### 1.1 Physical Setting

Fontaine is positioned in the northeastern part of the Sumeru Desert in the game world of Teyvat [2]. The nation is described as a "sea on land" located at the center of Teyvat [6]. According to in-game descriptions, Fontaine is the source of all waters on the continent .

The landscape of Fontaine is characterized by its unique vertical structure. The nation features two distinct cities: one above ground where the wealthy reside, and one underground where people with poverty live . The main city contains the Court of Fontaine and several high-rise buildings, with mechanical guards called Clockwork Mekas roaming the area .

The region's geography includes waterfalls, high plateaus, and underwater areas [6]. The description from the official Genshin website states: "A story that has never been heard, a legend that has been forgotten, like a lost kingdom sunken beneath the waves, yearning for a bard to sing its drowned songs" .

### 1.2 Regional Divisions

Fontaine is divided into multiple regions, with new areas added through game updates [7]. The main regions include:



* **Court of Fontaine Region**: The central area containing the nation's judicial and cultural center [7]

* **Beryl Region**: One of the initial regions of Fontaine [7]

* **Belleau Region**: Located underwater, as indicated by its name

* **Liffey Region**: Added in Version 4.1, containing the Fontaine Research Institute and the Fortress of Meropide [7]

* **Erinnyes Forest**: Added in Version 4.2, part of the world quests [8]

* **Morte Region**: Added in Version 4.2 [8]

* **Nostoi Region**: Added in Version 4.6, located in the southern part of Fontaine [8]

* **Sea of Bygone Eras**: Added in Version 4.6, also in the southern part of Fontaine

### 1.3 Exploration Features

Fontaine introduces new exploration mechanics to Genshin Impact, particularly focused on underwater exploration . The region features a diving system that allows players to explore underwater landscapes . This includes discovering underwater ruins, ancient structures, and unique marine life.

The region contains numerous collectibles and exploration points, including:



* **38 Waypoints** for fast travel

* **4 Shrines** for domain access

* **24 Seelie** creatures to collect

* **240 Chests** containing various rewards

* **12 Time Trails** for challenges

In addition to the standard Hydroculus collectibles found in other regions, Fontaine features **271 Hydroculi** spread across both surface and underwater areas .

## 2. Culture and Society

### 2.1 Justice and Law

Justice is the central ideal of Fontaine [9]. The nation's judicial system is highly sophisticated and intertwined with its cultural practices . The Court of Justice in Fontaine is called the **Opera Epiclese**, which functions as both an opera house and the main courthouse [10].

Under the rule of Furina (the human form of Focalors), the citizens treated their judicial trials like performances [11]. Trials that concern people's lives are viewed as entertainment [3]. This unique approach to justice reflects Fontaine's cultural emphasis on theatricality and public spectacle.

The judicial system includes the **Oratrice Mecanique d'Analyse Cardinale**, a large mechanical weighing scale created by Focalors [12]. This device converts people's belief in justice into **indemnitium**, a power source used in the Court of Fontaine and Erinnyes [12].

### 2.2 Arts and Entertainment

Fontaine is renowned as the hub of culture and the arts in Teyvat . The nation's cultural identity is deeply rooted in performing arts, particularly music, theater, and opera . As stated in the game: "To say that you grew up watching the theater is no different from claiming that you were raised drinking water" .

The **Opera Epiclese** serves as the main venue for both judicial proceedings and artistic performances [10]. It hosts various shows including Lyney's magic shows [10]. Theater has been a component of Fontainian culture for at least 500 years, dating back to the time of the famed playwright Coppelius [11].

Fontaine also holds annual cultural events:



* **Iridescence Tour**: The nation's largest music festival, which is trying to expand to other regions

* **Fontinalia Film Festival**: Organized by the Fontaine Film Association to promote cinema

According to in-game information, rock 'n' roll music was brought to Liyue from Fontaine . Mystery novels such as "Great Detective Hurlock" and "The Case Files of Miss Orith" are also popular in the nation .

### 2.3 Media and Communication

**The Steambird** is the most important newspaper in Fontaine and the most popular publication in Teyvat [13]. It has enthusiastic readers throughout the world of Genshin Impact [13]. The newspaper serves as the primary news source for the nation and has correspondents across Teyvat.

Notable journalists associated with The Steambird include:



* **Euphrasie**: The chief editor of The Steambird, who has reportedly traveled with the Traveler multiple times [13]

* **Charlotte**: A Cryo character and journalist for The Steambird [13]

* **Mona Megistus**: An astrologer who contributes to the newspaper [13]

The Steambird has covered significant events in the game's storyline, including the Traveler's battle with Dvalin, which reportedly occupied the front pages for three weeks with full-page illustrations [13].

### 2.4 Technology and Innovation

Fontaine is known for its advanced engineering technology and mechanical innovations . The nation has developed sophisticated clockwork technology, particularly the **Clockwork Mekas**, which are autonomous machines powered by **Arkhe energy** .

The Clockwork Mekas are classified into several types:



* **Normal Meks**: Lower-tier machines with basic functions

* **Specialist Meks**: More advanced machines with greater capabilities

* **Boss Meks**: High-tier machines with powerful abilities

These machines are used for various purposes including:



* Surveillance and reconnaissance

* Construction and maintenance

* Combat and defense

* Underwater operations

The development of Clockwork Mekas relies on precision processing and advanced material science [14]. Engineers in Fontaine have created specialized Mekas for specific tasks, including geological surveying, arithmetic enhancement, and underwater exploration .

### 2.5 Economy and Trade

Fontaine's economy is supported by various industries including:



* **Technology manufacturing**: Production of Clockwork Mekas and other mechanical devices [14]

* **Tourism and entertainment**: The nation's cultural events and judicial spectacles attract visitors

* **Journalism and media**: The Steambird and other publications [13]

* **Mining and resource extraction**: Particularly from underwater sources

The nation also has diplomatic and trade relations with other regions. For example, Fontaine has cultural exchanges with Liyue, as evidenced by the introduction of rock 'n' roll music to Liyue from Fontaine .

### 3.2 Key Characters

**Furina (Furina de Fontaine)**: Born on October 13, Furina served as the human form of Focalors, the Hydro Archon . She acted as the figurehead of Fontaine for 500 years while Focalors' divine self remained hidden . After the events of Archon Quest Chapter IV, Furina was freed from her role and gained a Hydro Vision .

**Neuvillette**: The Chief Justice of Fontaine, known as the Iudex, Neuvillette is renowned for his unassailable impartiality [15]. He is revealed to be the reincarnated Hydro Dragon Sovereign . After Focalors' sacrifice, Neuvillette regained his power as the Hydro Dragon and became the ruler of Fontaine .

**Wriothesley**: The warden of the Fortress of Meropide, Wriothesley is responsible for maintaining order in the underwater prison . He has built his own ship, The Wingalet, as a precaution against the prophecy .

**Lynette and Lyney**: Twin siblings who are magicians performing in the Opera Epiclese [4]. They were introduced as playable characters in Version 4.0 of the game .

### 3.3 Supporting Characters

**Focalors**: The original Hydro Archon and God of Justice, Focalors created Furina as her human form and separated her divinity from her body . She hid her divine self within the Oratrice Mecanique d'Analyse Cardinale for 500 years while Furina acted as the public face of the Archon .

**Egeria**: The previous Hydro Archon who ruled Fontaine before the Cataclysm [16]. She was responsible for creating the Oceanids and granting them human forms using Primordial Seawater, which led to the creation of the prophecy [16].

**The Melusines**: Water spirit creatures that serve various roles in Fontaine . They are often seen working as shopkeepers, guides, and assistants throughout the nation.

## 4. Story and Lore

### 4.1 The Prophecy of Fontaine

The **Prophecy of Fontaine** is a central storyline element in the region [5]. It is an apocalyptic prophecy that foretells the dissolution of all Fontainians in water [5]. The prophecy states: "In the end, the people will all be dissolved into the waters, and only the Hydro Archon will remain, weeping on her throne" [16].

The prophecy originated from the actions of Egeria, the previous Hydro Archon . During her reign, many of her Oceanid subjects desired human lives . Egeria used the life-granting powers of the Primordial Sea to transform these Oceanids into humans with faux blood . However, this act was considered a violation of the Heavenly Principles, as Archons were not permitted to create humans without permission .

As punishment, Celestia marked all Fontainians with "original sin" and created the prophecy that they would one day dissolve back into the Primordial Sea . The prophecy was carved into Irminsul and could not be avoided [17].

### 4.2 Focalors' Plan and Sacrifice

After Egeria's death during the Cataclysm, Focalors became the new Hydro Archon . From the beginning of her duties, Focalors had a plan to deceive the Heavenly Principles . She created a human vessel, Furina, and separated her divinity from her body .

For 500 years, Furina acted as the public face of the Hydro Archon while Focalors' divine self remained hidden within the Oratrice Mecanique d'Analyse Cardinale . Using the trials that occurred in the Opera Epiclese, Focalors converted the faith of her people into **indemnitium** .

The true purpose of the Oratrice was to collect enough indemnitium to execute the Hydro Archon . When the prophecy finally came to pass, Focalors sentenced herself to death and destroyed the Divine Seat of Hydro . This act returned the power of the Hydro element to Neuvillette, the reincarnated Hydro Dragon Sovereign .

### 4.3 The Resolution of the Prophecy

During the events of Archon Quest Chapter IV, the prophecy appeared to be fulfilled when the waters of Fontaine rose and many people were dissolved . However, Neuvillette, with his newly regained power as the Hydro Dragon Sovereign, used the Primordial Water to convert the faux blood of the Fontainians into real blood .

This transformation made the people "true" humans and removed their "original sin" . While there was indeed a flood as the prophecy foretold, the people were no longer dissolved in water because they no longer had Primordial Seawater in their bodies . The waters eventually receded, and the nation was saved .

Furina was freed from her role as the Hydro Archon and was able to live a human life . During her story quest, she helped a theater troupe with their final performance and gained a Hydro Vision at the end of her performance .

### 4.4 Historical Background

Fontaine has a complex history that predates the current storyline [18]. The nation was ruled by Egeria before the Cataclysm, during which time she created the Oceanids and later transformed many of them into humans .

The **Fortress of Meropide** was built during Egeria's reign as a home for criminals who had been exiled from Fontaine . Egeria also used this fortress to guard a large opening into the Primordial Sea .

The ancient kingdom of **Remuria** is also connected to Fontaine's history [8]. According to legend, Remuria was a prosperous kingdom that was prophesied to be destroyed by its own justice [19]. The kingdom eventually sank into the Abyss .

### 4.5 Cultural References

The story of Fontaine contains references to several real-world concepts and myths :



* **Original sin**: The concept of Fontainians being born with sin that cannot be absolved

* **Flood myths**: The prophecy of a great flood that would wash away the sins of the people

* **Noah's Ark**: References to the legend of Remus and the Golden Bee, which inspired Wriothesley's ship The Wingalet

## 5. Gameplay Features

### 5.1 Exploration Mechanics

Fontaine introduces several new gameplay mechanics to Genshin Impact . The most significant innovation is the **underwater exploration system**, which allows players to dive and explore underwater environments . This mechanic includes:



* **Diving controls**: Special movement mechanics for underwater navigation

* **Underwater combat**: Unique combat mechanics for fighting underwater enemies

* **Hydroculi collection**: 271 Hydroculi spread across both surface and underwater areas

* **Underwater puzzles**: Environmental puzzles specifically designed for underwater exploration

The region also features vertical exploration with both surface and underground cities . Players can use the **Aquabus** to travel between different areas of Fontaine .

### 5.2 Enemies and Challenges

Fontaine introduces new enemy types, primarily the **Clockwork Mekas** . These mechanical enemies are powered by Arkhe energy and come in various types with different abilities .

Clockwork Mekas have two energy alignments:



* **Pneuma**: Indicated by bright white artificied clockwork

* **Ousia**: Indicated by dark blue artificied clockwork

The enemies use different abilities depending on their Arkhe alignment and deal elemental damage accordingly . Players must use opposite Arkhe attacks to weaken them .

Other notable enemies in Fontaine include:



* **Oceanid creatures**: Water spirits that serve various roles

* **Abyss creatures**: Including the All-Devouring Narwhal encountered during the prophecy events

### 5.3 Domains and Challenges

Fontaine features several domains and challenge areas:



* **Artifacts and material domains**: Dungeons that drop Fontaine-specific artifacts and ascension materials

* **Time Trial challenges**: 12 time-limited challenges scattered throughout the region

* **World quests**: Story-driven quests that reveal more about Fontaine's lore and characters

The region also includes various environmental puzzles and challenges that utilize the new underwater mechanics .

## 6. Development and Release

### 6.1 Introduction in the Game

Fontaine was officially introduced in **Version 4.0** of Genshin Impact [7]. The region was first teased in the game's storyline during Archon Quest Chapter III, when the Traveler and Nahida discussed the next nation they would visit .

The development of Fontaine was announced during a special livestream where the game's developers revealed the region's unique features, including underwater exploration and the dual-city structure . The region was designed to offer a different gameplay experience compared to previous nations, with an emphasis on vertical exploration and underwater mechanics .

### 6.2 Version Updates

Fontaine has been expanded through several game updates [7]:



* **Version 4.0** (September 2023): Initial release of Fontaine, including the Court of Fontaine Region, Beryl Region, and Belleau Region [7]

* **Version 4.1** (November 2023): Added the Liffey Region, including the Fortress of Meropide and Fontaine Research Institute [7]

* **Version 4.2** (December 2023): Added the Erinnyes Forest and Morte Region [7]

* **Version 4.6** (2024): Added the Nostoi Region and Sea of Bygone Eras

Each update has introduced new areas to explore, new characters to play as, and new story content related to Fontaine's lore .

### 6.3 Reception and Impact

Fontaine has been well-received by the Genshin Impact community for its unique gameplay mechanics and compelling storyline . The underwater exploration system has been particularly praised for adding a new dimension to the game's exploration .

The region's complex narrative, particularly the prophecy storyline and the characters of Focalors and Furina, has been noted for its emotional depth and narrative complexity . The introduction of new playable characters from Fontaine has also been popular with players .

## 7. Trivia



* Fontaine is the fifth nation to be introduced in Genshin Impact, following Mondstadt, Liyue, Inazuma, and Sumeru

* The region's name is derived from the French word for "fountain" or "spring," reflecting its water-based theme [9]

* The Clockwork Mekas are the first Automaton enemy group to consist of common enemies in the game

* The Oratrice Mecanique d'Analyse Cardinale is based on a mechanical scale, symbolizing justice [12]

* The nation's judicial system is inspired by French and European legal traditions

* The Steambird newspaper is named after a real bird species, the steam bird [13]

* Fontaine's architecture combines elements of Gothic, Baroque, and Industrial Revolution styles
