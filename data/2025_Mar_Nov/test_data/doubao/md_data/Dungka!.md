# Dungka!

"Dungka!" is a song by Filipino boy band **SB19**, released as the sixth track on their EP "**Simula at Wakas**" on April 25, 2025 [1]. The song is a stylized version of the Filipino phrase "**Doon ka!**" which is used to tell someone to move farther, get out, or go away [2]. It is described as an upbeat rave track with hyper-pop elements, characterized by its defiant energy and maximalist production [1].

## Background and Production

"Dungka!" was written and composed by **John Paulo Nase** and **Joshua Daniel Nase** [3]. The production was handled by the same duo under their production name "RADKIDZ" (Pablo and Josue), with additional production credits going to John Paulo Nase [4]. The mastering was done by Heo Chan-goo at Knob Sound Korea, with assistance from Oh Won Lee, and immersive mastering by Waxie Joaquin [4].

The song is part of SB19's third and final EP in their **Ikalawang Yugto trilogy**, which began with 2021's "Pagsibol" and continued with 2023's "PAGTATAG!" [5]. The seven-track EP "Simula at Wakas" showcases the group's evolution as artists and individuals, with "Dungka!" representing a return to their experimental phase .

## Music and Lyrics

Musically, "Dungka!" is characterized as a **rip-roaring riot of defiant energy** that blends rave and hyper-pop elements [6]. The song features SB19's signature wordplay, with "Dungka" playing on "do'n ka," essentially telling critics and online bashers to step aside if they don't appreciate the music [7].

The lyrics express themes of **self-empowerment** and **standing one's ground** against negative criticism. The song serves as a response to online bashing and uninvited negative comments that the group has experienced throughout their career . As described in reviews, the song's overall vibe can be characterized as "**loud but confident, aggressive but colorful**" .

## Music Video

The music video for "Dungka!" was released on April 25, 2025, directed by **Kerbs Balagtas** of YouMeUs MNL . The video was released as a surprise drop with no prior teaser or fanfare . It reflects the chaotic but vibrant world of urban Manila, serving as a dynamic tribute to individuality, community, and the unfiltered beauty of everyday life .

The music video features an extensive lineup of guest stars and cameos, including:



* **Vice Ganda** (host and comedian)

* **Maymay Entrata** (actress and singer)

* **Alodia Gosiengfiao** (cosplayer and entrepreneur)

* **Sassa Gurl** (vlogger and content creator)

* **Jayat** (gamer and entertainer)

* **Kween Yasmin** (internet personality and content creator)

* **Malupiton** (internet personality and content creator)

* **Ghost Wrecker** (gamer and entertainer)

* **Shehyee** and **Smugglaz** (rappers)

* **Pat Lasaten** and **Agnes Reoma** (Ben\&Ben's keyboardist and bassist)

* **Mimiyuuuh** (vlogger and content creator)

The video concept was developed to mirror the track's chaotic energy through a visual celebration of diversity and self-expression . SB19 explained that their idea was to highlight the group's diversity and showcase each member's unique personality, which grew into something bigger, bringing in people from all walks of life to reflect the extraordinariness in differences .

## Reception and Impact

"Dungka!" has achieved significant commercial success and critical acclaim since its release. The song maintained the top spot of trending YouTube videos under the music category upon its release . It has been recognized with multiple awards, including:



* **Song of the Year** at the 10th P-pop Music Awards [8]

* **Pop Song of the Year** at the 首届菲律宾音乐奖 (First Philippine Music Awards) [9]

* **People's Choice Song** at the 首届菲律宾音乐奖

The song has been praised for its cultural significance and celebration of Filipino identity. Critics have noted how the music video bursts with authentic Filipino colors, dances, and traditions, describing it as "like a joyful fiesta on screen" . The song is seen as representing the group's response to online criticism while maintaining their artistic integrity and cultural roots .

## Cultural Significance

Beyond its commercial success, "Dungka!" holds cultural significance as a representation of **Filipino street culture, community spirit, and national pride** [10]. The song's message of empowerment and standing up against negativity resonates with Filipino audiences, particularly in the context of online discourse and social media culture [11].

The music video's inclusion of various Filipino personalities from different backgrounds—including celebrities, content creators, musicians, and internet personalities—symbolizes unity and collective action against negativity . As SB19 stated in a collective statement: "They're people who also want to speak up, who want change, who want to be part of a movement that challenges a flawed system" .

## Legacy

"Dungka!" stands as a significant milestone in SB19's career, marking their artistic evolution while staying true to their Filipino roots. The song has become an anthem for empowerment and self-expression, particularly among the younger generation [6]. It is considered one of the highlights of the "Simula at Wakas" EP, which serves as a culmination of a chapter in SB19's journey .

The song's success has further cemented SB19's position as one of the leading forces in contemporary pop music in the Philippines and Southeast Asia . It has been performed in various concerts and events, including the "Simula at Wakas" World Tour, which began with sold-out shows at the Philippine Arena in May 2025 .
