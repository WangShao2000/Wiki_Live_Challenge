# Jimmy Essex

Jimmy Essex is a British actor, dancer, and musician known for his role as Adam Donovan in the Channel 4 soap opera Hollyoaks [1]. He has also appeared in television series such as The Sandman and films including A Dark Path [2].

## Early life and education

Jimmy Essex was born on November 18, 1984, in England [3]. Some sources indicate he is from Harrow, London . He studied Musical Theatre at The Urdang Academy, graduating in 2009 . As part of his training, he developed skills as a dancer and also received stage combat training .

## Career

Essex began his career in entertainment with appearances in music videos and commercials [4]. In 2014, he appeared as a male dancer in several productions from the Mr. Bowler Presents series, including Hideaway, Pitiful, and Sacrifice .

His breakthrough role came in 2016 when he joined the cast of the Channel 4 soap opera Hollyoaks as Adam Donovan [3]. He remained in the role until his character's death in May 2018, appearing in 159 episodes [1]. According to the Hollyoaks fandom wiki, Hollyoaks was his only acting role at that time [1].

Following his time on Hollyoaks, Essex continued to build his acting portfolio with roles in television and film. In 2019, he appeared in the miniseries Trail Blazers . In 2020, he played the character Peter in the film A Dark Path . More recently, he had a role as Carrion in two episodes of the Netflix series The Sandman in 2022 [2].

Essex has also maintained an active career in theatre. He appeared in the play GH Boy at Charing Cross Theatre in 2020 [5]. In 2025, he is set to star alongside Billy Walker in the play Happy Ending by Ronnie Larsen at Waterloo East Theatre from September 27 to October 26 [6].

## Personal life

Essex is openly gay . He has been in a long-term relationship with Charles since 2013 . The couple got engaged on January 21, 2019, during a holiday in Sri Lanka . Charles is a musician .

He has a sister named Olivia, who is married and has a son . Essex maintains a healthy relationship with his family, having wished his father a happy birthday and his mother a happy mother's day via social media posts in 2016 and 2017 respectively .

Essex has built a significant social media presence, with his Instagram account having between 110,000 and 150,000 followers .
