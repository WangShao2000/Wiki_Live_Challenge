# Good Boy (2025 Leonberg film)

**Good Boy** is a 2025 American supernatural horror film directed by Ben Leonberg in his feature debut, co-written with Alex Cannon, and starring Leonberg's own Nova Scotia Duck Tolling Retriever, Indy, in the lead role . The film follows a loyal dog named Indy who moves with his owner to a rural family home and discovers supernatural forces lurking in the shadows [1]. It premiered at the 2025 South by Southwest Film Festival and received a wide theatrical release in the United States on October 3, 2025 [2].

The film has received significant critical acclaim, holding a 95% approval rating on Rotten Tomatoes based on 20 reviews, with critics calling it "one of the best horror movies so far this year" [3]. It has also been recognized with multiple awards and nominations, including wins at the Boston Society of Film Critics Awards and SXSW Film Festival .

## Plot

Good Boy follows Indy, a loyal Nova Scotia Duck Tolling Retriever, who accompanies his owner Todd (Shane Jensen) as they move from city life to a long-vacant family home in the countryside . From the start, Indy senses something is wrong with the house, though Todd remains oblivious to the supernatural forces at play .

As Indy explores his new surroundings, he becomes increasingly troubled by empty corners and detects an invisible presence that only he can perceive . The dog begins to experience visions and warnings from a long-dead dog that previously inhabited the house, as well as haunting images of the previous occupant's gruesome death . These paranormal encounters intensify as Todd's health mysteriously deteriorates from an unspecified lung illness [4].

As dark entities threaten Todd, Indy must summon his courage to protect his beloved owner from malevolent forces intent on dragging Todd into the afterlife [1]. The film's narrative is presented entirely from Indy's perspective, utilizing low-angle cinematography to maintain the dog's point of view throughout most of the film .

## Production

### Development and writing

Ben Leonberg conceived the idea for Good Boy after watching the horror classic *Poltergeist* for the hundredth time . He noticed that in many horror films, dogs often sense supernatural activity before humans do, and he wondered why no one had made a horror movie from a dog's perspective . This observation, combined with his ownership of Indy, led to the development of the film's concept.

Leonberg and Alex Cannon, who had been developing the project for over four years, co-wrote the screenplay . The writing process required stripping away many conventional dog movie tropes, particularly avoiding anthropomorphization of the canine protagonist . Leonberg was influenced by Jack London's approach to writing dog characters, aiming to portray Indy as driven by simple instinct and basic reasoning rather than abstract human thought .

### Casting and filming

The casting of Indy was unique in that he was Leonberg's own pet dog, whom the director had known since puppyhood . Indy had no formal acting training, couldn't hit marks, and was unaware of the camera's presence . However, he possessed a natural ability to stare intently without blinking, which Leonberg utilized as a powerful cinematic tool .

Filming took place over three years in Leonberg's own spooky-looking home outside the city . The extended production timeline allowed the filmmakers to observe and incorporate Indy's natural behaviors into the story . The cinematography was specifically designed to maintain a dog's-eye view, with most shots filmed from waist level or lower . This technical approach presented unique challenges, such as finding furniture large enough for both Indy and the camera crew to fit under .

The human cast includes Shane Jensen as Todd, Larry Fessenden in a cameo as Todd's late grandfather, Arielle Friedman as Vera, Anya Krawcheck as a doctor, and Stuart Rudin as Richard . Most human characters are seen only from a distance or from Indy's low-angle perspective, with their faces often out of frame or focus .

### Technical aspects

Good Boy has a runtime of 72 minutes (1 hour 12 minutes) [5]. The film was shot in English with a color palette and sound mix in D-Cinema 48kHz 5.1 format . The aspect ratio is 2.00:1 .

The film's sound design and music were crucial in creating the horror atmosphere, as Leonberg noted: "When you take out my talking on set, which is profoundly unscary, and replace it with all the amazing sound design and music, the shot of a dog reacting to that strange empty corner, it looks like a performance" .

## Release

Good Boy premiered at the 2025 South by Southwest Film Festival on March 8, 2025, in the Midnighter section [6]. The film then screened at multiple other festivals, including the Overlook Film Festival in New Orleans in April 2025, Melbourne International Film Festival, Film at Lincoln Center, Calgary Underground Film Festival, and others .

Initially, IFC Films planned a limited theatrical release for the film . However, following the viral success of the official trailer, which garnered over 2.2 million views on YouTube in one week and over 14 million views on Twitter, the distributor changed plans to a wide release . The trailer's popularity was attributed to Indy's adorable appearance and the film's unique premise .

The film was released in the United States on October 3, 2025 [7]. In the United Kingdom and Ireland, it was acquired by Vertigo Releasing and released on October 10, 2025 . The film is distributed by IFC Films in North America and Shudder internationally .

## Reception

### Critical response

Good Boy has received overwhelmingly positive reviews from critics. On Rotten Tomatoes, the film holds a 95% approval rating based on 20 reviews, with an average rating of 7.6/10 [3]. The site's consensus describes it as "a charming twist on the haunted house movie with a soulful performance from the pup at its center" .

Critics have particularly praised the film's originality and execution. Rafael Mota Mayor of IndieWire described it as "one of the best horror films so far this year" . The San Francisco Chronicle called it "horror at its best, because it makes you look at life differently" [8]. The Guardian praised Leonberg's craftsmanship, noting: "The craftsmanship on display here confirms that Leonberg possesses what many other, lazier horror directors who rely solely on jump scares currently lack: he dares to take on a considerably ambitious storyline and technical challenge, and largely succeeds" [8].

Reviewers have consistently singled out Indy's performance for praise. Kim Newman of Sight & Sound wrote: "It's a good, quiet little horror film – a study in creeping dread rather than shocks – but it's also a thoughtful study of the bond between man and animal" [9]. Rob Gonsalves noted: "Feels like a project that warrants applause for having been accomplished at all, and expecting some of the more challenging technical spots to be more clearly rendered, or expecting Indy to have the range of a trained dog actor, sounds a little churlish" [10].

### Box office performance

As of the film's release, Good Boy has grossed $6,147,805 in the United States and Canada, with an opening weekend of $2,343,694 . The worldwide total stands at \$6,299,172 . Given its low budget and independent production status, these figures represent a successful commercial performance.

### Awards and nominations

Good Boy has received multiple awards and nominations recognizing both its artistic merit and Indy's performance. At the Boston Society of Film Critics Awards, Indy won the Best In Show (Best Animal Performance) award . At the SXSW Film Festival, Indy received the inaugural Howl of Fame Award, created specifically for the film [11].

## Analysis and interpretation

Good Boy represents a significant innovation in the horror genre by telling a ghost story entirely from a dog's perspective [11]. This narrative approach allows viewers to experience the supernatural through heightened canine senses, creating a unique and immersive horror experience . The film's technical achievements include its consistent low-angle cinematography and sound design that effectively conveys the dog's perceptual experience .

The film has been interpreted as both a horror story and a thoughtful exploration of the bond between humans and animals [9]. The relationship between Indy and Todd is portrayed as one of unconditional loyalty, with Indy's inability to communicate the danger he perceives adding to the film's tension and emotional impact [11].

Critics have noted the film's minimalist approach to horror, focusing on creeping dread rather than jump scares [9]. This style, combined with Indy's natural performance, creates what some reviewers have described as a profound and moving viewing experience .

## Legacy and impact

Good Boy has generated significant interest in the horror community and beyond, with its trailer becoming a viral phenomenon . The film's success has highlighted the potential for innovative approaches to horror storytelling and has drawn attention to the capabilities of animal actors in leading roles [12].

The film's technical achievements, particularly in filming from a dog's perspective while maintaining narrative coherence, have been noted as groundbreaking in independent cinema [11]. Leonberg's creative use of his own pet and the three-year filming process demonstrate a unique approach to filmmaking that has inspired other independent filmmakers .

Good Boy has also sparked discussions about the role of animals in horror films and the potential for new narrative perspectives in the genre [11]. The film's success has led to increased interest in similar projects that explore unconventional viewpoints and the human-animal bond .
