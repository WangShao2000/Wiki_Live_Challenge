# Honey (Fighting Vipers)

**Honey** (Japanese: ハニー，Hepburn: Hanī), also known as **Candy** in North American and PAL localizations, is a fictional character from Sega's Fighting Vipers series of fighting games [1]. Introduced as a playable character in the original *Fighting Vipers* arcade game in 1995, developed by Sega's AM2 division, Honey is a 16-year-old girl (18 in *Fighting Vipers 2*) who dreams of becoming an apparel designer . She enters the Viper tournaments wearing self-made rubber armor to showcase her fashion designs while competing in martial arts combat [2].

The character was created by Sega AM2 character designer **Masahiro Sugiyama**, who conceived her as a wealthy young girl and "cute" counterpart to the game's other female characters [1]. Honey's distinctive appearance features a red rubber dress with heart-shaped breastplate, large bulbous pauldrons on each shoulder, and fairy-like wings on her back . Her fighting style incorporates cat-like movements and attacks, including signature moves such as "Cat Punch" and "Bootie Bop" .

Despite the Fighting Vipers series having limited commercial success outside Japan, Honey has become the franchise's most recognizable character [3]. She has gained significant popularity in the cosplay community, particularly in Japan, and has appeared in various Sega crossover titles, most notably as the anthropomorphic "Honey the Cat" in *Sonic the Fighters* .

## Development and Design

Honey was designed by **Masahiro Sugiyama**, a character designer at Sega AM2, who created her as part of the original eight-character roster for *Fighting Vipers* [1]. According to design documentation, her costume was specifically engineered to create visual interest through its unconventional materials and proportions . Her dress extends halfway up her chest with a prominent heart-shaped breastplate on the front, while each shoulder features large bulbous pauldrons that were added to make her small frame appear larger . The outfit is completed with fairy-like wings on her back, which would later become iconic elements of her character design .

The character's fighting style was designed to complement her personality and appearance. Sugiyama incorporated numerous cat-themed attacks into her moveset, including "Cat Snap," "Cat Scratch," and "Cat Upper," as well as the signature "Bootie Bop" attack . These moves were designed to reflect both her cute, playful personality and her unconventional fighting approach. Her fighting style is classified as specializing in jumping attacks, which allows her to utilize her agility and acrobatic abilities during combat .

During the localization process for Western markets, Honey's name was changed to "Candy" for reasons that remain unclear in official documentation [1]. This name change was implemented in all North American and PAL releases of the original *Fighting Vipers*, though the character would later revert to her original name "Honey" in the European Dreamcast release of *Fighting Vipers 2* . Interestingly, promotional materials for the game included early concept art showing Honey with a different hairstyle and without the fairy wings that would become her trademark feature .

## Appearances

### Fighting Vipers

In the original *Fighting Vipers* (1995), Honey is introduced as a 16-year-old fashion design student who enters the Viper tournament to promote her original fashion line . Her character bio describes her as typically shy and quiet, but when she puts on her self-made rubber dress, she becomes more confident and assertive . In the game's storyline, she views the tournament as an opportunity to showcase her 服装设计 talents while demonstrating her martial arts skills .

Honey's appearance in the original game features her 标志性 red rubber armor, which covers most of her body except for her midriff and limbs. The armor includes knee-high red boots, black stockings, and black gloves designed to resemble cat paws [4]). Her hair is styled in two large pigtails, and she wears a red headband with decorative hair barrettes . According to character statistics, she stands at 158 cm (5'3") tall and weighs 41 kg (90 lbs) in the original game .

### Fighting Vipers 2

In *Fighting Vipers 2* (1997), Honey returns as a playable character at age 18, now working as a trainee stylist . Her character bio is expanded to describe her as a girl who dreams of becoming an apparel designer, and even the Viper Hunt (a government crackdown on the Vipers) is seen as an opportunity to showcase her latest designs [2]. The game's storyline emphasizes her determination to pursue her fashion dreams regardless of external obstacles .

In *Fighting Vipers 2*, Honey's appearance undergoes some changes. She now wears a dark blue dress with a red apron over it, though she still retains elements of her original rubber armor design [4]). Her height increases slightly to 160 cm, while her weight is listed as unknown in the game's documentation . The game introduces new moves to her repertoire, including "Shoot the Moon" as her super knockout attack, which can only be performed when she has no armor remaining .

### Other Appearances

Honey has made several notable appearances outside the main Fighting Vipers series. She appears as a playable character in *Fighters Megamix* (1996), a crossover fighting game featuring characters from both *Fighting Vipers* and *Virtua Fighter* . In this title, she retains her original moveset from *Fighting Vipers* while being presented in a more polished visual style.

Her most significant crossover appearance came through an unusual development story. During the development of *Fighting Vipers*, character designer Masahiro Sugiyama reportedly added Sonic the Hedgehog and Tails as joke characters, possibly as a form of boredom relief [3]. This experimentation eventually led to the creation of *Sonic the Fighters* (1996), with Honey being reimagined as the anthropomorphic "Honey the Cat" [5]).

The character of Honey the Cat was initially cut from the arcade version of *Sonic the Fighters* but remained in the game's data . She was discovered years later through hacking, and due to fan demand, was officially restored as a playable character in the 2012 PlayStation 3 and Xbox 360 re-releases of the game . In the Sonic universe, Honey the Cat is portrayed as the founder, CEO, and designer of Honey Brand Clothes and Accessories .

Honey has also appeared in various other Sega properties. She is featured in the crossover tactical RPG *Project X Zone* (2012) , and has made cameo appearances in titles such as *Judgment* (2018) and *Lost Judgment* (2021), where the original *Fighting Vipers* arcade cabinet can be found in-game . Additionally, she appears as a collectible item in the browser game *Earth UFO Catcher* (2020) .

## Reception and Legacy

### Critical Reception

Honey's reception among critics and players has been notably mixed. While some gaming publications praised her distinctive design and gameplay style, others criticized her as an example of female character sexualization in video games [4]). A 1995 review in *Next Generation* magazine described *Fighting Vipers* as "a beautiful, highly polished, polygon-rendered and texture-mapped game featuring brand new characters," though it did not specifically mention Honey in its analysis [6].

The character's international name change from "Honey" to "Candy" in Western markets generated some discussion among fans and critics. Some speculated that the change was made to make the character more marketable to Western audiences, while others suggested it was due to potential trademark issues [7]. The graffiti in Honey's home stage Bayside, which reads "In Memory of Honey you will always be rememberd by your family and friends R.I.P," has been interpreted by some as a reference to this name change, symbolically representing the "death" of the Honey name in Western markets .

### Popularity and Cultural Impact

Despite mixed critical reception, Honey has become the most recognizable character from the Fighting Vipers series [3]. Her popularity is particularly pronounced in Japan, where she has become a staple of the cosplay community [8]. Archive footage from a 1995 Sega Fighting Vipers cosplay tournament at Tokyo's Roppongi GIGO center shows that nearly all participants chose to cosplay as Honey rather than other characters from the game [9].

The character's appeal extends beyond traditional gaming circles. She has been the subject of numerous fan works, including doujinshi (fan comics) and fan art . A Japanese store even sells replica costumes of Honey's outfit alongside those of other popular gaming characters . Her distinctive appearance, featuring the rubber armor and fairy wings, has made her particularly popular among cosplayers who appreciate the challenge of recreating her unique costume design [8].

### Legacy in Gaming

Honey's legacy in gaming extends far beyond her appearances in the Fighting Vipers series. Her transformation into Honey the Cat for *Sonic the Fighters* created an entirely new character who has found success in the Sonic the Hedgehog franchise . This character was eventually incorporated into the Archie Comics and IDW Sonic comic series, where she has appeared as a recurring character .

The character's influence can also be seen in later Sega titles. In the 2002 GameCube port of *Beach Spikers*, entering the name "FVIPERS" unlocks customization options based on Honey's appearance from *Fighting Vipers 2* . This Easter egg demonstrates the lasting impact of the character within Sega's development culture.

Furthermore, Honey's design has influenced character creation in other fighting games. Her combination of cute character design with unconventional fighting style has been cited as an inspiration for several later female fighting game characters . Her signature "Bootie Bop" move has even become something of a meme within the fighting game community .

## Trivia



* Honey's birthday is September 3, making her a Virgo .

* In the original Japanese version of *Fighting Vipers*, Honey's weight is listed as a secret, following a traditional practice in Japanese fighting games . However, this secret was revealed in the North American and European releases, where her weight is listed as 90 lbs (41 kg) .

* There is a rumor in the *Fighting Vipers 2* storyline that Honey is in love with fellow fighter Sanman, though this is presented as speculation rather than confirmed fact .

* Honey has several secret unlockable variations in different versions of the games, including "Aloha Honey" (Honey with a ukulele) and "Uniform Honey" (Honey in a school uniform) in some releases .

* The character's distinctive fairy wings on her back are part of the rubber dress she made herself, according to her character backstory .

* Honey's fighting style includes several cat-themed attacks, which may have influenced the decision to make her a cat in *Sonic the Fighters* .

* In the 2012 re-release of *Sonic the Fighters*, players can unlock Honey by pressing the Start button over Amy Rose's portrait on the character select screen .
