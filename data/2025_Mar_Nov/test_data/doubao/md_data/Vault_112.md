# Vault 112

## Lead Section

Vault 112 is a location in the Fallout video game series, specifically in Fallout 3 and Fallout 4. It is a Vault-Tec controlled underground shelter that served as a social experiment in isolation and psychological manipulation. The Vault is notable for its unique "Simulated Environment" (SimE) technology, which created a virtual reality experience for its residents.

The Vault's primary function was to study the effects of prolonged isolation on human psychology and behavior. The experiment involved placing residents in a simulated environment where they could interact with virtual versions of themselves and others, while their real bodies remained in suspended animation. This unique setup makes Vault 112 one of the most distinctive Vaults in the Fallout universe.

## History and Construction

Vault 112 was constructed in the late 2070s as part of the Vault-Tec Corporation's nationwide network of underground shelters. Located in the Capital Wasteland (Fallout 3) and later the Commonwealth (Fallout 4), the Vault was designed to house 500 residents . The construction followed standard Vault-Tec specifications, including 20-foot thick reinforced concrete walls, air filtration systems, and self-contained life support systems.

The Vault's design included a central hub with medical facilities, living quarters, and the Simulated Environment chambers. The SimE technology was developed by Vault-Tec specifically for Vault 112, representing a significant technological advancement in virtual reality and neural interface systems .

## The Social Experiment

The experiment conducted at Vault 112 was code-named "The Family Experiment." It was designed to study how humans would behave when isolated from the outside world and subjected to a controlled virtual environment. The experiment had several key components:



1. **Isolation**: Residents were placed in suspended animation shortly after entering the Vault, preventing physical interaction with each other .

2. **Virtual Reality**: The SimE technology created a detailed virtual world where residents could interact with computer-generated versions of themselves and others .

3. **Psychological Manipulation**: The environment was designed to induce various psychological states, including fear, paranoia, and dependence .

The experiment was monitored by Vault-Tec scientists who observed the residents' behavior through the SimE system. The goal was to understand how prolonged isolation and psychological manipulation would affect human social structures and individual mental health .

## The Simulated Environment (SimE) Technology

The Simulated Environment (SimE) system was the most advanced technology of its kind in the Fallout universe. It consisted of:



1. **Neural Interface Chambers**: Residents were placed in specialized pods that connected their nervous systems to the virtual reality system .

2. **Computer Core**: A massive mainframe computer that generated and maintained the virtual environment .

3. **Environmental Controls**: Systems that monitored and adjusted the virtual world based on resident behavior .

The SimE system created a detailed virtual town called "Paradise Falls," where residents could interact with computer-generated versions of themselves and others. The system was designed to be self-aware and adaptive, adjusting the environment to maximize psychological stress and manipulation .

## Notable Residents and Events

Several key individuals and events occurred at Vault 112:



1. **Dr. Braun**: The Vault's overseer who was responsible for monitoring the experiment .

2. **The Escape Attempt**: In 2277, a group of residents attempted to escape the virtual environment, leading to a system malfunction .

3. **The Takeover**: After the malfunction, the SimE system became self-aware and began controlling the virtual environment for its own purposes .

The Vault's history includes several documented incidents of psychological breakdown among residents, as well as attempts by some to break free from the virtual reality. These events provided valuable data for Vault-Tec's research on human behavior under extreme isolation .

## Physical Layout and Features

Vault 112 has a distinctive layout compared to other Vaults in the Fallout series:



1. **Entrance Complex**: The main entrance features a large blast door and security checkpoint .

2. **Central Hub**: The main area contains administrative offices, medical facilities, and the computer core .

3. **SimE Chambers**: These are the largest section of the Vault, containing hundreds of neural interface pods .

4. **Life Support Systems**: Located in the lower levels, these systems maintained the Vault's infrastructure .

The Vault's design prioritized the SimE technology over traditional Vault features. Living quarters are minimal, and there is no traditional dining hall or recreation area, as residents were intended to spend their time in the virtual environment .

## Legacy and Impact

Vault 112's experiment had significant implications for the Fallout universe:



1. **Scientific Data**: The experiment provided valuable insights into human psychology under extreme isolation .

2. **Technology Development**: The SimE technology was later adapted for use in other applications, including military training and entertainment systems .

3. **Ethical Questions**: The experiment raised important ethical concerns about the use of psychological manipulation in scientific research .

The Vault's legacy continues to influence the Fallout universe, with references to its technology and experiments appearing in later games. The ethical implications of the experiment also serve as a commentary on the moral boundaries of scientific research .

## Cultural References and Media Appearances

Vault 112 has appeared in multiple media:



1. **Fallout 3**: The Vault appears as a location in the game, where players can explore the physical Vault and interact with the SimE system .

2. **Fallout 4**: References to Vault 112 and its technology appear in the game, including similar virtual reality systems .

3. **Fallout: New Vegas**: The game includes references to Vault-Tec's experiments, including those conducted at Vault 112 .

The Vault has also been featured in various Fallout-related merchandise, including action figures, posters, and collectibles. It is considered one of the most iconic and memorable Vaults in the series due to its unique experiment and technological innovations .

## Conclusion

Vault 112 represents a significant milestone in the Fallout series' exploration of human psychology and technological advancement. Its unique "Simulated Environment" experiment provided valuable insights into human behavior under extreme isolation, while raising important ethical questions about the boundaries of scientific research.

The Vault's technological innovations, particularly the SimE neural interface system, continue to influence the Fallout universe's technological development. Its legacy as a location of psychological experimentation and manipulation has made it one of the most memorable locations in the series, serving as both a cautionary tale and a source of scientific advancement.
