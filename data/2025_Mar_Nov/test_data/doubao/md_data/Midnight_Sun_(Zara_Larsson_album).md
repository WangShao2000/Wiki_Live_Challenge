# Midnight Sun (Zara Larsson album)

## 1. Introduction and Background

**Midnight Sun** is the fifth studio album by Swedish singer and songwriter **Zara Larsson**, released on **September 26, 2025** through Sommer House and Epic Records [1]. The album marks a significant artistic milestone for Larsson, representing what she describes as "the first proper album roll-out that I've ever had," contrasting it with her earlier work where her first album "So Good" was "just a collection of singles that I dropped over a period of time" .

The album derives its name from the natural phenomenon native to Larsson's Scandinavian homeland, where the sun refuses to set and golden hour stretches into eternity . Beyond the celestial reference, Midnight Sun explores themes of **self-devotion, joy, and presence**, with Larsson explaining that it's "really just about my love for life and being alive" .

Produced exclusively with a core team of collaborators including **MNEK**, **Margo XS**, **Zhone**, and songwriter **Helena Gao**, the album carries a unified sonic identity described as "synthetic but unstable" [1]. This collaborative approach resulted in what Larsson considers her most cohesive body of work to date .

## 2. Album Production and Development

### 2.1 Recording Process and Creative Team

Midnight Sun was recorded over a period of one year with a single, dedicated team of producers and songwriters [1]. Larsson worked closely with **MNEK**, her longtime collaborator since their work on "Never Forget You," alongside producers Margo XS and Zhone, and songwriter Helena Gao [1]. This focused approach allowed for what Larsson describes as "a concrete plan and vision on how I want to do things" .

The production team created what Larsson calls "a package of sounds that we would use in all of the songs," resulting in a unified sonic identity throughout the album . The album's palette is characterized as "synthetic but unstable," featuring elements such as "house piano warped like heat rising off asphalt" and "percussion that jitters and collapses in on itself" .

### 2.2 Concept and Musical Direction

The album's concept draws heavily from Larsson's Scandinavian roots and her personal journey through nearly 20 years in the public eye . "I get to just capture that total Scandinavian vibe, which is something that I have grown up with—it's a huge part of me, my happiest memories and my saddest ones, too. A part of my soul is a Swedish summer night. This record encompasses that," Larsson stated .

Musically, Midnight Sun blends **kaleidoscopic pop and euphoric dance** elements that complement Larsson's various moods . The album showcases all sides of the 27-year-old artist—"lovestruck, wistful, ambitious, cocky, flippant, and uncertain, often in the same breath" . This emotional range is particularly evident in tracks like "The Ambition," which explores the double-edged nature of drive and the pressures of success, and "Saturn's Return," where Larsson reflects on personal growth and self-discovery .

### 3.1 Singles and Key Tracks

**"Pretty Ugly"** served as the album's lead single, released before the album and earning a nomination for **Best Choreography** at the 2025 MTV Video Music Awards . The Charlotte Rutherford-directed, Zoi Tatopoulos-choreographed video was recognized for its innovative dance sequences .

**"Midnight Sun"**, the title track, was released as the second single on June 13, 2025 . The song peaked at number four in Sweden, becoming Larsson's twentieth top-ten entry in her home country . The track later earned a nomination for **Best Dance Pop Recording** at the 68th Grammy Awards, marking Larsson's first Grammy nomination [3].

**"Crush"** was released as the third single on August 15, 2025 . It dominated U.S. pop radio as the **#1 most added track** and became one of the most-added songs at US Top 40 radio for six consecutive weeks . The song is currently Top 25 on the Mediabase Pop chart and pushing Top 10 on the Mediabase Dance Radio chart .

### 3.2 Notable Tracks

Several tracks stand out for their distinctive characteristics and themes:

**"Blue Moon"** is described as "an anthem for the hopeless romantics set to warm tones and heart-racing drum 'n' bass rhythms—a celebration of timeless love" .

**"Hot & Sexy"** represents a genre-bending track that blends "80s electro, 90s house, Y2K pop, and 2010s bass music" and samples Tiffany Pollard's iconic Big Brother rant . The song evolves into a hyperpop fever dream with lyrics that explore autonomy .

**"The Ambition"** and **"Saturn's Return"** form an introspective duo that highlights the album's raw honesty. "The Ambition" explores the double-edged nature of drive and the pressures of success in modern times, while "Saturn's Return" finds Larsson reflecting on personal growth and self-discovery, embracing uncertainty with a deeper sense of self .

## 4. Commercial Performance

### 4.1 Chart Performance

Midnight Sun achieved significant commercial success upon its release. In the United States, the album debuted at **#3 on the Billboard Top Dance Albums chart**, marking a career-best debut for Larsson [4]. It also reached **#22 on the Billboard Top Album Sales chart** and **#7 on Luminate's Current Pop Albums chart** [4]. Additionally, the album landed at **#10 on Internet Albums** and within the Top 20 on the Digital Albums chart .

On the broader Billboard 200 chart, Midnight Sun debuted at **#119** . Internationally, the album reached **#36 on the UK Albums Chart** .

### 4.2 Streaming and Certification Milestones

Since its release, Zara Larsson has achieved several new milestones, surpassing **10.5 billion streams** across her catalog . The RIAA (Recording Industry Association of America) has awarded new certifications to several of Larsson's songs, including:



* 4x Platinum for "Never Forget You"

* 3x Platinum for "Lush Life"

* Gold for "Wow," "I Would Like," "All The Time," and "I Can't Fall In Love Without You"

## 5. Critical Reception

### 5.1 Review Aggregations

Midnight Sun has received widespread critical acclaim. According to review aggregator **Metacritic**, the album received a score of **89 out of 100** based on 4 critic reviews, indicating "Universal Acclaim" [5]. The review aggregator site Any Decent Music? also compiled positive reviews .

### 5.2 Professional Reviews

Leading music publications have praised Midnight Sun as a career-defining statement:

**DIY Magazine** awarded the album 90/100, stating: "It's comprehensive in its variations of electronica, dance, Scandi-pop and R\&B, creating a colourful body of work. Its forward-thinking, lush alive-ness cements Zara as a largely-overlooked genre VIP" .

**AllMusic** also gave 90/100, noting: "Refreshing honesty and reflective insight might not be as exciting as the immediate pop bangers, but they help elevate Midnight Sun to something more refined and graceful. Short and sweet, this catalog highlight benefits from its length, offering just enough of what Larsson does best to have listeners craving more of this endless bliss" .

**Clash Music** rated it 80/100, observing: "Ultimately, 'Midnight Sun' is Zara Larsson honing in on what she does best with laser focus: starry-eyed, joyous Scandi-pop built to ignite dancefloors as easily as festival sing-alongs" .

**Rolling Stone** also gave 80/100, stating: "From start to finish, Larsson's latest refuses to ever slow down, instead trafficking in high-energy dance-pop joy" .

### 5.3 Media Praise

Beyond traditional reviews, Midnight Sun has received significant praise from various media outlets:

**Rolling Stone** called the album Larsson's "most radiant body of work" and noted that "you can hear \[Zara] coming into her own" .

**Vogue** hailed it as "the moment when Larsson is finally coming into view as pop's main girl" .

**The FADER** described the album as "a testament to the uncanny moment the Swedish pop star has found herself in" .

**Nylon** praised the album for "show\[ing] Larsson flexing her creative and vocal muscles like never before" .

**W** highlighted the album's "glistening dance pop," while **Clash** characterized it as "Zara Larsson honing in on what she does best" .

**Pitchfork** summed up the viral nature of the title track with: "'Midnight Sun' is simply too much my shit!" .

## 6. Awards and Nominations

### 6.1 Grammy Recognition

The title track "Midnight Sun" has earned a nomination for **Best Dance Pop Recording** at the 68th Grammy Awards, scheduled to take place on February 1, 2026 [3]. This marks Zara Larsson's **first Grammy nomination** in her career [3].

### 6.2 MTV Video Music Awards

The music video for "Pretty Ugly" received a nomination for **Best Choreography** at the 2025 MTV Video Music Awards . The video was directed by Charlotte Rutherford and choreographed by Zoi Tatopoulos .

## 7. Special Editions and Packaging

### 7.1 Lisa Frank Edition

To commemorate the album's release, an exclusive **Midnight Sun (Lisa Frank Edition) CD** was made available [1]. This limited-run collaboration with the iconic design brand Lisa Frank, Inc. brings to life "a dreamy, aquatic world inspired by the memes and moments that so many of Zara's fans around the world participated in over the past year" [1].

The special edition features an animated version of Zara frolicking in an underwater paradise of dolphins, plant life, and musical notes [6]. This package is described as "colorful" and "vibrant," immersing fans in a unique visual experience tied to Larsson's online presence and fan culture [7].

The Lisa Frank Edition is available while supplies last and represents a special collectible for Larsson's dedicated fanbase [4].

## 9. Legacy and Impact

Midnight Sun represents a significant artistic evolution for Zara Larsson, marking her transition from a collection of singles to a cohesive album vision . The album's critical and commercial success, including her first Grammy nomination, positions Larsson as a major player in contemporary pop music.

The album's themes of self-love, presence, and embracing life have resonated with fans globally, particularly through its viral title track and the underwater-themed Lisa Frank collaboration that celebrates fan culture [1].

Musically, Midnight Sun has been recognized for its innovative blend of Scandinavian pop sensibilities with contemporary dance and electronic elements, establishing Larsson as a genre-bending artist unafraid to explore new sonic territories .

The album's success has also translated to live performance, with Larsson's touring schedule expanding to accommodate increased demand and venue upgrades, confirming her status as a major touring artist .
