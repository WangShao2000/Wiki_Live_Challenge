# Love Me Forever (Pinkshift album)

Love Me Forever is the debut studio album by American rock band Pinkshift, released on October 21, 2022, via Hopeless Records [1]. The album was recorded with producer Will Yip (known for his work with Turnstile, Mannequin Pussy, and The Wonder Years) at Studio 4 in Conshohocken, Pennsylvania [2]. Love Me Forever features 12 tracks spanning 37 minutes and includes themes of anxiety, depression, insecurity, and heartbreak [3].

## Background and recording

Pinkshift was formed in 2018 when Paul Vallejo, a chemical and biomolecular engineering major at Johns Hopkins University, approached fellow student Ashrita Kumar about forming a band . They later recruited drummer Myron Houngbedji after staking out the Mattin Center's drum room [4]. Initially, the band members expected music to be a hobby between classes, with Kumar planning to apply to medical school and Vallejo considering engineering .

The band's path changed dramatically when their 2020 single "i'm gonna tell my therapist on you" went viral during the COVID-19 pandemic [5]. This unexpected success led them to tear up their original plans and pursue music professionally, despite initial resistance from their immigrant parents .

Love Me Forever was recorded in three weeks with producer Will Yip at Studio 4 in Conshohocken, Pennsylvania . Kumar compared Yip's role to that of a coach, describing the recording experience as "like summer camp" [2]. This was the first time the band had recorded in a professional studio, as their previous EP Saccharine had been recorded on a low budget in their practice room . After recording, they had just one day to rest before embarking on a two-month tour with PUP .

## Composition

Love Me Forever features 12 tracks with a total duration of 37 minutes and 53 seconds [6]. The album opens with "i'm not crying you're crying," a blistering track that sets the tone for the album's raw energy [7]. Other notable tracks include "the kids aren't alright," which Kumar described as "super focused, compared to those songs. It was very angry and pointed" .

The album's musical style defies simple categorization. While often labeled as pop-punk, Love Me Forever incorporates elements of punk, post-hardcore, grunge, and pop . The band has cited Nirvana, Arctic Monkeys, Soundgarden, Stone Temple Pilots, and No Doubt as influences [8]. Vallejo noted that the album represents "a good mix of vibes" with contributions from each member's different musical backgrounds .

Lyrically, the album documents Kumar's personal journey through their twenties during the pandemic. Kumar describes their writing style as "very direct," explaining that "Pretty much every song on the record helped me get through something different. Instead of going to therapy, I'll write lyrics" . The album follows a progression from initial frustration and confusion to eventual self-acceptance, with Kumar stating that by the end, they "were able to really just love myself a little bit more" .

## Release and promotion

Love Me Forever was released on October 21, 2022, through Hopeless Records [1]. The album was made available in multiple formats including CD, LP, and digital download [9]. In promotion of the album, Pinkshift embarked on their first headline tour in late 2022, which included sold-out shows in major cities like New York and Boston .

The band supported the album with several singles and accompanying content. "Get Out" was released as a single with a lyric video in 2022 [10]. "In a Breath" was also released as a single and later featured a live performance video [10]. "Burn the Witch" became a fan favorite and the band expressed interest in creating a narrative music video for it, with Houngbedji stating they would "love to have some kind of story line going along in the music video for that" .

## Reception

Love Me Forever received positive reviews from critics upon its release. The album was described as "sharp, skilful (sic), and bristling with infectious energy" . Kerrang! praised the album as "accomplished and wise beyond Pinkshift's years," noting the band's relatively limited touring experience at the time of release [11].

FLOOD magazine called the album "a stunning burst of influences and experiences coalescing in a swirling swathe of anger and injustice" . The review particularly highlighted Kumar's lyrics about anxiety, depression, insecurity, and heartbreak, noting how they "pack generations of trauma into their voice, but channel them into their own specific experiences" .

The album appeared on several year-end lists. It was ranked #19 on Kerrang!'s Albums of 2022 list [12] and was also featured on The Alternative's Top 50 Albums of 2022 and Chorus.fm's Top 30 Albums of 2022 . Afterglow included it in their "Editors' Picks for the Best Albums of 2022," describing it as "one of the best alternative albums of 2022" and "a rallying cry" [13].

## Singles and music videos

Several tracks from Love Me Forever were released as singles. "Get Out" was released as a single with a lyric video in 2022 [10]. "In a Breath" was also released as a single and later had a live performance video released [10]. "Burn the Witch" became a highlight of the band's live performances and was frequently featured in their setlists [14].

The band created several music videos for songs from the album. "Nothing (In My Head)" had an official video that explored themes of social media and its effects on mental health . Kumar described the video creation process: "We worked with Danny Suadne and he really helped bring that idea to life. Y'know what was in the song. Honestly, I didn't even know how to visualize that, so that was really cool" .

## Legacy and significance

Love Me Forever established Pinkshift as an important voice in contemporary rock music, particularly for its honest exploration of mental health issues and the challenges of young adulthood during the pandemic. The album's success helped the band transition from being Johns Hopkins students to professional musicians, with their story of balancing academic ambitions with artistic pursuits resonating with many young people .

The album also challenged traditional expectations of pop-punk, incorporating heavier elements and more diverse influences than typical for the genre. Kumar noted: "I think one of the things that we were excited about was like, it might not be pop-punk, but it isn't anything else specifically either and being able to take a stab at furthering the genre" .

Love Me Forever remains Pinkshift's highest-charting and most critically acclaimed release to date, having appeared on numerous "best of" lists and establishing the band as one of the most promising acts in modern rock music .
