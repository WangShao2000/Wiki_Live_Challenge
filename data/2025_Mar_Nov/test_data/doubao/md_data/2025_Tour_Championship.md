# 2025 Tour Championship

The **2025 Tour Championship** was the season-ending professional golf tournament of the PGA Tour's FedEx Cup playoffs, contested from August 21 to 24, 2025, at East Lake Golf Club in Atlanta, Georgia [1]. It featured a field of 30 players competing for a total purse of \$40 million . The tournament marked a significant format change from previous years, returning to a traditional 72-hole stroke-play format where all players began at even par [2].

## Format and Format Change

The 2025 Tour Championship implemented a **major format change** from the previous starting-strokes system that had been in use since 2019 [3]. Under the previous format, players began the tournament at different stroke counts based on their FedEx Cup standings, with the top-ranked player starting at 10 under par, the second at 8 under, and so on . The new format eliminated this staggered start, allowing all 30 players to begin at even par for the 72-hole stroke-play event [2].

The PGA Tour announced the format change on May 27, 2025, with Tour commissioner Jay Monahan stating, "Our Fan Forward initiative has helped us evaluate each part of the PGA Tour season and today's announcement is an important first step in the evolution of our postseason" . The change was approved by the Tour policy board and was driven by fan feedback that preferred scores closer to par and a more straightforward format .

## Tournament Summary

**Tommy Fleetwood** won the 2025 Tour Championship with a four-round total of 18 under par, three strokes ahead of runners-up Patrick Cantlay and Russell Henley [4]. The victory was particularly significant as it marked Fleetwood's **first career PGA Tour win** after 163 career starts and 43 top-10 finishes without a victory [5]. Entering the tournament, Fleetwood held the record for the most top-10s without a win in PGA Tour history, surpassing Brett Quigley's 34 [5].

Fleetwood led the tournament from Round 2 and maintained his advantage through the final round . On the 72nd hole, with a three-shot lead, he sliced his approach short of the green, chipped to 8 feet, and two-putted for bogey to secure the victory .

The new format meant that the Tour Championship winner automatically became the FedEx Cup champion, earning Fleetwood an additional \$10 million [6]. This was the first time since 2018 that the Tour Championship winner also claimed the FedEx Cup title .

## Notable Performances and Controversies

### Tommy Fleetwood's Historic Victory

Fleetwood's victory was widely celebrated in the golfing world, with many athletes and fans cheering him on . The Englishman had come close to victory several times earlier in the season, including at the Travelers Championship in June, where he bogeyed the 72nd hole while leading by one, and at the FedEx St. Jude Championship, where he lost in a playoff .

After the win, Fleetwood stated, "You just keep learning, don't you? I think this one, I guess there was Travelers, there was Memphis, obviously plenty before. This probably wasn't the most comfortable I've been here because, as they rack up, you obviously start to think of the things" .

### Patrick Cantlay's Slow Play

During the final round, Patrick Cantlay faced criticism for his slow pace of play [7]. Broadcaster Jason Page shared a clip on social media showing Cantlay shuffling before hitting a shot, which caught the attention of six-time major champion Phil Mickelson .

Mickelson posted a sarcastic comment on social media, writing: "He shuffled his right foot 26 times. 26 TIMES before he hit it. 26 x 36 other shots is 936 foot shuffles. That's more calf raises than I'll ever do. You also must be in peak physical condition to do this daily so let's stop all the hate and appreciate that golfers are athletes" .

Cantlay, who entered the final round tied for the lead with Fleetwood, shot a 1-over 71 and finished as runner-up alongside Russell Henley, three shots behind Fleetwood .

### Defending Champion's Performance

Defending FedEx Cup champion Scottie Scheffler, who had five wins during the 2025 season and entered the tournament leading the FedEx Cup standings, finished tied for fourth place at 14 under par . Scheffler had been critical of the previous starting-strokes format, calling it "silly" . Under the new format, he stated, "I battled all week to give myself a chance. I wasn't as sharp as I would have hoped to have. I had a good first round, but outside of that didn't really play my best the first few days. Still gave myself a shot. Just needed a few better swings" .

## Course Information

The 2025 Tour Championship was played at East Lake Golf Club in Atlanta, Georgia [1]. The course features a par of 70 and a total yardage of 7,440 [1]. East Lake has hosted the Tour Championship annually since 2004 and is known for its challenging layout and historical significance as one of the oldest golf courses in the United States [8].

## Impact of the Format Change

The new format generated significant discussion in the golfing community. Analysis by The Golfing Gazette showed that under the previous starting-strokes format, Scottie Scheffler would have won the tournament with a score of 24 under par, with Fleetwood finishing second at 23 under [9].

Several players expressed approval of the format change. Scheffler, who was a member of the 16-player Player Advisory Council that helped drive the change, stated, "We want the Tour Championship to be the hardest tournament to qualify for and the FedExCup trophy the most difficult to win. Shifting the Tour Championship to a more straight-up format with a tougher course setup makes it easier for fans to follow and provides a more challenging test for players – which brings out the best competition" .

The PGA Tour indicated that the bonus structure for the top 30 players would be adjusted "to account for the increased volatility, reward season-long performance and recognize the significance of the FedExCup" .

## Television and Media Coverage

The 2025 Tour Championship was broadcast on multiple networks, with weekend coverage on Golf Channel and NBC [10]. Final-round television coverage was scheduled from noon to 1:30 p.m. on Golf Channel, followed by 1:30 to 6 p.m. on NBC and Peacock . PGA Tour Live on ESPN+ provided exclusive four-stream coverage, including a main feed, a marquee group stream, featured groups, and featured holes [11].

## Conclusion

The 2025 Tour Championship will be remembered for several significant developments in PGA Tour history. Tommy Fleetwood's breakthrough victory after 163 career starts and 43 top-10 finishes without a win was a story that captivated the golfing world [5]. The tournament also marked the successful implementation of the new stroke-play format, which received positive feedback from players and fans alike .

The format change represented a significant evolution in the FedEx Cup playoffs, with the PGA Tour responding to fan feedback and player input to create a more straightforward and competitive format . The tournament demonstrated that the new format could produce compelling golf, as evidenced by Fleetwood's three-stroke victory over a strong field that included many of the world's top players.

The controversy surrounding Patrick Cantlay's slow play highlighted ongoing discussions about pace of play in professional golf, while Scottie Scheffler's fourth-place finish showed that even the best players could struggle to adapt to the new format .

Looking forward, the 2025 Tour Championship will be seen as a pivotal moment in the history of the FedEx Cup playoffs, establishing a new standard for the season-ending event that balances competitive integrity with fan appeal.
