# Cow (Mario)

Cow is a bovine character appearing in the Mario video game franchise, primarily known as an obstacle in the Mario Kart series. First introduced in 1996 as a background character in Mario Kart 64, Cow later gained prominence as a playable character in Mario Kart World (2025), becoming the first cow to be playable in the entire Super Mario franchise. The character has since become a viral phenomenon, generating significant fan interest and inspiring various controversies.

## 1. Character Description

### 1.1 Physical Appearance

Cow is a white bovine creature with black spots on its body and small horns on its head . The character features beady eyes and a nose ring . In its early appearances, Cow had no collar, but starting from Mario Kart DS (2005), it wears a blue collar with a yellow bell .

In Mario Kart 8 (2014) and subsequent games, cows appear in multiple color variations, including solid black, brown, or blue-spotted . The playable Cow in Mario Kart World is specifically identified as female .

### 1.2 Personality and Characteristics

The playable Cow character is classified as a heavyweight character in Mario Kart World, sharing statistics with other heavyweight characters such as Wario, Waluigi, Donkey Kong, Wiggler, Chargin' Chuck, and Pianta . The character's emblem consists of a stylized depiction of her face .

The development team noted that while Cow is a four-legged creature, she is depicted holding motorcycle handlebars with her front hooves when racing, though she strikes a proper quadrupedal pose when jumping .

## 2. History and Development

### 2.1 Early Appearances (1996-2014)

Cows first appeared in Mario Kart 64 (1996) as background characters in the course Moo Moo Farm . They made subsequent appearances in various Mario games including Mario Party 2 (2001), Mario Party 3 (2002), Mario Kart: Double Dash!! (2003), Mario Pinball Land (2004), Mario Kart DS (2005), Mario Kart Wii (2008), Mario Kart 7 (2011), Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games (2012), Mario Party: Island Tour (2014), and Mario Kart 8 (2014) .

In Mario Kart Wii (2008), cows were introduced as obstacles on the Moo Moo Meadows course for the first time . During the first lap, cows could be seen in fields, and starting from the second lap, they would run loose from opened gates and become obstacles that could flip racers if collided with .

### 2.2 Development as a Playable Character

The concept for Cow becoming a playable character originated from a sketch made during early development of Mario Kart World. According to Nintendo developers, when working on an early version of a ranch-themed course, a designer created a sketch of a cow driving a kart [1].

The character designer quickly put together a prototype of Cow that could race, and surprisingly, it "didn't feel out of place at all" . This led to the development team expanding the idea to other obstacle characters, including Cheep Cheep and Pokey, creating what they called "NPC drivers" .

The playable Cow was officially revealed in the Nintendo Direct for Mario Kart World on April 2, 2025 . Initially unlockable in version 1.0.0, she was made available from the start following a software update .

## 3. Cultural Impact and Reception

### 3.1 Viral Sensation

Since her reveal, Cow has become a viral phenomenon . The character's reception among fans has been overwhelmingly positive, with social media flooded with fan art and dedications . According to Polygon, shortly after the launch of Mario Kart World and the Nintendo Switch 2, online lobbies were "practically filled only with cows" .

The character's popularity has been described as exemplifying Nintendo's approach to "keeping joy in the game," with fans viewing Cow's playable status as a victory . Nintendo's official social channels have embraced the character's popularity, sharing behind-the-scenes sketches and inviting players to vote on future NPC driver candidates [2].

### 3.2 Meme Culture

Cows from the Moo Moo Meadows track had already been subjects of memes throughout the 2010s and 2020s . One of the earliest known memes explicitly focusing on the cows was posted by YouTuber VerdiChris on August 26, 2014, titled "Moo Moo Meadows in Real Life," which garnered over 25,000 views in ten years .

The meme culture intensified in the early 2020s with various TikTok posts gaining millions of views . The character's popularity culminated in her playable debut in Mario Kart World, further fueling the meme culture surrounding her.

## 4. Controversies

### 4.1 PETA Criticism

In August 2025, animal rights organization PETA (People for the Ethical Treatment of Animals) criticized the Cow character's design, specifically her nose ring [3]. In a letter to Nintendo President Shuntaro Furukawa on August 14, 2025, PETA's Senior Vice President of Marketing Engagement Joel Bartlett argued that nose rings are "forcibly pierced through one of the most sensitive parts of a cow's body" .

PETA stated that the nose ring "glosses over real-world violence and cruelty to animals," calling for Nintendo to "let Cow race freely—without reminders of the industries that treat animals like machines" . The organization noted that in the meat and dairy industries, nose rings are used to "exploit, control, and even drag cows and bulls to their deaths" .

### 4.2 "Dirty Cow Butt" Controversy

Shortly after Mario Kart World's release, a controversy emerged in Japan when players noticed that the Cow character's backside appeared dirty . One player posted a photo of the cow's backside on social media with the caption "Dirty butt!!!!!!" in Japanese .

The controversy gained attention when a real Japanese farmer named Renta commented on the issue, stating: "The strangest thing about this game is that the dirtiness of the cow's butts has been recreated with incredible realism" . Renta explained that while cows' backsides do often get dirty, especially when grazing, farmers try to keep their animals clean .

## 5. Legacy and Influence

### 5.1 Gameplay Innovation

The introduction of Cow as a playable character inspired several innovations in Mario Kart World. The concept of NPC drivers led to the creation of the Kamek spell item, which transforms all racers into randomly selected NPC characters—often Cow—while scrambling their stats for ten seconds [2].

The development team has stated that Cow is "actually a pivotal character in the Mario Kart series" as she inspired the inclusion of other obstacle characters as playable racers . This approach has fundamentally changed how the development team approaches character selection for future Mario Kart games.

### 5.2 Broader Franchise Appearances

Beyond Mario Kart, cows have made appearances in several other Mario franchise games. They appear in the Mario Party series, Mario Pinball Land, Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games, and Yoshi's Crafted World .

In Yoshi's Woolly World and Poochy & Yoshi's Woolly World, while cows are not physically present, a Moo Moo Yoshi design is available as an unlockable Yoshi pattern . In Yoshi's Crafted World, the Moo Moo Meadows Milk sponsor from Mario Kart 8 makes cameo appearances on cartons and signs .

## 6. Critical Assessment

### 6.1 Design Evolution

The evolution of Cow from a simple background character to a playable racer represents a significant shift in Nintendo's approach to character design. The character's design has evolved over time, with color variations introduced in Mario Kart 8 allowing for greater visual diversity .

The decision to make Cow playable has been met with widespread approval from fans, with developers noting that the positive reception "exceeded their expectations" [4]. The character's design, including the controversial nose ring, has sparked discussions about representation in video games and the intersection of entertainment with real-world issues.

### 6.2 Cultural Significance

Cow's rise to prominence reflects changing trends in video game culture, where minor characters can achieve major popularity through social media and fan engagement. The character has become more than just a game asset—she represents a cultural phenomenon that has brought joy to millions of players worldwide.

The character's impact extends beyond mere entertainment, having inspired academic discussions about game design, player psychology, and the role of animals in media. Cow's journey from background obstacle to playable icon demonstrates the unpredictable nature of viral culture and the power of community-driven enthusiasm.

## 7. Conclusion

Cow's journey from a simple background character in Mario Kart 64 to a playable sensation in Mario Kart World exemplifies the unpredictable nature of fan culture and the power of unexpected design choices. The character has transcended her initial role as a mere obstacle to become a cultural phenomenon, inspiring art, memes, and even philosophical discussions about the representation of animals in entertainment.

Despite controversies surrounding her design, particularly the nose ring that drew criticism from PETA, and the lighthearted "dirty butt" debate, Cow has largely been embraced as a symbol of Nintendo's commitment to creativity and fun. Her impact on the Mario franchise extends beyond mere gameplay, having inspired new game mechanics and fundamentally altering how developers approach character selection.

As Cow continues to race through Mario Kart World and potentially future games, her legacy as a beloved character and cultural touchstone seems secure. Whether viewed as a victory for creative game design, a symbol of fan empowerment, or simply a fun addition to the Mario universe, Cow has undoubtedly earned her place in video game history.
