# Calum Lill

**Calum Andrew Lill** (born September 1995) is an English actor known for his roles in British television soap operas [1]. He gained recognition for his portrayal of Joel Deering in the ITV soap opera Coronation Street from September 2023 to June 2025 [1]. Lill has also appeared in other notable British soaps including Hollyoaks, Doctors, and Holby City [2].

## Early life and education

Lill was born in September 1995 in Manchester, England [1]. He developed an interest in acting after participating in a drama class at school, initially motivated by the opportunity to work as an extra on the television series Waterloo Road . During his time on Waterloo Road, he had a scene with actress Chelsee Healey, who recommended drama classes that helped launch his career .

After completing his education, Lill was awarded a scholarship to study acting at the Arts Educational Schools in London, where he earned a BA in acting in 2018 [1].

## Career

### Early career and breakthrough

Following his graduation in 2018, Lill began his professional acting career with small roles in British television dramas . His early appearances included a brief role in the BBC daytime soap opera Doctors, which he described as "two days filming" that made him feel "like \[he'd] just been cast as the lead in the new Scorsese epic" . He later appeared in an episode of the medical drama Holby City .

The COVID-19 pandemic in 2020 significantly impacted Lill's early career, as he noted: "lockdown hit... That threw a spanner in the works" . Despite these setbacks, he continued to audition for roles and in late 2020 received an unexpected offer for approximately eight episodes on the Channel 4 soap opera Hollyoaks . The producers stated they "remembered \[him]" from previous auditions and he "didn't need to audition" . This was his first extended role lasting more than a couple of days .

### Hollyoaks and other early roles

In Hollyoaks, Lill portrayed PC Carlton Smith, a co-worker and friend of George Kiss, from March to July 2021 [2]. During this period, he also worked various jobs to support himself, including selling cars and working at a Junkyard Golf Club [3]. He later described his experience as a car salesman, saying "I was selling cars while auditioning for Corrie and I hated it" .

In 2022, after his Hollyoaks role ended, Lill worked at a paella stand at festivals alongside former Coronation Street actors Jenny Platt and Rupert Hill . This experience proved significant for his career, as Rupert Hill advised him that if Coronation Street producers were interested in him, they would contact him .

### Coronation Street role

Lill's breakthrough came when he was cast as Joel Deering in Coronation Street in 2023 . He had previously auditioned unsuccessfully for the show and was considering quitting acting after what he described as "14 or 15 years" of struggle . He recalled: "I thought, 'It's been 15 years, I'm going to have to drop it, nothing's happening'" .

His character Joel Deering was introduced as a duty solicitor helping the character Lauren Bolton, but later developed into a more complex role involving a romantic storyline with Dee-Dee Bailey . The character was eventually revealed to have a secret wife and young daughter, and later emerged as a villain who had groomed and physically assaulted Lauren Bolton .

To prepare for this challenging role, Lill studied police interviews with serial killer Ted Bundy and consulted with the Maggie Oliver Foundation, a charity supporting survivors of sexual exploitation . His performance earned him a nomination for Best Villain at the 2024 Inside Soap Awards [4].

Lill's character was killed off in September 2024 as part of a whodunit mystery storyline . He appeared in 76 episodes of Coronation Street from September 2023 to June 2025 .

### Other works

In addition to his television work, Lill has appeared in several short films including "I Can See Clearly Now," "Sleep Warm," "Heads or Tails," and "Down, Not Out" [1]. His film and television credits also include an appearance in the drama series In the Club [1].

## Personal life

Lill resides in Manchester with his fiancée, Roberta McClarron, who works in public relations and marketing . The couple began their relationship in 2021 and became engaged in 2022 . When announcing their engagement, Lill joked: "About time I made an honest woman of her" .

In December 2025, Lill announced via Instagram that he and McClarron are expecting their first child, due in April 2026 [5]. His social media post included a playful reference to his television career, with the caption "New character dropping April '26" .

Lill has been open about his struggles in the acting industry before achieving success. He revealed he was "close to quitting acting" before landing his Coronation Street role, describing the period as "tough" [6]. He has credited McClarron with providing support during his career difficulties, stating: "It wasn't just happiness for me getting the job, she knew what I was going through and that I was struggling" .
