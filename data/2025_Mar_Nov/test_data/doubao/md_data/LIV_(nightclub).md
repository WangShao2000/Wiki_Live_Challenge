# LIV (nightclub)

## Lead section

LIV is a nightclub located at the Fontainebleau Miami Beach hotel in Miami Beach, Florida [1]. It occupies more than 18,000 square feet of space and combines an ultra-exclusive lounge with a high-energy nightclub [2]. The venue was established in 2008 as part of the Fontainebleau's \$1 billion renovation and expansion project .

LIV is notable for its architectural design, which includes an 88-foot dome ceiling with more than 800 LED fixtures, private framed skyboxes, and a glowing staircase . The nightclub has achieved significant commercial success, ranking among the top five highest-grossing nightclubs in the United States with annual revenues of \$40-45 million [3].

The venue has hosted numerous high-profile events, including the Victoria's Secret Fashion Show after-party as its opening event in 2008 [4]. Major DJs and artists such as Steve Aoki, DJ Khaled, Diplo, and Lil Wayne have performed at LIV [5].

In 2023, LIV became involved in a trademark dispute with LIV Golf, successfully blocking the golf organization's trademark registration . The nightclub argued that LIV Golf's trademarks were "visually, phonetically and aurally similar" to its own [6].

## 1. History and development

### 1.1 Founding and early years

LIV was established in 2008 as the main new addition of the Soffer family's \$1 billion renovation of the Fontainebleau Miami Beach hotel . The nightclub is located in the former Club Tropigala, originally designed by architect Morris Lapidus in 1954 . The space had historical significance as a venue that hosted performers such as Frank Sinatra and Sammy Davis Jr. in its previous incarnation .

The development of LIV was led by David Grutman, who was approached by Jeff Soffer, whose family owned the Fontainebleau . Grutman negotiated to have complete control over the project, stating: "I don't want any partners. If I do it, it's going to be my show from start to finish" . Soffer agreed to these terms, and Grutman took the lead on developing the new nightclub.

The nightclub's opening night on November 6, 2008, hosted the Victoria's Secret Fashion Show after-party, with portions of the show replayed on video screens throughout the venue [4]. This high-profile event helped establish LIV as a premier nightlife destination from its inception.

### 1.2 Ownership changes

Initially, LIV was operated by Miami Marketing Group (MMG), with David Grutman as CEO and operating partner [7]. In 2013, 80% of MMG was acquired by Robert F.X. Sillerman .

In 2018, Grutman founded Groot Hospitality, which became the parent company of LIV and several other successful ventures including Story nightclub (2012), Komodo restaurant (2015), and others . The following year, in October 2019, Live Nation Entertainment acquired a majority stake in Groot Hospitality [8].

### 1.3 Financial performance

LIV has achieved remarkable commercial success since its opening. According to Nightclub & Bar magazine, the club earned approximately $25 million in 2010 . Following the EDM (electronic dance music) explosion, the club's revenue grew significantly, reaching $40 million by 2015 .

The nightclub consistently ranks among the top highest-grossing nightclubs in the United States. In 2012, LIV was ranked as the fifth highest-grossing nightclub in the United States, with estimated revenues of $35-45 million . By 2014, it maintained its position as the fifth highest-grossing club with revenues of $40-45 million [9]. This financial success has continued, with the club regularly ranking within the top 10 grossing clubs in the United States [10].

## 2. Design and architecture

### 2.1 Interior design

LIV features a striking architectural design that combines modern technology with elements of the original Fontainebleau's historic architecture. The venue occupies more than 18,000 square feet of space [11].

The nightclub's most distinctive feature is its 88-foot dome ceiling, which contains more than 800 LED fixtures . The dome is internally lit by approximately 585 Philips iColor Cove MX units that create a low-resolution video matrix, with an additional 300 ColorBlast 12 fixtures lining its perimeter .

The venue includes multiple levels, private framed skyboxes, a glowing staircase, and three full-service bars . The interior design incorporates classical chandeliers that create a visual connection to the warmth of the backlit VIP boxes while contrasting with the modernist space .

### 2.2 Technology and features

LIV incorporates state-of-the-art technology throughout the venue. The club has a Funktion-1 sound system with deep and powerful bass . LED spark lights, neon lights, and laser projections from the DJ booth add to the visual experience .

The nightclub features a sophisticated lighting control system managed by an e:cue system via a 17" touchscreen and fader unit . The system can run low-res video through the LED dome fixtures, as well as geometric effects, chases, and strobes . All architectural lighting elements can be controlled by the light operator at the same time as theatrical fixtures .

The venue has excellent sightlines throughout, allowing patrons to view performances from various locations . A removable railing allows the rear VIP level to be converted to a full stage, turning the space into a fully functional live performance venue .

### 2.3 Capacity and layout

LIV has a capacity of 1,200 people . The venue offers multiple seating and viewing options, including up to fifty banquette areas and six private skyboxes . The skyboxes are located on an upper level and provide table service .

The club has four full-service bars in total . Two are situated on the main floor, surrounding the VIP sections, and two are upstairs servicing the six exclusive skyboxes .

## 3. Operations and events

### 3.1 Music policy and entertainment

LIV's music policy encompasses hip-hop, R\&B, dance, and house music [1]. The nightclub features some of the hottest DJs and performers in the music industry five nights a week .

Major DJs and artists who have performed at LIV include Steve Aoki, DJ Khaled, Diplo, John Summit, Central Cee, Ty Dolla \$ign, Alesso, Meek Mill, Jeezy, Lil Wayne, Rick Ross, Sexyy Red, and Shakira [5].

The venue has become a go-to destination for unforgettable performances and afterparties during major events such as Art Basel Miami Beach and Miami Race Week [5].

### 3.2 Hours and admission

LIV is open Wednesday through Sunday from 11:00 PM to 5:00 AM . The minimum age requirement for entry is 21 years old .

The nightclub maintains a dress code that requires smart and elegant attire [12]. Shorts, jewelry, or athletic dress are not permitted for men . The door attendants have the final decision regarding entry .

### 3.3 Notable events

LIV has hosted numerous high-profile events since its opening. In addition to the Victoria's Secret Fashion Show after-party in 2008 [4], the venue has been a popular destination for various celebrity events and parties.

The nightclub has hosted performances and appearances by celebrities including Justin Bieber, Lil Wayne, Jamie Foxx, Johnny Manziel, Odell Beckham Jr., Khloe Kardashian, Tristan Thompson, Adrien Brody, and Victor Cruz .

LIV has also been the venue for various special events including New Year's Eve celebrations, Super Bowl parties, Art Basel events, and other major cultural and sporting events .

## 4. Controversies and legal issues

### 4.1 Trademark dispute with LIV Golf

In 2023, LIV became involved in a significant trademark dispute with LIV Golf, a Saudi-backed professional golf tour [6]. The nightclub, through its parent company Groot Hospitality, filed a notice of opposition against LIV Golf's trademark registration [6].

The nightclub argued that LIV Golf's trademarks were "visually, phonetically and aurally similar and the goods/services share similarities" [6]. Groot Hospitality alleged that LIV Golf's brand was highly similar to the nightclub in "sight, sound, meaning, and overall commercial impression" .

The nightclub's legal team pointed out that LIV had been using the LIV branding since 2008, when it was taken over by David Grutman . They argued that their trademarks were already registered "across the globe" and had been in use for 15 years .

In 2023, LIV successfully blocked LIV Golf from filing its trademarks . The nightclub warned that "the registration of the LIV Golf trademarks will dilute the distinctive quality" of the LIV Miami trademarks .

### 4.2 Other incidents

There have been various other incidents reported at LIV over the years. In 2012, the nightclub faced challenges when a competitor called Adore opened nearby . However, this competition was short-lived as Adore closed after only four months in business .

In 2023, during the COVID-19 pandemic, LIV was reported to have implemented various safety measures including temperature checks and capacity restrictions [13].

## 5. Cultural impact and legacy

### 5.1 Influence on Miami nightlife

LIV has had a significant impact on Miami's nightlife scene. The nightclub is credited with redefining nightlife on Miami Beach by melding the allure of the historical Fontainebleau with contemporary entertainment [14].

The venue's success has helped establish Miami as a major player in the global nightlife industry. Industry experts have noted that "Miami is the new Las Vegas," partly due to the success of clubs like LIV and its sister venue Story .

David Grutman, LIV's founder, has been called "the king of Miami nightlife" and is credited with revolutionizing the city's entertainment industry . His success with LIV has inspired other entrepreneurs and helped create a new standard for luxury nightlife venues.

### 5.2 Media and popular culture

LIV has been featured in various media outlets and has become a popular subject in entertainment news. The nightclub has been mentioned in numerous articles about celebrity parties, music events, and nightlife trends.

The venue has been featured in television shows, music videos, and social media content. Its status as a celebrity hotspot has made it a frequent topic in entertainment news and gossip columns.

### 5.3 Awards and recognition

While specific awards for LIV were not mentioned in the research materials, the nightclub's consistent ranking among the top-grossing nightclubs in the United States serves as recognition of its success [10]. The venue's inclusion in industry publications and its reputation as one of the hottest clubs in Miami demonstrate its significant cultural impact.
