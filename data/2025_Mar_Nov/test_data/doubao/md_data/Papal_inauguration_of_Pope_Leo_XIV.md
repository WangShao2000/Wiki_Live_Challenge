# Papal inauguration of Pope Leo XIV

The **papal inauguration of Pope Leo XIV** took place on **May 18, 2025**, in St. Peter's Square at the Vatican, marking the official beginning of his pontificate as the 267th Bishop of Rome and successor of Saint Peter [1]. The ceremony, formally known as the "Mass for the Beginning of the Petrine Ministry of the Bishop of Rome," was a solemn liturgical celebration rich in tradition and symbolism that attracted approximately 200,000 faithful [2].

## Background and context

Pope Leo XIV (formerly Cardinal Robert Francis Prevost) was elected on May 8, 2025, during the second day of the papal conclave, in the fourth round of voting . He chose the name "Leo XIV" in reference to his predecessor Leo XIII, who served as pope from 1878 to 1903 [3]. The newly elected pope is the first American to hold the papal office and the first member of the Order of Saint Augustine to become pope [4].

The inauguration date of May 18, 2025, coincided with the birthday of Pope Saint John Paul II . The ceremony followed the traditional pattern established by Pope Benedict XVI in 2013, with the specific rites of the Petrine office carefully structured to precede the Eucharistic prayer .

## The inauguration ceremony

### Liturgical structure and proceedings

The inauguration ceremony began with Pope Leo XIV riding through St. Peter's Square in the popemobile, waving to the massive crowd and receiving their enthusiastic cheers of "Viva il Papa!" . This initial procession has become a 标志性 element of papal inaugurations, symbolizing the pope's connection with the global Catholic community.

The liturgy formally commenced inside St. Peter's Basilica, where Pope Leo XIV, accompanied by the Patriarchs of the Eastern Catholic Churches, descended to the chapel housing the Tomb of Saint Peter . There, he paused for silent prayer and incensed the site, highlighting his deep spiritual connection with the Apostle and the martyrdom that established the Church in Rome . The pallium, the Ring of the Fisherman, and the Book of the Gospels had been placed in advance near the remains of Saint Peter, emphasizing the apostolic continuity of the papal office .

Following this moment of prayer, deacons carried these three sacred objects in a solemn procession to the altar on the parvis (forecourt) of the basilica . As Pope Leo XIV joined the procession, the traditional "Laudes Regiae" hymn was chanted, invoking the assistance of Christ, the saints, martyrs, and holy pontiffs of the Church .

### Symbolic elements and vestments

The central moments of the inauguration focused on the conferral of two ancient papal insignia: the **pallium** and the **Ring of the Fisherman** [5].

The pallium is a white woolen band embroidered with black crosses, made from lamb's wool, representing the Good Shepherd who carries his sheep . It features black silk crosses and symbolic pins recalling Christ's crucifixion . The garment is worn by metropolitan archbishops and, in a particular way, by the Bishop of Rome as a sign of his pastoral responsibility over the universal Church . The pallium symbolizes the pontiff's pastoral care for the Church and his role as shepherd to the flock .

The Ring of the Fisherman is a gold signet ring bearing the image of Saint Peter casting his nets, symbolizing the pope's role as successor to Saint Peter and herald of the Gospel . Historically used as a papal seal, the ring alludes to Peter's role as the fisherman who obeyed Christ's word and symbolizes the pope's authority to confirm his brethren in faith [6].

During the ceremony, three Cardinals—each from a different ecclesial order (deacons, priests, bishops) and representing different continents—presented these insignia to the Pope . One placed the pallium on his shoulders, another offered a prayer for divine guidance, and the third invoked Christ's blessing before presenting the Ring of the Fisherman .

### Liturgical readings and prayers

The Liturgy of the Word included readings that recalled Saint Peter's call and mission . The First Reading from Acts 4:8–12 declared Christ as "the stone rejected by the builders" . The Second Reading from 1 Peter 5:1–5, 10–11 connected Peter's legacy to the Pope's ministry . The Gospel from John 21:15–19, proclaimed in Latin and Greek, recalled Jesus' thrice-repeated call to Peter: "Feed my lambs. Tend my sheep" .

The Universal Prayer featured intercessions in multiple languages including Portuguese, French, Arabic, Polish, and Chinese . Prayers were offered for the Church, the Pope, world leaders, the suffering, and all the faithful gathered at the celebration .

### Homily and message

In his inaugural homily, Pope Leo XIV emphasized themes of **love and unity** as the two central dimensions of the Petrine ministry . He began by saying: "I come to you as a brother, who desires to be a servant of your faith and your joy, walking with you on the path of God's love, for he wants us all to be united in one family" [7].

The Pope spoke of his unworthiness and the humility with which he approached his new ministry: "I was chosen without any merit of my own, and now, with fear and trembling, I come to you as a brother" . He emphasized that the ministry of Peter is marked by self-giving love, stating: "The ministry of Peter is marked precisely by this self-giving love, because the Church of Rome presides in charity and its true authority is the charity of Christ. It is never about overpowering others, or religious propaganda, or the tools of power. It is always and only about loving as Jesus loved" .

A central theme of the homily was the Pope's vision for a united Church: "Let this be our first great desire: a united Church, a sign of unity and communion, becoming a ferment for a reconciled world" [8]. He acknowledged the divisions and conflicts of the modern world, saying: "In our time, we still see too much discord, too many wounds caused by hatred, violence, prejudice, the fear of the other, and an economic system that exploits the Earth's resources and marginalizes the poorest" .

Pope Leo XIV called for a Church that is "founded on the love of God and a sign of unity, a missionary Church that opens its arms to the world, that proclaims the Word, that allows itself to be unsettled by history, and that becomes a leaven of concord for humanity" .

### Post-liturgy events

Following the Mass, Pope Leo XIV returned inside St. Peter's Basilica to greet heads of state, royalty, and religious delegations assembled for the occasion . This greeting, conducted in the nave of the basilica in front of the Altar of the Confession, was an act of diplomatic and ecumenical respect rather than part of the liturgy .

Among the notable attendees were approximately a dozen heads of state, including U.S. Vice President JD Vance, Peruvian President Dina Boluarte, Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney, and Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy . Diplomatic protocol gave both the United States and Peru front-row seats due to Pope Leo XIV's dual citizenship .

Thirty-eight ecumenical delegations attended, including representatives from the Patriarchate of Constantinople and the Greek Orthodox Patriarchate of Alexandria [9]. The Jewish community was represented by a 13-member delegation, half of them rabbis, while other delegations represented Buddhist, Muslim, Zoroastrian, Hindu, Sikh, and Jain communities .

Security for the event was substantial, with Rome police deploying more than 6,000 officers, including snipers, anti-drone systems, and helicopters .

### Subsequent events

In the days following his inauguration, Pope Leo XIV continued traditional papal rituals. On May 25, he officially took possession of the Lateran Basilica, the cathedral of the Diocese of Rome and the highest-ranking church in the Catholic Church . He also visited the other papal basilicas of Saint Mary Major and St. Paul's Outside the Walls .

On June 8, the Church celebrated Pentecost, marking both the end of the Easter season and the conclusion of Pope Leo XIV's first month as pontiff .

## Historical significance

The inauguration of Pope Leo XIV holds particular historical significance as it marked several "firsts" in papal history. He is the first American to be elected Pope [4], and the first member of the Order of Saint Augustine to hold the Petrine ministry . His election and inauguration came after the death of Pope Francis and the subsequent conclave that elected him on May 8, 2025 .

The ceremony was also notable for the emotional response it elicited from the new Pope. When the pallium and fisherman's ring were placed on him, Pope Leo XIV appeared to choke up, as if the weight of responsibility of leading the 1.4-billion strong Church had just become fully apparent . He turned his hand to look at the ring and then clasped his hands in front of him in prayer .

The inauguration was broadcast globally, with millions watching the historic event. In the United States, there was particular celebration, with many Americans viewing the event as making the papacy more accessible and relatable . One American seminarian from Omaha, Nebraska, wrapped himself in an American flag purchased at a truck stop in Iowa to celebrate, saying: "Rome always felt like home for a Catholic, but now coming here and seeing one of our own on the throne of Peter... it almost makes Jesus himself more accessible" .

## Media coverage and reception

The inauguration received extensive media coverage from around the world. Major news outlets including the Associated Press, CBS News, Euronews, and Vatican Media provided comprehensive coverage of the event [10].

The homily was particularly noted for its emphasis on unity at a time when the Catholic Church, particularly in the United States, has been marked by significant polarization . Pope Francis's 12-year pontificate, which emphasized care for the poor and marginalized and criticism of the capitalist economic system, had often alienated conservatives . Pope Leo XIV's call for unity was seen as a significant message given these divisions.

The new Pope's decision to wear the traditional papal red cape (mozzetta) during the post-Mass greetings was also noted by media. Unlike his predecessor Francis, who had eschewed many formalities of the papacy as part of his simple style, Leo's return to traditional garb pleased conservatives and traditionalists .

However, Pope Leo XIV did break protocol at one point during the basilica greetings when he gave his older brother, Louis Prevost, a self-described political "MAGA-type," a bear hug when he and his wife came up to greet the Pope . This spontaneous gesture was seen as humanizing the papal office and demonstrating the new Pope's personal warmth.

## Legacy and future expectations

The inauguration of Pope Leo XIV established the tone for his pontificate, emphasizing themes of unity, charity, and missionary zeal . His inaugural homily is seen as providing a roadmap for renewal within the Catholic Church and its engagement with the world .

The new Pope has already stated his intention to continue the Vatican's work of conflict mediation . At the end of his inauguration Mass, he expressed hope for negotiations to bring a "just and lasting peace" in Ukraine and offered prayers for the people of Gaza . Ukrainian President Zelenskyy later had a formal audience with Pope Leo XIV at the Vatican, where he stressed the need for a "full and unconditional ceasefire as soon as possible" .

Pope Leo XIV's emphasis on being a "servant of the faith and joy" of the people  has set expectations for a pontificate that combines traditional Catholic teaching with a pastoral approach that emphasizes love and unity above all else. His vision of a Church that is "a leaven of concord for humanity"  has resonated with many Catholics and non-Catholics alike.

The inauguration also marked the beginning of what many hope will be a period of healing and reconciliation within the Catholic Church, particularly in the United States where divisions have been particularly acute. Pope Leo XIV's ability to bridge these divides while maintaining Catholic doctrine will likely be one of the key challenges and measures of success for his pontificate.
