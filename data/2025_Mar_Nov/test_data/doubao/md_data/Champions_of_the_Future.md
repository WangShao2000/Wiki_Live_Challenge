# Champions of the Future

Champions of the Future (COTF) is an international kart racing competition organized by the RGMMC Group and sanctioned by FIA Karting (CIK-FIA). Founded in 2020, COTF is one of 11 competitions on the international kart racing calendar in CIK-FIA classes . The series has become a prominent platform for young racing talent, with several past winners progressing to careers in single-seat racing [1].

## History and Organization

Champions of the Future was launched in 2020 by promoter RGMMC as a new international kart racing series [2]. The competition was designed to provide a high-quality racing platform for young drivers and teams, with the goal of identifying and developing future motorsport champions [1]. From its inception, COTF positioned itself as a feeder series for FIA Karting events, with its calendar closely aligned with major international karting championships .

The RGMMC Group, under the leadership of President James Geidel, has established COTF as one of the premier junior karting competitions globally . The organization's approach emphasizes competitive racing combined with comprehensive support for young drivers, including live streaming of events and professional race management .

## Competition Format

### Euro Series Structure

The Champions of the Future Euro Series follows a structured format with five rounds held at premier karting circuits across Europe . The 2026 season will visit La Conca (Italy), Valencia (Spain), Mülsen (Germany), Kristianstad (Sweden), and Sarno (Italy) [3]. The series traditionally runs from April to September, with each event featuring both OK and OK-Junior categories [4].

The competition format includes timed qualifying sessions, heat races, super heats, and a final race. Points are awarded based on finishing positions, with the driver accumulating the most points over the season crowned as the series champion . The 2023 season saw 170 drivers compete in OK-Junior and 142 in OK, demonstrating the series' international appeal .

### Classes and Categories

Champions of the Future primarily features two main categories:



* **OK Class**: For drivers aged 15 and above, using 125cc direct injection engines

* **OK-Junior Class**: For drivers aged 12-15, using 125cc direct injection engines with restricted performance [4]

The technical regulations for both classes align with FIA Karting standards, ensuring fair competition and safety . Tires are supplied by Maxxis, which became a partner of the series in 2024 .

## Notable Achievements and Winners

### Series Champions

The Champions of the Future series has produced several notable champions who have gone on to achieve success in higher levels of motorsport [1].

**OK-Junior Champions:**



* 2023: Lewis Wherrell (Great Britain)

* 2024: James Anagnostiadis (Great Britain)

**OK Champions:**



* 2023: Alex Powell (Jamaica)

* 2024: James Anagnostiadis (Great Britain)

### Driver Development Success

Several past COTF competitors have progressed to Formula 1 development programs:



* James Anagnostiadis, the 2024 double champion, was signed to the Mercedes Driver Development Programme shortly after his victory

* Sara Matsui earned a place in the Williams Racing Driver Academy

* Fionn McLaughlin was recruited by Red Bull Racing

These achievements underscore COTF's effectiveness as a talent identification and development platform .

### Record-Breaking Performances

The 2024 season saw several notable performances, including Dries Van Langendonck, the 2023 world champion in his category, who started from pole position and took six wins in seven heats at one event [5]. The series has also hosted races at historic circuits such as Val d'Argenton in France, which has previously hosted battles between Lewis Hamilton and Nico Rosberg, as well as Max Verstappen and Charles Leclerc .

## Champions of the Future Academy Program

### Program Overview

The Champions of the Future Academy Program (COTFA) is a global initiative launched to increase female participation and inclusion in national and international karting competitions by breaking down financial and technical barriers to entry . The program was developed in partnership with F1 Academy and represents a significant expansion of RGMMC's karting portfolio .

The Academy Program operates on an "Arrive and Drive" concept, where all participants use standardized equipment provided by the series . This approach ensures that drivers compete on equal terms, with talent rather than equipment quality determining results.

### Structure and Categories

The Academy Program features three age-based categories :



* **Minis (8-11 years)**: Using Parolin chassis powered by TM engines

* **Juniors (11-14 years)**: Using Kart Republic chassis powered by IAME engines

* **Seniors (14-17 years)**: Using Kart Republic chassis powered by IAME engines

The 2025 international series comprised six double-header race weekends, with events held in Portugal, Spain, Italy, Slovakia, and the United Arab Emirates . The program includes cost control measures such as limited entry fees and a chassis and engine lottery system .

### 2025 Season Results

The 2025 Academy Program season concluded with championships awarded to :



* Markas Silkunas (Lithuania) in OK-N Senior

* Conor Clancy (United Arab Emirates) in OK-N Junior

* Niccolo Perico (Italy) in Mini 60

* Wynn Godschalk (United States) in Mini U10

A notable achievement was Angelina Simons Torres from Spain becoming the second female driver to win in the OK-N Junior category in 2025, following Ella Hakkinen's victory in 2024 .

### Expansion Plans

The Academy Program is expanding globally :



* **United Kingdom Launch**: A British series will begin in 2026, organized over four rounds from March to August

* **Asia Expansion**: A special event will be held at the Coloane circuit in Macau from January 16-18, 2026

* **Middle East Series**: A regional series in the United Arab Emirates will run from November to February

## Impact and Recognition

Champions of the Future has established itself as a significant platform for young racing talent development. The series' alignment with the FIA Karting calendar positions it as a crucial preparation event for world championship races . The live streaming of events, including on-board cameras and professional broadcasting, has enhanced the series' visibility and appeal to sponsors and spectators .

The Academy Program's focus on female participation has been particularly successful, with female drivers expected to comprise over 30% of participants in some programs [6]. This initiative has received support from organizations like Motorsport UK, which launched its Girls Karting Academy in conjunction with the Champions of the Future Academy Program .
