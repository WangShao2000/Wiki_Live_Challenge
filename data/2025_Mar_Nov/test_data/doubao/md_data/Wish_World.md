# Wish World

**Wish World** is a concept that appears in multiple contexts across literature, media, gaming, and philosophy. It most notably refers to a fictional location in the Sonic the Hedgehog universe where thoughts can create reality, a parallel universe in Doctor Who, a 2022 video game, and a philosophical concept in Freudian psychology. The term has also been adopted by various entertainment properties, including animated series, comics, and music artists [1].

## 1 Fictional locations and concepts

### 1.1 Sonic the Hedgehog universe

In the Sonic the Hedgehog universe, **Wish World** is a location that appears in the Sonic the Comic series published by Fleetway Editions. The location first appeared in Sonic the Comic Issue #154 .

The Wish World exists in a basic state as an uninhabited lifeless husk of clay. However, by wanting something to exist, the thought can be molded by the world into a living creature or object, effectively giving those on the planet near-omnipotence. When the molder loses focus due to things like exhaustion, it causes the Wish World to revert to its original state .

In the storyline, Max Gamble arrived on the Wish World after following Amy Rose and Tekno the Canary through the Ring of Eternity to escape the police. He immediately set about building a world in his image, creating an old-style gangster town. Eventually, Amy and Tekno landed on the Wish World, unaware of the planet's true properties. After a series of adventures involving gangsters and superheroes, Gamble's mind became exhausted, causing the Wish World to revert to its original state .

### 1.2 Doctor Who universe

In the Doctor Who universe, the **Wish World** is a version of Earth created by the Rani and Mrs Flood based on Conrad Clark's warped fantasy of a "perfect world" by channeling and amplifying the power of Desiderium, an infant god .

The Wish World was fixed on Earth and based on Conrad Clark's bigoted and warped fantasy of an ideal world. Non-heterosexual attraction was unthinkable, with the idea of one man referring to another man as "beautiful" causing concern. Women were expected to be wives and mothers; single women spent their time in "blessed contemplation" and married women did not work. Handicapped and disabled people were literally invisible, unnoticed by others. Most people wore old-fashioned clothing despite the year being 2025, with most women wearing skirts or dresses instead of trousers, and men wearing suits. Advanced technology such as computers, mobile phones and tablets was also absent .

Doubt was strictly forbidden in the Wish World. Merely questioning or wondering about anything was considered a way to experience doubt, as was letting the mind wander or expressing distrust in Conrad. People were encouraged to report and avoid anyone experiencing doubt. Doubt in the reality of the Wish World also caused a phenomenon called the Slip, where breakable objects, usually mugs, would fall to the ground and break when someone was doubting nearby .

The Rani created the Wish World as part of a plan to access the Underverse so she could find the long-lost ancient Time Lord Omega. The Doctor and Belinda were trapped in this alternate reality, transformed into the married couple John and Belinda Smith, living at 11 Calico Close in London with their daughter Poppy .

### 1.3 Video games and interactive media

The term **Wish World** appears in several video game contexts:

**Wish World (2022 video game)**: Released on May 11, 2022, this is a 3D platformer developed by Zyph Reon and published by Joy Ark Games. The game is described as "leap into the world of wish, a unique plane of existence filled with adventure! Around the world of wish, the world is unfinished and must be restored, jump into the past and future using dimensional doors!" [4].

**Fable development history**: Fable, initially developed under the working titles "Wish World" and "Project Ego," is an action role-playing video game. The original concept for Wish World was a magical combat game where players could change the world around them to fit their demands. In this early version, players would pick a character archetype with a particular magic specialty, then be placed in a blank, dark void where they would generate the environment around them. Depending on their character type, they would create rivers and forests, mountains and volcanoes, or vast lakes [5].

### 1.4 Animated series and children's media

**Mighty Orbots**: "The Wish World" is an episode from the 1984 animated series Mighty Orbots, which falls under the categories of Saturday morning TV, 1980s television shows, anime, animated, and cartoon [6].

**Star Darlings**: In the Star Darlings series, Wish World is a location featured in the book "Sage and the Journey to Wishworld" (2014), the first book in the series by Ahmet Zappa and Shana Muldoon Zappa. In this series, twelve very special students have been selected from this year's class to reclaim the wish energy that powers their beloved planet, Starland [7].

**Wishenpoof**: "Welcome to Wish World" is a pilot episode for a proposed series for Amazon Studios. The animated pilot revolves around Bianca, who has "wish magic" which means if she wishes to play under the sea then becomes a mermaid [8].

### 1.5 Comics and graphic novels

**Magic Genies of Wishworld**: This is a comic series by IDW Publishing, based on the My Magic Genie and Wish World Kids toy lines owned by Hasbro. In the realm of Wishworld, a realm where magic and technology are one, five wish-granting genies and their guardians protect the realm from the forces of darkness. That all changes when one of the genies, Julie, betrays the other genies and their guardians, sealing them in magic bottles and scattering them throughout Wishworld [9]).

**Eight Billion Genies**: "Eight Billion Genies: Wishworld #1" is a comic published by 3-D Cowboy Comics, written by Charles Soule with art by Ryan Browne, released on October 11, 2023 [10].

## 2 Philosophy and psychology

In philosophy and psychology, **Wish World** refers to a concept in Freudian psychology. Sigmund Freud described religion as "an attempt to get control over the sensory world, in which we are placed, by means of the wish world, which we have developed inside us as a result of biological and psychological necessities" [11].

In narratology and possible worlds theory, Wish World (W-world) is defined as "the axiological system" that occupies personal values or desires, or in other words, what is deemed as good, bad, neutral, or desirable by a character [12]. Marie-Laure Ryan's possible worlds theory identifies wish worlds as one of the private worlds within narrative universes, alongside belief worlds (K-worlds), obligation worlds (O-worlds), and fantasy worlds (F-worlds) .

## 3 Real-world organizations and projects

Several real-world organizations use the name **Wish World**:

**One Wish World Corporation**: A nonprofit organization based in Salt Lake City, Utah, that empowers individuals through education and wellness programs. The organization focuses on family services and had revenue of $52,000 with assets of $2,116 [13].

**We Wish World Foundation**: A private not-for-profit company incorporated in India on August 21, 2023. Its registered office is in Thane, Maharashtra, India [14].

**Hyundai's Wish World**: As part of PROJECT HASHTAG 2024, Hyundai Motor and the National Museum of Modern and Contemporary Art, Korea (MMCA) presented "Wish Office," which features Wish World, a metaverse environment where everyone's wishes are fulfilled. Taking the form of a social experiment, this work, titled "The Wishes," revolves around challenging realities of personal struggles in contemporary society through a game-like experience [15].

## 4 Entertainment industry uses

**Music artists**: Several music artists use the name "Wish World":



* Naman Dhiman, known by his stage name Wish World, is a writer and rapper. His music includes tracks like "Falsafa" (2022) and "Zakham" (2022) [16].

* Max Riley released an album titled "Wishworld" in 2024 with 15 tracks [17].

* Max & Ninely also released an album titled "Wishworld" in September 2024 [18].

**Fan fiction and derivative works**: "Evil has a new Name: Wish World" is a fan fiction story related to the Miraculous Ladybug series, published on Wattpad. The story involves Marinette making a wish that has unexpected consequences [19].
