# Multo (song)

"Multo" is a synth-pop and pop rock ballad by Filipino alternative pop band Cup of Joe . Released on September 14, 2024, as the first single from their debut album "Silakbo" [1], the song has achieved unprecedented success in the Philippine music industry, spending 27 weeks at No. 1 on the Billboard Philippines Hot 100 as of October 2025 . The song's title translates to "ghost" in English [2] and explores themes of being haunted by unresolved emotions and memories [3].

## Background and release

"Multo" was written by Cup of Joe's lead vocalist Raphaell Ridao and his brother Redentor Immanuel Ridao during the COVID-19 lockdown restrictions . The song was born from Raphaell's personal experience of feeling "haunted" by unexplainable thoughts and emotions during the pandemic [3]. "Naumpisahan namin 'yung song with the thought of some feeling, some people feel things na kahit ano'ng gawin mo, kahit pilit mong nilalayo, sumusunod sa'yo," Ridao recalled in an interview with GMA News .

The song was released as a surprise to fans on September 14, 2024, with the band announcing it just three hours before its scheduled release . It was produced by Shadiel Chan  and released under Viva Records . The song marks a more introspective turn in the band's musical direction , departing from their usual upbeat pop sound to explore deeper emotional themes.

## Composition and lyrics

Musically, "Multo" is described as a synth-pop and pop rock ballad with melancholic elements . The song features ethereal melodies and hauntingly poignant lyrics . Rolling Stone Philippines' Elijah Pareño characterized it as a "sad banger" that disguises emotional collapse as a danceable moment .

The lyrics of "Multo" explore themes of longing, confusion, and the pain of unreciprocated feelings [4]. The song uses the metaphor of a "ghost" to represent emotions and memories that persist even after one tries to move on. Key lyrics include "Ako ay dahan-dahang nililibing nang buhay pa" (I am being buried alive little by little) and "Minumulto na 'ko ng damdamin ko" (I am being haunted by my emotions) [5].

The song is part of Cup of Joe's concept album "Silakbo," which explores the five stages of grief [6]. "Multo" specifically embodies the stage of denial, representing the "aching stillness" of refusing to accept loss [6].

## Reception and legacy

Since its release, "Multo" has achieved remarkable commercial success. The song first reached the No. 1 spot on the Billboard Philippines Hot 100 during the week of April 19, 2025, and has held that position for 27 consecutive weeks as of October 11, 2025 . This achievement makes it the longest-running No. 1 song in the chart's history .

The song has been streamed over 60 million times on Spotify  and has 2.55 million daily streams . Cup of Joe has reached over 11.2 million monthly listeners on Spotify, making them the Filipino act with the most monthly listeners . "Multo" also topped Apple Music Philippines' Top OPM Songs of 2025 chart [7].

On social media, particularly TikTok, "Multo" has become a viral hit. The song's refrain "Binaon naman na ang lahat" (I have already buried everything) has become a trending audio clip . The song has been used as an unofficial love song for a popular PBB (Pinoy Big Brother) love team  and has been featured in numerous edits involving K-drama breakups and other emotional content .

Critics have praised "Multo" for its poetic lyricism and cinematic soundscape, calling it a "signature blend of Cup of Joe's emotionally intelligent storytelling and genre-bending artistry" . The song has been described as "the soundtrack of every text you never sent" , resonating with listeners who find themselves dwelling on past relationships and unspoken emotions.

## International recognition

"Multo" has achieved historic milestones for Filipino music internationally. The song became the first Filipino song to enter the Top 40 of Billboard's Global Excl. US Chart, peaking at #33 . This achievement is considered a historic moment for Original Pilipino Music (OPM) .

The song's international success extends beyond chart performance. It has reached audiences in Southeast Asian countries and has been featured on international Spotify editorial playlists . This global reach has helped solidify Cup of Joe's position as ambassadors for OPM on the international stage.

## Music video

The music video for "Multo" features a beautifully minimalist aesthetic  that complements the song's emotional tone. The video has garnered millions of views on YouTube  and has been praised for its ability to enhance the song's haunting atmosphere through visual storytelling.

A stripped-down acoustic performance of "Multo" recorded at Cozy Cove has amassed over 3.3 million views . This performance, featuring raw vulnerability and minimal production, showcases the song's emotional core and the band's ability to deliver powerful performances without elaborate staging.

## Cultural impact

"Multo" has had a profound impact on Filipino popular culture. The song has been embraced as a representation of the collective experience of dealing with unresolved emotions and memories. Lead vocalist Gian Bernardino has emphasized that various interpretations of the song are valid, stating: "Dahil doon marami rin kaming natututunang perspective which inspires us, which motivates us. Kung ano man 'yung interpretation niyo ng 'Multo,' it's all valid" .

The song's success has also contributed to the resurgence of OPM in the global music scene. Keyboardist Xen Gareza attributes part of their international success to social media platforms, saying: "'Yung generation natin, we have the platform to reach international audiences so that's a big help and I believe na even then 'yung talent ng OPM talagang meron na" .

## Legacy

"Multo" has fundamentally altered Cup of Joe's artistic trajectory. The song represents the band's evolution from a group known for upbeat pop songs to serious pop storytellers unafraid to explore deep emotional themes . The lyrics "Pasindi na ng ilaw/ Minumulto na 'ko ng damdamin ko" (Turn on the light/I am being haunted by my emotions) have been interpreted as a metaphor for the band's own artistic journey, shedding their playful image to confront deeper emotional territories .

The song's success has also inspired other Filipino artists and has helped pave the way for increased international recognition of OPM. As Gian Bernardino stated: "Actually, long overdue na eh. OPM is very beautiful. It's very unique. It's something na could make waves internationally and I think it's time na rin kasi dati pa talaga dapat 'to" .

## Critical reception

Music critics have lauded "Multo" for its artistic merit and emotional depth. The song has been praised for its ability to balance infectious melodies with profound lyricism. Rolling Stone Philippines noted that the song "hits you straight in the feels as it delivers a poignant examination of the ghosts of our past, leaving one with a little more clarity on the ruins of their failed relationships" [8].

The song's minimalist approach to production has also been commended. Critics have highlighted how the stripped-back arrangement allows the emotional core of the song to shine through. The combination of Raphaell Ridao's emotive vocals and the song's haunting melody creates what many describe as a "mesmerizing" listening experience .

## Legacy and influence

"Multo" has established itself as a cultural touchstone in contemporary Filipino music. The song's ability to resonate with listeners across different age groups and backgrounds has made it an anthem for a generation navigating the complexities of modern relationships and emotions.

The song's success has also had a significant impact on the OPM industry. It has demonstrated that Filipino artists can create music that is both commercially successful and artistically meaningful. "Multo" has inspired a new wave of OPM artists to explore deeper emotional themes and has helped establish a new standard for Filipino popular music.

As Cup of Joe continues to build on the success of "Multo," the song remains a defining moment in their career and a landmark achievement for Filipino music. The band's lead vocalist Gian Bernardino has expressed gratitude for the song's success, stating: "Nakaka-proud, we're very thankful na part kami ng generation ng OPM na talagang nare-recognize internationally" .
