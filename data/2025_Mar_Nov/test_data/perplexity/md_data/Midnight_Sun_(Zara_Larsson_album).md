# Midnight Sun (Zara Larsson album)

***Midnight Sun*** is the fifth studio album by Swedish singer Zara Larsson. It was released on 26 September 2025 through Sommer House and Epic Records. The album follows her 2024 release *Venus* and features collaborations with songwriters and producers such as MNEK, Margo XS, Zhone, and Helena Gao. The record is described as a dance-pop and electropop project inspired by the phenomenon of the midnight sun in Scandinavia, exploring themes of ambition, romance, and self-reflection.[1][2][3][4]

The album was supported by three singles: "Pretty Ugly", "Midnight Sun", and "Crush". To promote the release, Larsson embarked on the Midnight Sun Tour, which commenced in Europe in October 2025. Upon release, *Midnight Sun* received generally positive reviews from music critics, who complimented its cohesive summer atmosphere and Larsson's vocal performance. The album debuted at number one on the Swedish Albums Chart.[5][3][6][7][1]

# Background and release
Following the release of her fourth studio album *Venus* in February 2024, Larsson began working on new material that would capture the essence of her Swedish heritage. In interviews, she stated that she wanted the album to reflect the "mythically endless" feeling of Swedish summer nights, where the sun barely sets. The project was recorded primarily during writing retreats in the Swedish countryside, marking a shift towards more personal and introspective songwriting compared to her previous works.[6][4][7]

Larsson announced the album title and release date in mid-2025, alongside the tracklist. *Midnight Sun* was released on 26 September 2025.[2][8][9]

# Composition and lyrics
*Midnight Sun* is characterized by its high-energy dance-pop and electropop soundscapes, blending "polished pop production" with elements of trance and Brazilian funk. The album's lyrical content traverses a spectrum of emotions associated with summer, from euphoric partying to late-night introspection.[3][1]

The title track, "Midnight Sun", sets the tone with a "poppy plucky drum and bass" influence, using the concept of 24-hour daylight as a metaphor for freedom and clarity. "Blue Moon" incorporates Baltimore club rhythms to narrate a love story, while "Eurosummer" and "Puss Puss" (Swedish for "kiss kiss") utilize nostalgic imagery of travel and romance.[7][1]

Larsson also explores darker and more complex themes. "Girl's Girl" addresses the guilt of being attracted to a friend's partner, and "The Ambition" discusses the pressure of professional competition. The closing track, "Saturn's Return", delves into the uncertainties of adulthood and the release of control.[1][7]

# Singles
"Pretty Ugly" was released as the album's lead single on 25 April 2025. The track, described as an energetic anthem with influences from early 2000s pop, received attention for its "cheeky" lyrics about vandalism and heartbreak.[5][7][1]

The title track, "Midnight Sun", was issued as the second single in June 2025. Critics highlighted it as a standout track, praising its "hypnotic" chorus and euphoric production.[1][5]

"Crush" was released as the third single in August 2025 prior to the album's launch.[10][3]

# Critical reception
*Midnight Sun* was met with positive responses from music critics. *Rolling Stone* praised the album for sustaining "cheerful atmosphere" and "high-energy dance-pop joy" throughout its duration, noting that Larsson effectively balanced commercial appeal with personal evolution. *Renowned for Sound* commented that while some tracks felt "a bit samey," the album successfully reflected a "playful side" of the singer, with the title track being a particular highlight.[7][1]
