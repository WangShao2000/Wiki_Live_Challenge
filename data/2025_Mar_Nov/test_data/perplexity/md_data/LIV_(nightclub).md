# LIV (nightclub)

**LIV** is a nightclub with two locations in the United States: LIV Miami, located inside the Fontainebleau Miami Beach in Miami Beach, Florida, and LIV Las Vegas, located inside the Fontainebleau Las Vegas on the Las Vegas Strip. Founded in 2008 by David Grutman, LIV Miami opened as part of the Fontainebleau's grand reopening following a $1 billion renovation. Both locations are owned and operated by Groot Hospitality, Grutman's hospitality company. The nightclub's name derives from the Roman numerals for 54, referencing the year the original Fontainebleau hotel opened in 1954, and is also intended as an homage to the legendary nightclub Studio 54. LIV has become recognized internationally as a major venue for electronic dance music and hip-hop entertainment, hosting numerous prominent DJs and performers weekly.

# History

### Opening and Early Years

LIV opened on November 18, 2008, as the primary entertainment centerpiece of the Fontainebleau Miami Beach's grand reopening following the property's comprehensive renovation. The nightclub's inaugural event was the Victoria's Secret fashion show, which was held at the hotel and featured performances by the R&B singer Usher and pop artist Mariah Carey. The opening represented a significant investment in Miami's nightlife infrastructure at the time.[1]

The Fontainebleau hotel, originally designed by architect Morris Lapidus and opened in 1954, had undergone extensive renovation and modernization under the ownership of Jeffrey Soffer's Fontainebleau Development beginning in 2005. The $1 billion renovation project aimed to restore and expand the historic property while preserving its iconic architectural elements.[2]

LIV was designed as an 18,000-square-foot venue featuring a multi-layered, nearly circular amphitheatrical layout that provides unobstructed views for patrons, performers, and DJs alike. The club incorporates three full-service bars, fifty banquette areas, and six private skyboxes. The centerpiece of the venue is an elevated main stage with a distinctive dome ceiling, which has become a recognizable visual identifier of the nightclub.[3]

### Ownership and Corporate Structure

In 2013, Robert F. X. Sillerman's company SFX Entertainment acquired an 80 percent stake in Miami Marketing Group (MMG), the organization that operated LIV. However, SFX experienced significant financial difficulties and filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy protection in 2016. By 2018, David Grutman had reacquired the nightclub and restructured his business ventures under a new holding company called Groot Hospitality, which consolidated his various hospitality investments including the Story nightclub, restaurants, and other venues.[4]

In October 2019, Live Nation Entertainment acquired a majority stake (51 percent) in Groot Hospitality for $25.5 million, at a valuation of $50 million. Grutman remained as CEO of Groot Hospitality alongside executives Chris Cuomo and Mo Garcia, continuing to oversee daily operations and strategic direction.[5]

### 2016 Renovation and The Spider

In 2016, LIV underwent a $10 million renovation that closed the venue for approximately nine weeks. The centerpiece of this renovation was the installation of an innovative lighting structure nicknamed "The Spider," a massive seven-legged LED apparatus featuring multiple staggered screens extending across the ceiling. Designed by Steve Lieberman of SJ Lighting, who had also created lighting installations for major music festivals including Ultra and Electric Daisy Carnival, the Spider cost approximately $3 million to construct and design. The structure allowed for dynamic visual effects that could be synchronized with music and dance performances.[6]

The reopening of LIV following the renovation featured a "Sneak Peek Weekend" that included performances by rapper Travis Scott on the opening night, electronic music producer Skrillex on the second night, and the weekly LIV on Sunday hip-hop event. The renovation marked a significant investment in the venue's technological capabilities and aesthetic appeal, intended to maintain LIV's competitive position among high-end nightclubs globally.[7]

### Expansion to Hard Rock Stadium

In 2012, LIV expanded its brand to Hard Rock Stadium (then known as Sun Life Stadium) in Miami Gardens, Florida. The expansion manifested as a branded level of premium club suites at the stadium venue, extending the LIV brand into the sports entertainment sector while maintaining the core nightclub operation at the Fontainebleau.[8]

### LIV Las Vegas

In December 2023, LIV opened a second major nightclub location at the newly completed Fontainebleau Las Vegas on the Las Vegas Strip in Nevada. The Las Vegas venue features multilevel design with mezzanine-level VIP suites, state-of-the-art sound and lighting technology, and dual-sided LED video walls with 360-degree special effects lighting. The opening marked Groot Hospitality's first significant expansion outside of South Florida and represented an extension of the LIV brand into one of the world's premier entertainment destinations.[9]

### COVID-19 Pandemic Impact

In 2020, LIV Miami closed its doors in response to restrictions imposed by Miami-Dade County authorities during the COVID-19 pandemic. These ordinances limited occupancy and operational capacity for nightclubs and similar venues. The closure represented the first major interruption to operations since the venue's opening in 2008.[10]

# Venue and Design

LIV Miami occupies over 18,000 square feet within the Fontainebleau hotel at 4441 Collins Avenue, Miami Beach. The venue's distinctive amphitheatrical design creates multiple sightline advantages that allow guests throughout the club to view performances and entertainment simultaneously. The architectural layout incorporates a central elevated stage with a distinctive dome ceiling that serves as the venue's architectural signature. Three full-service bars are staffed with experienced mixologists, and the VIP section includes fifty banquette areas and six private skyboxes that provide premium seating options with table service.[11]

The club's technical infrastructure includes extensive lighting and sound systems designed to support major performances and DJ sets. The 2016 installation of "The Spider" lighting structure significantly enhanced the technological capabilities of the venue, enabling complex visual effects synchronized with musical performances.[12]

# Programming and Events

LIV operates on a regularly scheduled programming calendar that features different music genres and themes across multiple nights per week. The venue is open Wednesday through Sunday, with operating hours from 11 p.m. to 5 a.m.[13]

### LIV on Sunday

The most prominent recurring event at LIV is "LIV on Sunday," a weekly hip-hop programming night that has become a signature offering. This event has generated significant cultural references in contemporary hip-hop music, with artists including Drake, Kanye West, and Lil Wayne referencing the event in their work. The weekly gathering attracts a diverse mix of musical talent, celebrity attendees, and enthusiasts of hip-hop and R&B music.[14]

### Musical Programming

LIV regularly features performances by internationally recognized DJs and electronic music producers. Past performers have included David Guetta, Calvin Harris, Tiësto, and Skrillex. The venue also hosts performances during special events and holiday celebrations. The programming varies by night of the week, with some evenings dedicated to house and electronic dance music, others to hip-hop and R&B, and specific nights to Latin hip-hop and reggaeton.[15]

# Recognition and Industry Status

LIV has received substantial recognition within the international nightlife industry. In 2010, the news organization Reuters ranked LIV as the second-highest-grossing nightclub in the world. Rolling Stone magazine included LIV among the best dance clubs in America, while Nightclub & Bar Magazine placed the venue at number five on its annual list of top 100 clubs in the country for 2014. Additional rankings from industry publications positioned LIV among the top nightclubs globally during the 2010s.[16]

The International Nightlife Association's "World's 100 Best Clubs" list has featured LIV, reflecting the venue's status within the global nightlife community. The venue has been described in industry coverage as being among the highest-grossing nightclubs in operation, not solely based on capacity but on the spending patterns and premium pricing available to clientele.[17]

# Ownership and Management

David Grutman, who founded LIV, is recognized as a significant figure in Miami's entertainment and hospitality industry. Rolling Stone magazine has listed Grutman among the 50 most important people in electronic dance music. His broader business portfolio under Groot Hospitality includes the Story nightclub, multiple restaurants (Komodo, Planta South Beach, Swan and Bar Bevy, and Papi Steak), the OTL coffee house, and Happy Place Donuts.[18]

Following the acquisition of majority ownership by Live Nation Entertainment in 2019, the nightclub operates within Live Nation's broader portfolio of entertainment venues and live event operations. This partnership has facilitated expanded programming opportunities and integration with Live Nation's artist and event networks.[19]

# Venue Policies

LIV enforces a strict dress code requiring proper nightclub attire. The venue prohibits tank tops, sleeveless shirts, shorts, excessively baggy clothing, sandals, flip-flops, dirty athletic sneakers, and athletic wear or jerseys. Women are required to wear shoes or heels, and hats are not permitted. The club reserves the right to deny entry at the doorman's discretion based on dress code compliance.[20]

Attendance is restricted to persons 21 years of age or older. The venue requires valid government-issued identification for entry, including U.S. driver's licenses and internationally recognized passports. Foreign government-issued identification documents that do not meet U.S. recognition standards are not accepted.[21]

The nightclub is available for private event bookings seven days per week in addition to its regular public operating schedule.[22]
