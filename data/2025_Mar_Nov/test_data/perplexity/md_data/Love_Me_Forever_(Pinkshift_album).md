# Love Me Forever (Pinkshift album)

**Love Me Forever** is the debut studio album by the American rock band Pinkshift, released on October 21, 2022, through Hopeless Records. The album marks the band's first full-length release after establishing themselves in the pop-punk scene following the viral success of their single "i'm gonna tell my therapist on you" in 2020. Recorded in March 2022 with producer Will Yip at Studio 4 in Conshohocken, Pennsylvania, the twelve-track album expands the band's musical palette beyond their early pop-punk style to incorporate elements of alternative rock, emo, grunge, hard rock, indie rock, and post-hardcore. The album's lyrics examine themes including abusive relationships, trauma, alienation, social media, and the experience of coming of age during the COVID-19 pandemic.

Music critics received the album positively, with publications including Alternative Press and Kerrang! listing it among the best albums of 2022. Paste Magazine and Brooklyn Vegan ranked it among their selections of the year's best punk albums. Four singles supported the album's release: "nothing (in my head)," "i'm not crying you're crying," "GET OUT," and "in a breath." The band embarked on "The Forever Tour," their first headlining North American tour, between October and November 2022. In July 2024, an extended play titled *Love Me for the Summer* featuring remixed versions of album tracks was released in collaboration with producer caco.

# Background and formation

Pinkshift formed in Baltimore, Maryland, when lead vocalist Ashrita Kumar and guitarist Paul Vallejo, both students at Johns Hopkins University, began writing music together in 2018 under the name Sugar Crisis. The pair composed their first demo song, "Mars," which they released in 2019. Seeking a drummer to complete their lineup, Kumar and Vallejo stationed themselves outside a drum practice room on campus and eventually recruited drummer Myron Houngbedji. The group renamed themselves Pinkshift in reference to the redshift phenomenon and subsequently located bassist Erich Weinroth, whom they found through profiles they had created on the dating application Tinder.

The band began performing at events around Johns Hopkins University. On March 13, 2020, they released their debut single "On Thin Ice," followed by their second single "i'm gonna tell my therapist on you," released on July 31, 2020. This second single achieved widespread popularity after the band's video spread across Reddit and other social media platforms, receiving support from established punk musicians including Chris No. 2 of Anti-Flag. The viral attention marked the beginning of Pinkshift's rise to prominence within the contemporary pop-punk scene.[1]

The band's success with their viral single led the members to reconsider their academic pursuits. On April 2, 2021, Pinkshift released their debut extended play, *Saccharine*, which received positive reviews from music critics. These favorable responses encouraged Kumar, Vallejo, and Houngbedji to commit to music as their primary career path rather than continuing their studies. On December 31, 2021, the band announced through Twitter that Weinroth had been removed from their lineup due to what they characterized as cultural and musical differences, leaving the band as a three-member unit. In the same year, Pinkshift signed to Hopeless Records, the independent label known for its roster of punk and alternative rock acts.

# Recording and production

To create *Love Me Forever*, Pinkshift worked with producer Will Yip, an experienced music producer who had previously collaborated with bands including Turnstile, Mannequin Pussy, and the Wonder Years. The band recorded the album over the course of three weeks in March 2022 at Studio 4 located in Conshohocken, Pennsylvania. According to vocalist Kumar, the recording experience functioned similarly to attending summer camp under Yip's guidance and instruction. The band proceeded directly from the recording sessions to embark on a two-month tour with the band PUP, allowing minimal time for rest between the completion of recording and the resumption of live performances.

# Musical composition and themes

*Love Me Forever* represents a stylistic expansion from Pinkshift's previous work in the pop-punk genre. The album incorporates multiple musical influences and styles, including alternative rock, emo, grunge, hard rock, indie rock, and post-hardcore elements. Band members cited different musical backgrounds as contributing to this diverse sonic palette. In interviews, guitarist Paul Vallejo explained that the band members intentionally worked to balance differing musical preferences on the record, with Kumar favoring slower and heavier, grunge-influenced material while Vallejo and drummer Myron Houngbedji leaned toward faster-paced compositions.

The album's lyrical content reflects the band members' personal experiences during the period spanning from late 2020 through 2022, a time encompassing much of the COVID-19 pandemic. Vocalist Kumar wrote lyrics exploring mental health topics including depression, anxiety, numbness, and feelings of isolation. The album addresses interpersonal themes such as heartbreak, betrayal, and abusive relationships, as well as broader societal concerns including the impacts of social media, systemic inequality, and environmental anxiety.

# Track listing and notable songs

The album consists of twelve tracks. The opening track, "i'm not crying you're crying," features the rapid drumming and guitar riffs characteristic of the band's sound and addresses emotional suppression and self-protective denial. "nothing (in my head)" explores themes of mental exhaustion and dissociation, with Kumar singing of feeling "god damn numb" while trapped in repetitive thought patterns and digital overload. "GET OUT" functions as an anthem addressing bodily autonomy and defense against systemic oppression, with the track building from quiet verses to intense choruses.

The album includes "Trust Fall," a composition addressing the aftermath of an abusive relationship, and "Cinderella," which employs metaphorical language to process trauma and betrayal. "BURN THE WITCH" recontextualizes the historical witch trials as a lens for examining contemporary groupthink and misogyny. "the kids aren't alright" presents the band's most direct social critique, specifically addressing perceived civilizational decline and societal injustice.

"in a breath" distinguishes itself as a piano ballad performed solely by vocalist Kumar accompanied by their own piano playing. The track represents Kumar's first public performance on the instrument and functions as a vulnerable moment within the album's sequence. The penultimate track "let me drown" maintains the album's heavier hardcore-influenced sound before the concluding song "Dreamer" presents a triumphant instrumental breakdown suggesting resolution and acceptance.

# Critical reception

*Love Me Forever* received widespread positive reviews from music critics upon its release. Stereogum described the album's sound as "sharp, skilful, and bristling with infectious energy," noting the band's capacity to balance catchiness with energetic performances. Alternative Press and Kerrang! both selected the album for their respective lists of the best albums of 2022, with Kerrang! ranking it at number nineteen on their compilation of fifty best albums released that year. Paste Magazine and Brooklyn Vegan each included *Love Me Forever* on their curated selections of the year's best punk albums. 

Consequence.net praised the album for its exploration of contemporary anxiety and societal collapse themes, describing it as "a useful companion for the world's downfall." FLOOD Magazine characterized the album as "a stunning burst of influences and experiences, all of which coalesce in a swirling swathe of anger and injustice." Multiple reviewers noted that while the album retained pop-punk elements, its incorporation of diverse musical styles and mature lyrical content expanded beyond the typical boundaries of the genre.

In recognition of their emerging prominence within the music industry, Pinkshift received a nomination at the 2022 Heavy Music Awards for Best International Breakthrough Band.

# Singles and promotion

Four songs from *Love Me Forever* were released as singles to promote the album. "nothing (in my head)" received an official music video directed by Daniel Zawodny and premiered on May 31, 2022. The video depicts imagery related to mental exhaustion and digital saturation, visually reinforcing the song's lyrical themes.

"i'm not crying you're crying," released as a single on July 12, 2022, included both a lyric video and a separate visualizer. The track served as the album's official opening and introduced audiences to the record's sonic and thematic direction.

"GET OUT" emerged as the third single, featuring aggressive instrumentation and Ashrita Kumar's commanding vocal delivery. Vocalist Kumar provided a statement regarding the song's meaning, explaining that it functions as an anthem for reclaiming personal space and expressing "pure and unapologetic rage" in defense of bodily autonomy and self-determination. The song's lyrics directly address systemic oppression and colonization.

"in a breath" was released as the album's fourth single, supported by an official live performance video recorded at Retro City Studios and directed by HATE5SIX. The intimate performance captured Kumar's first live piano performance, with footage showing the emotional vulnerability of the solo piece and the supportive response from attending bandmates.

## Tour

Following the album's release, Pinkshift embarked on "The Forever Tour," their first headlining North American tour. The tour commenced on October 20, 2022, in the band's hometown of Baltimore, Maryland, at the Ottobar venue, with the official album release show occurring the following day on October 21 in Washington, D.C. The tour extended through November 2022, concluding on November 18 in Boston, Massachusetts, at Brighton Music Hall.

Throughout the tour, Pinkshift played venues across the continental United States and Canada, including stops in major cities such as Los Angeles, Portland, Chicago, Detroit, and New York. The tour featured support from musicians Jigsaw Youth and Yasmin Nur. Earlier in the year, prior to their headlining tour, the band had participated in festival performances and supported other established acts including PUP, Destroy Boys, 100 gecs, and Bring Me the Horizon.

## Remix project

In July 2024, Pinkshift released *Love Me for the Summer*, an extended play developed in collaboration with music producer caco. The project features five remixed versions of songs from *Love Me Forever*, each reimagining the original tracks within different musical genres and production styles. The remixes include a pool remix of "BURN THE WITCH," a house remix of "the kids aren't alright" (retitled "we're all crying"), a trap remix of "nothing (in my head)," a DARIACORE remix of "the kids aren't alright" (retitled "the kids are totally screwed"), and an emo remix of "Trust Fall."
