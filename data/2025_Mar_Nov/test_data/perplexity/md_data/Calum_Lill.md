# Calum Lill

**Calum Lill** is a British actor born in 1995 or 1996. He is best known for his portrayal of Joel Deering, a solicitor villain in the ITV soap opera *Coronation Street*, where he appeared from 2023 to 2024. Prior to this prominent role, Lill worked in various capacities while pursuing his acting career, including as a car salesman and at a golf club, and maintained guest appearances in television drama series.[1]

# Early life

Lill was born in 1995 or 1996 and comes from a working-class family. During his childhood, he watched the BBC drama series *Waterloo Road*, which became his favorite show. He revealed that his childhood ambition was to obtain an acting role in *Waterloo Road*. Notably, his family home was used as a filming location for the series during its early years, coinciding with when his family was moving out.[1]

# Acting career

### Early television work

Following his graduation, Lill began his professional acting career with guest roles in British television dramas. In 2019, he portrayed Chez Wilson-Needham, a lead guest character, in an episode of the BBC soap opera *Doctors*. That same year, he appeared as Conor Henson in an episode of the medical drama *Holby City*. Speaking about these experiences, Lill commented that the two days of filming for *Doctors* felt particularly significant to him at the time.[1]

The onset of the COVID-19 pandemic in the United Kingdom significantly impacted Lill's career trajectory. With the television industry shutting down, he faced considerable financial hardship and nearly became homeless during this period.[1]

### Employment outside acting

Due to difficulties in securing consistent acting work, Lill undertook various employment roles between acting opportunities. He worked as a car salesman, a position he found particularly unfulfilling. He also worked at Junkyard Golf Club and at a paella stand at festivals. During this time, he moved from London to Manchester with his girlfriend to save money for a house while maintaining his pursuit of acting roles. At the paella stand, he met actors Rupert Hill and Jenny Platt, both of whom had appeared in *Coronation Street*, and they encouraged him to continue auditioning for the soap, assuring him that producers would contact him if interested.[1]

### Hollyoaks and breakthrough

In 2021, Lill was cast as Carlton Smith, a recurring character, in the Channel 4 soap opera *Hollyoaks*. This role provided him with substantial television experience in the soap opera format.[1]

### Coronation Street

In May 2023, Lill auditioned for the role of Joel Deering in *Coronation Street*, a solicitor character. Following his audition, he was selected from a group of five or six candidates for a final callback, after which he was ultimately cast in the role. Lill described being selected for the part as "incredible," stating: "I've never felt anything like it. I was walking on air...especially being a working class northern lad."[1]

#### Joel Deering character

Lill's portrayal of Joel Deering became a central focus of the show's storylines. The character was initially presented as a love interest for the character Dee-Dee Bailey (played by Channique Sterling-Brown). However, the character's true nature as a villain was gradually revealed to viewers through an extensive and serious narrative arc involving the sexual exploitation and coercion of a younger character named Lauren.[1]

In developing the storyline, *Coronation Street* worked with the Maggie Oliver Foundation, a charity supporting survivors of sexual abuse, from the beginning of the narrative to ensure accurate and respectful portrayal of the subject matter. This collaboration aimed to create content that could be truthful and potentially helpful to viewers experiencing similar situations.[1]

Lill received critical acclaim for his villainous portrayal. His performance resulted in multiple award nominations, including a nomination for Best Villain at the 2024 Inside Soap Awards. Additionally, the storyline element of Lauren's disappearance was shortlisted for "Best Showstopper" at the same awards ceremony. In 2025, Lill was shortlisted for Villain of the Year at the British Soap Awards.[2][3]

Lill departed from the role at the conclusion of his character's storyline in 2024.[1]

### Pantomime and recent work

Following his exit from *Coronation Street*, Lill performed in the pantomime production *Jack and the Beanstalk* at Bournemouth Pavilion, where he portrayed the character Fleshcreep. In November 2024, he appeared on the ITV morning show *Good Morning Britain* to promote an ITV competition to viewers.[1]

In 2025, Lill announced his involvement in theatrical projects. He starred in Tim Whitnall's play *The Sociable Plover* at the Old Red Lion Theatre in Islington from May 13-24, 2025. That same month, it was reported that he would briefly reprise his role of Joel on *Coronation Street* in an undisclosed storyline.[1]
