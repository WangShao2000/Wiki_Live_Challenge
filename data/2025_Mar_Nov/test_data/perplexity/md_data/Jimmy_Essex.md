# Jimmy Essex

**Jimmy Essex** is a British actor, dancer, and musician from Harrow, London. He is known for his television roles including Adam Donovan in the soap opera *Hollyoaks* (2016–2018) and appearances in the fantasy drama series *The Sandman* (2022–2025). Essex has also performed in stage productions and competed on the British talent competition *The X Factor* in 2012.

### Early life and education

Essex was born in 1984 or 1985 in Harrow, London. He trained at the Guildford School of Acting, graduating in 2005. During his training and early career, Essex developed experience in both acting and vocal performance.[1][2]

### Career

**Musical theatre and early performances**

Essex appeared in musical theatre productions including *Dreamboats and Petticoats* and *Mamma Mia!*, performing in national and international tours. In 2014, he appeared in the music video *Mr Bowler Presents - Hideaway*. He has worked as a backup dancer, including performances supporting Janet Jackson.[2][1]

**The X Factor (2012)**

In 2012, Essex competed in the ninth series of *The X Factor*, performing with the band MITSOTU, an acronym for "Music Is The Soul Of The Universe". The band consisted of Essex, James Collins, and Holly Cooper. The members had previously performed in London schools during 2011. All three members had "MITSOTU" tattooed on their left arms.[1]

The band auditioned for the show in June 2012 before an audience of 6,000 people and advanced after receiving four "yes" votes from the judges. The group did not progress to the live finals, being eliminated in favor of the competing group MK1. After the competition ended, the band continued performing at various venues including Kensington Roof Gardens.[1]

In a 2017 reflection on his *X Factor* experience, Essex said it "was such a fun time in my life and I did it with my two best friends." He stated he was glad the band's time on the show ended as it did, describing it as leaving "the door open for other things." He also noted that while he still enjoyed singing, he could not imagine performing publicly anymore.[1]

**Hollyoaks (2016–2018)**

In March 2016, Essex was announced as joining the cast of the British soap opera *Hollyoaks* as Adam Donovan. His character was introduced as one of three half-brothers alongside Jesse Donovan (Luke Jerdy) and Liam Donovan (Maxim Baldry/Jude Monk McGowan), all related to the character Grace Black (Tamara Wall). Essex appeared as a regular character from April 2016 until May 2018, with a final video recording appearance in December 2018.[3][1]

**Film and television (2016–2025)**

In 2016, Essex appeared in Coca-Cola advertisements filmed in Madrid.[1]

He appeared in the 2020 horror film *A Dark Path*, playing a character named Peter. In 2020, Essex performed the lead role of Robert Finch in the theatrical production *GHBoy* at the Charing Cross Theatre in London. The play, which explored themes related to the East London gay party scene, ran until December 20, 2020.[4][5]

Essex appeared in multiple episodes of the Netflix fantasy drama series *The Sandman* (2022–2025), which is based on Neil Gaiman's comic book series, portraying the character Carrion.[6]

In November 2025, Essex appeared in the British soap opera *EastEnders*, playing a character named Rich.[2]
